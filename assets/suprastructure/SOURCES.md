# The Suprastructure assets

Original Overprint artwork and outlined masthead from The Suprastructure, used here with the publication’s About text.

- `masthead.svg`: compact 2688 × 320 masthead.
- `logo.svg`: publication logo fitted to a circular crop.
- `ribbon.svg`: original full ribbon composition.

The SVGs contain their own vector artwork and require no external fonts or images. The masthead lettering derives from Special Gothic Expanded One, licensed under the included SIL Open Font License. Official font source: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/specialgothicexpandedone .
