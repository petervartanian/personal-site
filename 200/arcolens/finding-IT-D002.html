The severity-threshold share fell while the count rose

  • Suggested wording
  • Figures
  • Chart settings
  • Confidence and limitations

Suggested wording

In the September 27, 2026 export, 35 of 80 records dated 2021 (43.8%) and 90 of 452 dated 2025 (19.9%) have a direct or indirect severity score of at least 3 in any of the original ten harm dimensions. The qualifying count rose from 35 to 90 even as its share fell. Model-inferred scores are excluded, and zero or missing scores are treated as not qualifying. Changes in reporting, coverage, and classification can affect this comparison. It cannot establish whether AI became safer.

Keep in view: The measure excludes inferred scores and treats zero or missing scores as not qualifying. A lower recorded share does not show that AI became safer. Use a supporting table.

Confidence and limitations

  • Confidence
    • Calculation. High for the exported classifications and exact annual comparisons in this export.
    • Interpretation. Moderate as a description of database composition; low for any claim about real-world incidence, risk or safety.
  • Limitations
    • The count meeting the threshold rose from 35 to 90 while its share fell.
    • This depends on the original ten dimensions, direct/indirect channels and threshold 3.
    • The present aggregate chart cannot faithfully reproduce this channel-specific calculation.
