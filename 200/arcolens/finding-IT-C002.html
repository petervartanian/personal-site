Capability and robustness: a falling share, a rising count

  • Suggested wording
  • Figures
  • Chart settings
  • Confidence and limitations

Suggested wording

In the September 27, 2026 export, records primarily tagged “Lack of capability or robustness” fell from 41.2% of records dated 2021 to 10.0% of those dated 2025, while their count rose from 33 to 45. Other categories grew faster. The change describes database composition and does not establish that AI systems became more robust.

Keep in view: The comparison starts at a local high point in 2021. A falling share does not establish that AI systems became more robust.

Confidence and limitations

  • Confidence
    • Calculation. High for primary/any-label counting and exact annual comparisons in the export.
    • Interpretation. Moderate for a compositional database statement; low for any claim that robustness has improved or failures have become rarer.
  • Limitations
    • 2021 is a local high point in the longer series. The finding is specific to 2021-2025.
    • The category count rose from 33 to 45; its share fell because the rest of the database grew faster.
    • The partial 2026 cohort differs from 2025. It is excluded from these complete-year comparisons.
