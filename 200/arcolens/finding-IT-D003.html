Suggested wording

In the September 27, 2026 export, the share of incident records tagged “Tool” for behavior type rose each year from 2021 through 2025, from 19 of 80 (23.8%) to 256 of 452 (56.6%). Each denominator includes all records dated that year. The pattern describes database annotations and may reflect changes in reporting and coverage.
