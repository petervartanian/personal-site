False or misleading information is the primary tag for 13.9% of 2025 records

  • Suggested wording

Suggested wording

In the September 27, 2026 export, 63 of 452 records dated 2025 (13.9%) were primarily classified as “False or misleading information.” The annual figures were 1 of 80 (1.25%) in 2021, 9 of 108 (8.3%) in 2022, 21 of 179 (11.7%) in 2023, and 32 of 304 (10.5%) in 2024. The 2021 figure rests on a single record. These classifications come from a model. Reporting and coverage can affect the series.

Keep in view: The 2021 figure rests on one record, and the share dipped in 2024. Reporting and classification can affect the series. The exact-year chart preset has not been verified end to end.

The partial-year share is below 2025; the longer-term finding does not imply uninterrupted growth. Confidence and limitations

  • Confidence
    • Calculation. High for the exported classifications and exact annual comparisons in this export.
    • Interpretation. Moderate as a description of database composition; low for any claim about real-world incidence, risk or safety.
  • Limitations
    • The 2021 numerator is just one record: this is an exploratory finding from a small base.
    • The share did not rise every year: it fell between 2023 and 2024.
    • Reporting, classification and coverage changes remain alternative explanations.
