Human-controlled tags grew as supervised and autonomous tags fell

  • Suggested wording
  • Figures
  • Chart settings
  • Confidence and limitations

Suggested wording

In the September 27, 2026 export, the “Human-controlled” tag accounts for 19 of 80 records dated 2021 (23.8%) and 349 of 452 dated 2025 (77.2%). Over the same period, “Human-supervised” falls from 52.5% to 14.2%, and “Full autonomy” from 23.8% to 8.6%. The national-security autonomy field covers all records in this comparison. These model-assigned tags do not measure oversight quality or changes in AI deployment.

Keep in view: This field is populated across the full incident-record collection. Its model-assigned tags do not measure oversight quality or changes in AI deployment. Use a supporting table.

Confidence and limitations

  • Confidence
    • Calculation. High for the exported classifications and exact annual comparisons in this export.
    • Interpretation. Moderate as a description of database composition; low for any claim about real-world incidence, risk or safety.
  • Limitations
    • The public framework defines Human-controlled as AI assisting while humans make final decisions. The tag’s accuracy in individual records has not been established.
    • The national-security autonomy field is populated across this export. Its application outside national-security incidents requires care.
    • Use a supporting table. The current public chart has no grouping for this field.
