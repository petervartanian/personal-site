Intentional and human-caused incidents

Supported

Published wording

The proportion of intentionally caused incidents has increased each year from 2021-2025, as has the proportion attributed to Human rather than AI entities.

Source page (captured 2026-09-27)

Assessment

causal_intent: 2021: 17/80 (21.2%); 2022: 36/108 (33.3%); 2023: 90/179 (50.3%); 2024: 192/304 (63.2%); 2025: 291/452 (64.4%). causal_entity: 2021: 22/80 (27.5%); 2022: 41/108 (38.0%); 2023: 94/179 (52.5%); 2024: 198/304 (65.1%); 2025: 310/452 (68.6%). 2026 year-to-date through 2026-09-27 (incomplete; context only, not comparable with whole years): 130/231 (56.3%); 2026 year-to-date through 2026-09-27 (incomplete; context only, not comparable with whole years): 142/231 (61.5%).

Original time window: 2021–2025 inclusive, explicitly stated; two parallel proportions in one published bullet.

Proposed wording

Among records dated 2021–2025, the share tagged Intentional rose each year, from 17 of 80 (21.25%) to 291 of 452 (64.38%). The share whose causal entity was tagged Human also rose each year, from 22 of 80 (27.5%) to 310 of 452 (68.58%). These tags are model assessments of causation.

Display context. The numerical findings below use the September 27, 2026 Arcola export of 1,698 incident records. They describe this database and its automated classifications. Reporting, coverage, and classification changes can affect the patterns; these results do not estimate the prevalence of AI harm.
