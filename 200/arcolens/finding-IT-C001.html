Recorded annual counts rose from 2021 to 2025

Suggested wording

In the September 27, 2026 export, the number of incident records dated in each year increased from 80 in 2021 to 108 in 2022, 179 in 2023, 304 in 2024, and 452 in 2025. These record counts can reflect changes in reporting and coverage as well as underlying incidents.

Keep in view: The increase is specific to 2021–2025: 2020 had more records than 2021. Changes in reporting and coverage can change the series.

Confidence and limitations

  • Confidence
    • Calculation. High for the retained record counts and date parsing. Estimating new submissions, distinct events, or affected people would require additional data.
    • Interpretation. Low for actual incident incidence or safety changes. Moderate as a description of this database snapshot.
  • Limitations
    • The unit is an incident record. Counts of distinct events, reports, or people require separate verification.
    • The choice of 2021 matters: 2020 has 92 records, above 2021. Changes in reporting and coverage can change the series.
    • Measuring historical backfill requires earlier exports or entry timestamps. The documented ID assignment rule alone cannot establish entry dates or past classifications.
