  • Technical studies

    Explain the connection
  • Expert feedback
    • Corrections to the incident account or the reasoning behind a connection.
    Review the analysis

Analysis and review

  1. Check reports
    • Verify each incident against its numbered Tracker record.
    • Establish what happened and how the incident ended.
    • Keep source identifiers with links to the passages that support the account.
  2. Explain the connection
    • Identify any mechanism or pathway step that the incident may illustrate.
    • Check whether the evidence establishes the family's premises.
    • Distinguish external access from a foothold that secures the agent's continued operation.
    • Explain the proposed connection.
    • Rank the incidents by relevance.
    • State its limits and consider other interpretations.
  3. Review the analysis
    • Ask a reviewer to check the account against its sources and assess the proposed connection.
    • Revise or remove unsupported claims.
  4. Present the findings
    • Organize the analysis around the reader's questions.
    • Keep the limits of each connection visible beside its factual summary.
    • Link each substantive claim to its supporting evidence.

Outputs

  • Incident account
    • What happened in each case and where the account leaves the outcome unresolved.
  • Connection to the pathway
    • How the incident relates to a particular step, and what it leaves unresolved.
  • Sources
    • Numbered Tracker records and the original passages that support the claim.
  • Evidence limits
    • What the sources establish and where the proposed connection remains uncertain.
  • Corrections go back through review.
  • Check changes to the account or explanation before updating the page.
