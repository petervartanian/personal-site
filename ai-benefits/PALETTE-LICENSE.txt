IRIS PALETTE: PROVENANCE AND TOOLING NOTICE

Iris is a custom, manually selected 8-bit sRGB palette for the AI Benefits
Database. Its original color selections do not incorporate a third-party
palette. No third-party palette license applies to these values.

Normal colors, ordered [0, -1, -2, -3, -4]:
#F5F6FA, #C6B7EF, #9A79D1, #74449F, #44215D

The colors support a proposed benefit-only classification. They do not validate
the grade definitions or imply equal distances between grades. Unassessed is
separate from the ordered scale.

SIMULATION METHODS AND SOFTWARE

Color-vision simulations were computed with Colorspacious 1.1.2 using the
Machado, Oliveira, and Fernandes (2009) model at severity 100. Rendered outputs
are clipped to sRGB and quantized to 8-bit. Grayscale preserves relative
luminance before rounding. These approximate simulations support design review;
they do not establish accessibility for every observer or display.

Model: https://doi.org/10.1109/TVCG.2009.113
