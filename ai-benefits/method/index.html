Method

One shared collection. Different views for different tasks.

5 reported episodes and 5 comparative studies.

  1. Select

    Public research and institutional accounts with a reported gain and an identifiable AI role. Marketing claims alone do not qualify.

  2. Check

    Trace claims to source passages. Distinguish comparisons from reported episodes. Source checks do not independently verify events.

  3. Follow

    Keep review dates and published versions. Add corrections, later findings, and contrary evidence to the record and its history.

Scope

Benefits only, measured in their own terms, without a universal grade. Inclusion does not establish prevalence, net benefit, or overall endorsement.

Prepared through AI-assisted source review and editorial selection.

Tailor the view. Keep the same records, sources, limits, and saved versions. Original sources retain their own terms.
