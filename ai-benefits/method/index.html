Method

We document what AI helps people do, create, discover, and sustain.

Inclusion

Each record needs a reported gain, an identifiable AI role, and a public source. Keep research findings distinct from institutional accounts. Include costs, tradeoffs, and evidence gaps. Marketing claims alone do not qualify.

Source review establishes what an account reports. It does not independently verify the event. This selected collection does not estimate prevalence or net benefit.

Records

Gains
Describe what changed, using measures suited to the outcome. Separate observed gains, new opportunities, and expectations.
Beneficiaries
Identify who benefited and who could access the benefit. Note differences between groups and gaps in the evidence.
Evidence
Link claims to sources and comparisons. Describe what AI and people contributed, and what the evidence cannot establish.
Over time
Track how long gains last and what further work they enable. Keep observed persistence separate from expectations.
Replication
Document repeated results and the conditions involved. Where transfer is untested, state the questions to investigate.

Outcomes retain their own measures. There is no universal benefit grade.

Review and updates

Records are assembled through AI-assisted source review and editorial selection. The review date identifies a source check, not independent verification. Each record links to the passages supporting its claims and keeps earlier published versions.

Corrections and later contrary evidence belong in the record and its history. Inclusion does not imply overall endorsement.

Reuse

Download the current search results or an individual brief. Cite the saved version and original sources, retaining the comparator and limitations. Original sources retain their own terms.
