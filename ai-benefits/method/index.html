Begin with the benefit.

We document what AI helps people do, create, discover, and sustain. Each record connects a reported gain to its beneficiaries, evidence, and conditions for reproduction.

Gains and possibilities

Describe what changed and what became possible, using measures suited to the outcome. Keep achievements, newly available choices, and anticipated gains distinct.

Beneficiaries and distribution

Identify the people, organizations, or communities experiencing the gain. Record who has access, how benefits are distributed, and whose experience remains unreported.

Evidence and contribution

Link each claim to its source and comparison. Describe the contributions of AI, people, and institutions, alongside the source’s methods and material limits.

Persistence and accumulation

Track follow-up, continuing use, retained capabilities, and gains that enable further work. Distinguish observed persistence from expectations about future benefits.

Reproduction

Record where similar gains recur and the skills, infrastructure, and institutional conditions involved. Identify conditions to investigate when reproduction has not yet been tested.

What belongs here

Cases need a specific reported gain, an identifiable AI role, and a public source that can be checked. Research findings and institutional accounts retain their source labels. Marketing claims alone do not qualify. Relevant costs, tradeoffs, and unresolved questions remain attached to the case.

Assessment

Outcomes retain their own measures. A benefit profile records gains, beneficiaries, persistence, and conditions for reproduction. These fields are a proposed approach, not a validated universal ranking.

Reading a source establishes what it reports. Independent verification and causal attribution require further evidence. This selected collection does not estimate prevalence or net benefit.

Foundations

These references inform the approach. Their domain-specific scales do not establish a universal AI-benefit grade.

  • UNDP Human Development Report 2025 ↗

    People’s capabilities, choices, and opportunities provide the starting point.

  • Impact Frontiers ↗

    Separates the amount of change, people reached, and duration.

  • Trustible Benefits Framework ↗

    Organizes benefits around people, organizations, and society.

  • WHO ICF ↗

    Domain precedent for documenting functioning and environmental support.

  • EICAT+ ↗

    Ecological precedent for documenting positive effects and their persistence.

  • ESMO Magnitude of Clinical Benefit Scale ↗

    Clinical precedent for evaluating benefit against outcome-specific criteria.

  • IQWiG General Methods ↗

    Clinical precedent for distinguishing the size of added benefit from certainty.

Use the collection

Cite the case’s permanent link and original source. Retain the outcome measure, source type, and reported limitations when comparing or reusing records. The structured records are available as CSV and JSON. Original sources retain their own terms.
