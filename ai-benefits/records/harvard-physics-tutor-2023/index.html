Stronger physics learning with an AI tutor

Harvard University. Fall 2023 study, published June 2025

Read the source ↗Compare this recordAI-B-006
OutcomeEvidenceReplicationSources and citation

The reported benefit

Immediate physics test performance improved with the designed AI tutor: an adjusted effect of 0.63 standard deviations versus classroom active learning.

Who benefits
The eligible study population comprised 194 undergraduate physics students. The reported post-test counts describe observations in a crossover study, not independent parallel groups or proof that every student benefited.
AI contribution
Question-specific guidance and individualized feedback.
Human contribution
Instructors supplied curriculum, correct solutions, scaffolding, prompts, and videos. Students solved problems.

What the evidence supports

Randomized crossover study

Two lessons with conditions reversed between groups. Reported post-test observations: 142 AI, 174 classroom. The adjusted effect controls for prior proficiency, lesson, test version, and time on task.

Attribution and limits

One course with a test ceiling. The instructional package included self-pacing, videos, and at-home study. The comparison does not isolate generic AI or establish teacher replacement. Retention and later transfer were not measured.

Over time

Immediate assessment. Retention and later transfer were not measured.

Context for interpretation

Any wider deployment assessment would need to consider costs, access, and the reliability of feedback.

Could this benefit travel?

Expert-designed lessons, structured practice, and reliable feedback are candidate conditions to test.

Another setting

  • How should teachers adapt the lessons and feedback to a different subject?
  • Would the gain recur against that institution’s usual teaching?

More people

  • Who can access the tutor, and who needs additional support?
  • Do gains vary with prior knowledge or learning needs?

A longer horizon

  • Do students retain the learning months later?
  • How could retained learning expand opportunities over a lifetime?

These are research questions. Transfer to other settings has not been established by this record.
