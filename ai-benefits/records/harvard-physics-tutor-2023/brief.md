# Higher physics test scores with an AI tutor

Harvard University. Fall 2023 study, published June 2025.

AI Benefits Database, AI-B-006

## Task and setting

Tutoring introductory physics. Harvard University, United States. AI lessons were completed at home.

## Outcome

Immediate physics test performance improved with the designed AI tutor: an adjusted effect of 0.63 standard deviations versus classroom active learning.

The eligible study population comprised 194 undergraduate physics students. The reported post-test counts describe observations in a crossover study, not independent parallel groups or proof that every student benefited.

## Compared with

A designed AI tutoring package was compared with classroom active learning in a randomized crossover study.

## Evidence

Randomized crossover study. Two lessons with conditions reversed between groups. Reported post-test observations: 142 AI, 174 classroom. The adjusted effect controls for prior proficiency, lesson, test version, and time on task.

One course with a test ceiling. The instructional package included self-pacing, videos, and at-home study. The comparison does not isolate generic AI or establish teacher replacement. Retention and later transfer were not measured.

Immediate assessment.

Any wider deployment assessment would need to consider costs, access, and the reliability of feedback.

## Contributions

**AI:** Question-specific guidance and individualized feedback.

**People:** Instructors supplied curriculum, correct solutions, scaffolding, prompts, and videos. Students solved problems.

**System:** PS2 Pal. The article names the tutor PS2 Pal and identifies GPT-4. It does not give an exact GPT-4 snapshot or tutor release.

**Timing:** Fall 2023, course weeks 9–10.

## Conditions

- The study covered surface tension and fluid flow in an undergraduate course.
- Instructor-prepared prompts, solutions, videos, and structured practice accompanied GPT-4.
- Students used the tutor at home at their own pace.

The comparison tests this instructional package on immediate assessments, without establishing durable learning or results in other subjects.
