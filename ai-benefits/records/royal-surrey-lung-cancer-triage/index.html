The reported benefit

Dianne Covey was contacted about an abnormality the afternoon of her X-ray. Further investigation identified stage I lung cancer.

Who benefits
One patient, Dianne Covey, described in the hospital's public account.
AI contribution
Flagged the X-ray for urgent clinical review.
Human contribution
Her GP referred her. Hospital staff interpreted findings, contacted her, and investigated.
