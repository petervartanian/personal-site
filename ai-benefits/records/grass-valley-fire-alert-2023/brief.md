# Firefighters alerted before the first 911 call

Near Grass Valley, California. September 11, 2023.

AI Benefits Database, AI-B-002
Reviewed 2026-10-19
Permanent record: https://petervartanian.xyz/ai-benefits/records/grass-valley-fire-alert-2023/

## Outcome

AI alerted at 5:19 a.m. Firefighters were on scene before the first 911 call at 6:01 a.m. The fire remained under a quarter acre.

Earlier notification to firefighters is the reported operational benefit. Nearby residents are intended beneficiaries, but an individual or community welfare improvement is not measured.

## Evidence

First-party program account. The university operating the camera program reports the alert, first-call times, and final containment.

Earlier notification and containment are observed. A larger fire, specific losses, or casualties avoided are not established. The comparison is with the first 911 call, not every possible detection route.

One fire episode. Downstream community gains unmeasured.

No event-specific adverse effects are reported. False alarms, response costs, and surveillance effects are not evaluated.

## Contributions

**AI:** Detected a possible ignition in the Wolf Mountain 1 camera feed and alerted firefighters.

**People:** The program uses trained watchstanders to confirm detections. Firefighters responded and contained this fire.

**System:** ALERTCalifornia and CAL FIRE fire-detection AI. UC San Diego identifies Digital Path as the industry partner and ALERTCalifornia and CAL FIRE as program partners. The article reports no model identifier or software release.

**Timing:** Day and local alert time explicitly reported.

## Replication

Camera coverage, confirmation, dispatch, and available crews are candidate conditions to test.
