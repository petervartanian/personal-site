Firefighters received an earlier alert

Near Grass Valley, California. September 11, 2023

Read the source ↗Compare this recordAI-B-002
OutcomeEvidenceReplicationSources and citation

The reported benefit

AI alerted at 5:19 a.m. Firefighters were on scene before the first 911 call at 6:01 a.m. The fire remained under a quarter acre.

Who benefits
Earlier notification to firefighters is the reported operational benefit. Nearby residents are intended beneficiaries, but an individual or community welfare improvement is not measured.
AI contribution
Detected a possible ignition in the Wolf Mountain 1 camera feed and alerted firefighters.
Human contribution
The program uses trained watchstanders to confirm detections. Firefighters responded and contained this fire.
System
ALERTCalifornia and CAL FIRE fire-detection AI
Provider
Digital Path
Model or family
Not reported

System evidence
UC San Diego identifies Digital Path as the industry partner and ALERTCalifornia and CAL FIRE as program partners. The article reports no model identifier or software release.
Timing
Day and local alert time explicitly reported.

What the evidence supports

First-party program account

The university operating the camera program reports the alert, first-call times, and final containment.

UC San Diego reports the alert and call times, crews already on scene, and containment below a quarter acre.

Attribution and limits

Earlier notification and containment are observed. A larger fire, specific losses, or casualties avoided are not established. The comparison is with the first 911 call, not every possible detection route.

Over time

One fire episode. Downstream community gains unmeasured.

Context for interpretation

No event-specific adverse effects are reported. False alarms, response costs, and surveillance effects are not evaluated.

Could this benefit travel?

Camera coverage, confirmation, dispatch, and available crews are candidate conditions to test.

Another setting

  • Can cameras cover the relevant terrain?
  • Who confirms an alert and coordinates an effective response?

More people

  • Which communities can the camera network reach?
  • Can confirmation and response capacity grow with coverage?

A longer horizon

  • Do earlier alerts consistently translate into useful responses?
  • What operational learning improves the system over repeated use?

These are research questions. Transfer to other settings has not been established by this record.
