An incidental finding led to diagnosis and treatment

Turkey / India multicenter study. April 2023–December 2024 study period

Read the source ↗Compare this recordAI-B-003
OutcomeEvidenceReplicationSources and citation

The reported benefit

A 57-year-old man received a stage IB lung adenocarcinoma diagnosis and underwent right upper lobectomy.

Who benefits
One 57-year-old patient, identified as Case 1 in the published case series.
AI contribution
Flagged a lung nodule on a chest X-ray obtained before anti-TNF treatment for ulcerative colitis.
Human contribution
Clinicians confirmed the finding, pursued imaging and biopsy, and performed surgery.
System
qXR
Provider
Qure.ai
Model or family
Deep learning for chest X-ray nodule detection
Version
Not reported
System evidence
The CREATE paper names qXR from Qure.ai and describes a deep-learning algorithm. It does not specify a product version or checkpoint.
Timing
Study period. Individual episode dates are unreported.

What the evidence supports

Patient episode in a peer-reviewed case series

Documents detection, diagnosis, and treatment for Case 1.

The CREATE study documents detection, diagnosis, and treatment for Case 1 of five selected patients.

Attribution and limits

No comparator or long-term follow-up. Survival benefit unestablished. AstraZeneca funded the study. One author worked for Qure.ai.

Over time

Treatment reported. Long-term follow-up unavailable.

Context for interpretation

False flags can trigger unnecessary investigations. Accuracy and net benefit cannot be estimated from these selected cases.

Could this benefit travel?

Clinician review, confirmatory tests, and treatment referral are candidate conditions to test.
