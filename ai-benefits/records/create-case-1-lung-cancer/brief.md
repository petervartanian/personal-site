# Incidental lung cancer diagnosed and treated

Turkey / India multicenter study. April 2023–December 2024 study period.

AI Benefits Database, AI-B-003
Reviewed 2026-10-19
Permanent record: https://petervartanian.xyz/ai-benefits/records/create-case-1-lung-cancer/

## Outcome

A 57-year-old man received a stage IB lung adenocarcinoma diagnosis and underwent right upper lobectomy.

One 57-year-old patient, identified as Case 1 in the published case series.

## Evidence

Patient episode in a peer-reviewed case series. Documents detection, diagnosis, and treatment for Case 1.

No comparator or long-term follow-up. Survival benefit unestablished. AstraZeneca funded the study. One author worked for Qure.ai.

Treatment reported. Long-term follow-up unavailable.

False flags can trigger unnecessary investigations. Accuracy and net benefit cannot be estimated from these selected cases.

## Contributions

**AI:** Flagged a lung nodule on a chest X-ray obtained before anti-TNF treatment for ulcerative colitis.

**People:** Clinicians confirmed the finding, pursued imaging and biopsy, and performed surgery.

**System:** qXR. The CREATE paper names qXR from Qure.ai and describes a deep-learning algorithm. It does not specify a product version or checkpoint.

**Timing:** Study period. Individual episode dates are unreported.

## Replication

Clinician review, confirmatory tests, and treatment referral are candidate conditions to test.
