Two hikers found and guided to safety

Near Jindabyne, Australia. Reported June 2026

Read the source ↗Compare this recordAI-B-001
OutcomeEvidenceReplicationSources and citation

The reported benefit

Both hikers were located and guided to safety.

Who benefits
The two hikers are the reported direct beneficiaries. Rescuers are contributing practitioners. A separate benefit to their workload or safety is not established.
AI contribution
The agency attributes initial identification in the bush to the drone's AI software. Thermal imaging confirmed the find.
Human contribution
The drone operator confirmed the find using thermal imagery and used a spotlight to guide Police and State Emergency Service rescuers. The hikers also signaled with a phone light.

The report does not isolate AI's contribution from thermal imaging, the hikers' signal, or rescuers. It does not establish injuries or deaths avoided, or time saved.

Over time

Context for interpretation

No adverse effects are reported. Costs, surveillance implications, and false detections are not evaluated.
