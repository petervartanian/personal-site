## Outcome

Both hikers were located and guided to safety.

The two hikers are the reported direct beneficiaries. Rescuers are contributing practitioners. A separate benefit to their workload or safety is not established.

## Contributions

**AI:** The agency attributes initial identification in the bush to the drone's AI software. Thermal imaging confirmed the find.

**People:** The drone operator confirmed the find using thermal imagery and used a spotlight to guide Police and State Emergency Service rescuers. The hikers also signaled with a phone light.

**System:** Fire and Rescue NSW AI-assisted drone search. The agency report describes drone AI software and a thermal camera. It names no manufacturer, model, or software version.
