# Higher physics test scores with an AI tutor

Harvard University. Fall 2023 study, published June 2025.

AI Benefits Database, AI-B-006
Source review 2026-10-19
Current record: https://petervartanian.xyz/ai-benefits/records/harvard-physics-tutor-2023/
Cited version: https://petervartanian.xyz/ai-benefits/records/harvard-physics-tutor-2023/versions/3e3b523f1f99/

## Task and setting

Tutoring introductory physics. Harvard University, United States. AI lessons were completed at home.

## Outcome

Immediate physics test performance improved with the designed AI tutor: an adjusted effect of 0.63 standard deviations versus classroom active learning.

The eligible study population comprised 194 undergraduate physics students. The reported post-test counts describe observations in a crossover study, not independent parallel groups or proof that every student benefited.

## Compared with

A designed AI tutoring package was compared with classroom active learning in a randomized crossover study.

## Evidence

Randomized crossover study. Two lessons with conditions reversed between groups. Reported post-test observations: 142 AI, 174 classroom. The adjusted effect controls for prior proficiency, lesson, test version, and time on task.

One course with a test ceiling. The instructional package included self-pacing, videos, and at-home study. The comparison does not isolate generic AI or establish teacher replacement. Retention and later transfer were not measured.

Immediate assessment.

Any wider deployment assessment would need to consider costs, access, and the reliability of feedback.

## Contributions

**AI:** Question-specific guidance and individualized feedback.

**People:** Instructors supplied curriculum, correct solutions, scaffolding, prompts, and videos. Students solved problems.

**System:** PS2 Pal. The article names the tutor PS2 Pal and identifies GPT-4. It does not give an exact GPT-4 snapshot or tutor release.

**Timing:** Fall 2023, course weeks 9–10.

## Conditions

- The study covered surface tension and fluid flow in an undergraduate course.
- Instructor-prepared prompts, solutions, videos, and structured practice accompanied GPT-4.
- Students used the tutor at home at their own pace.

The comparison tests this instructional package on immediate assessments, without establishing durable learning or results in other subjects.

## Sources

- [AI tutoring outperforms in-class active learning: an RCT introducing a novel research-based design in an authentic educational setting](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-025-97652-6)

  - Two groups switched between AI tutoring and classroom instruction across two lessons. Locator: Results, opening paragraph, and Methods, Study design.

# Historians reconstructed text more accurately

Ancient Greek inscriptions. Published March 2022.

AI Benefits Database, AI-B-007
Source review 2026-10-19
Current record: https://petervartanian.xyz/ai-benefits/records/ithaca-historians-2022/
Cited version: https://petervartanian.xyz/ai-benefits/records/ithaca-historians-2022/versions/dbe9a5274e48/

## Task and setting

Restoring ancient Greek text. Texts from the ancient Mediterranean. The evaluators’ location is unreported.

## Outcome

On masked known text, historians’ top-choice reconstruction accuracy was 71.7% with Ithaca, compared with 25.3% unaided.

The human evaluation involved two graduate specialists in Greek history and epigraphy. Wider benefits to scholarship or the public were not measured.

## Compared with

Known masked text was reconstructed by historians with and without Ithaca.

## Evidence

Comparative research evaluation. The evaluation removed 1–10 known characters, allowing reconstructions to be checked against the original. Results are reported in Nature, Table 1.

Two trained evaluators do not represent all historians. Accuracy on masked known text does not establish accuracy on genuinely unknown text. The paper’s authors include Ithaca’s developers, and the corpus reflects prior scholarship.

One evaluation. Lasting changes in scholarly productivity were not measured.

Incorrect reconstructions and uneven corpus coverage are considerations for future use. This record does not estimate their downstream effects.

## Contributions

**AI:** Provided 20 ranked reconstruction hypotheses.

**People:** Interpreted alternatives using historical knowledge and searchable textual parallels.

**System:** Ithaca. The paper names Ithaca and describes its transformer architecture. JAX 0.2.9, Flax 0.3.0, and Haiku 0.0.4 are development-library versions. No Ithaca release or checkpoint identifier is reported.

**Timing:** Evaluation dates unreported.

## Conditions

- Evaluators had seven years of historical and linguistic training.
- Known text provided a reference against which to check reconstructions.
- Historians considered ranked suggestions alongside searchable textual parallels.

This controlled restoration task establishes no accuracy for genuinely unknown text, other languages, or different groups of historians.

# Support agents resolved more issues per hour

Philippines, United States, and other offices. 2020–2021 rollout, published 2025.

AI Benefits Database, AI-B-008
Source review 2026-10-19
Current record: https://petervartanian.xyz/ai-benefits/records/customer-support-assistant-2020/
Cited version: https://petervartanian.xyz/ai-benefits/records/customer-support-assistant-2020/versions/af7ad6ee18db/

## Task and setting

Answering customer questions. These are explicitly named agent locations. Other countries are mentioned but not named. Customers were U.S.-based small businesses.

## Outcome

Access to an AI assistant increased resolved customer issues per hour by about 15% on average in one software company.

The full study covers 5,172 agents and 3,006,395 chats. It does not show that every worker benefited or establish higher pay.

## Compared with

Agents with access were compared across time with their own pre-access performance and agents without access, adjusting for agent, calendar month, location, and tenure.

## Evidence

Nonrandomized workplace study. A difference-in-differences analysis of deployment across 5,172 agents estimates 0.301 additional resolutions per hour, or 15.2%, in the preferred model. That model uses 12,295 agent-month observations with the required outcome data.

One firm and a nonrandomized rollout. Causal interpretation depends on the comparison assumptions. Quality measures were unavailable for some subcontracted agents. Gains varied by experience and skill. Wages and economy-wide employment effects were not measured.

Repeated workplace observations around the 2020–2021 rollout. Long-run employment and wage effects were not measured.

The most skilled agents saw a small decline in conversation quality. The average customer satisfaction effect was not statistically significant.

## Contributions

**AI:** Suggested replies and relevant internal documentation during customer chats.

**People:** Agents diagnosed problems, chose whether to use or edit suggestions, and remained responsible for replies. Managers continued coaching.

**System:** Unnamed customer-support assistant. Section III.C identifies GPT-3 fine-tuned on customer conversations. The company, vendor, exact model snapshot, and software release are not disclosed.

**Timing:** The principal rollout occurred in fall 2020 and winter 2021. A single intervention date does not describe the staggered introduction.

## Conditions

- The model was fine-tuned on recorded support conversations labeled by outcomes, with greater weight on high performers.
- The assistant supplied response suggestions and links to the company’s internal technical documentation.
- Agents completed a three-hour onboarding session and could ignore or edit suggestions.

A company-specific customer-support deployment does not establish the same gain for generic chatbots, other occupations, or workers with different experience.

# Professional writing tasks took less time

Online experiment. January–February 2023.

Current record: https://petervartanian.xyz/ai-benefits/records/professional-writing-chatgpt-2023/
Cited version: https://petervartanian.xyz/ai-benefits/records/professional-writing-chatgpt-2023/versions/a5a9e26a2d8a/

## Task and setting

Drafting professional writing. Online recruitment through Prolific. Participant countries are unreported in the reviewed sources.

## Outcome

In a randomized writing experiment, ChatGPT access reduced average task time by 40% and increased assessed output quality by 18%.

The 453-person sample included marketers, grant writers, consultants, data analysts, human resources professionals, and managers. It was not a workforce census.

## Compared with

Random assignment to ChatGPT signup and guidance between tasks, compared with an Overleaf signup control. The control group received no instruction to use ChatGPT, but some did.

## Evidence

Randomized online experiment. 453 college-educated professionals completed occupation-specific tasks. Assignment to ChatGPT occurred between two tasks. Three blinded professionals assessed each output.

Short tasks emphasized generic writing and did not require precise factual accuracy or organization-specific knowledge. About 10–20% of controls also used ChatGPT. This measures immediate task performance, not productivity across a whole job.

Immediate task outcomes. Later surveys tracked reported use after two weeks and two months, without measuring workplace productivity.

Real work may require fact-checking, context gathering, and other effort absent from the experimental tasks. Effects on employment and pay were not measured.

## Contributions

**AI:** Generated text in response to participants’ writing prompts.

**People:** Participants supplied prompts and chose what to submit. Most users submitted unedited or lightly edited AI output. Blinded professionals assessed the writing.

**System:** ChatGPT. The author manuscript identifies GPT-3.5 and the experiment dates. It does not give an exact model snapshot or ChatGPT release identifier.

**Timing:** Main experiment ran January 27–February 21, 2023.

## Conditions

- Participants worked on short assignments tailored to their occupations.
- Treatment participants received ChatGPT signup instructions and example prompts.
- Participants received quality-linked payments and output was graded by blinded professionals.

The measured gain should not be applied directly to research-heavy, fact-sensitive, or organization-specific writing, or to total job productivity.

# Developers completed a coding task sooner

Remote participants, mainly India and Pakistan. May–June 2022.

AI Benefits Database, AI-B-010
Source review 2026-10-19
Current record: https://petervartanian.xyz/ai-benefits/records/copilot-http-server-2022/
Cited version: https://petervartanian.xyz/ai-benefits/records/copilot-http-server-2022/versions/b0617d671891/

## Task and setting

Completing a coding task. Remote participants, mainly in India and Pakistan. Other countries are not named in the report.

## Outcome

Among developers who finished the task, those assigned GitHub Copilot completed a JavaScript HTTP server in 55.8% less time, averaging 71.17 versus 160.89 minutes.

95 Upwork recruits were assigned. Seventy completed the task and survey. Faster completion is not proof of higher earnings or a benefit for every assigned developer.

## Compared with

Random assignment to GitHub Copilot access plus a one-minute introduction versus no Copilot. Both groups could use their usual web resources. The reported time contrast includes only successful completers.

## Evidence

Randomized coding experiment, preprint. 95 developers were randomized, 45 to Copilot and 50 to control. The completion-time comparison covers 35 successful participants per group. The reported 95% confidence interval for the reduction is 21–89%.

One bounded JavaScript task. Completion-time results condition on successful completion. The success-rate difference was not statistically significant. The study did not evaluate broader code quality, security, maintenance, or team productivity. Authors included GitHub and Microsoft employees.

One coding assignment in May–June 2022. Later maintenance and ongoing workplace productivity were not measured.

Passing the twelve task tests does not establish secure, maintainable production code. Those properties were outside the evaluation.

## Contributions

**AI:** Suggested code and functions while developers wrote the server.

**People:** Developers implemented and submitted the program. They could use web search and other usual resources. A fixed suite checked twelve required behaviors.

**System:** GitHub Copilot. The paper identifies Codex and evaluates Copilot before general availability in June 2022. It gives no exact Codex snapshot or extension version.

**Timing:** The paper reports an experiment from May 15 to June 20, 2022. Its recruitment figure separately dates the Upwork posting to May 25.

## Conditions

- Developers received a starter repository and explicit HTTP-server requirements.
- A visible, unmodifiable suite of twelve tests determined successful completion.
- The Copilot group received a one-minute introduction, and both groups could use usual web resources.

A faster standardized task among completers does not establish equivalent gains on existing codebases, collaborative projects, other languages, or code needing security and maintenance review.
