No AI system took part

8
  • Spelling by a completely locked-in ALS patient (Chaudhary et al. 2022)

    The Nature Communications paper states that a multichannel decoding algorithm was not implemented: letters were selected by firing-rate thresholds with auditory neurofeedback, and the only support-vector machine belonged to an earlier eye-tracking system. No machine-learning system produced the benefit.

  • ChatGPT as manuscript editor for a missed-pneumothorax case report (Brown 2023)

    ChatGPT was used only to draft and edit the Cureus paper about a delayed pneumothorax diagnosis at Jersey General Hospital, and the paper's AI content is a proposal of how AI could have helped. No AI system took part in the patient's care.

  • BirdCast and the lights-out schemes of Raleigh and Bird Safe Philly

    Raleigh's Lights Out (27 August 2026) follows a fixed seasonal schedule from 11 p.m. to 6 a.m., with BirdCast mentioned only as a public resource, and Bird Safe Philly's page neither mentions BirdCast alerts nor reports collision results, so no AI output changed either decision. The BirdCast-driven case, Houston's Lights Out Nights, is AIBD-0103.

  • Titian catheter-robot ablation at Shaoxing People's Hospital (OECD AI Monitor entry)

    The only event in OECD AI Monitor entry 2025-12-13-c088, the Titian robot ablation on a 64-year-old woman with paroxysmal atrial fibrillation at Shaoxing People's Hospital (Xinhua Zhejiang, 13 December 2025), used a teleoperated master-slave catheter robot steered by the surgeon at a console, and the article never mentions AI, autonomy or inference. The entry's CA-GPT item is a 96-patient comparative preprint rather than an event, and its third source concerns an unrelated neurosurgical robot in Fushun.

  • Wandercraft Atalante exoskeleton walking for people with complete paraplegia

    Independent studies exist, including the 2026 Journal of NeuroEngineering and Rehabilitation trial of 11 participants at Nimes University Hospital, but the exoskeleton walks on trajectories precomputed by nonlinear optimisation and tracked by a hybrid zero dynamics controller, with no machine learning or AI involved.

  • Estonia's small-claims 'robot judge'

    The Estonian Ministry of Justice stated on 16 February 2022 that it does not develop an AI robot judge for small-claims or general court proceedings to replace the human judge, so there is no system and no outcome to verify.

  • Rajesh Dandotiya bank-fraud intervention

    The intervention in the Rajesh Dandotiya case was made by human bank staff rather than by an AI system, so there is nothing to record.

  • Singapore Police Force Project A.S.T.R.O. scam alerts

    The Singapore Police Force's own description (MHA Minister's Awards page, 29 October 2024) says Project ASTRO uses robotic process automation to send SMS alerts to scam victims, and the Straits Times (16 April 2023) describes officers and OCBC staff analysing fund flows and mass-sending SMSes with it. The S$442 million said to have been averted since April 2023 is a police aggregate for automated messaging, not for model-based detection.
