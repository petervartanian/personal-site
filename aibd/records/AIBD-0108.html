Citable summary

Two Model X cars stopped as an oak fell on the A31 in Storm Dennis; eight occupants walked away, both drivers credit the emergency brakes. One driver credited automatic braking, while the other was unsure whether it was enabled. Without vehicle logs or an engineering reconstruction, the accounts cannot establish the braking mechanism or the number of lives that it saved. By Laurence Sanderson's account his car was on Autopilot and the emergency braking stopped it so the trunk hit the bonnet rather than the cabin. Josh Whitelock said he was not sure whether his loan car's automatic brakes were enabled and credited its other safety features. No Tesla data, police finding or engineering assessment of whether either system braked, or whether the drivers would have stopped in time themselves, is reported. Elon Musk's reply, “Glad you're all ok”, acknowledges the outcome, not the mechanism.
