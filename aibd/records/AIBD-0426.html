AI weather forecasts cut penalties at a renewable power station in China

Huadian and Huawei report that power forecasts from a fine-tuned Huawei Pangu weather model reduced assessment penalties by 27% and 15% at a Jiangsu pilot in the first half of 2024.

What happened

Huadian and Huawei describe AI-based meteorological power forecasting that runs on dedicated computing hardware. Beijing Jiutian Meteorological Technology supplied weather data and modeling support. According to the contributors, the Jiangsu pilot reduced assessment penalties by 27% and 15% during the first half of 2024. The contributors also report that the forecasts were computed faster and were more accurate. Huawei reported on 9 July 2025 that the pilot station belonged to China Huadian Jiangsu New Energy Company. Huawei also reported that forecast accuracy at the station reached 97.24% at 15 minutes and 91.72% at four hours. Wider adoption of renewable power and climate benefits are intended downstream effects. No independent account of the penalty reductions had appeared by October 2026.
