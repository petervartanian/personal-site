AI animated archival photographs for a historical film

Giovanni Abitante used unnamed generative video tools to animate archival photographs for I Am an American, a short film that received 2025 awards; no source measures its effect on viewers.

What happened

Giovanni Abitante used generative video to animate Dorothea Lange’s photographs and reconstruct scenes for I Am an American, a short film about the wartime incarceration of Japanese Americans. In a July 2025 festival interview, he described AI as a production technique within a project that depended on archival research and writing. The MetaMorph AI Film Award named the film runner-up in its 2025 Mockumentary category. The Perugia Social Film Festival lists a 2025 Best Documentary award from the Burano Artificial Intelligence Film Festival. The Perugia festival’s program lists the film and its recorded testimony. The festival included the film in its first showcase of generative-AI shorts, held from 24 September to 4 October 2026. The evidence establishes a completed creative work and its public exhibition. No source measures an improvement in historical understanding or audience engagement.
