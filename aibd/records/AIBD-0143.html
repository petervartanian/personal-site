What happened

ECMWF announced that on 25 February 2025 it began producing operational forecasts with AIFS Single, the deterministic version of its Artificial Intelligence Forecasting System. It claimed tropical cyclone track gains of up to 20%. ECMWF's evaluation of 2025 forecasts (8 May 2026) reports AIFS errors 5 to 15% lower than the IFS for most surface and upper-air variables. Cyclone position was substantially better but intensity under-predicted. NHC's Science Operations Officer said ECMWF's AI-driven global system was available for evaluation at NHC during the 2025 season. No specific event in which AIFS beat the IFS is documented in the ECMWF sources read. The Severe Event Catalog page for Storm Eowyn states wind gusts were not available for AIFS.

What it was built to do. Operational data-driven global medium-range weather forecasting at ECMWF, run alongside the physics-based IFS.

The system's part

ECMWF's verification compares AIFS directly with the IFS. The only non-ECMWF source read (NWS Q&A) confirms NHC evaluated ECMWF's AI system in 2025 but states no performance result. ECMWF is both the developer and the deployer, so its evaluations are the body reporting on its own system.

Replication

What produced it. Operational twice-daily AIFS Single runs initialized from ECMWF analyses. ECMWF's standard headline-score and tropical cyclone verification against the IFS.

What must hold again. Intensity under-prediction persists per ECMWF and the AIFS-TC preprint. No ECMWF-documented named event where AIFS beat the IFS was found. Event-level superiority remains unverified.
