Source information as of June 2025

Hui Xin improves transactions in two telecom service halls

China Telecom reports over 50% fewer key process errors and over 30% better marketing success in an assistant deployed in two service halls.

ⓘ

What happened

The Hui Xin assistant interprets conversations, retrieves information, and completes forms. The source reports deployment in two service halls, fewer key process errors, better marketing success, and 20–30% of representatives’ time freed for other work. Shorter customer waits and broader accessibility are also described, without separately established outcomes.

Purpose. Support service representatives with accurate, lower-effort transactions.

AI’s contribution

Not assessed. The account does not isolate AI’s contribution from the surrounding workflow.

Beneficiaries

  • an organization

    Deployment in two China Telecom halls reportedly cut key process error rates by over 50% and improved marketing success by over 30%.

  • a person

    The source reports that representatives gain back 20–30% of their time from form-filling and related work, a concrete task-time benefit.

Further evidence

Setting. The account does not establish conditions sufficient to reproduce the benefit.

Conditions. The account does not establish requirements for reproducing the benefit in other settings.
