AI cameras name 18 armed teenagers in Việt Trì within half an hour

Tuổi Trẻ (14 June 2026): on the night of 13 June Phú Thọ police saw a group preparing weapons to fight in Việt Trì; the AI camera system identified all 18 faces in about 30 minutes; all confessed.

ⓘ

What happened

On the night of Saturday 13 June 2026, Phú Thọ provincial police detected a group of teenagers gathering in Việt Trì ward. Officers were monitoring and mining data from the camera system. The teenagers were preparing weapons and vehicles to fight and disturb public order, Tuổi Trẻ reported on 14 June. Within about 30 minutes the AI camera system of the provincial command center had accurately identified the faces of all 18 participants. They were aged 14 to 18. They came from Việt Trì, Vân Phú and Nông Trang wards and Phù Ninh and Bình Phú communes (Tuổi Trẻ, VietNamNet). Tiền Phong says the whole case was clarified in just over two hours. It says 17 of the 18 were pupils of schools including Vũ Thê Lang High School and Dữu Lâu Middle School. One had dropped out. It says the provincial police summoned all 18, and that at the police station they admitted the violations.

No report says whether a fight took place.

Purpose. The provincial AI camera system was installed to monitor traffic routes and key areas and detect violations for investigation and enforcement, per Tuổi Trẻ. Identifying people gathering with weapons is that purpose.

AI’s contribution

The facial recognition of the provincial AI camera system supplied the identities. Tuổi Trẻ and VietNamNet say all 18 faces were accurately identified within about 30 minutes, after officers monitoring camera data had noticed the gathering. The summons, questioning and confessions were police work. The sources are three national outlets reporting the provincial police's own account, with no vendor, algorithm or accuracy figure given.

Further evidence

Setting. A provincial camera network with facial recognition feeding the police command center. Officers watching camera data at night who noticed the gathering. Reference images that let the system put names to 14-to-18-year-olds. Police able to summon the 18 and their families within hours.

Conditions. Camera coverage of the gathering point with usable faces. The subjects are enrolled in the reference database. Police verification of each match before action.
