China Telecom HongXin builds a low-code platform for AI applications

China Telecom HongXin describes its AI Full-Process Low-Code Middle Platform, an internal tool for building AI applications in transport, governance, and environmental monitoring.

What happened

China Telecom HongXin Information Technology describes a platform for building and deploying AI applications, including road, intrusion, waste, and fire monitoring. The company reports that the testbed is used internally and that the use case is part of a broader product. It describes intended gains in service and development and reports no measured improvement from any deployment. ITU republished the same text in its final report of October 2025 with only wording edits. No later source reports an outcome for any user of the platform.
