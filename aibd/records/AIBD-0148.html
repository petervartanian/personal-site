Relation to AI

By the operators' account, the Veesion software flagged the concealment and alerted the remote dispatcher, and the operator then filed a police report. Police caught the perpetrator from the footage within hours. Délmagyar's account of the thief that it reports credits the camera footage and does not mention an alert. It also reports that thieves entered and left behind customers who had opened the door with the app. The operators said that the system was being fine-tuned after these cases, with a security guard in the store for a few days. No source separates what the alert added from what recorded footage alone would have given police.

AI cameras in Szeged's unstaffed-at-night Coop flagged a shopper hiding goods; police caught them within hours, the operators said. The press-release accounts do not establish whether the theft was prevented, goods were recovered, or losses fell overall. Independent police confirmation and false-alert information are missing from the reviewed material. By the operators' account, the Veesion analysis flagged the shopper's concealment gesture in real time and alerted the remote dispatcher. The camera footage let police catch the perpetrator within hours. No police statement, loss figure or description of what the dispatcher did at the moment of the alert was found. Whether the theft was stopped in progress or only documented, and whether the goods came back, is unknown. The claim rests on a joint press release by the deployer and its integrator, relayed by independent outlets.
