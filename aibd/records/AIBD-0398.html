RuiPath shortens pathology review at Ruijin Hospital

Huawei reports that RuiPath reduced pathology-review time at Ruijin Hospital, including initial review from 20–40 to 10–15 minutes per patient.

Limits
The submission describes a reduction from 40 to 15 minutes as a 75% efficiency improvement, although the elapsed-time reduction is 62.5%. It also supplies different task-specific ranges. These measures need defined baselines, and faster review does not establish improved diagnosis or treatment.
Costs and harms
The submission mixes processing, initial review, and confirmatory-review timings. Its claim of a 75% improvement does not follow directly from reducing 40 minutes to 15 minutes, so the underlying timings should remain separate.

What happened

Ruijin Hospital and Huawei unveiled RuiPath on 18 February 2025, and the hospital said that its development took around three years. The RuiPath pathology pilot covers 11 subspecialties at Ruijin Hospital. In its submission to the International Telecommunication Union, current as of June 2025, Huawei reports that review and report preparation became shorter. Huawei reports that physicians doing the initial review now spend 10–15 minutes per patient with AI-assisted image analysis and report editing, down from 20–40 minutes. Huawei also claims benefits in model accuracy and data engineering. Some of its percentage and timing statements differ. No patient-specific treatment improvement is reported. Xinhua reported on 31 August 2026 that Ruijin Hospital and Huawei Cloud had released RuiPath 2.0. It reported that more than 90 hospitals in China use RuiPath. No independent study of its effect on review times has been published.

Details

AI system

Model

The 2025 submission identifies a multimodal RuiPath architecture and says that DeepSeek R1 contributed through distillation. It does not specify an exact distilled checkpoint.

Inputs and outputs

Whole-slide images and clinical information were converted into image features, diagnostic suggestions, and draft pathology reports for review.

Workflow

The account does not establish conditions sufficient to reproduce the benefit.

Human oversight

Pathologists supplied image-text annotations, reviewed the model’s suggestions, and edited or confirmed reports. Specimen information entered by voice was checked by a person.

Benefits

Reach

The Ruijin pilot covers 11 subspecialties, and Xinhua reports that more than 90 hospitals in China use RuiPath. Neither figure counts pathologists or patients who benefited. [1] [2] [3]

Duration

Xinhua reported on 31 August 2026 that RuiPath 2.0 had been released and that more than 90 hospitals in China use RuiPath. No source reports review times after the Ruijin pilot. [1] [2] [3]

People and places

Dependencies

The design connected hospital pathology records to Huawei’s DCS AI infrastructure and ModelEngine, with high-performance computing and parallel storage.

Follow-up

Conditions for replication

The account does not establish requirements for reproducing the benefit in other settings.

Availability

The submission described pilot testing at Ruijin Hospital across 11 subspecialties. Its planned wider operation and code release in late 2025 were future commitments in that account.
