A university's deepfake detector is about 95% accurate in a challenge

Hangzhou Dianzi University reports that its content moderation system detected deepfakes with about 95% accuracy in a challenge and that public security bureaus in China apply it.

What happened

The organizers of the Inclusion 2024 deepfake detection challenge report that 1,500 teams took part and that they ranked entries by the area under the curve (AUC). Their report, published on 30 December 2024, does not name the Hangzhou Dianzi University team. In its account for the International Telecommunication Union, the university describes a system that detects forged media and reviews generated content. The account names Tong Qiao of the university's School of Cyberspace as the contact. The university reports that the system reached about 95% detection accuracy in the challenge. It reports applications with police. It also describes further development of the platform. No source measures prevented fraud losses or effects on public safety.
