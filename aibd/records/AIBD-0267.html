Brief from Peter

In a case report published on 16 January 2026, Hajebian and Friel describe a 22-month-old girl with skull defects that the treating physician did not initially recognize on her computed tomography scan. Her mother ran a three-dimensional reconstruction of the scan through a smartphone app, which the authors describe as powered by Med-Gemini-3D. The app returned “Catlin mark skull”, a historical name for enlarged parietal foramina. Genetic evaluation then identified a CDC42BPB variant. The authors state that establishing the diagnosis facilitated earlier therapeutic interventions.1 The role of AI rests on the authors' description alone. The abstract does not identify the app, and Google had said in May 2024 that Med-Gemini was not available for commercial use.2
