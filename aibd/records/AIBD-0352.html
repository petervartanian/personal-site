EasyScan GO reaches 91.1% sensitivity for malaria at 11 sites

Against expert microscopy, the EasyScan GO machine-learning microscope read 2,250 malaria blood films at 11 sites with 91.1% sensitivity and 75.6% specificity; no patient outcome was measured.

Conditions for replication

Good slide quality, which the authors found significantly affected all performance parameters. Parasitemia above about 200 parasites/uL, below which sensitivity fell to 57%. Thick-film-only software (the tested version did not separate non-falciparum species).

Later findings

A second evaluation in London, with the same funder and software team, found similar sensitivity and higher specificity. It also found that parasite counts fell short of WHO expert level and that the device reported every non-falciparum infection as P. Vivax.
