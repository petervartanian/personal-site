AI generates property images for design discussions

Benefits

Benefit

3D Architectural Solutions offers EDIT by DIY Render to generate and revise images of interiors, exteriors, and design plans. Its product page shows examples and describes uses in renovation discussions and property visualization. The company’s terms identify Google Gemini and Replicate services and warn that outputs can be inaccurate. The company acknowledges that precise refinements can be difficult. The reviewed external material supports the offered capability, but contains no product-specific evaluation of time savings, sales conversion, or sale prices. [1] [2]
