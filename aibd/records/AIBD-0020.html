AlphaTensor finds a 47-multiplication 4x4 scheme over GF(2)

AlphaTensor found a 47-multiplication 4x4 scheme over GF(2) and a 96 for 5x5; Kauers and Moosbauer improved the latter to 95.

ⓘ

What happened

On 5 October 2022 Nature published AlphaTensor, a reinforcement-learning system from DeepMind (Fawzi, Balog, Huang, Hubert, Romera-Paredes and others). It treats the search for matrix multiplication algorithms as a one-player game, TensorGame. A deep neural network guides a planning procedure derived from AlphaZero. It found an algorithm multiplying two 4x4 matrices over the field with two elements in 47 multiplications. That beat Strassen's two-level algorithm for the first time in about 50 years. It also found a 96-multiplication scheme for 5x5 matrices over that field. On 8 October 2022 Manuel Kauers and Jakob Moosbauer posted a preprint, revised on 13 October. It started from the published 5x5 scheme and improved it to 95 multiplications.

Purpose. Built to discover matrix multiplication algorithms by searching for low-rank tensor decompositions; the new schemes were that purpose.

AI’s contribution

The reinforcement-learning agent found the decompositions, which are explicit and checkable. The independent preprint treats the 96-multiplication 5x5 result as established and improves it, which corroborates the result's existence. The 47-multiplication 4x4 scheme rests on the vendor paper's published decomposition.

Beneficiaries

  • society

    Researchers in algebraic complexity gained new explicit decompositions that were improved upon within a week by Kauers and Moosbauer. The gains are field-restricted. Nature reports the 4x4 improvement for matrices “in a finite field” and Kauers and Moosbauer give the 5x5 record of 96 multiplications over Z2.

Further evidence

Setting. Formulation of algorithm discovery as a tensor-decomposition game. Neural-network-guided search with exact verification of each decomposition. Publication of explicit decompositions that others can check and extend.

Conditions. Decompositions must be verified exactly over the stated ring or field. Independent reproduction or extension by researchers outside the vendor.
