A third company linked to suspected money laundering

Bank F’s network tool found phone numbers and email addresses shared by three companies. Further account checks indicated possible money laundering.

AI system

System

Bank F network (graph) analytics solution implemented in Hong Kong in 2013

Application developer

Bank F (anonymized subsidiary and regional head office of a large global bank); the report names no vendor

Workflow

Integration of wholesale client, correspondent banking, trade, payments and external registry data in one network model. A human intelligence lead (the relationship manager's TBML suspicion) used as the seed for network expansion.

Relation to AI

The HKMA report attributes the identification of Company C to the network analytics tool's matching of linked phone numbers and email addresses. A human relationship manager supplied the initial lead. Whether the tool uses machine learning is not stated. The bank built and deployed the tool itself. The HKMA, which wrote the report, is the regulator.
