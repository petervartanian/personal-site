Foncier innovant finds 140,000 undeclared pools in French aerial photos

Foncier innovant, a tool of France's tax administration that detects pools in aerial photographs, found 140,000 taxable pools in 2023, which the administration values at €40 million in local tax.

Relation to AI

Foncier innovant extracted pool outlines from IGN aerial images and compared them with tax declarations. Tax officers systematically verified anomalies before contacting owners. DGFiP’s 2023 annual report states that 140,000 taxable pools were detected in 2023, compared with 20,000 in 2022, representing€40 million in additional local property-tax revenue. A separate overview in the report says more than 120,000 pools. The figures are the deploying administration’s account, not an independent error-rate evaluation.
