ChatGPT sends a woman with a spider bite to the ER; staff agree

ChatGPT sent Holli of Wolfforth, Texas to the ER for a worsening spider bite on 27 August 2025; doctors agreed and said the tissue was dying.

Workflow

Escalating symptoms (days of vomiting, inability to keep water down, new numbness in the arm) in a person hesitating to seek care. A yes/no triage question put to a chatbot that answered without hedging: “you need to go immediately”. An emergency department within reach that could assess the bite the same day.

Human oversight

Not assessed

Relation to AI

ChatGPT's contribution, on her account, was an unambiguous instruction to go immediately when she was unsure. The assessment and any treatment were the ER's. Whether earlier arrival changed her outcome is unknown. Everything traces to her TikTok, relayed by Reach plc tabloids and Indian lifestyle desks. Hindustan Times states that it “has not independently verified the claims”.

Field

Healthcare (emergency self-triage). Consumer use of a general chatbot for triage advice.

Purpose

ChatGPT is a general-purpose conversational assistant. Answering a user's question, here whether to go to the emergency room, is its core function. OpenAI told the Mirror that its services “are not intended for use in the diagnosis or treatment of any health condition”. The Economic Times quoted it. The system gave triage advice rather than a diagnosis.
