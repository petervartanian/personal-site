Taoyuan's AI sound cameras pinpoint a noisy BMW within a day

Mobile AI sound cameras of Taoyuan's environmental department located a loud white BMW within a day of residents' complaint, and officers stopped it on 23 April 2025.

  • Not rated a person

    Residents on Jieshou Road had written that the car's late-night passes seriously affected their quality of life and sleep. The reports say that the car was identified and referred for a modification review, and they do not document whether the noise ceased afterwards, so no realized outcome for the residents is reported.

  • 1 an organization

    The bureau closed one complaint in 24 hours and used the case to publicize its sound-camera enforcement. Its claim that the cameras “greatly raise the success rate” of tracking noise vehicles comes without figures.

What happened

Residents of Bade District, Taoyuan, complained in the Facebook group 記憶八德. A white BMW with a loud exhaust kept roaring down Jieshou Road late at night. The Taoyuan City Department of Environmental Protection received the complaint on the night of 22 April 2025. Its Quiet Taoyuan (靜桃) task force immediately set up AI sound camera equipment at the relevant spots. By analyzing the noise data against the complaints, it worked out where and when the car appeared. On 23 April environmental and police officers stopped it near Daxing Road. The car was referred to the motor vehicle office for a modification review. The reports said that a fine of up to NT$3,600 under the Noise Control Act and an improvement order would follow. All five cited reports reproduce statements from the Taoyuan City Department of Environmental Protection, and none adds reporting of its own. In January 2026 the bureau said that noise complaints had fallen 38 percent since the Quiet Taoyuan project began. The bureau also reported that 50 sound-camera sets were installed across the city.

Details

AI system

Inputs and outputs

The task force combined recorded noise data with complaint locations and times to identify where the car was likely to reappear.

Workflow

A complaint that named the road and the time window (late night on Jieshou Road). This told the task force where to place the cameras the same night. Mobile AI sound cameras already in the bureau's inventory under the Quiet Taoyuan project. Deployment did not wait on procurement. A standing environmental-police joint enforcement arrangement that could put officers at the predicted place and time on 23 April.

Human oversight

Environmental officers and police stopped the car and referred it for inspection and enforcement.

Benefits

Reach

No source counts the residents who benefited. They live near Jieshou Road in Bade District, where residents had complained about the car's late-night noise. The bureau also benefited by closing one complaint in 24 hours. [1] [2] [3] [4] [5]

Duration

The car was located and stopped within 24 hours of the complaint, and the sources do not say whether its noise stopped afterwards. In January 2026 the bureau reported 50 sound-camera sets installed citywide and noise complaints down 38 percent since the Quiet Taoyuan project began. [1] [2] [3] [4] [5]

Follow-up

Conditions for replication

The target must be a repeat offender with a pattern. The method matches noise data against complaint times and places, and cannot catch a one-off pass. Officers must be free to intercept on the night the analysis points to. The cameras locate; they do not stop. A legal basis for the follow-up: here the Noise Control Act (fine up to NT$3,600 and an improvement order). Also the Road Traffic Management and Penalty Act (up to NT$24,000 for a removed muffler).
