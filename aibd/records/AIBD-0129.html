AI system

System

AI sound camera equipment (AI聲音照相) of the Quiet Taoyuan (靜桃) project, part of the bureau's network of 'improved mobile sound cameras' (改良式移動聲音照相)

Workflow

A complaint that named the road and the time window (late night on Jieshou Road). This told the task force where to place the cameras the same night. Mobile AI sound cameras already in the bureau's inventory under the Quiet Taoyuan project. Deployment did not wait on procurement. A standing environmental-police joint enforcement arrangement that could put officers at the predicted place and time on 23 April.

Relation to AI

The bureau's own description is the only account of the system's contribution. The task force placed AI sound camera equipment at the relevant locations on the night of the complaint. Analysis of the noise data with the complaint reports gave the car's haunts and times. Environmental and police officers then found it near Daxing Road on 23 April. The sources do not say how the cameras tie noise to a vehicle. They report no decibel reading. They give no counterfactual for how long a conventional patrol would have taken. The stop, the referral and the pending penalty were the officers' work.

Follow-up

Conditions for replication

The target must be a repeat offender with a pattern. The method matches noise data against complaint times and places, and cannot catch a one-off pass. Officers must be free to intercept on the night the analysis points to. The cameras locate; they do not stop. A legal basis for the follow-up: here the Noise Control Act (fine up to NT$3,600 and an improvement order). Also the Road Traffic Management and Penalty Act (up to NT$24,000 for a removed muffler).

Later findings

8 January 2026. By January 2026 Taoyuan reported 50 sound-camera sets installed citywide, noise complaints down 38% since the Quiet Taoyuan project began, and an AI voiceprint system being integrated into the sound cameras.

Assessed benefits

People. Organizations.

Depth

People: 2. Organizations: 1.

Reasons

People: Residents on Jieshou Road had written that the car's late-night passes seriously affected their quality of life and sleep. They saw it identified and referred for a modification review and fine within a day. The reports do not document whether the noise ceased afterwards. Organizations: The bureau closed one complaint in 24 hours and used the case to publicize its sound-camera enforcement. Its claim that the cameras “greatly raise the success rate” of tracking noise vehicles comes without figures.

Entry details

Field

Environmental enforcement (vehicle noise control). Local government.

Purpose

The bureau places mobile AI sound cameras on main roads and residential streets to collect noise data. Combined with complaint data, this gives the time and place of illegally modified noisy vehicles. Locating this car was that purpose.
