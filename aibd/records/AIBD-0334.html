Corte Constitucional's PretorIA remains in user testing in 2021

PretorIA, presented by the Corte Constitucional on 27 July 2020 to support tutela selection (620,242 tutelas in 2019), was in user-testing in Apr 2021 (Dejusticia); no quantified outcome found.

ⓘ

What happened

On 27 July 2020 Colombia's Corte Constitucional presented PretorIA at a virtual press conference organized by Corferias, according to Ámbito Jurídico. It is an artificial-intelligence system meant to support the selection of tutela (constitutional protection) rulings for review. The court receives about 620,242 tutela files a year (more than 2,500 rulings a day). PretorIA was to group, analyze and classify them by criteria the court prioritizes, offering search, categorization (initially for health rulings) and statistics. Developers claimed it could process rulings in under two minutes where a person needs at least a day to read 30 files. Semana (3 July 2020) reported that the program was developed by the court with the Universidad de Buenos Aires and the Universidad del Rosario.

Alongside, the court unanimously decided to stop receiving paper files and to accept electronic filing from 30 July 2020. In January 2020 the court's president Gloria Ortiz had described the forthcoming tool to El Tiempo as Prometea, the Argentine system it derives from. Dejusticia's independent research (19 April 2021, with Derechos Digitales) found PretorIA still in a testing phase with court staff. It performed four functions (classification of lower-court rulings, search, non-narrative summaries and statistics), supported by a four-person in-house AI and data team. The Universidad de los Andes algorithm registry records a 2018 pilot and 2020 deployment without quantified metrics. No court statement of a verified outcome was reachable (the court's PretorIA page renders no text).

Purpose. Support, without replacing, the selection of tutela rulings for Constitutional Court review by classifying, searching and summarizing incoming rulings. The claimed benefit is faster, better-informed selection.

AI’s contribution

PretorIA classifies and indexes lower-court tutela rulings for the court's selection staff. Selection remains a human decision (“amplía las capacidades de conocimiento del juez, sin reemplazarlo”, per Ámbito Jurídico). The only descriptions read are news reports of the court's own launch presentation and Dejusticia's research note. It states the system was still in user testing in April 2021. No court evaluation, metric or case outcome was found, and the court's PretorIA web page was not available.

Further evidence

Setting. Transfer of the Prometea approach from the Universidad de Buenos Aires to the court, with the Universidad del Rosario. Electronic filing of tutela files to the court from 30 July 2020, replacing paper. A four-person in-house AI and data-science team at the court. Classification categories defined by the court, starting with health rulings.

Conditions. Rulings arriving in digital, machine-readable form. Classification accuracy acceptable to selection staff (not published). Human selection staff using the tool's output rather than bypassing it. Continued institutional ownership after the launch partners withdrew.
