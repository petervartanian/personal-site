Brief from Peter

Where breast screening already uses two radiologists, the ScreenTrustCAD study measured what an AI reader adds beside them.1 At Capio Sankt Göran Hospital in Stockholm, from 1 April 2021 to 9 June 2022, Karolinska Institutet researchers ran Lunit's Insight MMG in the background on the screening mammograms of 55,581 women. Two radiologists alone would have found 250 cancers, and with the AI as a third reader 269 were found. In the paired design, two radiologists and the AI read the examinations independently, so the 19 extra cancers came from examinations that only the AI flagged.2 The researchers also found that one radiologist with AI was non-inferior to two radiologists, with 261 cancers against 250. The study ran at one hospital with one mammography vendor, and Lunit was among its funders.3

Details

AI system

Reported configuration

1.1.6

Workflow

The AI read every examination as an independent reader alongside two radiologists. Any abnormal read by any reader sent the examination to a consensus discussion. Radiologists in the consensus discussion saw the AI score and the marked area.

Measurement

Measurement

Screen-detected cancers within 3 months, by reader combination. [3]

Setting and sample

55,581 women aged 40 to 74 screened at Capio Sankt Göran Hospital, 1 April 2021 to 9 June 2022. [3]

Measures

Screen-detected breast cancers. [3]

Results

269 vs 250 cancers with AI as third reader (relative proportion 1.08); 261 vs 250 for one radiologist plus AI. [3]

Additional value from AI

19 additional cancers. [3]

Limitations

One hospital, one AI product, one mammography vendor; interval cancers not yet reported; Lunit co-funded the study. [3]

Denominators

AI read every examination. [3]

Materials for replication

Registered as NCT04778670; appendix with protocol. [3]

Follow-up

Conditions for replication

A consensus step must exist where AI-flagged cases are reviewed by radiologists. The AI threshold must be set to match radiologist sensitivity on local data. The AI must perform on the local equipment as it did on Philips mammography units.
