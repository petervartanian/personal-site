Swindon reports cheaper, faster Easy-Read documents

Swindon reports that Simply Readable creates Easy-Read documents in under 30 minutes at much lower cost, with input from residents with learning disabilities.

ⓘ

What happened

Swindon deployed Simply Readable to produce Easy-Read documents with AI, co-designed with residents with learning disabilities. The council reports document conversion in under 30 minutes and costs of 7–10 pence for material previously costing upwards of £600. Its return-on-investment estimates have not been independently checked. A separate tool provides translation.

Purpose. Produce accessible Easy-Read documents more quickly and cheaply for residents who need them.

AI’s contribution

AI converts complex material into Easy-Read outputs through a council-built workflow. Residents influenced the design. The source reports time, cost, and access benefits, but does not establish measured comprehension or independence gains.

Beneficiaries

  • an organization

    The council reports lower conversion costs and shorter document-production time.

  • a person

    The project supplies accessible Easy-Read material designed with residents, supporting improved information access. Actual comprehension or independence gains are not measured.

Further evidence

Setting. The council co-designed the tool with residents with learning disabilities, including layout, language, spacing, and images. The cloud-based workflow uses Amazon Bedrock and integrates with translation services.

Conditions. The conditions needed to reproduce the benefit are unspecified.
