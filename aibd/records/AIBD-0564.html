Swindon council uses AI to make Easy Read documents faster and cheaper

Swindon Borough Council reports that Simply Readable, its tool built on Amazon Bedrock, makes Easy Read documents in under 30 minutes for 7–10 pence each.

What happened

Swindon deployed Simply Readable to produce Easy-Read documents with AI, co-designed with residents with learning disabilities. The council reports document conversion in under 30 minutes and costs of 7–10 pence for material previously costing upwards of £600. Its return-on-investment estimates have not been independently checked. A separate tool provides translation. In a post of 1 December 2023, the council's head of emerging technology wrote that manual Easy Read cost about £120 a page and that the tool had not yet launched. UKAuthority reported on 1 December 2023 that the tool used Claude 2 for text and Stable Diffusion for images and that a council team would check and amend the outputs.

Sources

  1. Report, by the organization that used it.

    Council or project account published in the Local Government Association AI case-study bank. The account provides no independent outcome evaluation.

    The LGA case study presents the council’s account of its own project. The underlying evaluation files, calculation worksheets, and independent replication were not reviewed. LGA Swindon case study: “Simply Readable benefits” and “Measuring return on investment”

    Previously, a complex 5-10 page document could cost upwards of £600 to convert. With Simply Readable, costs have reduced to just 7-10 pence, delivering significant savings that can be redirected towards other critical social services.

  2. Descriptive report, by the organization that used it.

    Sarah Peña, Swindon Borough Council makes vital public information more accessible using Amazon Bedrock, AWS Public Sector Blog, 1 December 2023. The author leads the council's emerging technology team and writes on the cloud vendor's blog; the post gives the council's own cost baseline and says that the tool was not yet launched.

    It can typically cost £120 ($150) to adapt content manually for a single page with just five lines and five images. An average document of five pages would take two weeks to adapt, and cost £500 ($750).

  3. News report, by the organization that used it. Context or contrary evidence.

    UKAuthority, Swindon develops GenAI for Easy Read documents, UKAuthority, 1 December 2023. The trade outlet has no stake in the tool; its story repeats the council announcement and adds no reporting of its own on results.

    It uses two large language models - Anthropic's Claude 2 for analysing and summarising documents and Stability AI's Stable Diffusion for producing images from text and prompts - and a council team will maintain oversight of the outputs, amending them where needed.

Details

AI system

Reported configuration

Anthropic Claude 2 for text and Stability AI Stable Diffusion for images, through Amazon Bedrock (as of December 2023)

Role

Simply Readable converts complex information into an easy-read draft with accessible formatting. [1]

Workflow

The council co-designed the tool with residents with learning disabilities, including layout, language, spacing, and images. The cloud-based workflow uses Amazon Bedrock and integrates with translation services.

Human oversight

UKAuthority reported on 1 December 2023 that a council team would maintain oversight of the outputs and amend them where needed. The sources do not describe how these checks work after launch. [1] [2] [3]
