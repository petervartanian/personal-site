Turkmenistan fixes eight methane leaks after UN satellite alerts

UNEP lists eight methane leaks in Turkmenistan that were mitigated from November 2024 to March 2026 after alerts from its Methane Alert and Response System (MARS), which uses AI.

The principal intended benefit is lower methane emissions and their climate effects. Operators may also retain gas that otherwise would escape, but the eight cases do not provide a combined financial benefit.

Editor’s note. The documented action repairs methane leaks; retained gas is a plausible operator benefit, while no aggregate recovered-gas valuation is supplied.
