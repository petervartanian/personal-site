DeepSeek-R1 proposes FMF; MEFV testing confirms the diagnosis

colchicine stopped attacks after ~30 years of symptoms

Patient's DeepSeek-R1 dialogue proposed FMF; MEFV genetic testing confirmed it and colchicine stopped attacks after ~30 years of symptoms.

What happened

A 34-year-old man of Egyptian origin with recurrent fever, migratory abdominal pain, arthralgia, myalgia and erythematous skin lesions since early childhood began a dialogue with the DeepSeek-R1-0528 model on 28 June 2025. The model refined its differential toward familial Mediterranean fever and recommended specialist evaluation, inflammatory markers, genetic testing and colchicine under supervision. He arranged genetic testing, which found two pathogenic MEFV variants (p.Met694Val and p.Val726Ala), and obtained colchicine 0.5 mg twice daily from an Egyptian pharmacy; his attacks ceased, but alanine aminotransferase rose to 160 U/L. Colchicine was temporarily discontinued, his liver enzymes were monitored by his primary care physician, and after they improved it was resumed at 0.5 mg daily under close supervision. He presented to the Department of Medicine III, University Medical Center Hamburg-Eppendorf, Germany, in February 2026, already taking 0.5 mg daily; the clinic confirmed FMF and he has remained attack-free.

What it was built to do. DeepSeek-R1 is a general-purpose reasoning chatbot; answering a user's medical questions with a differential diagnosis falls within its general question-answering function, though it is not a validated medical device.

The system's part

The LLM generated the FMF hypothesis and the suggested work-up over a multi-turn dialogue; confirmation came from MEFV genotyping and clinical response to colchicine. The authors stress that the model repeatedly advised specialist supervision and that the self-directed genetic testing and self-medication were the patient's own actions.

Replication

No attempt to repeat it has been recorded.

What produced it

  • A patient with a long-standing, characteristic but undiagnosed phenotype describing it in detail to a reasoning LLM over multiple turns
  • Access to genetic testing for the suspected condition

What must hold again

  • Genetic or clinical confirmation by a specialist before the diagnosis is accepted
  • Treatment initiated under medical supervision (the case documents hepatotoxicity from self-dosing)
