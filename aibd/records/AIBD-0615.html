Mindspark lab periods raise math and Hindi scores in Rajasthan

In an 80-school trial in Rajasthan, India, students in the 40 schools that used Mindspark in school hours scored 0.22 SD higher in math and 0.20 SD higher in Hindi after 18 months.

What happened

Karthik Muralidharan and Abhijeet Singh evaluated Mindspark, the personalized adaptive learning software of Educational Initiatives, in government Adarsh schools in four districts of Rajasthan, India, with the Government of Rajasthan. They randomly assigned 80 schools with about 6,500 students. The 40 treatment schools received a Mindspark computer lab and a local lab assistant, and they replaced about a quarter of weekly math and Hindi time in primary grades, and 40 to 50 percent in middle grades, with lab periods on the software. The other 40 schools continued regular teaching. Baseline tests took place in October 2017. After 18 months, students in treatment schools scored 0.22 standard deviations higher in math and 0.20 standard deviations higher in Hindi on independently designed tests. The authors describe this as a 50 to 66 percent increase in the productivity of school time. Gains did not vary by gender, socioeconomic status or initial scores. The program had no effect on school exam scores. The estimate covers the whole program, including the computers and the lab assistants. The paper is an NBER working paper revised in November 2025.

Details

AI system

Workflow

Schools moved part of regular math and Hindi time into computer lab periods on the software. Each school had a lab assistant who kept the computers working. Gains rose with the time that students spent on the platform.

Measurement

Measurement

Independently designed and administered math and Hindi tests, scaled with item response theory. [1]

Setting and sample

80 Adarsh public schools (about 6,500 students) in four districts of Rajasthan, India. [1]

Measures

Test scores in standard deviations after 18 months. [1]

Results

0.22 SD in math and 0.20 SD in Hindi. [1]

Additional value from AI

Not isolated; the estimate covers the software with labs, lab assistants and timetable changes. [1]

Limitations

Working paper; no effect on school exams; Adarsh schools have better infrastructure than other public schools. [1]

Denominators

About a quarter of math and Hindi time in primary grades and 40 to 50 percent in middle grades over 18 months. [1]

Materials for replication

AEA RCT Registry AEARCTR-0002546. [1]

Follow-up
