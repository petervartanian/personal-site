Apple Watch heart-rate alarms lead doctors to find atrial fibrillation

Overnight heart-rate alarms from an Apple Watch Series 2 prompted a woman to visit a United Kingdom hospital, where an electrocardiogram showed atrial fibrillation and she began an anticoagulant.

Workflow

The wearer owns an Apple Watch with heart-rate notifications, Irregular Rhythm Notification or the ECG app enabled. An arrhythmia occurs while the watch is worn and the alert or recording leads the wearer to seek care. A clinician confirms the rhythm on a 12-lead ECG or monitoring and treats it.

  • Outline. Claimed by the parties. Only the people or organizations involved, or sources whose independence has not been assessed, support the benefit.

The fill changes with the evidence. The depth color stays the same.
