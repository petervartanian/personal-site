Fabric GEM flags ANTXR2 and diagnoses an infant in five days

AI-assisted rapid genome sequencing scored a homozygous ANTXR2 variant at 4.7, diagnosing an infant within five days and changing her care plan.

ⓘ

What happened

The report's abstract describes a six-month-old infant with a history of painful extremity contractures. Its case presentation opens with a four-month-old girl referred to the pediatric emergency department at Robert Wood Johnson University Hospital. She was referred for failure to thrive with abdominal distension and global developmental delay. The paper dates her abnormal neurological development to two months of age. It included bilateral upper-extremity hypotonia, a weak palmar grasp, pain with all passive and active extremity movement and bilateral contractures. A bleeding, ulcerated infantile hemangioma in the center of the neck was managed from three months of age. The diminishing appetite and abdominal distension were noted at four months.

Rapid whole-genome sequencing at Rady Children's Institute for Genomic Medicine used the Fabric GEM artificial-intelligence analysis tool with Phevor re-ranking. The sample was shipped on 5 January 2023 and preliminary results were returned on 10 January 2023. GEM scored a homozygous ANTXR2 variant, c.652T>C (p.Cys218Arg), at 4.7. That gave a diagnosis of infantile hyaline fibromatosis syndrome. A multidisciplinary meeting held the same day conveyed the implications, coordinated gastrostomy tube placement and discussed long-term goals. Treating clinicians reported the case in Cureus on 9 June 2024.

Purpose. Rank candidate variants from rapid whole-genome sequencing against the patient's phenotype so that clinicians can reach a molecular diagnosis quickly.

AI’s contribution

GEM scored and surfaced the homozygous ANTXR2 variant at 4.7. The paper says Bayes factor scores greater than 2 are considered strong support. Phevor also identified the variant but scored it 1. That is below the Phevor score of 5 or above that the paper treats as further guidance on what to prioritize. The clinicians then confirmed and interpreted the finding and made the diagnosis. The report is written by the treating clinicians and a Rady clinical genomics scientist, not by the tool's vendor.

Beneficiaries

  • a person

    An identifiable infant received a definitive molecular diagnosis of a disorder the authors describe as frequently causing early death before age two. This triggered a same-day multidisciplinary meeting that conveyed the implications, coordinated gastrostomy tube placement and discussed long-term goals. The authors add that extensive genetic counseling is essential for parents desiring future children, given a 25% risk for the offspring of carrier parents. They do not report that counseling was delivered.

  • an organization

    A single case in which preliminary results returned five days after the sample shipped, on 5 January 2023, with results on 10 January 2023. Genomic panels and a microarray sent from the institution were still pending and anticipated to take longer than one month. The report does not say what became of those tests.
