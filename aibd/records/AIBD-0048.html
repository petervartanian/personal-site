What happened

The report's abstract describes a six-month-old infant with a history of painful extremity contractures, while its case presentation opens with a four-month-old girl referred to the pediatric emergency department at Robert Wood Johnson University Hospital for failure to thrive with abdominal distension and global developmental delay. The paper dates the abnormal neurological development, which included bilateral upper-extremity hypotonia, a weak palmar grasp, pain with all passive and active extremity movement and bilateral contractures, to two months of age; a bleeding, ulcerated infantile haemangioma in the center of the neck was managed from three months of age, and the diminishing appetite and abdominal distension were noted at four months.

Rapid whole-genome sequencing was performed at Rady Children's Institute for Genomic Medicine using the Fabric GEM artificial-intelligence analysis tool with Phevor re-ranking. The sample was shipped on 5 January 2023 and preliminary results were returned on 10 January 2023: GEM scored a homozygous ANTXR2 variant, c.652T>C (p.Cys218Arg), at 4.7, giving a diagnosis of infantile hyaline fibromatosis syndrome. A multidisciplinary meeting held the same day conveyed the implications, coordinated gastrostomy tube placement and discussed long-term goals. Treating clinicians reported the case in Cureus on 9 June 2024.

What it was built to do. Rank candidate variants from rapid whole-genome sequencing against the patient's phenotype so that clinicians can reach a molecular diagnosis quickly.

The system's part

GEM scored and surfaced the homozygous ANTXR2 variant at 4.7, where the paper says that Bayes factor scores greater than 2 are considered strong support; Phevor also identified the variant but scored it 1, below the Phevor score of 5 or above that the paper treats as further guidance on what to prioritize. The clinicians then confirmed and interpreted the finding and made the diagnosis. The report is written by the treating clinicians and a Rady clinical genomics scientist, not by the tool's vendor.

Replication

What produced it. Rapid whole-genome sequencing of a critically ill infant. Structured phenotype terms entered for the patient. AI ranking of candidate variants reviewed and confirmed by clinicians.

What must hold again. Rapid whole-genome sequencing of the proband. Structured phenotype terms entered for the patient. Clinician review and confirmation of the ranked variant.
