Street AI cameras identify a masked burglar in Ho Chi Minh City

The Ho Chi Minh City police's unnamed street AI camera system identified a masked burglar who took two laptops and 14 million VND on 23 December 2025; police detained him on 24 December.

  • 1 a person

    Within two days the intruder who took H.A.T.'s laptops, papers and 14 million VND was identified and detained, and he confessed. No source reports recovery of the property or the money. The benefit to the tenant is the resolution of the case.

  • 2 an organization

    Tân Hưng Ward Police and the Criminal Police Division cleared a burglary by a masked intruder in under 72 hours. Seven outlets carried the case as the police's demonstration of the AI camera system.

What happened

Early on 23 December 2025 an intruder entered a rented room at 62 Street 51, Tân Hưng ward, Ho Chi Minh City. He took two laptops and the wallet, identity papers and bank card of the tenant, H.A.T., 33. A withdrawal of 14 million VND was made from the tenant's bank account. The tenant reported the burglary that morning. Tân Hưng Ward Police and the Criminal Police Division used the street AI camera system. It analyzed the masked man's gait, build, clothing, movement and facial data, identified him and reconstructed his route. At 11 p.m. on 24 December officers found Phạm Ngọc Huy, 23, of Ninh Bình, at an internet café on Tô Ký street. He had a previous conviction for theft. He confessed. VietNamNet reports that police held the suspect for investigation of theft on 25 December 2025. Dân trí reports that he told investigators that he had sold the stolen property. No source reports that the property was recovered.

Details

AI system

Inputs and outputs

Street-camera images supplied appearance and movement features that the system compared to identify a suspect and reconstruct his route.

Workflow

Street AI cameras covering the route the intruder took, with footage retained for police review. A model that matches people on gait, body shape, clothing and movement as well as face, so a mask does not defeat identification. A prompt police report, the morning of the burglary, and a ward investigation team able to act on the match within hours.

Human oversight

Tân Hưng Ward Police and the Criminal Police Division acted on the match, found the suspect at an internet café and obtained his confession. The sources report no independent check of the match. [1] [2] [3] [4] [5]

The immediate benefit was investigative progress for the tenant and police. Recovery of the stolen property or money is not established.

Follow-up

Conditions for replication

The suspect passes through camera coverage and can be found at a fixed location afterwards, here an internet café. Identification is confirmed by police work and a confession. The sources report no independent check of the match. The system and vendor are unnamed, so performance beyond this case cannot be checked.
