HCMC AI cameras identify a masked burglar; police detain him

HCMC street AI cameras identified a masked burglar who took two laptops and 14 million VND on 23 Dec 2025; police held him on 24 Dec.

What happened

Early on 23 December 2025 an intruder entered a rented room at 62 Street 51, Tân Hưng ward, Ho Chi Minh City, taking two laptops and the wallet, identity papers and bank card of the tenant, H.A.T., 33; 14 million VND was withdrawn from his account. He reported it that morning. Tân Hưng Ward Police and the Criminal Police Division used the street AI camera system, which analysed the masked man's gait, build, clothing, movement and facial data, identified him and reconstructed his route. At 11 p.m. on 24 December officers found Phạm Ngọc Huy, 23, of Ninh Bình, a convicted thief, at an internet café on Tô Ký street; he confessed. Property recovery is not reported.

What it was built to do. Ho Chi Minh City's street AI cameras are run by the police for security and investigation; VnExpress describes the technology analysing and matching biometric features, and identifying a suspect from footage is the use the police describe. The system and its vendor are not named.

Who benefited

  • -1
    personal

    H.A.T. had the intruder who took his laptops, papers and 14 million VND identified, detained and confessing within two days; no source reports recovery of the property or the money, so the benefit to him is resolution, not restitution.

  • -2
    organizational

    Tân Hưng Ward Police and PC02 cleared a masked-intruder burglary in under 72 hours, and seven outlets carried the case as the police's demonstration of the AI camera system.

The system's part

The system's contribution was identification: it matched the masked intruder's gait, body shape, clothing, movement and facial data and reconstructed his route, which pointed police to him. The detention, confession and case-building were police work. The system and its vendor are not named, no official is quoted by name, every report draws on the police account, and the OECD Monitor tags the entry under privacy and data governance and respect of human rights, the concerns that come with public facial recognition.

Replication

No attempt to repeat it has been recorded.

What produced it

  • Street AI cameras covering the route the intruder took, with footage retained for police review.
  • A model that matches people on gait, body shape, clothing and movement as well as face, so a mask does not defeat identification.
  • A prompt police report, the morning of the burglary, and a ward investigation team able to act on the match within hours.

What must hold again

  • The suspect passes through camera coverage and can be found at a fixed location afterwards, here an internet café.
  • Identification is confirmed by police work and a confession; the sources report no independent check of the match.
  • The system and vendor are unnamed, so performance beyond this case cannot be checked.
