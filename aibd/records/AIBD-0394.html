HIBA offers multilingual health information in Lebanon and Jordan

The HIBA chatbot, built on GPT-4, gives health information in text and audio; its makers report about 700 user queries in six months and use in shelters in Lebanon.

What happened

HIBA was built by the Humanitarian Engineering Initiative of the American University of Beirut with a Gates Foundation Grand Challenges grant. The HIBA chatbot combines retrieval, language models, speech recognition, and speech synthesis. It supports 28 languages. Testing included groups of 6, 20, and 50 participants, and the team used their feedback to refine language, usability, and content. The team reports that HIBA was being deployed in shelters for displaced people in Lebanon as of October 2024. In a document of 8 May 2025, the team reports that users submitted about 700 queries over six months, often on reproductive, sexual and mental health. The team's account to the International Telecommunication Union, current as of June 2025, describes access and user-testing work. It presents improved health literacy and better health decisions as expected outcomes.

Details

AI system

Model

HIBA used GPT-4 for language generation, Whisper V3 for speech recognition, and ElevenLabs for spoken responses.

Reported configuration

GPT-4 (OpenAI) for response generation, with Whisper V3 for speech recognition and ElevenLabs for speech synthesis

Inputs and outputs

Users sent text or voice questions. Retrieval supplied curated public-health material, and the assistant returned text or spoken information in supported languages.

Autonomy

HIBA was designed to provide health information, with guardrails against diagnosis or treatment guidance.

Workflow

The account does not establish conditions sufficient to reproduce the benefit.

Human oversight

Developers and community participants reviewed content, language, and usability. Testing removed diagnostic material and unsuitable technical or U.S.-specific wording.

Benefits

Reach

The team reports that users submitted about 700 queries over six months. That figure counts questions, and no source gives the number of users. Testing involved groups of 6, 20 and 50 participants. [1] [2]

People and places

Dependencies

The WhatsApp service used Pinecone retrieval, DigitalOcean hosting, and Docker. METABase and Flower supported performance monitoring.
