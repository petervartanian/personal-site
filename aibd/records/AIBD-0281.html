Face2Gene's DeepGestalt changes geneticists' testing in 17 cases

Face2Gene's DeepGestalt facial analysis changed geneticists' testing decisions in 17 retrospectively selected cases involving 19 patients, leading to confirmed diagnoses.

American Journal of Medical Genetics Part A 197(11); online 4 July 2025. Abstract read on Europe PMC (PMID 40613735); one author at FDNA Inc.

    We present 17 cases involving 19 patients, including one pair of affected siblings and one case involving a child and her affected mother

  3. Peer-reviewed study Independent author Context or contrary evidence

    Wenderott K, Krups J, Zaruchas F, Krawitz P, Weigl M, Lesmann H. Workflow analysis and evaluation of a next-generation phenotyping tool: A qualitative study of Face2Gene. European Journal of Human Genetics, 23 May 2025. The authors are at the University of Bonn and University Hospital Bonn and declare no competing interests. They interviewed 15 German geneticists, ten of them routine users, and report that Face2Gene has become part of diagnostic routines; they did not examine the cases in this series.

    Despite being an additional step in the diagnostic pathway, Face2Gene has been effectively incorporated into geneticists' diagnostic routines, facilitating decision processes, and potentially expediting diagnoses for some patients.

  4. Peer-reviewed study Independent author Context or contrary evidence

    Bruwer Z, Mc Donald H, Zieff MR and colleagues (University of Cape Town, Stellenbosch University, the Broad Institute and KEMRI-Wellcome). Utility of Face2Gene's DeepGestalt and D-Score applications in paediatric neurodevelopmental disorders in South Africa. European Journal of Human Genetics, 27 August 2026. No author is at FDNA; the authors found that DeepGestalt placed the confirmed diagnosis first in 19% of 36 South African children, which limits how far the case series can be generalized.

    These findings suggest that under-representation of African populations may limit clinical performance and equity of AI-based facial phenotyping tools.
