What happened

Veterinarians at Ludwig-Maximilians-University Munich ran a blinded prospective study on a conventional one-site piglet-producing farm in Germany. Its nursery holds 600 pigs per airspace in 20 pens. Two batches were monitored for 39 days each. Two SoundTalks monitors (SoundTalks NV, Leuven) counted coughs around the clock and distinguished pig coughs from other barn noise. They computed a daily respiratory health status (ReHS) from 0 to 100, with yellow alerts at 40–59 and red at 0–39. The LEDs were set to a blinded “science mode” so the investigators were not cued. Oral fluids and bioaerosol samples were screened by qPCR for swine influenza A virus, PRRSV, Mycoplasma hyopneumoniae, Actinobacillus pleuropneumoniae and PCV2. All but M. hyopneumoniae were detected. High swIAV-RNA loads were significantly associated with a decrease in the AI-calculated respiratory health score.

Boehringer Ingelheim Vetmedica funded the study and three of its employees are co-authors. A 2026 Konkuk University trial in Korea used the same device's ReHS as an outcome measure across 2,000 grower-finisher pigs. The date is the LMU paper's publication date.

What it was built to do. Continuously count pig coughs, separate them from barn noise and compute a daily respiratory health status. Yellow and red alerts give an early warning of respiratory disease.

The system's part

The AI produced a respiratory health score that tracked swine influenza A loads measured by qPCR. Alerts were hidden from investigators, so the study measures association rather than any alert that changed what happened. Funding and three co-authors came from Boehringer Ingelheim Vetmedica. The paper states the funders were not involved in collection, analysis or interpretation.

Replication

What produced it. Two SoundTalks monitors in a 600-pig nursery airspace. Parallel oral fluid and bioaerosol qPCR sampling. Circulating swine influenza A virus producing coughing.

What must hold again. Monitors able to separate pig coughs from barn noise. Pathogen loads high enough to depress the score.
