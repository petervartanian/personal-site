SoundTalks health score falls with high swine influenza A loads

off the scale

LMU Munich blinded study in a German nursery: swine influenza A loads in oral fluids and bioaerosols were associated with falls in the AI's respiratory health score; no intervention outcome.

What happened

Veterinarians at Ludwig-Maximilians-University Munich ran a blinded prospective study on a conventional one-site piglet-producing farm in Germany whose nursery holds 600 pigs per airspace in 20 pens; two batches were monitored for 39 days each. Two SoundTalks monitors (SoundTalks NV, Leuven) counted coughs around the clock, distinguished pig coughs from other barn noise and computed a daily respiratory health status (ReHS) from 0 to 100, with yellow alerts at 40-59 and red at 0-39; the LEDs were set to a blinded 'science mode' so the investigators were not cued. Oral fluids and bioaerosol samples were screened by qPCR for swine influenza A virus, PRRSV, Mycoplasma hyopneumoniae, Actinobacillus pleuropneumoniae and PCV2; all but M. hyopneumoniae were detected. High swIAV-RNA loads were significantly associated with a decrease in the AI-calculated respiratory health score. The study was funded by Boehringer Ingelheim Vetmedica and three of its employees are co-authors. A 2026 Konkuk University trial in Korea used the same device's ReHS as an outcome measure across 2,000 grower-finisher pigs. The date is the LMU paper's publication date.

What it was built to do. Continuously count pig coughs, separate them from barn noise and compute a daily respiratory health status with yellow and red alerts as an early warning of respiratory disease.

The system's part

The AI produced a respiratory health score that tracked swine influenza A loads measured by qPCR. Alerts were hidden from investigators, so the study measures association rather than any alert that changed what happened. Funding and three co-authors came from Boehringer Ingelheim Vetmedica; the paper states the funders were not involved in collection, analysis or interpretation.

Replication

What produced it. Two SoundTalks monitors in a 600-pig nursery airspace. Parallel oral fluid and bioaerosol qPCR sampling. Circulating swine influenza A virus producing coughing.

What must hold again. Monitors able to separate pig coughs from barn noise. Pathogen loads high enough to depress the score.
