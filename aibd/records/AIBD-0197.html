Citable summary

LMU Munich blinded study in a German nursery: swine influenza A loads in oral fluids and bioaerosols were associated with falls in the AI's respiratory health score; no intervention outcome. Association between cough scores and viral RNA does not establish that alerts improved treatment, survival, growth, or antibiotic use. Blinded monitoring supports an observational measurement result rather than a demonstrated intervention benefit. The funder’s involvement and full diagnostic performance were not reassessed.
