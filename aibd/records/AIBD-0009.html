AI intrusion alert stops Kamrup Express as about 60 elephants cross

16 October 2024: AI intrusion alert and crew braking stopped 15959 Kamrup Express as about 60 elephants crossed near Lamsakhang, Assam

ⓘ

AI system

AI-based Intrusion Detection System (distributed acoustic sensing on optical fiber), Northeast Frontier Railway

Workflow

Fiber-optic acoustic sensing with AI classification installed along an elephant corridor. Alert routed to loco pilots and station masters in time to brake.

Human oversight

Not assessed

Relation to AI

The operator's release says the drivers noticed the herd and applied the emergency brake and were “also alerted” by the IDS. One local report says the braking followed the IDS warning. The AI alert is documented, but its share of the causal credit is not separable from the crew's own sighting. Hence protective response, prevention unproven.

Results

Reference: on an average 20 elephants die due to train collisions in the country every year and a majority of these incidents take place in the Northeast Frontier Railway. Result: since the launch of the system, these 11 corridors have not reported any train-elephant collision.
