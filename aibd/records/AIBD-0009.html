Train crew brake for about 60 elephants as an AI system flags the herd

On 16 October 2024 Northeast Frontier Railway's AI-based Intrusion Detection System alerted the crew of the Kamrup Express, who also saw about 60 elephants crossing in Assam and braked.

  • 2 an organization

    Northeast Frontier Railway's express train stopped short of about 60 elephants, and no collision occurred. A collision would have carried a risk of derailment and disruption.

  • 4 society

    A herd of about 60 wild elephants, a protected species, was on the track as the express approached, and the train stopped before reaching it. The sources report that no collision occurred, so a serious harm to the herd was averted. The Rajdhani Express collision of December 2025 nearby killed seven elephants.

The system’s part
The operator's release says the drivers noticed the herd and applied the emergency brake and were “also alerted” by the IDS. One local report says the braking followed the IDS warning. The AI alert is documented, but its share of the causal credit is not separable from the crew's own sighting. Hence protective response, prevention unproven.
Limits
The crew both saw the elephants and received an alert. The cited reports do not establish that the AI warning caused the stop or that a collision would otherwise have occurred. The railway's counts of elephants saved do not say how many elephants were exposed to trains, so they cannot be compared across periods or sections.
Costs and harms
The sources report no cost or harm from the stop. The system covers only part of the network: On 20 December 2025 a Rajdhani Express killed seven elephants at Sangjurai, where the railway said that no such system was installed.
Later
Scaled up, 5 December 2025. By December 2025 the system covered 141 route kilometers of Northeast Frontier Railway and tenders had been awarded for 981 more route kilometers nationwide.

What happened

On 16 October 2024 at about 8:37 p.m., train 15959 Kamrup Express was running from Guwahati toward Lumding. It neared a herd of about 60 wild elephants crossing the track between Habaipur and Lamsakhang stations in Assam. Northeast Frontier Railway said that loco pilot J.D. Das and assistant loco pilot Umesh Kumar noticed the herd and applied the emergency brake. It said that the AI-based Intrusion Detection System operating on that section also alerted the drivers. That system uses existing optical fibers as sensors to identify animal movements and alert control offices, station masters, gatemen and loco pilots. The train stopped and no collision occurred. Northeast Frontier Railway reported that it saved 414 elephants in 2023 and 383 up to 16 October 2024. By December 2025 the system covered 141 route kilometers of Northeast Frontier Railway, and tenders had been awarded for 981 more route kilometers nationwide. On 20 December 2025 a Rajdhani Express killed seven elephants at Sangjurai, about 30 kilometers away, where the railway said that no such system was installed. The Times of India reported on 21 December 2025 that the system runs on the Habaipur to Lumding section and covers about 70 kilometers of track in Assam. On 8 October 2026 The Sentinel reported that the railway counted 255 elephants protected in 2026 across four divisions.

Details

AI system

Autonomy

The warning system alerted railway personnel. The drivers applied the brakes and stopped the train.

Workflow

Fiber-optic acoustic sensing with AI classification installed along an elephant corridor. Alert routed to loco pilots and station masters in time to brake.

Human oversight

The system sends alerts to station masters and loco pilots. The Sentinel reported in October 2026 that the alerts prompt them to reduce train speed to 25 kilometers per hour when wildlife is near the tracks. In this case the loco pilot and the assistant loco pilot saw the herd and applied the emergency brake. [1] [2] [3] [4] [5] [6] [7]

Benefits

Beneficiaries

The approximately 60 elephants crossing the railway were the immediate beneficiaries of the safe stop.

Reach

About 60 wild elephants crossed safely in this case. The railway reported that it saved 414 elephants in 2023 and 383 up to 16 October 2024. The Sentinel reported on 8 October 2026 that the railway counted 255 elephants protected in 2026. [1] [2] [3] [4] [5] [6] [7]

Duration

The benefit was a single stop on 16 October 2024. By December 2025 the system covered 141 route kilometers of Northeast Frontier Railway. In October 2026 the railway reported 255 elephants protected in 2026. [1] [2] [3] [4] [5] [6] [7]

Follow-up

Conditions for replication

Train speed and sighting distance allow a stop after the alert. Crew act on the alert. The operator attributes this stop partly to the crew's own sighting.
