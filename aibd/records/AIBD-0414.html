What happened

Pilots in Fresco and Sassandra, Côte d’Ivoire, combine radar, optical, and lidar imagery with 300 ground-truth inventory plots, which the team measured from January to May 2025. In its submission of June 2025, the team says that the system produces monitoring outputs for conservation and carbon accounting, with expansion toward Senegal. The submission does not quantify a restoration effect or a resulting carbon-market transaction. The team released its 300 field plots as an open dataset on 2 October 2025. Its accompanying paper derives biomass from allometric equations and lists the machine-learning maps as future work. On 28 May 2026 the team opened a code repository for CarbonLens, a platform for AI carbon-stock maps of Senegal.

AI system

Model

The mangrove system uses random forests and XGBoost; convolutional models are proposed as a later enhancement.

Inputs and outputs

The system combines radar, optical, and lidar imagery with measurements from 300 ground plots to estimate mangrove biomass and carbon stocks.
