Summary
ML poverty maps chose 1,163 poorest urban wards for Nigeria's COVID-19 cash register; 49,000 beneficiary households as of 15 October 2021
Account
Lacking an urban social registry, Nigeria's National Social Safety Nets Coordinating Office (NASSCO) built the Rapid Response Register for a government-funded COVID-19 cash transfer. Ward-level poverty maps came from machine learning (ML) algorithms applied to satellite imagery with household-survey labels, by Berkeley and Columbia researchers with World Bank economists. The maps selected 1,163 of the poorest urban wards, population over 20 million. Residents self-registered by USSD after SMS blasts (first wave June 2021), then in-person verification. Human Rights Watch records the program began in January 2021. The PNAS authors state disbursement began mid-January 2021 (day not given; first day of month used). World Bank monitoring reports 49,000 beneficiary households of the government's COVID-CT as of 15 October 2021. It reports over 1.75 million registrations by November 2021, against a government target of one million households.

Account
Lacking an urban social registry, Nigeria's National Social Safety Nets Coordinating Office (NASSCO) built the Rapid Response Register for a government-funded COVID-19 cash transfer. Ward-level poverty maps came from machine learning (ML) algorithms applied to satellite imagery with household-survey labels, by Berkeley and Columbia researchers with World Bank economists. The maps selected 1,163 of the poorest urban wards, population over 20 million. Residents self-registered by USSD after SMS blasts (first wave June 2021), then in-person verification. Human Rights Watch records the program began in January 2021. The PNAS authors state disbursement began mid-January 2021 (day not given; first day of month used). World Bank monitoring reports 49,000 beneficiary households of the government's COVID-CT as of 15 October 2021. It reports over 1.75 million registrations by November 2021, against a government target of one million households.
Replication
Produced by
  • Deep-learning poverty map trained on geo-located household surveys
  • Government decision to adopt the ward ranking for eligibility
  • SMS blasts and USSD self-registration
  • In-person verification, de-duplication and payment onboarding
Must hold
  • Ground-truth survey data for training labels
  • Ward boundaries compatible with map resolution
  • Mobile-phone access in target wards
  • Government funding for transfers and identity verification capacity
