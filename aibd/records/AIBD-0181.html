What happened

The OECD AI Incidents Monitor entry dated 16 January 2026 summarizes Ghanaian reports that the Ghana Revenue Authority launched Publican AI, a trade data analytics platform to identify customs fraud, undervaluation and falsified import documentation, and states that the system has already recovered GH¢15 million from fraudulent importers, sourcing a GhanaWeb story titled 'Government recovers GH¢15 million through new trade data system'; the entry is classified as an AI Incident with the harm type economic harm to property interests. The Ghanaian Times (Kingsley Asare, 19 January 2026) reported Commissioner-General Dr Anthony Sarpong saying that the Publican AI system would give 100 percent coverage of assessment, that assessments could be completed within five minutes instead of about two hours, that pilots showed 40-45% revenue gains, and that rollout would begin at Tema Port on 1 February 2026; that article cites no realized recovery. The vendor is described only as a Cyprus-based firm. The date used is the AIM entry date.

What it was built to do. Identify customs fraud, undervaluation and falsified import documentation and give full coverage of customs assessment.

Replication

No attempt to repeat it has been recorded.

What produced it. Trade data analytics applied to every import declaration rather than sampled assessments.

What must hold again. Access to shipment declarations and valuation references. Enforcement capacity to collect assessed underpayments. Rollout beyond the Tema Port start.
