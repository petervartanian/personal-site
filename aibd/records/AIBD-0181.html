What happened

The OECD AI Incidents Monitor entry dated 16 January 2026 summarizes Ghanaian reports that the Ghana Revenue Authority launched Publican AI. That is a trade data analytics platform to identify customs fraud, undervaluation and falsified import documentation. The entry states that the system has already recovered GH¢15 million from fraudulent importers. Its source is a GhanaWeb story titled “Government recovers GH¢15 million through new trade data system”. The entry is classified as an AI Incident with the harm type economic harm to property interests. The Ghanaian Times (Kingsley Asare, 19 January 2026) reported remarks by Commissioner-General Dr Anthony Sarpong. He said that the Publican AI system would give 100 percent coverage of assessment. He said that assessments could be completed within five minutes instead of about two hours. He said that pilots showed 40–45% revenue gains.

He said that rollout would begin at Tema Port on 1 February 2026. That article cites no realized recovery. The vendor is described only as a Cyprus-based firm. The date used is the date of the Monitor entry.

What it was built to do. Identify customs fraud, undervaluation and falsified import documentation and give full coverage of customs assessment.

The system's part

Not assessed. Neither source describes how the analytics identify undervaluation or what share of the claimed recovery the system produced. The system is vendor-built and deployed by the GRA.

Replication

No attempt to repeat it has been recorded.

What produced it. Trade data analytics applied to every import declaration rather than sampled assessments.

What must hold again. Access to shipment declarations and valuation references. Enforcement capacity to collect assessed underpayments. Rollout beyond the Tema Port start.
