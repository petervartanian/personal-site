GRA's Publican AI recovers GH¢15 million, OECD AIM summary says

the system has already recovered GH¢15 million from fraudulent importers

GRA's Publican AI customs analytics, supplied by an unnamed Cyprus-based firm, rolled out from 1 February 2026 at Tema Port; the OECD AI Incidents Monitor summary says GH¢15 million was recovered, ...

What happened

The OECD AI Incidents Monitor entry dated 16 January 2026 summarises Ghanaian reports that the Ghana Revenue Authority launched Publican AI, a trade data analytics platform to identify customs fraud, undervaluation and falsified import documentation, and states that the system has already recovered GH¢15 million from fraudulent importers, sourcing a GhanaWeb story titled 'Government recovers GH¢15 million through new trade data system'; the entry is classified as an AI Incident with the harm type economic harm to property interests. The Ghanaian Times (Kingsley Asare, 19 January 2026) reported Commissioner-General Dr Anthony Sarpong saying that the Publican AI system would give 100 per cent coverage of assessment, that assessments could be completed within five minutes instead of about two hours, that pilots showed 40-45% revenue gains, and that rollout would begin at Tema Port on 1 February 2026; that article cites no realised recovery. The vendor is described only as a Cyprus-based firm. The date used is the AIM entry date.

What it was built to do. Identify customs fraud, undervaluation and falsified import documentation and give full coverage of customs assessment.
