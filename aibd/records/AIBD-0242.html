What happened

Mohamed Abdel-Aty and Shengxuan Ding of the University of Central Florida's Smart and Safe Transportation Lab assembled 2,100 automated-vehicle crash records. They came from the California DMV and NHTSA's Standing General Order databases (1,099 SAE Level 4 ADS, 1,001 Level 2 ADAS). They added 35,113 human-driven-vehicle crashes from California's SWITRS and fitted a matched case-control logistic regression. Compared with human-driven vehicles, the odds of an ADS crash in rain were 0.335 times, and most pre-crash movements reduced crash odds. But the odds of an ADS crash at dawn or dusk were 5.250 times and in turning maneuvers 1.988 times those of a human-driven vehicle. The authors attribute the advantages to object detection, precision control and decision-making, and the disadvantages to sensor adaptation to changing light and limited situational awareness.

The paper was received 30 Sep 2023, accepted 2 May 2024 and published 18 Jun 2024, the date given. The analysis does not attribute vehicles to specific developers, so no individual maker is credited. The Europe PMC full text is among the sources.

What it was built to do. Automated driving systems are built to drive without human intervention. The study measures their crash occurrence against human-driven vehicles.

The system's part

The study compares the conditions under which ADS and human-driven crashes occurred. It does not observe interventions and cannot separate exposure differences from system performance. Independent university authors; regulator databases as sources.

Replication

What produced it. Mandatory crash reporting for automated vehicles to the California DMV and under NHTSA's Standing General Order. A state police-reported crash database (SWITRS) with matching variables for road, environment and pre-crash movement. Matched case-control logistic regression across road, environment and crash type.

What must hold again. Continued regulator collection of ADS and ADAS crash reports. Comparable variable definitions between ADS reports and human crash records. Enough ADS crashes in each condition to estimate odds ratios.
