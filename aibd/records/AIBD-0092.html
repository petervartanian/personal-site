Study suggests seven-day flood warnings in parts of Mali

Forecasts could allow earlier action in parts of Mali, but Google Flood Hub produced frequent false alarms. No benefit from an implemented early action was measured.

ⓘ

What happened

Researchers evaluated Google Flood Hub and GloFAS forecasts against river-flow observations and records of flood impacts in Mali. Their results suggest that some early-action plans could extend from four to seven days without much loss of forecast skill. Performance varied across districts and thresholds, and no correctly predicted floods were found in the northern districts that were evaluated. At a seven-day lead time, Google Flood Hub’s false-alarm ratio was around 0.8 across discharge thresholds, compared with around 0.5 for GloFAS. The authors describe GloFAS as more balanced in several comparisons. They used a ten-day matching window to compare forecasts with reported impacts, which affects the interpretation of performance. This was a retrospective forecast evaluation; it did not measure benefits from an implemented early action.

The researchers are independent of Google, although one coauthor works at the Joint Research Centre, which contributes to GloFAS.

Purpose. AI-based riverine flood forecasting for ungauged basins to support early warning and anticipatory action.

AI’s contribution

The study directly evaluates Google Flood Hub’s machine-learning forecasts. It measures forecast performance, not a humanitarian outcome or a deployed change to the Mali Red Cross’s early-action protocol.

Further evidence

Setting. Global AI flood forecasts available for ungauged Malian rivers. Evaluation against discharge observations and text-mined district-level impact data across lead times and thresholds.

Conditions. Forecast thresholds and matching windows require local validation; performance differs substantially by district. Frequent false alarms need to be considered alongside the potential for earlier warnings. The proposed change to early-action planning requires implementation and outcome evaluation.
