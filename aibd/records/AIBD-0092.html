Study suggests that parts of Mali could get seven days of flood warning

A retrospective evaluation suggests that Google Flood Hub forecasts could extend some early-action plans in Mali from four to seven days, with frequent false alarms; no early action was measured.

Relation to AI

The authors compare Google Flood Hub's machine-learning forecasts with the physics-based GloFAS and with Mali's current trigger model on the same floods. They report that Flood Hub detects more floods than GloFAS at return-period thresholds but raises more false alarms. Any humanitarian benefit would depend on the Red Cross adopting the triggers and acting on them, which no source reports.

Results

Flood Hub and GloFAS keep enough skill beyond a four-day lead time in frequently flooded regions; at seven days Flood Hub's false-alarm ratio is about 0.8 against about 0.5 for GloFAS.
