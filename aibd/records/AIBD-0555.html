Northumberland builds FloodAI to forecast flash floods in six catchments

Northumberland County Council's FloodAI machine-learning service aims to forecast flash floods in six catchments; it was still a prototype in October 2025.

What happened

In October 2024 the Environment Agency reported that the Northumberland FloodAI project team was applying AI to a network of 43 smart sensors and river gauges and aimed to develop automated flood warnings by 2025. In June 2025 the council reported that the FloodAI research project was developing forecasts for six flash-flood-prone catchments. The council said that the system was at alpha stage and approaching limited piloting. The Environment Agency's national report of 13 August 2025 still described the model as in development and testing. On 24 October 2025 the Northumberland Gazette reported that the warning system was still a prototype. No source documents an issued warning or an avoided flood impact.

Details

AI system

Role

The proposed models forecast flash floods in small catchments using hydrological and weather data. [1]

Workflow

The project covers six catchments and combines university research with council, Arup, Isle Utilities, and Environment Agency participation. Training sources include Met Office, Environment Agency, and hydrological-model data.

AI contribution

Machine-learning models combine environmental and hydrological data to support flash-flood forecasting. The council described the service as alpha stage in June 2025. On 24 October 2025 the Northumberland Gazette reported that the warning system was still a prototype. No warning, response or avoided harm is attributed to operational use. [1] [2] [3]

Benefits

Reach

The project targets six Northumberland catchments that do not have regular Environment Agency flood warnings. No source counts the people or properties in those catchments or reports a warning that reached anyone. [1] [2] [3]

Duration

The Environment Agency gave a target of automated flood warnings by 2025. In October 2025 the warning system was still a prototype. No source reports an issued warning. Project funding runs until 2027. [1] [2] [3]
