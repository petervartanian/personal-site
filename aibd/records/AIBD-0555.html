What happened

Northumberland's FloodAI research project is developing forecasts for six flash-flood-prone catchments. At publication, the system was at alpha stage and approaching limited piloting. No issued warning or avoided flood impact is documented.

What it was built to do. Develop forecasts for flash-flood-prone catchments that lack suitable warning coverage.

Not assessed. Machine-learning models combine environmental and hydrological data to support flash-flood forecasting. At publication, the service was at alpha stage. No warning, response, or avoided harm is attributed to operational use.
