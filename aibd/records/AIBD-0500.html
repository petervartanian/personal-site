A Gaza university plans offline AI teaching for crisis zones

Al-Aqsa University is developing an offline multi-agent AI learning system that is expected to sustain teaching in crisis zones; no source reports a result.

What happened

Al-Aqsa University in Gaza is developing a low-power, offline learning system for crisis settings and is seeking partners to expand it. The project team expects the system to keep learning going around the clock. The team also expects automatic grading to reduce the workload of instructors. No report after June 2025 describes a result from the system.

Sources

  1. Report, by the makers.

    Project account in International Telecommunication Union, AI for Good: Innovate for Impact, 2025. Information current as of June 2025. Outcomes are reported by the project team. The account provides no independent outcome evaluation.

    Read Raspberry Pi hosting, local Wi-Fi, on-device models, a mesh design, and planned Arabic support.     Expected benefits include 24/7 learning continuity, reduced instructor workload through auto-grading and scalable deployment to other conflict or disaster zones.

Details

AI system

Configuration

The described design places lightweight tutors and recommendations on Raspberry Pi servers, with smartphone access over local Wi-Fi and a mesh for sharing educational content.

Workflow

Conditions that could produce the benefit are unspecified.
