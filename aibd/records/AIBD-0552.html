Lancashire uses AI traffic insights to help plan bus routes

Lancashire County Council reports that its Future Mobility Platform, which combines Aimsun, VivaCity and Alchera tools, has informed bus-route planning; no measured outcome is reported.

What happened

Lancashire combines Aimsun, VivaCity and Alchera tools for traffic forecasts, junction observations and congestion analysis in its Future Mobility Platform. On 18 March 2025 the council announced an Aimsun Live pilot on the A6 London Road in Preston and said that it would evaluate the pilot's impact over 12 months. The council's case study of 25 March 2025 says that insights from the platform have been used to plan bus routes. It gives no before-and-after congestion, travel-time, service or safety outcome. On 15 October 2026 New Civil Engineer reported, in coverage of Alchera's funding round, that the Alchera platform studies more than 300 junctions and 85,000 network links in Lancashire. No result from the Preston evaluation has appeared.

Details

AI system

Role

VivaCity classifies road users, while Aimsun forecasts traffic and Alchera identifies congestion hotspots. [1]

Inputs and outputs

Roadside observations feed traffic models that council engineers use for transport planning. [1]

Workflow

The program integrates historical and real-time transport data and draws on cross-council technical and procurement expertise. Different vendor components contribute predictions, observations, and hotspot analysis.
