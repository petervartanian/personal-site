What happened

AIforPet's product TTcare Equine analyzes the identity and the gait of horses. The ITU account names the Korea Racing Authority as the pilot partner, and one of its listed contacts has a Korea Racing Authority email address. AIforPet reports that the pilots demonstrated practical feasibility and user acceptance. It plans broader datasets covering more breeds, ages, and activities. AIforPet's requirements state that diagnostic results should serve as reference information and that the final decision should rest with the veterinarian. AIforPet intends the system to improve horse care and to support sustainable livestock management. No source quantifies a change in management or in horse welfare.

Details

AI system

Model

The submission names RTMPose for pose estimation, SegFormer and Mask R-CNN for identification imagery, and MoCoDAD and CNN-LSTM for lameness analysis.

Inputs and outputs

Smartphone photographs supplied horse-identification features, while videos supplied gait measurements and possible movement abnormalities.

Workflow

The account does not establish conditions sufficient to reproduce the benefit.

Human oversight

Veterinarians retained the final diagnostic decision. The source explicitly describes model results as reference information.

Follow-up

Conditions for replication

The account does not establish requirements for reproducing the benefit in other settings.

Availability

TTcare Equine was described as a pilot with the Korea Racing Authority. Its data and code were private.

Evidence

Original evidence

The only source is AIforPet's own account to the International Telecommunication Union, current as of June 2025. The International Telecommunication Union provides no independent evaluation of the outcomes. The account names the Korea Racing Authority as the pilot partner. [1]
