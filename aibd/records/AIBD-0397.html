MEDIAIPLUS reports faster clinical-trial preparation with its AI tools

MEDIAIPLUS reports that its MediC and FiCRO tools cut trial-data alignment time by 85% and outsourcing costs by 10% and raised trial success by 25% in early deployments.

What happened

MEDIAIPLUS describes AI tools for protocol preparation and matching sponsors with research organizations. Early deployments reportedly reduced data-alignment time by 85% and outsourcing costs by 10%, while improving trial success by 25%. Blisstech is named as a validation partner. The submission does not give trial counts, comparison periods, or a patient benefit attributable to the tools. In December 2025 MEDIAIPLUS chief executive Jung Jihee told Fortune Korea that FiCRO cuts the time to select a research organization by about 87% and supplies 70% of company revenue. The company's savings figures vary across its statements, and no client or independent evaluator has published results.

Details

AI system

Inputs and outputs

Trial requirements, registry data, and research-organization profiles supported protocol suggestions, recruitment and feasibility analysis, and ranked partner recommendations.

Workflow

The account does not establish conditions sufficient to reproduce the benefit.

Human oversight

Sponsor staff and clinical experts would review proposed protocols and partner matches before committing a trial or contract. The described matching API includes expert review.
