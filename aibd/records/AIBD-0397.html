MediC and FiCRO report faster clinical-trial preparation

What happened

MEDIAIPLUS describes AI tools for protocol preparation and matching sponsors with research organizations. Early deployments reportedly reduced data-alignment time by 85% and outsourcing costs by 10%, while improving trial success by 25%. Blisstech is named as a validation partner. The submission does not give trial counts, comparison periods, or a patient benefit attributable to the tools.

What it was built to do. Streamline protocol planning and selection of clinical research partners.
