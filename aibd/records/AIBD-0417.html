WIMPLE enables recycling collection in Busan public spaces

Seoreu reports that WIMPLE, its AI-powered recycling bin, made recycling collection possible in Busan public spaces and improved collection scheduling.

What happened

Seoul Economic Daily reported on 7 November 2023 that Dongseo University and Seoreu had begun a field test of the bin at the Seomyeon underground shopping center in Busan. The article states that the bin is expected to raise recycling rates by double digits, and it reports no measured result. The Busan Facilities Corporation pilot separates recyclables at the point of disposal. In its ITU account of 2025, Seoreu reports better separation, reduced contamination, and improved collection scheduling. The account adds that citizens became proficient with the bin after about one year. A related Canadian sorting system reportedly achieved over 98% accuracy. Wider recycling-rate and pollution benefits remain intended and have not been measured in the Busan pilot.
