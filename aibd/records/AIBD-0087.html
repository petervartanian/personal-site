What happened

In its 30 December 2024 statement on the year's results, the Controladoria-Geral da União (CGU) reported that an alert from its ALICE robot led CGU auditors to examine the budget of a federal road-works engineering tender, after which federal managers made adjustments that reduced the reference value by about R$100 million (CGU audit report 1632530). The statement names neither the contracting agency nor the date of the case, and its 'this year' framing attaches only to a separate water-bottle example, so the date observed is the date of the release that reports it. The same release gives 2024 aggregates: 118,069 procurement processes analyzed, 206 preventive audits opened on purchases worth R$25.57 billion, and R$257.25 million saved in concluded audits.

ALICE reads planning documents and notices on Compras.gov.br with artificial-intelligence techniques and issues risk alerts that auditors confirm.

What it was built to do. Continuous automated audit of federal procurement to flag budget and pricing risks before contracting

The system's part

The CGU states the chain explicitly: 'A partir do alerta do ALICE, auditores da CGU examinaram a adequação de um orçamento de uma obra rodoviária, o que gerou ajustes efetuados pelos gestores federais.' The adjustment was made by managers after a human audit; ALICE's role was the initial alert. Developer and deployer are the same public body; the page was retrieved from the Internet Archive because the live gov.br page was restricted during Brazil's 2026 electoral period.

Replication

What produced it. ALICE alert on a road-works budget. CGU auditors' examination of the budget's adequacy. Federal managers adjusting the reference value before contracting.

What must hold again. Budget spreadsheets published with the notice. Audit review before the tender closes. Managers' willingness to revise estimates.
