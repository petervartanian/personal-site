CGU's ALICE alert leads to a R$100 million cut in a road-works tender

A CGU ALICE alert on a federal road-works tender led managers to cut its reference value by about R$100 million, per CGU's 30 December 2024 results release

ⓘ

What happened

In its 30 December 2024 statement on the year's results, the Controladoria-Geral da União (CGU) reported on an alert from its ALICE robot. The alert led CGU auditors to examine the budget of a federal road-works engineering tender. Federal managers then adjusted it, reducing the reference value by about R$100 million (CGU audit report 1632530). The statement names neither the contracting agency nor the date of the case. Its “this year” wording applies only to a separate water-bottle example. The same release gives 2024 aggregates: 118,069 procurement processes analyzed and 206 preventive audits opened on purchases worth R$25.57 billion. It reports R$257.25 million saved in concluded audits. ALICE reads planning documents and notices on Compras.gov.br with artificial-intelligence techniques and issues risk alerts that auditors confirm.

Purpose. Continuous automated audit of federal procurement to flag budget and pricing risks before contracting

AI’s contribution

The CGU states the chain explicitly: “A partir do alerta do ALICE, auditores da CGU examinaram a adequação de um orçamento de uma obra rodoviária, o que gerou ajustes efetuados pelos gestores federais.” The adjustment was made by managers after a human audit. ALICE's role was the initial alert. Developer and deployer are the same public body. The page is on record as an archived copy, because the live page was restricted during Brazil's 2026 electoral period.
