Millions of students get real-time study help from the QANDA app

Mathpresso reports that its QANDA app gives students real-time question solving, adaptive feedback and study plans; no source measures learning gains.

What happened

Mathpresso added AI answer search to its QANDA tutoring app in October 2017. On 25 August 2023 Chosun Edu reported the beta launch of Poly, a tutor built on large language models inside the QANDA app, and said that ten million students used the app each month. A Wikipedia article flagged for a close connection with the company reports 90 million registered users and 8 million monthly active users in 50 countries as of March 2024. In a project account current as of June 2025, Mathpresso describes real-time question solving, adaptive feedback and study plans through the deployed QANDA platform. No source measures learning gains or reductions in educational inequality.

Details

AI system

Role

OCR extracts students’ questions from images, and educational language models use retrieved material to generate answers and adaptive feedback. Broader help with notetaking and memorization is described as future work.

Workflow

Conditions that could produce the benefit are unspecified.

Benefits

Reach

Chosun Edu reported on 25 August 2023 that ten million students used the app each month. A Wikipedia article flagged for a close connection with the company reports 90 million registered users and 8 million monthly active users in 50 countries as of March 2024. These counts measure use of the app, and no source counts students whose learning improved. [1]

Duration

Mathpresso added AI answer search to the app in October 2017, and the project account of June 2025 describes the platform as deployed. Reports from 2023 and 2024 give monthly user counts. No source follows learning outcomes over time. [1]
