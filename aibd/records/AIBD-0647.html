SELENA+ results on the spot bring more Tanzanians to eye care

In a Tanzanian trial of 2,378 people with diabetes, SELENA+ results and counselling at screening raised clinic attendance for referable retinopathy from 18.2% to 35.9%.

What happened

Researchers from the London School of Hygiene & Tropical Medicine and Kilimanjaro Christian Medical Centre ran an individually randomized trial at diabetes and noncommunicable disease clinics in 24 hospitals in the Kilimanjaro and Arusha regions of Tanzania. Between 8 March 2023 and 31 August 2024 they enrolled 2,378 adults with diabetes. In the AI group, retinal photographs went to the SELENA+ software from EyRIS, and each person received the result with face-to-face counselling before leaving the photography room. In the standard group, people received a result from human graders by telephone a few weeks later. Among the 904 participants whom masked UK-certified graders judged to have referable diabetic retinopathy, 157 of 437 (35.9%) in the AI group attended the central ophthalmology clinic within eight weeks, against 85 of 467 (18.2%) in the standard group. The trial tests the whole pathway, so the effect combines the instant result with face-to-face counselling. Even in the AI group, nearly two in three people with referable disease did not attend within eight weeks. The British Council for the Prevention of Blindness, the Christian Blind Mission and the Sir Halley Stewart Trust funded the trial.

Details

AI system

Workflow

The AI gave a grade at the clinic before the person left. Staff counselled people face to face and referred them at once. The comparison was deferred human grading with telephone counselling weeks later.

Measurement

Measurement

Attendance at the central ophthalmology clinic within 8 weeks, among participants with referable retinopathy by masked UK-certified graders. [1]

Setting and sample

2,378 adults with diabetes at 24 hospitals in Kilimanjaro and Arusha, 8 March 2023 to 31 August 2024; 904 with referable disease. [1]

Measures

Clinic attendance within 8 weeks. [1]

Results

35.9% vs 18.2%; odds ratio 2.52. [1]

Additional value from AI

17.7 percentage points. [1]

Limitations

AI effect not separated from face-to-face counselling; attendance remained low in both groups. [1]

Denominators

AI grading with immediate result for all participants in the intervention group. [1]

Materials for replication

Registered as ISRCTN18317152; protocol published in BMJ Open. [1]

Follow-up

Conditions for replication

The AI must work offline and with the local cameras, two criteria that the trial team used to choose it. Staff must be available to counsel people at the point of screening. People must be able to travel to the central eye clinic.

Replication attempts

2 follow-up entries. Benefit recurred: 2.

Entry details

Sector

Healthcare.

Purpose

SELENA+ was built to grade retinal photographs for diabetic retinopathy. In this trial it gave a result at the point of screening so that staff could counsel and refer people at once; the higher attendance was the purpose of that use.
