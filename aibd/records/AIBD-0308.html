What happened

OPTiM Corporation announced on 6 October 2017 a demonstration trial of its “AI Physical Security Service” from 10 October 2017. It would run at two Kyushu Railway Company (JR Kyushu) stations: 和白駅 (Wajiro) on the Kashii Line and 二島駅 (Futajima) on the Chikuho Main Line. The service, on “OPTiM Cloud IoT OS”, has AI monitor network-camera video in real time. When it detects an anomaly, it shows an alert and emails administrators. This is to reduce the burden on operators who watch many monitors and to raise safety in the station. The release includes an image of station camera analysis identifying people, trains and the yellow platform line. The release is the only source (on optim.co.jp and republished by @Press). JR Kyushu is not quoted, no trial end date, detection target list or result is given, and no later report of outcomes was found. The date observed is the trial start date.

Station romanizations are ours.

What it was built to do. Trial of AI video monitoring to detect anomalies on station platforms and alert administrators. No detection event is documented.
