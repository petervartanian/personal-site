What happened

Kyushu Railway Company (JR Kyushu) made Wajiro and the other Kashii Line stations except Kashii and Chōjabaru unstaffed from 14 March 2015 and serves their passengers remotely. OPTiM Corporation announced on 6 October 2017 a demonstration trial of its “AI Physical Security Service” from 10 October 2017. It would run at two JR Kyushu stations: 和白駅 (Wajiro) on the Kashii Line and 二島駅 (Futajima) on the Chikuho Main Line. The service, on “OPTiM Cloud IoT OS”, has AI monitor network-camera video in real time. When it detects an anomaly, it shows an alert and emails administrators. OPTiM says that this is to reduce the burden on operators who watch many monitors and to raise safety in the station. The release includes an image of station camera analysis identifying people, trains and the yellow platform line. OPTiM published the release on optim.co.jp, and @Press republished it. The release is the only source on the JR Kyushu trial. JR Kyushu is not quoted. The release gives no trial end date and no list of detection targets. No result of the JR Kyushu trial has been published. The only reported detection rate, 96.2% against a 90% target, comes from OPTiM's later trial at JR East's Sawa station, announced on 26 June 2018. Station romanizations are ours.

Details

AI system

Inputs and outputs

Station-camera video produced anomaly alerts on screen and emails to administrators.

Workflow

Network cameras at two JR Kyushu stations streamed to OPTiM's cloud AI service. Real-time analysis identifying people, trains and the platform yellow line and flagging anomalies. Alert display and email to administrators.

Human oversight

The service shows an alert on the monitor and emails administrators when it detects an anomaly. The release gives no response procedure, and no source reports how staff responded. [1] [2] [3] [4]

Benefits

Beneficiaries

The intended beneficiaries were station passengers and staff who otherwise watched multiple monitors; the retained launch account does not establish an achieved safety benefit.

People and places

Dependencies

The trial required connected station cameras, access to OPTiM Cloud IoT OS, and administrators who could receive alerts.

Follow-up

Conditions for replication

Operators or administrators who act on the alerts; the release gives no response procedure. Detection accuracy adequate for station use; no figures were published.
