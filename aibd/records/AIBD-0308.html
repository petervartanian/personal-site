OPTiM trials AI camera monitoring at two JR Kyushu stations

off the scale

OPTiM's release says its cloud AI monitors station camera video in real time, recognizing people, trains and the platform yellow line, and alerts administrators on anomalies; no results published.

What happened

OPTiM Corporation announced on 6 October 2017 that from 10 October 2017 it would run a demonstration trial of its 'AI Physical Security Service' at two Kyushu Railway Company (JR Kyushu) stations, 和白駅 (Wajiro) on the Kashii Line and 二島駅 (Futajima) on the Chikuho Main Line. The service, which runs on 'OPTiM Cloud IoT OS', has AI monitor network-camera video in real time and, when it detects an anomaly, shows an alert and emails administrators, to reduce the burden on operators who watch many monitors and to raise safety in the station; the release includes an image of station camera analysis identifying people, trains and the yellow platform line. The release is the only source (on optim.co.jp and republished by @Press); JR Kyushu is not quoted, no trial end date, detection target list or result is given, and no later report of outcomes was found. The date observed is the trial start date. Station romanisations are ours.

What it was built to do. Trial of AI video monitoring to detect anomalies on station platforms and alert administrators; no detection event is documented.
