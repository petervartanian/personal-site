OPTiM trials AI camera monitoring at two JR Kyushu stations

OPTiM's release says its cloud AI monitors station camera video in real time, recognizing people, trains and the platform yellow line, and alerts administrators on anomalies; no results published.

ⓘ

What happened

OPTiM Corporation announced on 6 October 2017 a demonstration trial of its “AI Physical Security Service” from 10 October 2017. It would run at two Kyushu Railway Company (JR Kyushu) stations: 和白駅 (Wajiro) on the Kashii Line and 二島駅 (Futajima) on the Chikuho Main Line. The service, on “OPTiM Cloud IoT OS”, has AI monitor network-camera video in real time. When it detects an anomaly, it shows an alert and emails administrators. This is to reduce the burden on operators who watch many monitors and to raise safety in the station. The release includes an image of station camera analysis identifying people, trains and the yellow platform line. The release is the only source (on optim.co.jp and republished by @Press). JR Kyushu is not quoted, no trial end date, detection target list or result is given, and no later report of outcomes was found. Station romanizations are ours.

Purpose. Trial of AI video monitoring to detect anomalies on station platforms and alert administrators. No detection event is documented.

Since then
OPTiM announced on 26 June 2018 that a trial of the same service with Itec Hankyu Hanshin at JR Sawa station on the Joban Line achieved a 96.2% detection rate of people or objects crossing the platform's yellow line while no train was present, against a 90% target; no result for the JR Kyushu stations was found.オプティム< 3694 >は26日、アイテック阪急阪神(本社:大阪府大阪市)と共同でJR常盤線佐和駅において、オプティムが提供する「AI Physical Security Service」の実証実験を実施し、目標を上回る検知率96.2％を達成したと発表。今回、目標として設定した検知率(電車が駅に不在時に、ホーム際の黄色い線より線路側に人もしくは物体が侵入した際にAIが検知した割合)は90％としていた。 source

Further evidence

Setting. Network cameras at two JR Kyushu stations streamed to OPTiM's cloud AI service. Real-time analysis identifying people, trains and the platform yellow line and flagging anomalies. Alert display and email to administrators.

Conditions. Operators or administrators who act on the alerts; the release gives no response procedure. Detection accuracy adequate for station use; no figures were published.
