What happened

The Amelia Scott uses Adobe Lightroom AI in artifact-imaging workflows, producing digital twins and tactile display objects. The council reports 50% faster scan/image processing and better scan quality, plus actual interactive exhibitions and digital/physical-twin outputs. Potential commercial revenue is not realized income. A richer visitor experience is not equivalent to measured learning gains. Training, partnerships, and digital production jointly contribute to these results. The source does not isolate AI’s contribution to employment or visitor learning.

What it was built to do. Improve artifact-imaging workflows and create digital and tactile museum experiences.

The source attributes faster masking, editing, and improved scan preparation to Lightroom AI within a broader production workflow. Training, partnerships, and other tools also contribute, so employment and visitor-learning effects cannot be isolated to AI.
