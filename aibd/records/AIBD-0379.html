Robot dog checks armed man on Hollywood bus; standoff ends in arrest

LAPD deployed its Boston Dynamics Spot quadruped during a 8 November 2023 standoff with an armed man asleep on a Hollywood Metro bus; he woke, surrendered and was arrested; the gun was a BB gun.

ⓘ

What happened

Just before 4 a.m. on 8 November 2023, a passenger on a Metro bus in Hollywood reported an armed man aboard. The bus was at Melrose and Western avenues. The driver pulled over and evacuated the other passengers. Officers got no response from the man, and a two-hour standoff followed (Los Angeles Times via Police1, 9 November 2023). The LAPD sent in its Boston Dynamics Spot quadruped. Its camera confirmed the man was asleep with a gun (Police1). The LAPD's quarterly report to the Police Commission says the robot was a barrier between suspect and firearm (NBC Los Angeles, 8 March 2024). The man woke, left the bus and was taken into custody about 6:40 a.m. The weapon was a BB gun (Police1). Deputy Chief David Kowalski approves each use. The robot cost nearly $300,000 (NBC). International Business Times' October 2024 account says the robot removed the gun.

Purpose. Acquired, in Deputy Chief Kowalski's words, “to save lives” by putting a camera and an articulating arm into dangerous scenes ahead of officers. This standoff was that use.

AI’s contribution

The accounts describe a remotely deployed quadruped with a camera and an articulating arm. None says that any autonomous function made a decision in this incident. The robot contributed a camera view confirming the man was asleep and, per the LAPD's report, a physical barrier between him and the gun. The Monitor's “AI-powered” label rests on the Boston Dynamics platform, not on anything the system is reported to have decided.

Beneficiaries

  • an organization

    The LAPD could confirm from the robot's camera that the man was asleep. By its own report, it kept the robot between him and the gun. The two-hour standoff ended in an arrest with no injury reported. A measurable result for the unit, with no counterfactual that officers would otherwise have been hurt.

  • unrated
    a person

    The passengers had been evacuated before the robot was used, and the suspect was taken into custody unharmed. None of the reports ties either outcome to the robot rather than to the man waking and leaving the bus.

Further evidence

Setting. An armed or apparently armed person unresponsive in a confined space that officers cannot safely approach. A tactical unit with a camera-and-arm ground robot on call and a deputy chief's approval for each use.

Conditions. Officers position the robot and watch its camera before any approach rather than acting on its own motion. Each deployment is approved in advance and reported to the Police Commission and City Council.
