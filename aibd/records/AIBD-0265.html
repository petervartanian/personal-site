An implanted interface restored communication for nearly seven years

A woman with locked-in amyotrophic lateral sclerosis used the Utrecht NeuroProsthesis, an implanted brain-computer interface, for nearly seven years of independent at-home use, until March 2023.

Costs and harms

The participant underwent two operations and extensive training. The follow-up supplement records a procedure-related fever with hospital readmission. Loss of reliable control ended home use in March 2023. In 2016 Niels Birbaumer warned that such implants may carry an unknown risk for people with advanced ALS, and Phillip Kennedy said that one to two letters a minute did not justify a craniotomy. Benefit marks

Depth

The number and color show the depth of a benefit.

  1. 1Depth 1. A convenience, or time or money saved.
  2. 2Depth 2. A measurable improvement in a result or ability.
  3. 3Depth 3. A lasting change, an ability restored, or a risk removed.
  4. 4Depth 4. A serious harm averted or a serious condition resolved in time.
  5. 5Depth 5. A life or livelihood preserved, or a change documented across a population.

Beneficiary

  • Circle: a person
  • Square: an organization
  • Triangle: society

Evidence

  • Solid. A court or regulator found a benefit, or researchers independently studied it.

  • Hatched. Independent reporting.

  • Outline. Claimed by the parties. Only the people or organizations involved, or sources whose independence has not been assessed, support the benefit.

The fill changes with the evidence. The depth color stays the same.
