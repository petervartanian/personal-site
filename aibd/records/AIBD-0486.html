Nego-Wiz saves KT Commerce 8,300 hours by automating price negotiations

KT Commerce says that Nego-Wiz, AI O2O's price negotiation software, automated 60,000 tasks and saved 8,300 hours in partial use during 2024.

  • 2 an organization

    KT Commerce says that partial use in 2024 saved 8,300 hours. The developer reports 80 percent less negotiation time and average profit margins 5 to 6 percent higher. A higher margin is a measurable business result, and no outside party has checked it.

What happened

A KT Commerce official said in May 2025 that partial use of Nego-Wiz in 2024 had automated 60,000 tasks and saved 8,300 hours. AI O2O reports that Nego-Wiz shortened negotiations during a KT Commerce pilot from December 2024 to March 2025. That account does not state how much time the pilot saved. The Korea University Industry-Academic Cooperation Foundation reports in its profile of AI O2O that the system automates 28,000 negotiations a year at KT Commerce. The foundation also reports that negotiation time fell by 80 percent and that average profit margins rose by 5 to 6 percent. No outside party has checked these figures. On 5 February 2026 a profile of the developer in Hankyung JOB&JOY reported that KT Commerce still used the system for tens of thousands of negotiations a year.
