Locust control fed by eLocust3m may have averted USD 1.56 billion

PlantVillage's eLocust3m app gathered over 240,000 locust records, and AI removed unusable ones for the control operation that FAO credits with averting USD 1.56 billion in losses.

Reasons

Society: FAO states that East African control operations prevented the loss of 4 million tonnes of cereal and 800 million liters of milk. FAO says that they protected the food security of 36.6 million people. The Times quotes FAO's USD 1.5 billion averted since February 2020. PlantVillage's AI removed the unwanted data. Over 240,000 records were collected in a year from scouts and citizens who could not have used the discontinued eLocust3 tablets.
