Facial recognition finds a wanted woman in downtown Cuiabá

Diário de Cuiabá (14 July 2025): Sesp-MT's Vigia Mais MT cameras matched a woman with a June 2024 trafficking warrant in central Cuiabá on 12 July; Ciosp tracked her live and the 1st BPM arrested her.

ⓘ

What happened

On Saturday 12 July 2025 the video-monitoring system of Mato Grosso's State Secretariat of Public Security (Sesp) helped arrest a woman in downtown Cuiabá. It acted through the Vigia Mais MT program. She had an open warrant for drug trafficking. That is per the same text carried by Diário de Cuiabá and O Documento on 14 July and CliqueF5 on 15 July 2025. Cameras in the city center identified her by facial recognition. After the system confirmed her identity, the monitoring center of the Integrated Public Security Operations Center (Ciosp) tracked her movements in real time. It passed them to a team of the 1st Military Police Battalion, which made the approach. Her warrant had been issued on 19 June 2024 by the 9th Criminal Court Specialized in Drug Offenses. She was taken to a Civil Judicial Police unit.

The reports add that from April to July 2025 about 60 fugitives were located and arrested by state security forces.

Purpose. Vigia Mais MT is the state's video-monitoring program. Its facial recognition compares faces seen on public cameras with wanted persons so that Ciosp can direct patrols to them. Locating a person with an open warrant is that purpose.

AI’s contribution

The reports credit the facial recognition system with the identification. Cameras in central Cuiabá matched her face and the system confirmed her identity. Ciosp operators then tracked her and a Military Police team made the arrest. The sources do not name the vendor, the algorithm or any match score. They come from a single text that reads as the state's release.

Beneficiaries

  • an organization

    Sesp-MT's Ciosp located a woman wanted since a 19 June 2024 warrant and tracked her in real time, per the 14 July 2025 reports. It handed her to the 1st Military Police Battalion, which arrested her. A measurable result for the unit.

  • society

    One person with an open drug-trafficking warrant was taken into custody. The reports give no evidence of any further harm prevented. So the public benefit is the execution of a warrant.

Further evidence

Setting. Public cameras in central Cuiabá feeding the Vigia Mais MT facial recognition. A warrant record for the woman in the list the system compares against. Ciosp operators able to confirm the match and follow her across cameras in real time. A Military Police team near enough to make the approach.

Conditions. The person passes a camera with a usable view of the face. The warrant is current in the database the system queries. Operators confirm the match before a patrol is sent.
