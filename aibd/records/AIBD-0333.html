Victor converts 10 million appeal pages to text at Brazil's top court

Victor, the AI project of Brazil's Supreme Court and the University of Brasília, has converted appeals to text since December 2020, with more than 10 million pages processed by May 2021.

Relation to AI

By the STF's own account of 19 August 2021, the AI classifier that would flag repercussão geral themes was not in production. The only live component was automatic OCR of incoming appeals, which the STF frames as part of the Victor project. Any theme indication is validated by the ministers. The time savings announced in 2018 (three hours or 40 minutes to five seconds) were forecasts made by chief justices at launch. All cited sources are the deployer's news service (STF) and the CNJ, which the same chief justice presided. No independent evaluation was found.
