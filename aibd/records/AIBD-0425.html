Planned generative AI would rebuild disaster scenes from compact data

Tata Consultancy Services proposes a system that uses semantic scene understanding and generative rendering so that disaster responders can share visual information over limited bandwidth.

What happened

Three Tata Consultancy Services researchers described the disaster-tracking scenario in a paper at the 17th International Conference on Communication Systems and Networks in Bengaluru on 6 January 2025. The authors present disaster tracking as one of two application scenarios and report lessons from an initial proof of concept for one of them. The concept seeks to reconstruct useful visual scenes from compact transmitted information. In the International Telecommunication Union entry, current as of June 2025, the team states that the concept is not fully deployed. The team also reports that existing datasets do not suit the purpose and that it needs to develop its own. Faster disaster response and reduced consequences are intended benefits. No source reports an observed response or rescue outcome.
