Birmingham reports faster redaction and automated email indexing

Birmingham City Council reports that its Foundry tools, built with Microsoft Copilot and UiPath, redact a document in 90 seconds, compared with 10 minutes by hand, and index 55% of emails.

  • 1 an organization

    The council reports time and cost savings from redaction and mailbox indexing. Translation savings and avoided fines remain prospective.

  • 1 a person

    Staff report less repetitive work, more time for meaningful duties, and improved job satisfaction. Lasting changes in well-being are unmeasured.

The council also reports weaker performance on complex translation and restrictions on sensitive inputs, and neither commissioners' report gives a measured result for the tools.

What happened

Birmingham reports that its Foundry tools automate redaction, mailbox indexing, administrative work, and simple translation. Redaction reportedly takes 90 seconds instead of 10 minutes with 95% success. Mail indexing reportedly handles 55% of emails, saving 5,500 staff hours and £100,000 annually. The former £350,000 translation budget is not a demonstrated saving. The source also reports translation limitations and guardrail difficulties. In their second report, dated 31 January 2025 and published on 31 March 2025, the government-appointed commissioners wrote that the Foundry signals early progress and that they support it. Their third report, dated 23 October 2025 and published on 1 December 2025, says that the council was still using the approach and is at an early stage. Neither report gives a measured result for the tools.

Details

AI system

Role

Copilot helps summarize documents, UiPath supports redaction and routes incoming revenues-and-benefits emails, and a SharePoint-hosted translation tool handles simple documents.

Workflow

Council officers and developers co-designed tools around four back-office functions in iterative development cycles. The reported redaction tool covers three document types, and the mailbox tool handles a share of incoming emails.
