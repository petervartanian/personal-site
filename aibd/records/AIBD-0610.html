Viz.ai stroke alerts shortened thrombectomy start times in Houston

In a stepped-wedge trial at four Houston stroke centers, Viz.ai automated large vessel occlusion detection with phone alerts cut door-to-groin time by 11.2 minutes.

What happened

Investigators at UTHealth Houston activated Viz.ai software at four comprehensive stroke centers in the greater Houston area in a random stepped order between 1 January 2021 and 27 February 2022. The software read CT angiograms of patients with suspected stroke, flagged large vessel occlusions within minutes and pushed alerts with images to the care team's phones. The analysis covered 243 patients with large vessel occlusion who arrived through the emergency department and received thrombectomy. Before activation, the median time from arrival to groin puncture was 100 minutes. After activation, door-to-groin time fell by 11.2 minutes (95% confidence interval 4.2 to 18.2), and time from CT to the start of thrombectomy fell by 9.8 minutes. The trial found no difference in clot-dissolving drug timing, length of stay or functional independence at 90 days. Dr Sheth, the senior author, reported grants from Viz.ai during the study.

Details

AI system

Workflow

Stroke imaging was sent automatically to the cloud software, which returned results in under five minutes on average. Clinicians and radiologists received alerts and images on a phone app.
