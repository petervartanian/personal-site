UNHCR develops AI to detect delays in refugee status decisions

UNHCR's Uncovering Delays project applies machine learning and process mining to historical case data to find delays in refugee status decisions; no time saving is reported.

  • Not rated a person

What happened

The project uses data science and AI to examine the procedures for determining refugee status. In its June 2025 account, UNHCR names faster administration and earlier access to rights and services as intended benefits. UNHCR expects pilot deployments in a later phase. On 8 August 2025 Anadolu Agency reported that the project was in its early stages and worked on historical anonymized case data. Adriana Rojas Arnaud of UNHCR told the agency that UNHCR had begun to see early results, but she gave no figures. UNHCR planned to work with live data and to build a business intelligence tool for managers. No source documents a reduction in processing time or an outcome for any applicant's legal status.

Details

AI system

Model

The process-analysis design includes linear regression and regression or decision trees to identify factors associated with refugee-status delays.

Role

The proposed AI analyzes the refugee-status determination process to identify administrative bottlenecks.

Workflow

The account does not establish conditions sufficient to reproduce the benefit.

Human oversight

UNHCR planned a business intelligence tool to help managers decide which changes to make from trends that machine learning and process mining detect. Anadolu Agency also interviewed a United Nations Human Rights Office spokesperson on safeguards. [1] [2]
