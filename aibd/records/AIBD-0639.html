What happened

Researchers at the School of Social Sciences of Leeds Trinity University in England ran a randomized trial with adults who had persistent tinnitus. They recruited through online advertisements with help from the charity Tinnitus UK. Participants used Woebot, a general cognitive behavioral therapy chatbot from Woebot Health, or Tinnibot, a tinnitus-specific app, or waited for eight weeks. The authors describe Woebot as a conversational agent that uses natural language processing. Among 105 participants, Woebot users lowered depression scores on the PHQ-9 from 10.03 to 8.06 and anxiety scores on the GAD-7 from 8.82 to 7.35. The waitlist did not change. Tinnibot users improved more and were the only group with a clinically meaningful drop in tinnitus distress. The authors declare no conflicts of interest, and a grant from the Australian Academy of Technology and Engineering funded the study. Tinnitus diagnoses were self-reported, all outcomes were self-reported, and the trial was not registered in advance. The authors report that the app was withdrawn from public use in June 2025.

Details

AI system

Workflow

Participants used the app daily for about 5 to 10 minutes over eight weeks. The trial recruited adults with self-reported persistent tinnitus who were not in other tinnitus treatment.

Measurement

Measurement

Self-report GAD-7, PHQ-9, Insomnia Severity Index and Tinnitus Functional Index before and after eight weeks. [1]

Setting and sample

105 UK adults with self-reported persistent tinnitus, three arms of 36 with three withdrawals. [1]

Measures

Change in anxiety, depression, insomnia and tinnitus distress. [1]

Results

Woebot: PHQ-9 -1.97, GAD-7 -1.47; Tinnibot: PHQ-9 -3.09, GAD-7 -2.50, TFI -13.23; control no reliable change. [1]

Additional value from AI

Isolated against no treatment. [1]

Limitations

Self-reported diagnosis and outcomes; trial not prospectively registered; small arms; post-intervention only. [1]

Denominators

Daily use for eight weeks. [1]

Materials for replication

Data available from the corresponding author on request. [1]

Follow-up

Conditions for replication

The app remains available to users. Users engage with it most days for several weeks.

Replication attempts

1 follow-up entry. Benefit did not recur: 1.
