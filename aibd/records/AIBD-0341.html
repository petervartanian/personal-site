DeepMind says GraphCast forecast Lee's landfall nine days ahead

forecast Lee's Nova Scotia landfall about nine days ahead

DeepMind says a live GraphCast run on ECMWF's site forecast Lee's Nova Scotia landfall about nine days ahead; NHC records landfall 16 Sep 2023; no forecaster use of the run is documented.

What happened

Google DeepMind's blog post of 14 November 2023 states that in September 2023 a live version of its publicly available GraphCast model, deployed on the ECMWF website, predicted about nine days in advance that Hurricane Lee would make landfall in Nova Scotia. MIT Technology Review (14 November 2023) relayed the claim from DeepMind staff research scientist Rémi Lam and added that traditional forecasting models pinpointed the hurricane to Nova Scotia only six days in advance; the article quotes no ECMWF staff on Lee. ECMWF's Newsletter 177 editorial (Autumn 2023) confirms that GraphCast, Pangu-Weather and FourCastNet were made available on ECMWF's public charts pages from ECMWF's own initial conditions. The NHC tropical cyclone report on Lee (21 March 2024) records that Lee, by then post-tropical, made landfall on Long Island in southwestern Nova Scotia around 2000 UTC on 16 September 2023 with maximum sustained winds of about 55 kt, that NHC official track errors were exceptionally low through 72 h and below five-year means through 120 h, and it mentions no AI model. The earlier verifier found no ECMWF document recording the Lee forecast, no AI-model entry in the NHC 2023 verification report, and only an intensity-structure mention of Lee in the independent CIRA preprint. The date used is the landfall date; the forecast itself is dated only as 'September' by the vendor. No fetched source documents a forecaster or authority acting on the GraphCast Lee run, so this is recorded as a general benefit without an attributable event.

What it was built to do. Machine-learning global medium-range weather forecasting (10 days, 0.25 degree), including severe-event prediction such as tropical cyclone tracks; this forecast was that purpose.

The system's part

The only sources for the nine-day Lee forecast are the maker's own blog and a news report quoting the maker's scientist; ECMWF's editorial confirms only that GraphCast ran experimentally on its charts, and NHC's Lee report records exceptionally low official track errors without mentioning AI guidance. The AI output is therefore documented as a forecast, not as an input to any decision.

Replication

What produced it
  • GraphCast trained on decades of ERA5 reanalysis and run live from ECMWF initial conditions on ECMWF's experimental charts
  • A long-lived Cabo Verde hurricane whose track was, per NHC, easier than normal to forecast (low climatology-persistence errors)
  • Vendor retrospective comparison of its own run against conventional models
What must hold again
  • Independent verification of the specific nine-day run against the official track record (not found in any ECMWF or NHC document fetched)
  • Access to ECMWF initial conditions for the live run
  • A forecaster or authority actually consulting the AI run for it to become an attributable benefit
