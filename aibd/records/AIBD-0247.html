What happened

Response.jp reported on 11 Nov 2022 that Seibu Railway would begin full operation of a “踏切異常検知システム” on 14 Nov 2022. It combines two technologies. One is an AI monitoring system developed by OKI and Marubeni Network Solutions. It uses skeleton recognition to detect people and objects left in crossings. The other is a 3D image-analysis system from Computer Systems Laboratory that uses stereo-camera depth. When a detection occurs, the special signal lamps that warn drivers of danger operate. Live video of the site is sent to the operations control center. Operation started at three crossings on the Ikebukuro and Shinjuku lines, with two more planned within fiscal 2022. The trial that began in December 2021 was reported to have confirmed stable operation. The sources are press-release based. The OECD AI Incidents Monitor lists the deployment as an aggregate benefit.

No detection event or accident outcome is documented.

What it was built to do. The system is built to detect people or vehicles remaining inside a crossing and warn approaching train drivers.
