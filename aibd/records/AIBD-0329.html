What happened

The OECD AI Incidents entry says that WINGS ICT Solutions deployed its wi.breathe platform in Troizinia-Methana, Greece. It says “the system enabled authorities to detect and prevent a fire within one minute”. The Monitor's eight listed Greek pages carry one text of 12 May 2026 announcing completion of the project. It describes hybrid optical-thermal cameras at Vigla (Poros), Tsepeleni Aderon and Agios Georgios Methanon. It describes a “specially trained WINGS artificial-intelligence model”, cloud infrastructure and 5G/4G networks. It also describes 24/7 surveillance, smoke and fire detection “even within 1 minute”, and alerts in real time. It carries a quote from Mayor Anastasios Mougios thanking the company. Its only event claim is one sentence.

It says that wi.breathe, “already from its trial operation in the area, detected in real time the start of a fire in the Troizina area and immediately notified the competent authorities, preventing its spread”. The sentence gives no date, time, place, responder, Fire Service or Civil Protection statement, or size or damage figure. The “within one minute” in the Monitor's summary is the release's capability claim. GOVNews bylines the text “newsroom”, and Reporter.gr headlines it “Wings:”. On capital.gr it is labeled a press release, and Eleftheros Typos files it under press releases; that page is not among the sources. WINGS's own English post of the same day (wings-ict-solutions.eu) announces “the successful completion of the wi.breathe project”, with the same sites and mayor's quote. It omits the detection entirely.

Its product page says that “wi.breathe.fire autonomous fire detection through AI and video analytics is deployed in Central Greece”. The municipality's website search for “wi.breathe” returns only a 3 February 2025 post. It covers a Best City Awards prize for the wi.BREATHE early fire-detection system and names no detected fire. A search for “fire Methana” returns a 13 June 2026 post. It covers the mobilization and immediate extinguishing of a fire in a rough area of Methana. Its listing does not mention the cameras. The sources include the municipality's 2 July 2026 statement on a large fire between Agios Georgios and Agios Nikolaos on the Methana peninsula. That statement does not mention them either. No independent, dated account of the trial detection was found.

What it was built to do. wi.breathe is sold for early wildfire detection and air-quality monitoring. The Troizinia-Methana installation was built to detect smoke and fire from hilltop cameras and alert civil-protection authorities. That is exactly the benefit claimed.
