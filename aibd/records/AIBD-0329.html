wi.breathe cameras reportedly flag a Troizina wildfire

WINGS says that its wi.breathe cameras and AI smoke and fire detector alerted authorities to a Troizina wildfire on 28 August 2025; the municipality's statement that day does not mention them.

What happened

ERT, Greece's public broadcaster, reported on 28 August 2025 that 45 firefighters, 13 vehicles, two planes and four helicopters had been sent to the fire at Diavologefyro near Troizina, and that footage showed the fire close to houses. Contemporary reporting by Saronic Magazine describes the fire spreading uphill, hours of firefighting, precautionary evacuations and damage to a shed. The municipality’s statement that evening credits a coordinated response involving more than 80 firefighters and six aircraft. It thanks the fire service, the police and volunteers and credits cooperation among agencies. It does not mention the cameras. These accounts establish the scale of the fire and the response, and none of them verifies the AI’s contribution. WINGS reported on 2 September 2025 that its wi.breathe cameras in Methana had detected the wildfire in Troizina on 28 August and alerted authorities. The company quoted Mayor Tasos Ath. Mougios crediting the system with preventing a larger disaster. The mayor's credit to wi.breathe appears only in this WINGS release. No alert log, detection time, response chronology or estimate of avoided damage accompanies that account. WINGS later announced completion of the wider camera installation on 12 May 2026. Circulated completion publicity gives a one-minute detection figure; that figure describes the system's capability, and no source measures it for the August fire.

Details

AI system

Inputs and outputs

Optical and thermal camera imagery is analyzed for smoke and fire. The platform delivers alerts through a dashboard, email, and text messages.

Workflow

Monitoring cameras that feed an AI smoke and fire detector. Alerts that reach civil-protection staff who can investigate and mobilize a response.

Human oversight

The system sends alerts to civil-protection authorities by dashboard, email and SMS, and people must act on them. No source describes how staff checked the alert of 28 August 2025. [1] [2] [3] [4] [5]

AI contribution

WINGS says that its AI detected fire and smoke from monitoring cameras and alerted authorities to the fire of 28 August 2025, and its release quotes the mayor crediting the system. The municipality's statement that day and ERT's report do not mention the cameras. No source gives the time of the AI alert, so the AI's share in the response is unknown. [1] [2] [3] [4] [5]

People and places

Dependencies

The installation depends on camera coverage, cloud processing, mobile connectivity, and notification channels that civil-protection staff can monitor.

Follow-up

Conditions for replication

Camera coverage and image quality must permit detection of the developing fire. Connectivity and notification channels must deliver alerts promptly. People and firefighting resources must be available to act on an alert.
