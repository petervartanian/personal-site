WINGS says wi.breathe cameras detected a fire start in Troizina

unrated societyunrated an organization

Identical press-release text ran on 12 May 2026 (GOVNews, Newsbeast, Reporter.gr); WINGS's English post omits the detection; the municipality's site has no post on it. No date, place or responder.

What happened

The OECD AI Incidents Monitor record 2026-05-12-c1ff says that WINGS ICT Solutions deployed its wi.breathe platform in Troizinia-Methana, Greece, and that 'the system enabled authorities to detect and prevent a fire within one minute'. The Monitor's eight listed Greek pages carry one text of 12 May 2026 announcing completion of the project: hybrid optical-thermal cameras at Vigla (Poros), Tsepeleni Aderon and Agios Georgios Methanon, a 'specially trained WINGS artificial-intelligence model', cloud infrastructure and 5G/4G networks, 24/7 surveillance, smoke and fire detection 'even within 1 minute', alerts in real time, and a quote from Mayor Anastasios Mougios thanking the company. Its only event claim is one sentence: that wi.breathe, 'already from its trial operation in the area, detected in real time the start of a fire in the Troizina area and immediately notified the competent authorities, preventing its spread'. The sentence gives no date, time, place, responder, Fire Service or Civil Protection statement, or size or damage figure; the 'within one minute' in the Monitor's summary is the release's capability claim. GOVNews bylines the text 'newsroom', Reporter.gr headlines it 'Wings:', and the earlier verifier found capital.gr labeling it a press release and Eleftheros Typos filing it under press releases (that page returned 403 here). WINGS's own English post of the same day (wings-ict-solutions.eu) announces 'the successful completion of the wi.breathe project' with the same sites and mayor's quote and omits the detection entirely; its product page says that 'wi.breathe.fire autonomous fire detection through AI and video analytics is deployed in Central Greece'. The municipality's website search for 'wi.breathe' returns only a 3 February 2025 post on a Best City Awards prize for the wi.BREATHE early fire-detection system, which names no detected fire; a search for 'fire Methana' returns a 13 June 2026 post on the mobilization and immediate extinguishing of a fire in a rough area of Methana, whose listing does not mention the cameras, and the earlier verifier found that the municipality's 2 July 2026 statement on a large fire between Agios Georgios and Agios Nikolaos on the Methana peninsula does not mention them either. No independent, dated account of the trial detection was found.

What it was built to do. wi.breathe is sold for early wildfire detection and air-quality monitoring; the Troizinia-Methana installation was built to detect smoke and fire from hilltop cameras and alert civil-protection authorities, which is exactly the benefit claimed.
