China Unicom says that Yuanjing made city management 25% more efficient

China Unicom's Yuanjing platform uses AI for dispatch, monitoring and event management, and the company reports a 25% improvement in urban management efficiency.

  • 1 an organization

    China Unicom reports a 25% improvement in urban management efficiency, which amounts to time and labor saved by city operators. The report names no city or measurement method for the figure.

  • Not rated a person

    Residents are intended to receive faster responses to requests, but no source reports a realized change for residents.

  • Not rated society

    China Unicom intends cities to become more inclusive and resource-efficient, and no source reports a measured change.

What happened

China Unicom describes AI dispatch, monitoring and event management for cities in its Yuanjing platform. In the 2025 report of the International Telecommunication Union (ITU), current as of June 2025, China Unicom states that the platform produced a 25% improvement in urban management efficiency in practical applications. The report names no city or measurement method for this figure, and it lists its deployment sites as private. No source measures a change in city services for residents.

Sources

Details

AI system

Role

Yuanjing retrieves policy and service information, checks it against structured data, answers inquiries, and can dispatch work orders automatically.

Configuration

The platform can run on government-owned servers or China Unicom cloud infrastructure, with a local knowledge base for each city.

Workflow

Conditions that could produce the benefit are unspecified.

Human oversight

The submission describes expert review of high-risk decisions and escalation of low-confidence cases, while high-confidence outputs can trigger actions automatically.
