What happened

Glajchen et al. (JACC: Case Reports, 3 June 2026) present ForeSight-HF, a clinical decision-support tool for heart-failure management in skilled nursing facilities that uses OpenAI's GPT-5 mini (accessed late 2025) on HIPAA-compliant infrastructure to synthesize unstructured documentation into heart-failure likelihood scores, exacerbation risk scores and management recommendations, reviewed by physicians in weekly interdisciplinary meetings on the ProviderLoop platform. Two cases illustrate it: a congested, well-perfused patient for whom it recommended intensified diuresis, and a hypovolaemic, hypoperfused patient for whom it recommended diuretic de-escalation. For each, the paper states that the patient remained at the facility for four months “managed independently of the AI system”.

The disclosures state that platform infrastructure was provided by ProviderLoop and that Dr Bander is its founder and owner; an earlier check set the case aside because outcomes are not attributable to the system. The date is the publication date.

What it was built to do. Generate heart-failure risk scores and management recommendations from nursing-facility documentation

The system's part

The model generated recommendations that the authors judge appropriate in two contrasting cases, but the paper states the patients were managed independently of the system. The only source is the vendor-affiliated case report (senior author is the platform's founder and owner).

Replication

No attempt to repeat it has been recorded.

What produced it. GPT-5 mini prompted with unstructured facility documentation. Weekly physician review of model output on the vendor platform.

What must hold again. Prospective validation showing recommendations change management and outcomes. Independent evaluation outside the vendor's authorship.
