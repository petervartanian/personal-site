ForeSight-HF scores two nursing-home heart-failure cases

Mount Sinai authors describe ForeSight-HF (GPT-5 mini on ProviderLoop) recommending diuresis for a warm/wet and de-escalation for a cold/dry nursing-home patient; both were managed independently.

ⓘ

What happened

Glajchen and colleagues describe ForeSight-HF in JACC: Case Reports, published online on June 3, 2026. The tool uses GPT-5 mini to produce heart-failure assessments and treatment suggestions from nursing-facility notes. The paper presents two illustrative cases and explicitly states that its outputs did not guide clinical care. Both patients were managed independently of the system for four months. In the second case, the clinicians’ actual diuretic and medication decisions differed from the AI recommendations. Weekly physician review is part of the proposed workflow, not evidence that the model directed these patients’ treatment. ProviderLoop supplied the platform, and senior author Jeffrey Bander disclosed that he founded and owned it. The patients’ clinical outcomes cannot be attributed to the AI.

Purpose. Generate heart-failure risk scores and management recommendations from nursing-facility documentation

AI’s contribution

The model generated illustrative recommendations for two selected cases. The authors expressly state that its outputs did not guide care. Case 2 describes differences between the recommendations and the treatment that clinicians actually provided. No prospective effect on treatment or health was tested.
