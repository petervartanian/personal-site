ForeSight-HF drafts heart-failure advice for two nursing-home patients

Mount Sinai authors report that ForeSight-HF (GPT-5 mini on ProviderLoop) generated diuresis advice for a warm/wet and de-escalation advice for a cold/dry nursing-home patient; neither guided care.

What happened

Glajchen and colleagues describe ForeSight-HF in JACC: Case Reports. The tool uses GPT-5 mini to produce heart-failure assessments and treatment suggestions from nursing-facility notes. The paper states that the authors accessed GPT-5 mini in late 2025. It presents two illustrative cases and states explicitly that the tool's outputs did not guide clinical care. Both patients were managed independently of the system for four months. In the second case, the clinicians’ diuretic and medication decisions differed from the AI recommendations. The proposed workflow includes weekly physician review. The paper gives no evidence that the model directed these patients’ treatment. ProviderLoop supplied the platform, and senior author Jeffrey Bander disclosed that he founded and owned it. The patients’ clinical outcomes cannot be attributed to the AI. The journal received the manuscript on 6 March 2026 and published it online on 3 June 2026. At Mass General Brigham, a separate team piloted GPT-5.2 heart-failure therapy recommendations in early 2026. Helseth and colleagues reported on 29 August 2026 that at least one recommendation was implemented at 13 of 56 visits (23%), with no control group. As of 29 October 2026, Europe PMC listed no trial, follow-up report or citing article on ForeSight-HF.

Details

AI system

Model

GPT-5 mini.

Role

The model generated care recommendations for illustrative cases that the authors compared with actual treatment.

Configuration

The authors accessed GPT-5 mini in late 2025.

Inputs and outputs

Unstructured nursing-facility notes were summarized into baseline and weekly updates. The tool generated heart-failure likelihood, confidence, exacerbation-risk, and hemodynamic assessments with care suggestions.

Autonomy

The tool generated illustrative advice and did not direct treatment in either reported case.

Workflow

GPT-5 mini prompted with unstructured facility documentation. A proposed workflow with physician review of the model output on ProviderLoop; the illustrated patients were managed independently of it.

Human oversight

The intended workflow requires physician authorization and weekly review with a remote cardiologist and on-site staff. The two illustrated patients were managed independently of those recommendations.

AI contribution

The authors used the model to generate recommendations for two selected patients and state that its outputs did not guide care. They did not test whether the recommendations improved treatment or health. [1]

Benefits

Beneficiaries

Clinicians in nursing facilities and their patients are the intended beneficiaries. These two cases demonstrate recommendation generation, not a measured clinical benefit from using it.

Measurement

Measurement

The authors ran GPT-5 mini with disease-specific prompts on nursing-facility documentation and set its scores and recommendations beside the care that clinicians gave independently. [1]

Setting and sample

Two heart-failure patients in a skilled nursing facility, chosen to show contrasting Forrester hemodynamic quadrants. [1]

Measures

Heart Failure Likelihood Score, exacerbation risk and Forrester classification, with narrative management recommendations. [1]

Results

The system classified one patient as Warm/Wet and recommended maintaining diuretic therapy with intensified monitoring, and classified the other as Cold/Dry and recommended diuretic de-escalation; neither patient needed hospitalization during follow-up. [1]

Additional value from AI

Not measured; outputs did not guide care. [1]

Limitations

Two purposively selected cases with no accuracy metric and no comparison group; the senior author founded the platform; the authors call for prospective validation. [1]

Denominators

None; patients were managed independently of the system. [1]

Follow-up

Prospective validation showing recommendations change management and outcomes. Independent evaluation outside the vendor's authorship.
