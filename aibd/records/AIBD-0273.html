ForeSight-HF scores two nursing-home heart-failure cases

Mount Sinai authors describe ForeSight-HF (GPT-5 mini on ProviderLoop) recommending diuresis for a warm/wet and de-escalation for a cold/dry nursing-home patient; both were managed independently.

AI system

System

ForeSight-HF (large-language-model clinical decision support on the ProviderLoop platform)

Model

GPT-5 mini.

Release

Exact release not established.

Application developer

ProviderLoop (New York) with Mount Sinai clinicians

Role

The model generated care recommendations for illustrative cases that the authors compared with actual treatment.

Configuration

The authors accessed GPT-5 mini in late 2025.

Workflow

The researchers supplied unstructured nursing-home documentation to GPT-5 mini through ProviderLoop. Their proposed workflow includes physician review, but the two illustrated patients received care independently of the model. [1]

Relation to AI

The model generated illustrative recommendations for two selected cases. The authors expressly state that its outputs did not guide care. Case 2 describes differences between the recommendations and the treatment that clinicians actually provided. No prospective effect on treatment or health was tested. [1]

AI contribution

The authors used the model to generate recommendations for two selected patients and state that its outputs did not guide care. They did not test whether the recommendations improved treatment or health. [1]

Conditions for replication

Prospective studies would need to test whether clinicians use the recommendations and whether patients benefit. An evaluation outside the developer’s team would help establish whether the findings hold elsewhere. [1]

Later findings

The two illustrative cases were managed independently of the system for four months, and the cited source reports no later deployment data, trial or follow-up.
