What happened

The Federal Authority for Government Human Resources (FAHR) runs the FAHR Smart App for employees of United Arab Emirates federal entities. The app includes AI support, learning recommendations, and leave and attendance functions. Its first phase launched in April 2024, following user-experience work. On the United Arab Emirates App Store, a user wrote on 10 May 2024 that the new update made applying for services easier with fewer steps. A United States App Store user reported on 16 November 2024 that the app was always under maintenance or showed errors. Neither review mentions AI features. In its submission to the International Telecommunication Union, current as of June 2025, FAHR describes intended gains in workforce efficiency and employee experience. It reports no measured time saving, service improvement or satisfaction change. FAHR reported on 11 September 2026 that its HR AI Agent was among its award-winning solutions. As of October 2026, the app holds 4.4 out of 5 from about 2,500 ratings on the United Arab Emirates App Store.
