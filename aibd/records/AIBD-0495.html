Researchers design InsurVision to analyze insurance claims faster

Researchers at IIT Hyderabad are building InsurVision, a set of language-model agents meant to analyze insurance claims; faster settlements remain an intended benefit.

  • Not rated an organization

    Insurers are intended to process claims faster and more accurately.

  • Not rated a person

    Claimants are intended to receive faster support after losses; no claim outcome is documented.

What happened

Shreesh Gupta and Arjit Gupta of IIT Hyderabad proposed InsurVision as a set of language-model agents that check policies, review photos, videos and documents, and decide claims. The team reports that its implementation plan is in progress. Faster settlements and disaster recovery remain intended benefits. The team planned pilots with insurers and disaster response agencies as future work.

Details

AI system

Model

The proposal lists GPT-4.1, Gemini 2.5 Pro, and Gemma 3 as model options, without establishing a single deployed configuration.

Reported configuration

GPT-4.1, Gemini 2.5 Pro and Gemma 3 (listed as technology keywords)

Role

The proposed agents check policy validity, analyze video, images, and documents, estimate costs, decide whether to approve a claim, and generate a report.

Workflow

Conditions that could produce the benefit are unspecified.

Human oversight

The proposal includes human expert feedback to reduce biased assessments. It does not establish that a claims handler must approve each payment decision.
