Weather-aware optical communications remain in simulation

MUST has simulated an optical-communication design while training an AI attenuation model, with hardware integration still planned.

ⓘ

What happened

The project uses climate information to predict free-space optical signal attenuation. MATLAB simulation is complete for the initial design, while the AI model is still being trained and integrated. More resilient and efficient communication is the intended benefit.

Purpose. Adapt optical communication to changing atmospheric conditions.
