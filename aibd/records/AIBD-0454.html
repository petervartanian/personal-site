An AI model for weather-aware laser links is still in training

MUST simulated a free-space optical link in MATLAB while training an AI model to predict attenuation from climate data; its July 2025 code holds laser-link sketches and no AI model.

What happened

The project uses historical climate data to predict the attenuation of a free-space optical signal. In the 2025 ITU account the team reports that the design and initial simulation are complete in MATLAB Simulink. The AI model is still being trained and has yet to be integrated. The intended benefit is more resilient and efficient communication. The code repository named in the use case holds two Arduino sketches for a keypad-driven laser link, uploaded on 15 July 2025 by the use case contact, with no trained AI model.
