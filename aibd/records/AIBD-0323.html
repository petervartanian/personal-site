Workflow

A moored passive acoustic buoy with automated detection and classification (Baumgartner et al. 2019 system) plus analyst review. Sightings from naturalists and NOAA aerial surveys, a daily blue whale habitat model, and AIS tracks processed by Global Fishing Watch. An existing NOAA voluntary speed-reduction zone with BWBS incentives for shipping lines.

Relation to AI

The automated detector on the buoy flags and classifies candidate whale calls, which an analyst confirms before they enter the daily rating. The habitat model is a statistical ensemble and the report cards are AIS arithmetic. The cooperation and risk figures belong to NOAA's and BWBS's voluntary slow-down program, which Whale Safe monitors and publicizes. No cited source separates Whale Safe's contribution from incentives, outreach and retailer pressure. The developer's own news office is the source of the claim that cooperation rose every year since launch.
