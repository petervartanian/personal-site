Brief from Peter

The Trotter Fire, west of Tehachapi in Kern County, California, started on 14 August 2023 and was held to 52 acres. An evacuation order in Bear Valley Springs was lifted the same afternoon. Those facts come from Cal Fire's page on the fire and a same-day report by KBAK in Bakersfield, and neither says how the fire was detected.1 In a feature published on 27 August 2026, Microsoft says that ALERTCalifornia's cameras and AI helped crews detect and contain the fire. The same feature says that Microsoft recently announced a $5 million contribution to ALERTCalifornia.2 Neal Driscoll, ALERTCalifornia's founder, estimates in the feature that without AI-powered imaging the fire may have spread to nearly 4,000 acres. No other source gives that estimate. The feature quotes a deputy chief of the Kern County Fire Department who also works for ALERTCalifornia.3 No source says which agency received an alert or when it arrived relative to 911 calls.
