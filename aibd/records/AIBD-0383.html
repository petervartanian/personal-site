Sky Sentinel AI turret may have downed four Shahed drones

not on the depth scale

Independently reported, 4 sources.

Ukraine's Sky Sentinel, an AI-controlled heavy-machine-gun turret, is reported to have downed four Russian Shahed drones with one prototype; dates and places are withheld (May-June 2025).

What happened

Sky Sentinel is an autonomous turret, a heavy machine gun on a 360-degree mount. Its software, written by Ukrainian engineers, takes radar input and separates drones from birds. It tracks the target, computes the ballistic solution and fires without a human. UNITED24 Media, the outlet of the UNITED24 fundraising platform, reported on 26 May 2025 that one prototype had downed four Shahed drones at the front. Dates and places were withheld for security. Its $1.5 million campaign for 10 turrets at about $150,000 each was complete. Forbes (David Hambling, 27 May 2025), as relayed by Euromaidan Press on 28 May 2025, repeats the four kills. It gives a pointing precision of 17 micro-radians. The Defense Post (3 June 2025) adds that 10 to 30 units would be needed to protect a city. It adds that production is scaling to dozens a month.

The Monitor's statement that a human authorizes each shot is not in any read report.

What it was built to do. Built to shoot down Shahed-type attack drones and other small air targets automatically. The reported interceptions are that purpose.

The system's part

Per UNITED24 Media and Forbes via Euromaidan Press, the AI does the whole engagement. It takes radar data, distinguishes drones from birds and tracks the flight path. It computes the shot with wind and target speed, and fires conventional rounds. The four kills are the makers' claim relayed by the fundraiser. No independent confirmation, footage date or location is given. The Monitor's human-authorization step does not appear in the read reports.

Replication

No attempt to repeat it has been recorded.

What produced it. Radar cueing that hands the turret a track on a slow, low-flying drone. A fixed heavy-machine-gun mount with near-zero mechanical play and fast optics. Rules that let the system engage without a human in the loop.

What must hold again. Targets fly within the gun's effective range and are not masked by birds or clutter. Enough units are fielded to cover a city, which the makers put at 10 to 30.
