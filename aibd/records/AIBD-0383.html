Ukraine's AI-aimed Sky Sentinel turret shoots down Shahed drones

A prototype of Sky Sentinel, a Ukrainian AI-aimed machine-gun turret, downed six Shaheds in six uses, a unit commander said; more than 12 turrets were on duty in Kyiv by October 2026.
