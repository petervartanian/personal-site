What happened

In a blue book of September 2024, the China Academy of Information and Communications Technology and the research institutes of China Telecom, China Mobile and China Unicom reported that the Jiangsu branch's configuration audit reached 94% recall and 85% precision for abnormal configurations in production. They name the branch's own operations center as the source of those figures. In a use case that the International Telecommunication Union published on 29 October 2025, China Telecom describes a framework that coordinates large and small models for network operations. It reports the same recall and precision figures. It reports that compliance of network device configurations improved to over 95%. It reports that anomaly detection fell from 10 minutes to 1 minute, with an average accuracy above 90%. The use case also reports substantial savings in staff time. China Telecom reports that digital employees cut operations staff by 30%. It also makes claims about customer satisfaction. The operational figures do not establish that a particular outage was prevented. They also do not establish that the staffing changes benefited employees. No independent evaluation of the figures had appeared by October 2026.

Details

AI system

Reported configuration

Cloud-Network Emergency Reasoning Model: a Qwen model fine-tuned on reasoning data generated by DeepSeek-R1, then tuned with GRPO

Role

The framework coordinates large and small models to detect network faults and check or improve configuration compliance.

Autonomy

The contributor reports that agents can create an incident group, invoke response agents, locate faults, and carry out fault handling automatically. The extent of supervised versus unattended production use is not documented.
