Proposed AI rainfall prediction for basin irrigation

depth unassessed

Claimed by the parties, 2 sources.

IARI proposes AI rainfall prediction to avoid unnecessary irrigation when rain is expected. The report separates this proposed extension from its existing soil-moisture automation.

What happened

IARI proposes using AI rainfall predictions to control irrigation gates. A forecast of rain the next day would close the gate. Otherwise, the motor could open it when soil moisture is low. The existing research-farm controller uses sensors and preset moisture thresholds. Water savings and other benefits from the proposed AI extension remain unmeasured. A cited water-saving figure comes from separate research.

What it was built to do. Use AI rainfall forecasts to avoid unnecessary watering while maintaining suitable soil moisture.

The system's part

Not assessed. The sequence diagram explicitly proposes AI rainfall prediction as an input to irrigation-gate decisions. Its effect has not been separately evaluated; existing threshold-based automation is a different component.

Replication

What produced it. The proposed design combines AI rainfall forecasts with soil-moisture readings and irrigation-gate control.

What must hold again. Working rainfall predictions, local inputs, soil-moisture sensors, and reliable gate control would be needed. Sufficient conditions for a beneficial outcome remain unknown.
