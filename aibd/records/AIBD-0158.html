ChatGPT and AlphaFold pick vaccine targets; dog's tumor halves

AI-chosen targets, a UNSW-made mRNA vaccine and a checkpoint inhibitor halved Rosie's largest tumor by early 2026; partial, not a cure.

AI system

System

ChatGPT (OpenAI) and AlphaFold (Google DeepMind), with Grok (xAI) reported for the final mRNA construct, used with Conyngham's own analysis to select tumor neoantigen targets for a personalized mRNA vaccine

Application developer

OpenAI (ChatGPT); Google DeepMind (AlphaFold); xAI (Grok)

Workflow

Whole-genome sequencing of tumor and healthy tissue, about 150 billion DNA letters for $3,000, from which neoantigen targets could be chosen with AI help. An academic RNA facility, the UNSW RNA Institute, willing to make a custom mRNA vaccine from an owner-designed sequence in under two months. A veterinary school with ethics cover and an immunotherapy specialist to administer it, after about three months of ethics paperwork. An owner with 17 years of machine-learning experience able to run the process, pay for it and drive ten hours for each dose.

Human oversight

Researchers sequenced the tumor and manufactured the vaccine. A veterinarian administered the treatment.

Relation to AI

The AI tools worked at the design stage. ChatGPT suggested immunotherapy, pointed Conyngham to UNSW's genomics center and helped him plan the analysis. AlphaFold modeled mutated proteins. He says he “confirmed, again using AI, that those targets were good cancer candidates”. Humans sequenced the genome (Martin Smith), made the mRNA and nanoparticles (Pall Thordarson) and injected it (Rachel Allavena). A checkpoint inhibitor was given at the same time. UNSW says “We don't know how much of the response came from the checkpoint inhibitor and how much from the mRNA vaccine”. The AI's share of the outcome therefore cannot be isolated. It is a single uncontrolled case, and no outcome data have been published outside interviews and statements.

AI contribution

Conyngham used ChatGPT to plan the investigation and AlphaFold to model mutated proteins when selecting targets for a personalized cancer vaccine for his dog. Grok was also reported as assisting with the final mRNA construct.

Follow-up

Conditions for replication

The response cannot be credited to the vaccine alone. A checkpoint inhibitor was given at the same time, and UNSW says the split is unknown. One uncontrolled animal. Mast cell tumors behave unpredictably, and some of Rosie's tumors did not respond. The benefit was time, not cure. The cancer returned after a later surgery, and the company founded on the case wrote in August 2026 that her story may have ended.

Later findings

17 August 2026. The GAMGEE launch release of 17 August 2026 says Rosie's cancer returned after a later surgery and writes of her in the past tense.

Assessed benefits

People. Organizations.

Depth

People: 2. Organizations: 1.

Reasons

People: Rosie was given months to live after surgery and chemotherapy. Her largest tumor halved and she regained mobility by late January 2026. Per ABC on 22 June 2026, she was still running and playing. The response was partial and confounded by a checkpoint inhibitor, and her cancer later returned. Organizations: UNSW's RNA Institute and Ramaciotti Center got what Thordarson called the first personalized cancer vaccine designed for a dog. They describe the case as a milestone. Conyngham's spin-out GAMGEE raised a $4 million seed round in August 2026 on the strength of it.

Entry details

Field

Veterinary oncology / personalized mRNA vaccine design. Companion animal with mast cell cancer.

Purpose

ChatGPT is a general-purpose assistant and AlphaFold a protein-structure predictor. Conyngham used the first to plan and navigate the veterinary and genomic work. He used the second to model mutated proteins as candidate targets. Both are uses within what each tool was built to do. Neither was built to treat Rosie. The vaccine was designed by Conyngham, made by UNSW and given by a veterinary school. The therapeutic response is a downstream use of the tools rather than their product purpose.
