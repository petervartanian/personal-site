Insilico's AI-designed drug improves lung function in a small trial

Rentosertib, designed with Insilico Medicine's generative-AI platform, raised mean forced vital capacity by 98.4 mL at 60 mg daily in a phase 2a trial, against a fall of 20.3 mL on placebo.

  • 2 a person

    Patients on 60 mg once daily gained a mean 98.4 mL in forced vital capacity over 12 weeks, while patients on placebo lost 20.3 mL. This is a measured improvement in a lung function test, which was a secondary endpoint in a small trial. Three patients on 60 mg and one on placebo had an acute exacerbation, and no lasting change in any patient's disease is reported.

  • Not rated society

    Any wider benefit depends on confirmation in larger, longer trials, which the authors say are warranted. The phase 3 trial began dosing in September 2026 and has no results.

What happened

Rentosertib, a TNIK inhibitor designed with Insilico Medicine's generative-AI platform, was tested in a randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled phase 2a trial (NCT05938920). The authors report that the trial enrolled 71 patients with idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis at 21 sites in China from 19 July 2023 to 11 June 2024. The registry lists 22 locations, a start date of 19 June 2023 and study completion on 8 August 2024. The primary endpoint was safety. Treatment-emergent adverse events were similar across arms. Patients on 60 mg once daily showed a mean forced vital capacity change of +98.4 mL versus -20.3 mL on placebo over 12 weeks. In the 60 mg group, 12 of the 18 patients completed the 12-week period. Insilico sponsored the trial. Eleven authors are its employees, and the first author is at Peking Union Medical College Hospital. Results appeared in Nature Medicine on 3 June 2025. The authors call for larger, longer trials. On 11 May 2026 the authors of an independent network meta-analysis reported a non-significant trend toward a higher risk of serious adverse events with rentosertib than with placebo. On 7 July 2026 Insilico announced that it was initiating a phase 3 trial. The registry lists 9 September 2026 as the actual start date of GENESIS-IPF-3, a 52-week phase 3 trial at Chinese hospitals, and plans for 320 participants. On 10 September 2026 Insilico announced that it had dosed the first patient, and it stated that the drug has no regulatory approval.

Details

AI system

Role

The tools identified a drug target and designed a candidate molecule.

Configuration

PandaOmics supported target discovery. Chemistry42 supported molecular design within Insilico Medicine’s Pharma.AI platform.

Workflow

Generative-AI target identification (TNIK) and molecule design by the sponsor. Sponsor-run 12-week, double-blind phase 2a trial at Chinese centers with safety as the primary endpoint.

Human oversight

Researchers selected and tested the AI-designed molecule; trial clinicians administered assigned doses and monitored adverse events and lung function.

Benefits

Reach

The trial enrolled 71 patients with idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis. In the 60 mg group, 12 of the 18 patients completed the 12-week period. The sources report the lung-function gain as a group mean and identify no individual beneficiary. [1] [2] [3] [4] [5] [6] [7] [8]

Duration

Assigned treatment lasted 12 weeks, with adverse-event monitoring for one further week and continued collection of specified later events.

Measurement

Measurement

Spirometry; change in forced vital capacity from baseline to week 12, a secondary efficacy measure. Safety was the primary endpoint. [1]

Setting and sample

71 adults with idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis at 21 sites in China, randomized 1:1:1:1 to three doses or placebo. [1]

Measures

Mean change in forced vital capacity in mL at 12 weeks. [1]

Results

+98.4 mL (95% CI 10.9 to 185.9) on 60 mg once daily versus -20.3 mL (95% CI -116.1 to 75.6) on placebo. [1]

Additional value from AI

Not measured. The trial tests the drug; it does not compare AI-led discovery with any other way of finding a drug. [1]

Limitations

Small groups of about 18 patients; 12 weeks only; 12 of 18 patients in the 60 mg group completed the placebo-controlled period. Seven patients on rentosertib stopped treatment because of liver toxicity, and three patients on 60 mg had an acute exacerbation of IPF against one on placebo. The sponsor ran the trial and eleven authors are its employees. [1]

Denominators

Oral rentosertib for 12 weeks. [1]

Follow-up

Conditions for replication

The FVC signal must be confirmed in a larger, longer, geographically diverse phase 3 trial. Hepatic adverse events and the serious-adverse-event trend must remain acceptable.
