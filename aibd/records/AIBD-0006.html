Insilico's AI-designed drug improves lung function in a small trial

Rentosertib, designed with Insilico Medicine's generative-AI platform, raised mean forced vital capacity by 98.4 mL at 60 mg daily in a phase 2a trial, against a fall of 20.3 mL on placebo.
