Insilico's rentosertib improves FVC in a phase 2a IPF trial

Phase 2a GENESIS-IPF trial in China: 60 mg daily rentosertib +98.4 mL FVC vs -20.3 mL placebo at 12 weeks; cohort-level, sponsor-run

ⓘ

What happened

Rentosertib, a TNIK inhibitor designed with Insilico Medicine's generative-AI platform, was tested in a randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled phase 2a trial (NCT05938920). It enrolled 71 patients with idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis at 21 sites in China from 19 July 2023 to 11 June 2024 (paper). The registry lists 22 locations, a start date of 19 June 2023 and study completion on 8 August 2024. The primary endpoint was safety. Treatment-emergent adverse events were similar across arms. Patients on 60 mg once daily showed a mean forced vital capacity change of +98.4 mL versus -20.3 mL on placebo over 12 weeks. Insilico sponsored the trial. Eleven authors are its employees and the first author is at Peking Union Medical College Hospital. Results appeared in Nature Medicine on 3 June 2025. The authors call for larger, longer trials.

Purpose. Rentosertib was designed as an antifibrotic treatment for idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis. The trial tested the safety and preliminary efficacy of exactly that intended benefit.

AI’s contribution

The AI role is upstream: generative-AI tools identified TNIK as a target and designed the molecule. Any clinical effect is pharmacological. The trial was not powered for efficacy and FVC was not the primary endpoint. An independent network meta-analysis ranks rentosertib favorably on FVC decline but reports a non-significant trend toward more serious adverse events.

Since then
Insilico announced a 320-patient, 47-center, 52-week phase III trial of rentosertib in China on 7 July 2026, moving the drug past the 71-patient phase 2a stage.The prospective, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled, parallel-group Phase III study is expected to enroll a total of 320 participants across 47 centers in China. The primary endpoint is the annual rate of decline in forced vital capacity (FVC) over 52 weeks. source

Further evidence

Setting. Generative-AI target identification (TNIK) and molecule design by the sponsor. Sponsor-run 12-week, double-blind phase 2a trial at Chinese centers with safety as the primary endpoint.

Conditions. The FVC signal must be confirmed in a larger, longer, geographically diverse phase 3 trial. Hepatic adverse events and the serious-adverse-event trend must remain acceptable.
