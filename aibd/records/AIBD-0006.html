Insilico's rentosertib improves FVC in a phase 2a IPF trial

Phase 2a GENESIS-IPF trial in China: 60 mg daily rentosertib +98.4 mL FVC vs -20.3 mL placebo at 12 weeks; cohort-level, sponsor-run

AI system

System

Insilico Medicine generative-AI drug discovery platform (target discovery and generative chemistry) that produced rentosertib (ISM001-055)

Release

Exact release not established.

Application developer

Insilico Medicine

Role

The tools identified a drug target and designed a candidate molecule.

Configuration

PandaOmics supported target discovery. Chemistry42 supported molecular design within Insilico Medicine’s Pharma.AI platform.

Workflow

Generative-AI target identification (TNIK) and molecule design by the sponsor. Sponsor-run 12-week, double-blind phase 2a trial at Chinese centers with safety as the primary endpoint.

Relation to AI

The AI role is upstream: generative-AI tools identified TNIK as a target and designed the molecule. Any clinical effect is pharmacological. The trial was not powered for efficacy and FVC was not the primary endpoint. An independent network meta-analysis ranks rentosertib favorably on FVC decline but reports a non-significant trend toward more serious adverse events.

Follow-up

Conditions for replication

The FVC signal must be confirmed in a larger, longer, geographically diverse phase 3 trial. Hepatic adverse events and the serious-adverse-event trend must remain acceptable.

Later findings

7 July 2026. Insilico announced a 320-patient, 47-center, 52-week phase III trial of rentosertib in China on 7 July 2026, moving the drug past the 71-patient phase 2a stage.
