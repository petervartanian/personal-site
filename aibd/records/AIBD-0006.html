Insilico's rentosertib improves FVC in a phase 2a IPF trial

unrated a personunrated society

mean forced vital capacity change of +98.4 mL versus -20.3 mL on placebo over 12 weeks

Phase 2a GENESIS-IPF trial in China: 60 mg daily rentosertib +98.4 mL FVC vs -20.3 mL placebo at 12 weeks; cohort-level, sponsor-run

What happened

Rentosertib, a TNIK inhibitor designed with Insilico Medicine's generative-AI platform, was tested in a randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled phase 2a trial (NCT05938920) in 71 patients with idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis conducted at 21 sites in China from 19 July 2023 to 11 June 2024 (paper); the registry lists 22 locations, a start date of 19 June 2023 and study completion on 8 August 2024. The primary endpoint was safety; treatment-emergent adverse events were similar across arms. Patients on 60 mg once daily showed a mean forced vital capacity change of +98.4 mL versus -20.3 mL on placebo over 12 weeks. Insilico sponsored the trial; eleven authors are its employees and the first author is at Peking Union Medical College Hospital. Results appeared in Nature Medicine on 3 June 2025; the authors call for larger, longer trials.

What it was built to do. Rentosertib was designed as an antifibrotic treatment for idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis; the trial tested the safety and preliminary efficacy of exactly that intended benefit.

The system's part

The AI role is upstream: generative-AI tools identified TNIK as a target and designed the molecule; any clinical effect is pharmacological. The trial was not powered for efficacy and FVC was not the primary endpoint. An independent network meta-analysis ranks rentosertib favourably on FVC decline but reports a non-significant trend toward more serious adverse events.

Replication

No attempt to repeat it has been recorded.

What produced it

  • Generative-AI target identification (TNIK) and molecule design by the sponsor
  • Sponsor-run 12-week, double-blind phase 2a trial at Chinese centers with safety as the primary endpoint

What must hold again

  • The FVC signal must be confirmed in a larger, longer, geographically diverse phase 3 trial
  • Hepatic adverse events and the serious-adverse-event trend must remain acceptable
