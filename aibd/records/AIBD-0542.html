Barnsley reports less administrative work with Copilot

Barnsley reports that Copilot saves time on meeting notes, summaries, procurement, legal work, and social-care administration.

  • 1 an organization

    The council reports time savings in deployed administrative workflows. No source shows a lasting change in its services.

  • 1 a person

    Staff report less repetitive administrative work and more time for other duties.

What happened

Barnsley reports that its expanded Microsoft Copilot deployment saves staff time in transcription, summaries, administration, procurement, legal work, and finance. Regular use is reported at 70%. Financial returns are still being evaluated. The council had issued Copilot licenses across the organization by the first week of January 2024. On 1 February 2026, the Local Democracy Reporting Service reported that the council would keep funding the licenses at £300,000 a year and that its cabinet report said that £1m in savings from the Microsoft program had still to be delivered.

Details

AI system

Role

Copilot assists council staff with transcription, summaries, and administrative work across services including procurement, legal work, finance, and social care.

Configuration

The council expanded its Copilot pilot from 300 to 2,000 licenses, distributing them from late December 2023 through early January 2024. [1]

Workflow

A council rollout expanded from 300 to 2,000 Copilot licenses, with champions, workshops, and departmental use cases.

Human oversight

The council’s policy requires people to check AI-generated content before use. It also specifies additional data-handling rules for high-risk work. [1]

Benefits

Reach

The council expanded its rollout to 2,000 Copilot licenses and reports that 70% of users engage regularly. These figures count licensed and active users, and no source counts the staff whose time was saved. [1] [2]

Follow-up

Conditions for replication

The council requires that people vet AI-generated content before use. The source does not establish which training or adoption conditions are necessary to reproduce the savings.

Evidence

Original evidence

The council's own case study in the Local Government Association bank describes the expansion from 300 to 2,000 licenses, the staff tasks, mandatory human vetting and planned measurement of financial returns. BBC News carried the Local Democracy Reporting Service report of 1 February 2026 on the council's cabinet report, in which the productivity benefit is the council's own statement. [1] [2]
