What happened

New Civil Engineer's Tom Pashby reported on 6 Jul 2026, in a story about the system flagging a snake as a track defect on 3 July, that LNER runs the Pantograph Damage Assessment System (PANDAS) and One Big Circle's Automated Intelligent Video Review (AIVR) on a number of its trains to assess overhead line equipment and track and report potential damage to Network Rail. The article reproduces LNER's 9 April 2026 announcement: in January 2026 a track defect reported by a train driver in Cambridgeshire caused over 10,000 delay minutes, multiple cancellations and a full day of disruption, whereas a week later the AIVR system identified a minor fault near Retford with the potential to become a larger issue, allowing engineers to carry out an overnight repair with no delay to passengers and zero delay minutes.

An LNER spokesperson said the information is fed back to Network Rail to target areas for attention, and a Network Rail spokesperson said the snake would be raised automatically as standard practice. Network Rail's own 2022 story describes AIVR as a phone-sized front-of-train device applying machine learning to video of more than 20,000 miles of track. The exact day of the Retford detection is not given; the date used is LNER's announcement date as cited by NCE. LNER's announcement itself and later Southeastern and South Western Railway AIVR releases were not read.

What it was built to do. AIVR is built to capture and analyze front-of-train video with machine learning so that track and lineside defects are found and fixed before they cause disruption.

The system's part

AIVR's analysis raised the fault report that Network Rail engineers acted on; no human had reported it. Whether the minor fault would have escalated is LNER's claim, and detection rates or false-positive rates are not published.

Replication

What produced it. Front-of-train cameras on scheduled passenger services covering the same track daily. Machine-learning review that raises candidate defects to the infrastructure manager. A maintenance organization able to schedule overnight possessions.

What must hold again. Engineers triage AI reports promptly. False positives (such as the snake) do not swamp the reporting channel. The fault is visible from a train-mounted camera.
