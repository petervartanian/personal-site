Nuru may have lifted cassava yield from 0.5 kg to 2–5 kg a plant

PlantVillage's own 23 Mar 2020 post: after 18 months using Nuru, introduced via Self Help Africa and Ugunja Resource Center, Arisat reports 2.0–5.0 kg per plant against 0.5 kg; no independent source.

Workflow

A smartphone with Nuru supplied through a lead-farmer scheme (Self Help Africa, Ugunja Resource Center) with training. Use of the diagnosis to uproot diseased plants and select clean cuttings for replanting.

Human oversight

Not assessed

Relation to AI

Only the maker's account exists for this case. PlantVillage's post attributes the yield change, in the farmer's words, to Nuru's leaf-scanning diagnosis guiding removal of diseased plants and selection of clean cuttings. The independent evidence concerns the model's accuracy (Mrisho et al. 2020, developer co-authored) and another farmer's self-report in Business Insider, not Arisat. So the AI's share of her outcome cannot be assessed.

Conditions for replication

Symptoms are visible on leaves. Mrisho et al. report accuracy rising when six leaves per plant are scanned. The app's effect is separable from certified seed, spacing and market changes, which the post does not attempt.

Replication attempts

1 follow-up entries. Benefit recurred: 1.

Later findings

10 August 2022. By August 2022 Business Insider reported Nuru extended to eight more countries, with PlantVillage's David Hughes claiming cassava yield increases of 130% to 530%, and CGIAR's project page records 240 phones distributed to Bungoma, Kilifi and Tanzania in April 2021.
