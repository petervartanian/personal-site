Workflow

A camera-analytics system (AI門神) already watching the office over the temple's existing CCTV. It was set to alert on small amounts of smoke and to message a named chain of people (front desk, general manager, chairman) over LINE. A staffed night watch. The temple has kept someone on duty every night since it moved to the site in 1980 (民國69年). So the alert had a clerk a minute away with an extinguisher. Smoke appeared before the lithium batteries burst, allowing an alert before the fire intensified. The precise warning interval remains unresolved. A fire brigade eight minutes away and a reinforced-concrete annex that held the fire to one room, as the Third Brigade commander said.

Relation to AI

The system's documented contribution is the first alert. TVBS reports that the system detected smoke at 05:30. UDN reports that the lithium batteries burst at about 05:33, while repeating the chairman's claim of a one-minute lead. The precise warning interval remains unresolved. It messaged the front desk, the general manager and the chairman in sequence over LINE. That is how a chairman asleep in Nara came to follow the fire on the temple's cameras. The suppression was human. The clerk's extinguisher failed once the batteries burst. The Yunlin County Fire Bureau's eight trucks and eighteen firefighters, called at 05:36, controlled the fire at 06:10. UDN reports that a residential smoke alarm (住警器) sounded as well. The Third Brigade commander 陳岳謄 attributed the containment to the building's reinforced-concrete structure and the crews' quick arrival without mentioning the AI. The LINE relay from the vendor is the chairman's account, not the bureau's.
