LEADERG's AI door god flags smoke; temple fire held to 10 m²

AI camera analytics flagged smoke in an empty temple office a minute before a battery fire; held to 10 m², no injuries, main hall intact.

ⓘ

AI system

System

AI門神：宮廟AI防火防盜系統 (AI Door God: temple AI fire-and-theft prevention system), AI image recognition over the temple's existing CCTV with alerts by SMS, LINE and email

Model

Not assessed

Release

Exact release not established.

Application developer

立達軟體科技股份有限公司 (LEADERG Inc.), Taiwan

Role

The software detected smoke in camera images and alerted temple staff.

Configuration

The image-recognition software ran on an Advantech IPC-730 computer with a SKY-QUAD-5000A-32 accelerator card. Advantech reports processing about one frame every five seconds.

Inputs and outputs

The software analyzed CCTV images and sent smoke alerts to temple staff through LINE.

Autonomy

Not assessed

Workflow

AI門神 detected smoke through cameras that already covered the temple and alerted staff through LINE. The night clerk reached the room with an extinguisher. Firefighters arrived eight minutes later.

Human oversight

A clerk attempted to extinguish the fire. Firefighters controlled it after the temple called the fire service.

Relation to AI

protective response

AI contribution

According to the temple’s log, AI Door God detected smoke at 05:30 and sent alerts through LINE. The chairman followed the developing fire through the temple’s cameras.

Results

The temple reports that the software detected smoke about one minute before the batteries burst. A conventional smoke alarm also sounded. The AI alert’s separate effect on the damage is unmeasured.

Limitations

The temple’s account supplies the alert chronology. A residential smoke alarm also sounded. The fire service attributed containment to the building’s concrete structure and the firefighters’ arrival, without identifying a separate contribution from AI.

Conditions for replication

Cameras need to cover rooms where a fire could start. Alerts need to reach staff who can respond within minutes. Conventional smoke alarms remain necessary.

Reasons

The fire damaged one office, while the main hall and worship areas remained usable. The temple stayed open. The fire bureau credited the concrete structure and firefighters’ arrival for containment. The AI alert provided earlier warning, but the evidence does not isolate its contribution to the damage avoided.

Field

Fire safety (religious site). Temple and cultural-asset protection: AI video analytics on existing CCTV.

Purpose

AI門神 (“AI door god: temple AI fire-and-theft prevention system”) was built to run AI image recognition over a temple's existing CCTV. It was built to detect excess smoke, flame and suspicious behavior, and to alert staff by SMS, LINE or email. CTS reported in December 2024 that Wude Temple's door-god system would sound an alarm at once on abnormal smoke. Flagging smoke before a fire was exactly its designed purpose.
