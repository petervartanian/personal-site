CapisciAMe recognizes words from severely impaired speech

CapisciAMe, a speech recognizer tailored to a woman with aphasia and dysarthria, identified 72.65% of 936 recorded test words; twelve professional listeners averaged 56.75% on a separate task.

Costs and harms

The tailored recognizer probably captured stable features of this woman's speech that familiar listeners missed, but its closed vocabulary made the task much easier than unrestricted conversation.

Editor’s note. The model outperformed listeners on the same 13 targets after extensive speaker-specific training. That supports a personalized recognition advantage within the tested task, not a broad conversational ability.
