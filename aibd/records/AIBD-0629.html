What happened

Cognoa of Palo Alto, California, developed the Cognoa ASD Diagnosis Aid, machine learning software that combines a caregiver questionnaire, analyst ratings of home videos and a clinician questionnaire. In a prospective, double-blinded study at 14 US sites, 425 children aged 18 to 72 months with a developmental concern completed both the device assessment and a specialist diagnosis between August 2019 and June 2020. FDA reviewers report that the device gave a positive or negative result for 135 children. For those children it had a positive predictive value of 81%, a negative predictive value of 98%, a sensitivity of 98% and a specificity of 79%. The device gave no result for 68% of children. FDA granted the De Novo request on 2 June 2021. The reviewers note that the average age at diagnosis was 2.8 years in the trial, against 4.3 years nationally. Clinicians in the study were blinded to the device output, so it did not change care.

Details

AI system

Workflow

Caregivers answered a questionnaire and recorded home videos through a smartphone app. Trained analysts rated the videos and a clinician completed a questionnaire.

Measurement

Measurement

Prospective double-blinded single-arm diagnostic accuracy study against a DSM-5 specialist reference standard. [2]

Setting and sample

711 enrolled, 425 completers at 14 US sites. [2]

Measures

PPV, NPV, sensitivity, specificity, no-result rate. [2]

Results

PPV 81%, NPV 98%, sensitivity 98%, specificity 79%; no result for 68%. [2]

Limitations

No result for 68% of children; high dropout; outputs blinded so care was unchanged. [2]

Denominators

One assessment per child. [2]

Materials for replication

ClinicalTrials.gov NCT04151290. [2]

Follow-up

Conditions for replication

The child is 18 to 72 months old with a developmental concern. The clinician uses the result with other clinical evidence.

Grades

Observed or intended

Intended. Clinicians in the study were blinded to the device output, so the measured accuracy changed no diagnosis and the sources report no realized benefit. [2]
