AI Helpy gives tailored feedback in Korean AI digital textbooks

Elice’s AI Helpy gives tailored feedback in Korean AI digital textbooks, which lost their textbook status in August 2025; no source measures a learning gain.

  • 1 a person

    The makers report that the deployed textbook system gives students immediate personalized feedback in five supported languages. No source measures a learning gain.

  • Not rated an organization

    The makers state that AI Helpy reduces teacher workload. No source measures that saving. Teachers interviewed by Lee and Kim reported added workload from AI digital textbooks.

What happened

Elice announced on 6 February 2025 that its information textbooks with AI Helpy had passed state screening. AI digital textbooks entered Korean schools in March 2025. Elice describes an AI textbook platform with tailored feedback, language support and teacher oversight, including a pilot. Elice reports no measured learning gain and no measured saving of teacher time. In interviews with seven secondary teachers in the first semester of 2025, Lee and Kim found that use of AI digital textbooks was low and that teachers reported added workload. Independent studies report that adoption in the first semester of 2025 stood in the 30 percent range. In August 2025 the National Assembly passed a law that made AI digital textbooks educational materials and removed their textbook status.

Details

AI system

Autonomy

The platform constrains responses with curriculum boundaries and filters for toxic or hallucinatory content.

Workflow

Conditions that could produce the benefit are unspecified.

Human oversight

Elice describes teacher oversight of its platform, and the benefit depends on teachers who supervise student interactions. Teachers interviewed by Lee and Kim reported difficulty in controlling students in classes with AI digital textbooks. That study does not name Elice. [1] [2] [3] [4]

Benefits

Duration

AI digital textbooks entered Korean schools in March 2025. In August 2025 the National Assembly passed a law that made them educational materials and removed their textbook status. Independent studies report that adoption in the first semester of 2025 stood in the 30 percent range. No source reports whether schools kept using AI Helpy after that change. [1] [2] [3] [4]

Follow-up

Conditions for replication

Curriculum-aligned content and supported languages. Teacher supervision of student interactions.

Evidence

Original evidence

The ITU project account and the newsroom release of 6 February 2025 come from Elice. Two peer-reviewed studies of 30 November 2025 come from independent researchers. Lee and Kim study AI digital textbooks in general and do not name Elice. Oh studies textbooks from other publishers. [1] [2] [3] [4]
