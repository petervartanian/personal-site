What happened

ScamShield is a Singapore government app developed by Open Government Products, a unit of GovTech, with the National Crime Prevention Council and the Singapore Police Force; GovInsider (June 2021) described an on-device machine-learning model that files suspected scam SMS into a junk folder and blocks calls from numbers on a police blocklist, reported 722,865 SMS messages reported and 5,537 numbers blocked since launch with a 95% detection accuracy claim, and quoted product manager Lennard Lim on the need for the model to adapt as scammers change their attacks.

In an oral reply to parliamentary questions on 22 November 2023, Minister of State Sun Xueling said that ScamShield had about 750,000 downloads as of October 2023, that about 80,000 unique scam-tainted phone numbers had been blacklisted and blocked, that about 5 million SMSes had been flagged to users as potential scams, and that it has been effective in protecting users; she also cited about 180 million suspected scam calls blocked at network level in the first half of 2023, which is a telco measure separate from the app. No victim-level evaluation appears in the fetched sources; GovInsider's date comes from the RSS feed.

What it was built to do. Filter scam SMS on-device and block calls from police-blacklisted numbers to protect users from scams.

The system's part

GovInsider attributes SMS filtering to an on-device machine-learning model and call blocking to a police-maintained blocklist, so only the SMS figures involve the model; the parliamentary reply gives counts without attributing them to AI. Developer (Open Government Products, a public body) and deployer (the Police and NCPC) are both government bodies, and the reply was given by their ministry.

Replication

What produced it. On-device SMS classification combined with a police-maintained blocklist of scam numbers. User reports feeding the blocklist (722,865 SMS reported by mid-2021).

What must hold again. Adoption at scale (about 750,000 downloads by October 2023). Police intelligence to keep the blocklist current. Model updates as scam wording changes.
