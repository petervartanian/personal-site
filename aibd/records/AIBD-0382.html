ASENA flags a car at a Şırnak checkpoint; 86.6 kg of skunk found

Şırnak police stopped a car selected by the ASENA narcotics analysis software; the dog Hector found 86.6 kg of skunk in 119 packages in the trunk, and three lawyers were remanded (March 2025).

What happened

In May 2022 Interior Minister Süleyman Soylu said that ASENA starts following people who go to a province, take a route or stay at a hotel for the first time. He claimed that it had helped solve 3,593 cases. In March 2025 Şırnak Provincial Police stopped a vehicle at the Köy Hizmetleri (Village Services) police checkpoint after ASENA selected it for inspection. DHA calls ASENA police software “in artificial intelligence format”. The narcotics dog Hector then found 86 kilograms 600 grams of skunk in 119 packages hidden in the trunk. Three occupants, M.E., H.K. and Ş.A., were detained (Sözcü/DHA and Habertürk/DHA, 17 March 2025). Diken, citing İHA, gives ASENA's full name as Analiz Sistemleri Narkotik Ağı (Narcotics Analysis Systems Network). It says that the three were lawyers registered with the Mardin Bar Association. They were taken to court after police processing and remanded in custody (Diken, 18 March 2025). None of the reports gives the day of the stop, what data ASENA analyzed or how it chose the car, or any official's name. No later report gives the outcome of the case against the three lawyers after the remand that Diken reported on 18 March 2025.

Details

AI system

Role

ASENA selected the car for inspection. Hector, the detection dog, found the hidden packages during the physical search.

Autonomy

Police carried out the stop and search and brought the occupants to court. No automatic arrest decision is documented.

Workflow

A police analysis program that ranks vehicles for interdiction. A fixed checkpoint at which a flagged car can be stopped and searched. A narcotics dog and officers prepared to search the trunk.

Human oversight

ASENA selected the car, and officers with the narcotics dog Hector searched it and made the arrests. No source says whether an analyst reviewed the flag before the stop. A court then remanded the three suspects. [1] [2] [3] [4] [5]

Follow-up

Conditions for replication

The flag must be followed by a lawful stop and a physical search. The software only selects. Seized material and arrests must be carried through prosecution.
