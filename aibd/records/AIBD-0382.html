ASENA flags a car at a Şırnak checkpoint; 86.6 kg of skunk found

Şırnak police stopped a car selected by the ASENA narcotics analysis software; the dog Hector found 86.6 kg of skunk in 119 packages in the trunk, and three lawyers were remanded (March 2025).

ⓘ

What happened

Şırnak Provincial Police stopped a vehicle at the Köy Hizmetleri (Village Services) police checkpoint after ASENA selected it for inspection. DHA calls ASENA police software “in artificial intelligence format”. The narcotics dog Hector then found 86 kilograms 600 grams of skunk in 119 packages hidden in the trunk. Three occupants, M.E., H.K. and Ş.A., were detained (Sözcü/DHA and Habertürk/DHA, 17 March 2025). Diken, citing İHA, gives ASENA's full name as Analiz Sistemleri Narkotik Ağı (Narcotics Analysis Systems Network). It says the three were lawyers registered with the Mardin Bar Association. They were taken to court after police processing and remanded in custody (Diken, 18 March 2025). None of the reports gives the day of the stop, what data ASENA analyzed or how it chose the car, or any official's name.

Purpose. Police analysis software built to pick out vehicles and people likely to be carrying narcotics for checkpoint stops. This seizure was that use.

AI’s contribution

ASENA supplied the selection and nothing more. DHA's wording is that the vehicle was identified and stopped “thanks to the ASENA software”. The dog Hector then found the packages and officers made the arrests. No report describes ASENA's inputs, its ranking method, or whether an analyst reviewed the flag before the stop.

Beneficiaries

  • society

    86.6 kg of skunk was taken out of circulation and three suspects remanded. A measurable interdiction result, but the reports specify no downstream harm averted.

  • an organization

    The Şırnak police turned a software flag into a large seizure and three remands, per the DHA and İHA reports. A measurable result for the unit.

Further evidence

Setting. A police analysis program that ranks vehicles for interdiction. A fixed checkpoint at which a flagged car can be stopped and searched. A narcotics dog and officers prepared to search the trunk.

Conditions. The flag must be followed by a lawful stop and a physical search. The software only selects. Seized material and arrests must be carried through prosecution.
