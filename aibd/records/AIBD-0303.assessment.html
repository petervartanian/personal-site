Summary
Mr. Joseph Holycross reported that doctors confirmed the atrial fibrillation that Samsung Health Monitor detected on his Galaxy Watch4 Classic.
Account
CyberGuy published Kurt Knutsson's interview with Joseph and Jamie Holycross on 1 October 2026. Joseph, 39, is a father of four. The event date and place are not given. He had attributed a racing heart to caffeine for one and a half to two years. His wife Jamie, a respiratory therapist, noticed that he was short of breath. She urged him to run the electrocardiogram (ECG) feature on his Samsung Galaxy Watch4 Classic. He says that the result read “atrial fibrillation” in bold. He repeated the recording several times, then went to the emergency room (ER), where a doctor reviewed the watch tracing and confirmed AFib. He stayed about a week in hospital. About ten days later he returned for open-heart surgery to repair “several things on my heart”. Asked whether he would have gone to the ER without the watch, he said “Nope”. The family says that doctors warned that he “probably would have had a stroke, heart attack, or both” and recommended screening for his siblings and children.
