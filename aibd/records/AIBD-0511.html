AI-guided robots park cars and could raise the capacity of garages

Shanghai AI Research Institute says that AI-assisted robotic parking runs in operating garages and estimates that single-layer layouts can raise capacity by 60–80%.

  • Not rated an organization

    The institute states that capacity 'can reach' 60–80% more with a single-layer layout and about 50% more for a lot of about 100 spaces. Both are conditional estimates, and no garage reports a realized gain.

  • 1 a person

    The institute describes easier parking and vehicle retrieval in garages that already use the system. The claim is qualitative, and no source measures a change in waiting time.

What happened

Shanghai AI Research Institute describes AI-assisted robotic parking in operating garages, with further projects under construction. Perception, planning and scheduling coordinate vehicle storage and retrieval. The institute estimates that single-layer layouts can increase capacity by 60–80%. It gives a second estimate in the same account: An ordinary lot of about 100 spaces can be converted within one month and can then gain about 50% more spaces, with an annual return on investment that can reach 30%. The institute names no garage where either figure was measured. It describes easier parking and retrieval in qualitative terms only. No source measures a change in waiting time.

Details

AI system

Autonomy

Robots store and retrieve vehicles automatically. Entrance scans reject oversized vehicles, radar can stop or redirect a robot around obstacles, and the operating zone is separated from people.

Workflow

Conditions that could produce the benefit are unspecified.

Follow-up

Conditions for replication

Garage space compatible with robotic storage and retrieval. Vehicle-handling hardware and concurrent scheduling.
