Costs and harms

Denser vehicle storage may increase capacity while making retrieval depend more heavily on robot availability and the sequence of other requests.

Editor’s note. The claimed capacity gains come from robotic layouts rather than extra conventional bays. Such layouts rely on coordinated retrieval, and no site-level waiting-time or failure comparison is reported.

Citable summary

Shanghai AI Research Institute describes robotic parking deployments and says that single-layer layouts can increase capacity by 60–80%. The percentage is potential, not a measured outcome. The 60–80%, over-110%, and roughly-50% capacity figures describe different configurations or general renovation scenarios and should not be combined. The 30% annual return is also an unsupported estimate here. No site-level waiting-time, cost, safety, or occupancy evaluation establishes the net benefit.
