LFDCS glider detections trigger a NOAA Right Whale Slow Zone

WHOI/NOAA glider's automated call classifier detected right whales on 27 December 2023; NOAA declared a Slow Zone east of Portland, ME.

AI system

System

DMON instrument running the Low-Frequency Detection and Classification System (LFDCS) on a Slocum glider

Application developer

Woods Hole Oceanographic Institution (Mark Baumgartner)

Workflow

Glider or buoy carrying DMON/LFDCS in right whale habitat. Shore-based analyst validation protocol. NOAA Slow Zone program (established late 2020) accepting acoustic triggers.

Relation to AI

Automated classification produced the candidate detections. Analysts validated them on shore before NOAA acted. The resulting Slow Zone is voluntary.

AI contribution

Automated classification produced the candidate detections. Analysts validated them on shore before NOAA acted. The resulting Slow Zone is voluntary.
