What happened

The Ministry of Justice (MoJ) Justice AI Unit and HM Prison and Probation Service (HMPPS) adapted Minute, a meeting transcription and summary tool from the government's Incubator for AI, into Justice Transcribe for probation supervision meetings. A pilot with 40 probation officers in Kent, Surrey, Sussex and Wales ran from May to July 2025. Officers who completed the final survey reported that the tool cut the time to write up meeting notes by 73% on average, to about 5 minutes of review and editing, and 29 of 30 said that note quality had improved. Use widened from October 2025, and by the end of March 2026 the tool reached about 11,000 sentence management staff. The National Audit Office (NAO) published a case study on 14 October 2026. It reports that over 1,600,000 meetings were summarized between 7 October 2025 and 14 September 2026, an estimated 266,667 hours saved according to HMPPS. The NAO notes that HMPPS has no data on the time actually saved. These estimates rest on modeling assumptions, and HMPPS plans further evaluation. HMPPS quality checks found inaccuracies, mostly misspelled names and agencies, in 20% of the final case records that they examined.

Details

AI system

Workflow

The team tailored summary templates and prompts to probation meetings. Officers reviewed and edited each AI summary before it entered the case record. Regional implementation teams, video tutorials and on-site support backed the national rollout.

Measurement

Measurement

End-of-pilot survey of officers; national estimates from usage counts, with no data on actual time saved. [1]

Setting and sample

Pilot of 40 officers (30 survey responses), May to July 2025; over 1,600,000 meetings from 7 October 2025 to 14 September 2026. [1]

Measures

Time to write up meeting notes; staff hours saved. [1]

Results

73% average reduction reported by pilot staff; about 266,667 hours saved (HMPPS estimate). [1]

Limitations

Self-reported pilot data; national figures rest on modeling assumptions; inaccuracies found in 20% of final case records examined. [1]

Denominators

Officers used the tool in supervision meetings. [1]

Materials for replication

None public. [1]

Follow-up

Conditions for replication

Officers check summaries for errors such as misspelled names. The service keeps monitoring adoption, accuracy and model performance.
