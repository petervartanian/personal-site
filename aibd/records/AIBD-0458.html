A TikTok creator gains 300,000 followers in two months using PixVerse

PixVerse reports that a TikTok creator in Brazil used its AI video generator to gain 300,000 followers and over 210 million views in two months.

What happened

In the ITU entry, current to June 2025, PixVerse reports that TikTok creator @nerdsoul in Brazil used PixVerse to reimagine iconic characters through aging transformations. It reports that the videos generated over 210 million views and that the creator gained 300,000 followers in two months. The entry also describes an unnamed Portuguese creator whose 30-minute film was selected for Fantasporto. It also reports 1.4 million hashtagged videos. The sources do not establish earnings or preserved livelihoods for these creators. They do not show any reduction in inequality across a population. In June 2026 Motivai Private Limited, which operates PixVerse, filed an upgrade entry in the ITU interim report. The entry lists the platform as in operation and adds accounts of creators in Spain, France and China. It reports that the work of one of them has accumulated more than 20 million views and that she has since built a career as an independent film and television creator. It does not update the NerdSoul or Fantasporto cases. No outside source confirms the NerdSoul figures or the Fantasporto selection.

AI system

Role

PixVerse provides generative video tools that creators use to make character videos and films.

Inputs and outputs

PixVerse accepts inputs such as a photograph or a line of text and generates video. The source describes custom diffusion and video-transformer components.
