Short account

Recorded; review pending

State what happened, for whom, and the main qualification.

Summary
AI analysis of 2,600 drone frames flagged a red helmet in the Perotti couloir at 3,150 m; Ivaldo's body was recovered by 31 July 2025.
Account
Nicola Ivaldo was a surgeon aged 64 according to the Club Alpino Italiano's Lo Scarpone (another report says 66). He disappeared on 14 September 2024 per CAI during a solo outing on Monviso in Piedmont, Italy. Quadricottero dates his departure, when he parked his car at the Pontechianale dam, to 15 September. Searches over ten months found nothing. On 29 July 2025 the Soccorso Alpino e Speleologico Piemontese (CNSAS) flew two drones close to the north face. They collected about 2,600 high-resolution frames over 183 hectares in five hours. The frames were analyzed pixel by pixel with AI image-analysis software. Developer TEXSAR identifies it as the open-source Automated Drone Image Analysis Tool (ADIAT). The frames yielded a tiny red anomaly in the Perotti couloir: his helmet. His body was located at about 3,150 m and recovered by 31 July 2025. That date comes from TEXSAR's page (which cites Wired). It is consistent with bluewin's report of under 72 hours from the 29 July flight to the recovery. CAI gives only July 2025 and Quadricottero no date.
