What happened

In February 2022, Sophia Chan became unresponsive after an aneurysm ruptured. At Mayo Clinic in Jacksonville, AI helped clinicians locate it within minutes. She underwent surgery, although her husband was initially told that she was unlikely to survive. After weeks of stabilization, she recovered. Mayo reports that she had resumed an active family life two years later.

What it was built to do. Accelerate stroke diagnosis and treatment.
