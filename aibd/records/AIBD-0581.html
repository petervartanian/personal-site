Observed February 2022

AI helps locate Sophia Chan's aneurysm before emergency treatment

Sophia Chan survived a ruptured aneurysm after AI-assisted diagnosis and emergency treatment at Mayo Clinic.

ⓘ

What happened

In February 2022, Sophia Chan became unresponsive after an aneurysm ruptured. At Mayo Clinic in Jacksonville, AI helped clinicians locate it within minutes. She underwent surgery, although her husband was initially told that she was unlikely to survive. After weeks of stabilization, she recovered. Mayo reports that she had resumed an active family life two years later.

Purpose. Accelerate stroke diagnosis and treatment.

AI’s contribution

AI helped locate the aneurysm. Clinicians provided treatment. The software and its separate effect on survival are unspecified.

Beneficiaries

  • a person

    Chan survived a critical hemorrhage and regained an active life. Mayo credits AI with aiding diagnosis.

Further evidence

Setting. AI-assisted imaging, surgery, and intensive care.

Conditions. Timely imaging, clinical review, and access to emergency treatment.
