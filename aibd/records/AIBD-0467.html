What happened

Mbeya University of Science and Technology (MUST) in Tanzania built an AI-driven wireless audio conference system that uses smartphones as microphones. The system manages speaking queues automatically and transcribes meetings. In an International Telecommunication Union (ITU) report that is current as of June 2025, the makers report that they ran the system in academic and public service settings. They also report that they connected it to public-address systems at local town hall meetings. They give no measured results from those pilots. They expect lower equipment costs and smoother participation. No source after June 2025 reports further use or measured results for the system.
