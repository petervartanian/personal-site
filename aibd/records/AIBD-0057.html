People with low vision complete more daily tasks with OrCam readers

In independent studies of 6 to 100 participants, people scored higher on reading and daily tasks with OrCam readers; in a Dutch study only three of 20 patients kept using the device.

Later findings

Reasons

People: Study participants showed measured gains in reading and daily-living task scores. In the 2015 study, 12 patients went from 2.5 to 9.5 of 10 tasks with the device. These are test results, and no lasting change in a person's life is reported. In the Dutch study only three of 20 patients kept using the device. Society: The authors tested usability in cohorts at several sites and report no societal outcome that can be attributed to the devices.
