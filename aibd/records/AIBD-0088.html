NHC blends DeepMind's ensemble into Hurricane Melissa forecasts

NHC blended Google DeepMind's AI ensemble into official Melissa forecasts from its first advisory on 21 Oct 2025; Cat 5 landfall in Jamaica 28 Oct 2025.

What happened

From its first advisory on 21 October 2025, the US National Hurricane Center (NHC) blended Google DeepMind's experimental AI hurricane model (ensemble mean GDMI) into its official forecasts for Tropical Storm Melissa: Discussion 1 placed the official track between the DeepMind ensemble mean and the consensus aid HCCA, and Discussion 2 described the track as a blend of the DeepMind mean, HCCA and TVCA. Discussion 8 (23 October) moved the track toward a blend of the DeepMind mean and HCCA; Discussion 10 blended the HCCA and GDMI aids and set an intensity forecast in best agreement with GDMI after all 50 DeepMind members showed a major hurricane. On 28 October 2025 Melissa made landfall in southwestern Jamaica near New Hope with 185 mph winds and 892 mb pressure. IFRC reported 45 deaths and about 1.6 million people affected, and relayed a World Bank/IDB estimate of US$8.8 billion in physical damage; its CHF 80,000 Imminent DREF was approved on 23 October with a five-day lead time.

What it was built to do. Experimental AI tropical cyclone forecast model providing ensemble track and intensity guidance to NHC forecasters.

The system's part

NHC forecasters state in Discussions 8, 10, 12, 13 and 14 that official track and intensity forecasts were blended with or aligned to the Google DeepMind ensemble; the official forecast remains a human synthesis of several aids, so the AI contribution to outcomes in Jamaica is not isolated.

Replication

What produced it
  • Experimental Google DeepMind ensemble guidance available in real time to NHC forecasters
  • Forecasters choosing to blend GDMI with consensus aids (HCCA) in official track and intensity forecasts
  • A slow-moving, rapidly intensifying hurricane where models disagreed on track relative to Jamaica
What must hold again
  • AI ensemble output delivered on time and verified before operational use, as NHC describes
  • Human forecasters retain the final official forecast and integrate conventional NWP guidance
  • Outcome verification depends on later best-track analysis; the Tropical Cyclone Report was not yet published when checked
