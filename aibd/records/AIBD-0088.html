NHC blends DeepMind's ensemble into Hurricane Melissa forecasts

NHC blended Google DeepMind's AI ensemble into official Melissa forecasts from its first advisory on 21 October 2025; Cat 5 landfall in Jamaica 28 October 2025.

ⓘ

What happened

The US National Hurricane Center (NHC) blended Google DeepMind's experimental AI hurricane model (ensemble mean GDMI) into official forecasts for Tropical Storm Melissa. It did so from its first advisory, 21 October 2025. Discussion 1 placed the official track between the DeepMind ensemble mean and the consensus aid HCCA. Discussion 2 described the track as a blend of the DeepMind mean, HCCA and TVCA. Discussion 8 (23 October) moved the track toward a blend of the DeepMind mean and HCCA. Discussion 10 blended the HCCA and GDMI aids. It set an intensity forecast in best agreement with GDMI after all 50 DeepMind members showed a major hurricane. On 28 October 2025 Melissa made landfall in southwestern Jamaica near New Hope with 185 mph winds and 892 mb pressure. IFRC reported 45 deaths and about 1.6 million people affected.

It relayed a World Bank/IDB estimate of US$8.8 billion in physical damage. Its CHF 80,000 Imminent DREF was approved on 23 October with a five-day lead time.

Purpose. Experimental AI tropical cyclone forecast model providing ensemble track and intensity guidance to NHC forecasters.

AI’s contribution

NHC forecasters state in Discussions 8, 10, 12, 13 and 14 that official track and intensity forecasts were blended or aligned with Google DeepMind's ensemble. The official forecast remains a human synthesis of several aids. So the AI contribution to outcomes in Jamaica is not isolated.

Beneficiaries

    a person

    No cited source attributes an individual-level outcome to the AI guidance. Impacts are reported only at population level.

  • an organization

    NHC's official track and intensity forecasts were materially shaped by GDMI (Discussions 8, 10, 13, 14). NHC's Science Operations Officer called the AI guidance very valuable for Melissa.

  • society

    Official forecasts for a Category 5 landfall affecting about 1.6 million people. A CHF 80,000 Imminent DREF was approved on 23 October with a five-day lead time. The review says its early actions were informed by forecast risks (NHC is not named). Material to many, but no counterfactual that the AI changed outcomes.

Further evidence

Setting. Experimental Google DeepMind ensemble guidance available in real time to NHC forecasters. Forecasters choosing to blend GDMI with consensus aids (HCCA) in official track and intensity forecasts. A slow-moving, rapidly intensifying hurricane where models disagreed on track relative to Jamaica.

Conditions. AI ensemble output delivered on time and verified before operational use, as NHC describes. Human forecasters retain the final official forecast and integrate conventional NWP guidance. Outcome verification depends on later best-track analysis. The Tropical Cyclone Report was not yet published and is not among the sources.
