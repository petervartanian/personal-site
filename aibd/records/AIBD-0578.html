In a Taiwan trial, fewer patients died when AI flagged high-risk ECGs

In a Taiwan trial of 15,965 patients, a neural network flagged high-risk electrocardiograms for physicians, and 90-day mortality was 3.6% versus 4.3% with usual care.

  • Outline. Claimed by the parties. Only the people or organizations involved, or sources whose independence has not been assessed, support the benefit.

The fill changes with the evidence. The depth color stays the same.
