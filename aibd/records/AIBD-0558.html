AI system

System

Hey Geraldine bespoke AI social-care knowledge assistant

Application developer

Peterborough City Council, Outcomes Matter Consulting, and Datnexa

Workflow

The council and partners developed the assistant in six weeks and gathered staff feedback. The source describes genuine staff questions alongside testing and demonstration interactions.

Relation to AI

The assistant supplies guidance and information associated with an experienced colleague’s expertise. The source gives a specific successful information-retrieval account and an aggregate time-saving claim, without establishing downstream care improvements.
