Source published 18 December 2024

Peterborough reports quicker advice through Hey Geraldine

Peterborough reports 15 minutes saved per conversation with Hey Geraldine. A manager describes obtaining equipment information while a colleague was away.

ⓘ

What happened

Peterborough deployed Hey Geraldine, a social-care knowledge assistant modeled on an experienced occupational-therapy colleague. The council reports 15 minutes saved per conversation. A deputy therapy manager describes obtaining needed equipment information while the real Geraldine was on leave. The reported 1,200 questions include testing and demonstrations.

Purpose. Provide social-care staff with accessible guidance and reduce routine demands on specialist colleagues.

AI’s contribution

The assistant supplies guidance and information associated with an experienced colleague’s expertise. The source gives a specific successful information-retrieval account and an aggregate time-saving claim, without establishing downstream care improvements.

Beneficiaries

  • an organization

    The council reports less staff time answering routine questions.

  • a person

    A specific manager obtained needed information during a colleague’s leave. This is a convenient information-access benefit, without a documented care outcome.

Further evidence

Setting. The council and partners developed the assistant in six weeks and gathered staff feedback. The source describes genuine staff questions alongside testing and demonstration interactions.

Conditions. The conditions needed to reproduce the benefit are unspecified.
