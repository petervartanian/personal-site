A chatbot answers questions about UAE Ministry of Finance services

The UAE Ministry of Finance reports that MOF IntelliServe, a chatbot built on Azure OpenAI, gives instant bilingual answers and document search and reduces routine manual effort.

  • 1 a person

    The live service is described as providing immediate bilingual answers and document retrieval, a convenience and search-time benefit. Learning or financial outcomes are not established.

  • 1 an organization

    The Ministry reports automation of routine inquiries and document handling, reducing manual effort. Monetary savings remain expected and unquantified.

Depth runs from 1, a convenience or time saved, to 5, a life or a livelihood preserved.

What happened

In October 2023 the UAE Ministry of Finance showed a prototype generative AI chatbot at GITEX Technology Week, according to its news release of 23 October 2023. The release does not name IntelliServe. In its submission to the ITU AI for Good report of 2025, the Ministry reports that MOF IntelliServe is fully deployed and operational. It reports that the assistant provides instant bilingual answers and document search, reducing routine manual effort. It names Celebal Technologies as the builder and Microsoft as the provider of the Azure OpenAI infrastructure. The Ministry gives no figures for calls or time saved. Cost savings remain expected.

  3. Press release, by the organization that used it.

    UAE Ministry of Finance, Ministry of Finance Utilizes Advanced Technology and Artificial Intelligence Solutions to Develop Three Government Services, news release, 13 November 2023. The ministry says that it added generative AI to its inquiry service after programs that ran from August to October 2023, and that answers became faster and more accurate. It gives no figures and does not name IntelliServe.

    Regarding the third service, submitting inquiries about the ministry's services, its development was based on redesigning the customer's experience by integrating generative artificial intelligence technology, significantly enhancing the user experience and improving the efficiency, accuracy, and speed of obtaining information.

    UAE Ministry of Finance, Ministry of Finance Showcases 8 Innovative Digital Projects and Services at GITEX Global 2024, news release, 16 October 2024. The ministry says that its generative AI project began in October 2023 and had finished three phases. It does not name IntelliServe.

    Three phases of the project, which commenced in October 2023, have been completed, and the expected completion date is by the end of 2025.
