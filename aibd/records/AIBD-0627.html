What happened

Imagen Technologies of New York makes OsteoDetect, machine learning software that marks distal radius fractures on wrist X-rays. In a fully crossed reader study, 24 clinicians read 200 cases with and without the software, with at least one month between sessions. The readers were radiologists, orthopedic surgeons, emergency physicians, emergency physician assistants, internists and family physicians. Three hand surgeons set the ground truth. FDA reviewers report that the area under the ROC curve rose from 0.840 unaided to 0.889 aided. Sensitivity rose from 74.7% to 80.3% and specificity from 88.9% to 91.4%. Every type of reader improved. FDA granted the De Novo request on 24 May 2018. The study used archived images, so it measured reader accuracy and did not measure patient outcomes.

Sources

  1. Peer-reviewed study, by the makers.

    Lindsey R, Daluiski A, Chopra S, et al. Deep neural network improves fracture detection by clinicians. Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences 2018;115(45):11591-11596.

    The average clinician experienced a relative reduction in misinterpretation rate of 47.0% (95% CI, 37.4-53.9%).

  2. Regulatory document, by authors with no tie to the system.

    U.S. Food and Drug Administration, Center for Devices and Radiological Health. De Novo classification request for OsteoDetect (DEN180005): decision summary. Granted 2018-05-24.

    By comparison, the study demonstrated non-aided sensitivity and specificity of 74.7% [72.8%,76.5%] and 88.9% [87.6%,90.0%], respectively.

Details

AI system

Workflow

Clinicians reviewed the marked images together with the original images. Cases included PA and lateral wrist views of adults.

Measurement

Measurement

Fully crossed multi-reader multi-case study with a one-month washout; ground truth by three hand surgeons. [2]

Setting and sample

24 clinicians of six types; 200 cases. [2]

Measures

Reader AUC, sensitivity and specificity. [2]

Results

AUC 0.840 to 0.889; sensitivity 74.7% to 80.3%; specificity 88.9% to 91.4%. [2]

Additional value from AI

AUC difference 0.049 (95% CI 0.019 to 0.080). [2]

Limitations

Retrospective archived images; enriched case mix; no patient outcomes. [2]

Denominators

200 aided and 200 unaided reads per clinician. [2]

Materials for replication

None public. [2]

Follow-up
