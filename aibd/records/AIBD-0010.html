What happened

On 7 June 2026 a 25-year-old man from the Sardhana area of Meerut, Uttar Pradesh, posted an Instagram video. In it he appeared to drink a white liquid. Police said Meta sent a suicide alert about the post to the Social Media Center at Uttar Pradesh Police headquarters at 6:53 pm. The center alerted Meerut Police, and a Sardhana team reached his home within eight minutes. They found him in a critical condition with a bottle of suspected liquid nearby and took him to hospital, where his condition improved after treatment. He told police he was under stress following the end of a relationship. Police data cited by PTI credit such alerts with 3,011 lives saved statewide from 1 January 2023 to 31 May 2026.

What it was built to do. Built to flag posts and live videos indicating suicide or self-harm risk for review and, in imminent cases, referral to emergency services. This benefit is that purpose.

The system's part

Police sources say Meta sent the alert after the post. They do not state whether Meta's classifier or a user report flagged it. Meta's documentation and NPR describe the process as machine-learning detection with human review before contacting authorities. The record relies on that description and should be read with that caveat.

Replication

What produced it. Platform detection and review that flags self-harm posts and contacts a police nodal center with account phone and location details. Police nodal center with a standing arrangement to dispatch local officers immediately.

What must hold again. Account details resolve to a reachable address within minutes. Local officers are available to respond immediately.
