What happened

Quantitative Insights of Chicago developed QuantX, software that analyzes a breast lesion on MRI and combines its features through an artificial intelligence algorithm into a single score. In a sequential reader study, 19 radiologists read 111 breast MRI cases first without the score and then with it. FDA reviewers report that the summary area under the ROC curve rose from 0.7055 to 0.7575, a difference of 0.0520 with a p-value of 0.0408. The reviewers write that the study marginally met its primary endpoint. A second method of estimating the AUC gave a confidence interval that crossed zero. Training materials gave readers an optimistic view of the software, and the case set was enriched. FDA granted the De Novo request on 19 July 2017 and found that the probable benefits outweigh the probable risks.
