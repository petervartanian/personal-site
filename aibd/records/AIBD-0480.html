What happened

ZTE reports that it deployed its Nebula vision system at Taicang Port. An edge detector flags possible fire or smoke, and a larger cloud vision model checks the context to reject false positives. ZTE reports that the system eliminated 92% of false fire alarms and detected unsafe behavior. ZTE reports that it tested the system at Taicang Port on 65 videos, 30 with smoke and 35 without. It states that accuracy exceeded 90% and that the false alarm rate stayed below 5%. No independent source verifies these gains. Prevented injuries remain unestablished.

Details

AI system

Model

The submission names Nebula CV-grounding 2.0 as the large vision model.

Role

Small models screen surveillance footage at the edge, and a larger cloud model verifies suspected smoke or unsafe behavior before an alert is escalated.

Workflow

The account does not separately establish the conditions responsible for the benefit.

Human oversight

Safety outcomes depend on human response to the alerts. ZTE's account does not describe who reviews the alerts or how. [1]

Measurement

Measurement

On-site test of the deployed detector on recorded videos at Taicang Port, run by the developer. [1]

Setting and sample

65 videos at Taicang Port: 30 with smoke and 35 without, in daytime, nighttime, rain and fog. [1]

Measures

Detection accuracy, false alarm rate and missed detection rate; share of edge false positives removed by cloud verification. [1]

Results

Accuracy above 90%, false alarm rate below 5%, missed detection rate below 1%; 92% of edge false positives removed. [1]

Limitations

The developer ran the test and reports bounds only. The sample is small, the 92% figure has no stated base, and the account gives no test dates or effects on safety. [1]

Follow-up

Conditions for replication

Existing CCTV coverage with edge detection and cloud verification. Port-specific training and scenario configuration.

Evidence

Original evidence

The only source is ZTE's own submission to the ITU AI for Good report of 2025. It reports the 92% reduction in false positives and tests on 65 videos, with accuracy above 90% and a false alarm rate below 5%. [1]
