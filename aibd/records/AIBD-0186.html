Relation to AI

GAO found that FPS leads started about 20 percent of contractor investigations in fiscal 2015 and 2016. Contractor officials told GAO that FPS sped up the finding and triage of suspect providers, but that it generally did not speed up the investigations themselves. Investigators did the case work, and the FPS prepayment edits apply Medicare rules without scoring risk, so the dollar totals combine model-generated leads, rule-based edits and human investigation.

Citable summary

CMS's return-on-investment report for the Fraud Prevention System's fourth implementation year counts $654.8 million in inappropriate Medicare payments identified or prevented in 2015 against $57.1... The $654.8 million total combines prevented payments, projected avoidance, and overpayments referred for recovery. It is not a cash-recovery total. Certification of the accounting method does not isolate AI's contribution from investigators or establish which learning technique FPS used. The report counts savings from actions taken due to the FPS and from investigations the FPS expedited, augmented or corroborated. So the system's own contribution cannot be separated from investigators' work. The technique is not described. CMS deployed the system and paid unnamed developing and modeling contractors, so developer and deployer are not the same body.
