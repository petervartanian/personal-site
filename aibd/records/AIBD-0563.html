What happened

Surrey Heath used two Copilot licenses to test meeting recaps, email summaries, policy drafts, and presentations. The council describes useful early results and seeks a stronger business case. Time savings, financial returns, and benefits from wider deployment remain unmeasured. The council's ICT manager, James Rutter, said that AI could transcribe meetings accurately but struggled with the context and nuance that minutes need. In a larger government pilot from October to December 2024, the Department for Business and Trade found that Copilot did best at drafting and summarizing, but its evaluators found no evidence that the time saved improved productivity.
