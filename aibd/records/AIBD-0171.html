ENEA tests ML heatwave grid-failure forecasts; no outcome given

The OECD AI Incidents Monitor records that ENEA, Politecnico di Bari and Università Roma Tre developed and tested an ML system to predict heatwave-induced grid failures (May 2023); no outcome given.

What happened

The OECD AI Incidents Monitor record 2023-05-24-c85b, summarising Italian press items of 24 to 26 May 2023 (Askanews, Teknoring, Ingenio and others, not fetched), states that researchers from ENEA, Politecnico di Bari and Università Roma Tre developed and tested an AI-based system using machine learning to predict and prevent electrical grid failures caused by heatwaves, aimed at urban areas affected by climate change. The monitor labels it an AI hazard rather than an incident because the system is designed to prevent harm and no realised event is reported. Only the registry page was fetched; no deployment, grid operator or avoided outage is documented.

What it was built to do. The system was built to forecast heatwave-related failures on electricity networks so operators can act in advance; the record documents development and testing only.
