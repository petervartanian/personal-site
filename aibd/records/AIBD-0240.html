Anthropic flag on a shooting threat leads to a Florida arrest

Anthropic safeguards flagged a threat to shoot up a Florida sheriff's office; reviewers tipped police, who arrested the user; no gun found.

Workflow

A vendor practice of screening consumer chats for threatening content and escalating severe cases to a human review team. That team can refer them to law enforcement, which Anthropic's consumer terms and privacy policy permit. A tip carrying enough account information for the sheriff's office to identify a named resident within days. A local agency that treated a stated threat against its own building as credible enough to send deputies the same night.

Human oversight

Reviewers assessed the messages before making the referral. Deputies identified and interviewed the user and made the charging decision.

Relation to AI

The arrest report says the platform “utilizes safety and security measures designed to monitor for key phrases and potentially threatening content”. It says the messages were “escalated to a human review team”. An automated layer therefore surfaced the two messages, and people judged their severity and made the referral. The sources do not say whether the flag came from a classifier or a keyword rule. Straight Arrow News, citing court records, says the review team escalated to the FBI, which notified the sheriff's office. Identifying the user, the home visit, the Miranda interview and the charging decision were the work of Lee County deputies and Intelligence Detective Thomas Alejo. The arrest report calls the platform “Anthropic Artificial Intelligence”. Press reports name Claude, and court records note the user told the detective she used Perplexity.

AI contribution

The platform automatically flagged threatening messages for human review. Reviewers referred the messages to law enforcement.

Purpose

Anthropic's consumer terms (effective 8 October 2025) reserve the right “at our sole discretion” to report Inputs to law enforcement. Its privacy policy (effective 10 September 2026) allows disclosure the company believes “reasonably necessary” to “prevent serious harm to any person or to property”. The arrest report says the platform's safety measures are “designed to monitor for key phrases and potentially threatening content”. Flagging a stated shooting threat, reviewing it by hand and referring it to police was that purpose. Of an August 2026 referral Anthropic said “This is our safeguards process working as intended”.
