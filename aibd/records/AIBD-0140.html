Workflow

A printed urgent-care wrist X-ray that the parent could photograph and upload. Grok on X with image input, which Musk was publicly promoting for medical images in January 2025. A parent who distrusted the first reading because of worsening signs (cold, tingling hand; immobile thumb; a wrist that did not look right). She had already asked for the films for the PCP. Access within days to a PCP referral and a wrist specialist who re-imaged and examined the wrist.

Relation to AI

Screenshots attached to the post show Grok answering three questions about the uploaded image. It said there is “a clear fracture line visible in the distal radius”. It said the line is “a fracture line rather than a fused growth plate”. It said the break is “quite obvious” with “dorsal displacement”. Each time it advised a professional consultation. The orthopaedist's reading matched. But the mother states that she did not mention Grok to the specialist and that the diagnosis was “completely independent”. She had already asked for the X-ray prints to take to the PCP. Grok's contribution was therefore to strengthen her resolve to seek a second opinion. Everything rests on her account. No clinician, clinic or record has spoken publicly. The next day Grok told a physician who asked that it cannot diagnose medical injuries.

Conditions for replication

Grok's reading was correct for this image on the mother's account only. Asked by a physician the next day, it said it cannot diagnose injuries. So its output is not reliable across users or images. A clinician re-examined and re-imaged the wrist. Grok's output was never used as the diagnosis or the basis of treatment. The only sources are the mother's post and news reports built from it. No clinic, clinician or record is public, and the surgery-avoidance statement is second-hand.
