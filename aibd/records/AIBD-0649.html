Model Dermatology helps trainees diagnose skin lesions in Seoul

In a Seoul trial of 576 patients with suspicious skin lesions, non-expert physicians using Model Dermatology diagnosed 53.9% correctly against 43.8% without it.

What happened

Dermatologists at Asan Medical Center, Ulsan University College of Medicine, in Seoul, South Korea, ran a single-center unmasked randomized trial of the Model Dermatology algorithm, a convolutional neural network that the dermatologist Seung Seog Han develops and maintains as a web service. Consecutive adults with one or more skin lesions suspicious for skin cancer were randomly allocated to four non-dermatology trainees and four dermatology residents, who examined them with or without real-time help from the algorithm. Among 576 patients with Fitzpatrick skin types III or IV, the physicians' diagnoses were correct in 53.9% of the 295 cases with help and in 43.8% of the 281 cases without (P = 0.019). The gain came from the non-dermatology trainees, whose accuracy was 54.7% with the algorithm and 30.7% without; for the dermatology residents it was not significant. Han is the first author of the trial report.

Details

AI system

Workflow

Physicians saw the algorithm's suggestions in real time during the examination. Most of the gain came from physicians with little dermatology experience. Patients had Fitzpatrick skin types III or IV.

Measurement

Measurement

Top-1 diagnostic accuracy against final diagnosis. [2]

Setting and sample

576 consecutive adults with suspicious skin lesions at a tertiary hospital in Seoul; eight non-expert physicians. [2]

Measures

Diagnostic accuracy. [2]

Results

53.9% vs 43.8% overall; 54.7% vs 30.7% for non-dermatology trainees. [2]

Additional value from AI

10.1 percentage points overall. [2]

Limitations

Unmasked single-center design; no significant gain for dermatology residents; the developer is the first author. [2]

Denominators

Real-time algorithm assistance in the AI group. [2]

Materials for replication

Algorithm available at b2020.modelderm.com. [2]

Follow-up

Conditions for replication

Users must be non-experts for whom the suggestions add information. The algorithm must be right often enough; wrong top suggestions lowered trainee accuracy.
