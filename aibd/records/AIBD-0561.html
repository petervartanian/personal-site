South Cambridgeshire reports fewer calls and better routing

South Cambridgeshire reports lower call volumes after chatbot rollout and better call classification, without numerical savings or satisfaction results.

ⓘ

What happened

South Cambridgeshire deployed EBI chatbot and call-routing functions to handle routine queries. The council reports fewer calls than in the prior year during the chatbot's first three months and better call classification/routing. No numerical call-volume reduction is supplied. The 93% accuracy figure is a council test result, not independently verified service correctness. Call-volume comparisons do not isolate causality.

Purpose. Resolve routine queries and route contacts so that staff can focus on more complex needs.

AI’s contribution

The deployed chatbot and routing functions answer or classify routine contacts. The council reports fewer calls and better routing, but year-on-year comparisons do not isolate causality and no resident-satisfaction result is supplied.

Beneficiaries

  • an organization

    The council reports reduced routine-call demand and better routing, supporting modest operational efficiency.

  • a person

    Officers receive more calls on the correct topics, reducing routine friction. Improved resident satisfaction is an aim rather than a reported result.

Further evidence

Setting. The model was configured with council website and policy material, predefined flows, and staff testing. The rollout included chatbot, live chat, and interactive voice response, with further integrations planned.

Conditions. The conditions needed to reproduce the benefit are unspecified.
