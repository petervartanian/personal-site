qXR nodule flags lead to five early-stage lung cancer diagnoses

3 a person

CT/biopsy confirmed early-stage lung cancer in five adults; four had surgery

qXR flagged nodules on X-rays done for other reasons; CT/biopsy confirmed early-stage lung cancer in five adults; four had surgery.

What happened

In the CREATE study (NCT05817110), a prospective multicentre study in which the deep-learning software qXR (Qure.ai) was adopted for routine use on all chest X-rays across participating centers from April 2023 to December 2024, five cases were identified through the AI pathway. The paper names neither the centers nor their cities; its clinical authors are affiliated with Hacettepe University (Ankara, Turkey) and with Aarthi Scans and Sri Ramachandra Medical College and Hospital (Chennai, India). The March 2026 Cureus report describes five individuals, none with respiratory symptoms, in whom qXR flagged suspicious pulmonary nodules on X-rays obtained for non-respiratory reasons: a 57-year-old man screened before anti-TNF therapy for ulcerative colitis, a 64-year-old man with fever, a 65-year-old woman at a smoking-cessation consultation and two men aged 35 and 75 at annual health checks. CT and biopsy confirmed early-stage lung cancer in all five; four underwent curative surgery, two with adjuvant chemotherapy, and one chose chemotherapy alone. Whether the original X-ray reports had noted the nodules is not stated.

What it was built to do. qXR is built to detect abnormalities including pulmonary nodules on chest X-rays; flagging incidental nodules is its intended function.

The system's part

qXR's nodule flag on X-rays ordered for unrelated reasons prompted low-dose CT, with or without PET-CT, and biopsy in each case. The report does not state whether radiologists had independently reported the nodules, so the AI's contribution rests on the authors' judgment that the cases would otherwise have been missed or delayed. One of ten authors is a Qure.ai employee and the study was funded by AstraZeneca.

Replication

No attempt to repeat it has been recorded.

What produced it

What must hold again

  • Tissue confirmation before treatment
