What happened

Generate Biomedicines designed GB-0669 using machine learning. It is a monoclonal antibody against the conserved SARS-CoV-2 spike S2 stem helix. A phase 1 randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled single-ascending-dose trial, registered as NCT07050511, enrolled 51 healthy adults. 36 received GB-0669 and 15 placebo, across five dose cohorts from 100 to 2,400 mg. The trial ran from 18 July 2023 to 22 November 2024. The results were published in the Journal of Infectious Diseases on 14 July 2026. No dose-limiting toxicities occurred, and all adverse reactions were grade 1 or 2. Pharmacokinetics were dose-proportional with a 54-day half-life. Dose-dependent serum live-virus neutralization occurred at 600 and 1,200 mg. No patient benefit was demonstrated, and the authors propose phase 2 evaluation in immunocompromised patients.

What it was built to do. Use generative machine learning to design a therapeutic antibody against a conserved, poorly immunogenic region of the SARS-CoV-2 spike protein.
