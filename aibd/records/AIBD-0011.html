SARUAV finds an unconscious 65-year-old alive in tall grass

29 June 2021: SARUAV CNN flagged a 65-year-old man unconscious in tall grass after 24+ h; GOPR Bieszczady retrieved him alive

ⓘ

What happened

On Monday 28 June 2021 at about 4 pm a 65-year-old man with Alzheimer's disease left his home in Cergowa, south-east Poland. He had a stroke the previous day. Police and tracking dogs did not find him after a storm. On Tuesday 29 June the Bieszczady Mountain Rescue Service (GOPR Bieszczady) flew four drone flights. It ran SARUAV, software from University of Wrocław researchers that uses convolutional neural networks (CNNs) to find people in nadir aerial images, on 782 images. After 4 h 31 min of analysis the system detected him and returned his coordinates. He was lying unconscious in grass over two meters tall after more than 24 hours outdoors. GOPR rescuers carried him out and handed him to the ambulance service. No cited source gives the day of the find outright. 29 June follows from the 28 June 4 pm departure and the GOPR communique quoted by Świat Dronów.

It opens “Od wczoraj – 28 czerwca” (“since yesterday, 28 June”) and says the program flagged him after almost 24 hours.

Purpose. Built to detect people automatically in drone imagery for search and rescue; this benefit is that purpose.

AI’s contribution

The CNN detector returned the man's coordinates after police and tracking dogs had failed. Rescuers then retrieved him. Three of the five case-report authors work for SARUAV Ltd., so the independent confirmation is the GOPR Bieszczady account quoted in the Polish press.

Beneficiaries

  • a person

    A 65-year-old man with Alzheimer's and a stroke a day earlier lay unconscious in two-meter grass after over 24 hours outdoors. He was found and handed to the ambulance service alive.

  • an organization

    GOPR Bieszczady closed a search that police, dogs and ground teams had not resolved, in what it called the first such find in Poland.

Further evidence

Setting. Nadir drone imagery at close range over the search area. CNN detector run during the search, with rescuers able to verify hits and reach the coordinates.

Conditions. The person is within the imaged area and visible from above. Processing completes while the person can still be helped.
