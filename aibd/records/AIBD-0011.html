SARUAV detects an unconscious man in tall grass, and rescuers save him

SARUAV, software that uses convolutional neural networks, detected an unconscious 65-year-old man in tall grass on drone images after more than 24 hours, and mountain rescuers retrieved him alive.

The University of Wrocław, under whose auspices the developer operates, reports that a South Tyrol mountain rescue team used SARUAV to find a missing person at the beginning of August 2024.
