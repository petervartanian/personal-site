SpliceAI flags a hidden genetic cause of a baby's fatal kidney disease

SpliceAI scored a hidden PKHD1 variant at 0.98, and after RNA studies confirmed it, the parents of the infant who died used the diagnosis to plan IVF with embryo genetic testing.

  • 3 a person

    An identifiable family received a definitive molecular cause of their son's death. They used it for IVF with preimplantation genetic testing to avoid a recurrence of the lethal disease. The infant himself died at two days of life.

Depth runs from 1, a convenience or time saved, to 5, a life or a livelihood preserved.

The system’s part
SpliceAI's score of 0.98 nominated the maternally inherited deep intronic variant as the second allele. Among 78,575 rare intronic PKHD1 variants in gnomAD only one scored higher. Pathogenicity was then established by RT-PCR and Sanger sequencing of maternal urine-derived cells and GeneDx's likely-pathogenic classification. The AI's contribution was candidate selection. Without it the second allele would have remained unexplained. Authors declare no relevant financial interests.
Limits
The SpliceAI score nominated a candidate variant; RNA experiments and clinical interpretation supported the diagnosis. This case does not show that AI prevented the infant’s death, and proceeding to IVF with PGT-M does not establish a successful pregnancy or healthy birth. One family cannot establish diagnostic performance across families.

What happened

A male infant was delivered at an outside hospital at 34 weeks and 2 days. Both kidneys were enlarged with innumerable tiny cysts, consistent with autosomal recessive polycystic kidney disease. He was cared for in the Mount Sinai neonatal intensive care unit in New York and died at two days of life. Whole-genome sequencing found a known pathogenic missense variant from his father. SpliceAI scores in the NICUnet Rareservoir then identified a maternally inherited deep intronic PKHD1 variant scoring 0.98. RNA studies on kidney epithelial cells derived from his mother's urine confirmed that the variant creates a novel exon with a premature stop codon. The laboratory GeneDx classified it as likely pathogenic. After genetic counseling, the parents proceeded with in vitro fertilization (IVF) and preimplantation genetic testing for monogenic disorders within one year of the death. The case report was published online on 10 January 2024. Its authors write that splicing algorithms had until then been used in retrospective research. They write that such algorithms had not been used as a first step in clinical practice. In 2025 an Israeli team reported that a common deep intronic PKHD1 variant in Bedouin families received the lowest and most benign scores across splicing prediction tools among all known PKHD1 splicing variants. In 2026 a team in the Philippines and Japan used SpliceAI the same way to find a deep intronic IFT172 variant in a boy with nephronophthisis.

Details

AI system

Inputs and outputs

SpliceAI scored rare intronic PKHD1 variants and nominated the maternally inherited variant with a predicted splice effect of 0.98.

Workflow

Whole-genome sequencing with SpliceAI scoring of rare intronic variants in a disease gene already implicated by one pathogenic allele. Access to parental samples for RNA-level validation.

Human oversight

Researchers tested RNA from maternal renal cells, and the clinical laboratory classified the variant before genetic counseling and reproductive planning.

Benefits

Beneficiaries

The parents gained an explanation of the infant's disease and information for IVF with preimplantation genetic testing. The diagnosis did not alter the infant's survival.

Duration

The authors of the case report write that the parents proceeded with IVF and preimplantation genetic testing within one year of the death. No later source describes the outcome of the IVF cycle or any further use of the diagnosis by the family. [1]

Follow-up

Conditions for replication

Functional or RNA confirmation before clinical use of the variant. Genetic counseling and access to IVF with PGT-M.
