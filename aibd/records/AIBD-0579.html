COMPOSER sepsis alerts are linked to lower mortality

UC San Diego links COMPOSER to 17% lower sepsis mortality relative to a modeled counterfactual. The comparison was not randomized.

ⓘ

What happened

UC San Diego Health evaluated COMPOSER in two emergency departments among 6,217 adults with sepsis. The model went live on December 7, 2022, with follow-up through April 30, 2023. Post-deployment mortality was 9.49%, compared with a modeled counterfactual of 11.39%: an adjusted reduction of 1.9 percentage points (95% confidence interval 0.3–3.5). Raw pre-deployment mortality was 10.3%. The estimated benefit was statistically significant at one hospital and not the other. The study used historical controls, without randomization or a concurrent untreated group.

Purpose. Identify emerging sepsis so that clinical teams can assess and treat patients sooner.

AI’s contribution

COMPOSER analyzed patient data and alerted nurses to possible sepsis. Nurses reviewed patients with physicians, who selected treatment. The outcome concerns AI-assisted clinical care.

Further evidence

Setting. Continuous analysis of electronic patient data and alerts delivered to nursing staff. Nurse review, physician assessment, and treatment at UC San Diego Medical Center and Jacobs Medical Center.

Conditions. Reliable data and prompt clinical assessment are likely requirements. Local evaluation is needed to establish whether the benefit recurs.
