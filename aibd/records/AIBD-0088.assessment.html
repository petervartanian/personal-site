Short account

Recorded; review pending

Summary
NHC blended Google DeepMind's AI ensemble into official Melissa forecasts from its first advisory on 21 October 2025; Cat 5 landfall in Jamaica 28 October 2025.
Account
The US National Hurricane Center (NHC) blended Google DeepMind's experimental AI hurricane model (ensemble mean GDMI) into official forecasts for Tropical Storm Melissa. It did so from its first advisory, 21 October 2025. Discussion 1 placed the official track between the DeepMind ensemble mean and the consensus aid HCCA. Discussion 2 described the track as a blend of the DeepMind mean, HCCA and TVCA. Discussion 8 (23 October) moved the track toward a blend of the DeepMind mean and HCCA. Discussion 10 blended the HCCA and GDMI aids. It set an intensity forecast in best agreement with GDMI after all 50 DeepMind members showed a major hurricane. On 28 October 2025 Melissa made landfall in southwestern Jamaica near New Hope with 185 mph winds and 892 mb pressure. IFRC reported 45 deaths and about 1.6 million people affected. It relayed a World Bank/IDB estimate of US$8.8 billion in physical damage. Its CHF 80,000 Imminent DREF was approved on 23 October with a five-day lead time.

Replication
Produced by
  • Experimental Google DeepMind ensemble guidance available in real time to NHC forecasters
  • Forecasters choosing to blend GDMI with consensus aids (HCCA) in official track and intensity forecasts

Must hold
