GI Genius helped endoscopists find more adenomas in a randomized trial

In the randomized Italian trial that FDA reviewed, colonoscopy with GI Genius found adenomas in 55.1% of low-risk patients, against 42.0% with standard colonoscopy.

  • 2 an organization

What happened

Cosmo Artificial Intelligence of Dublin, Ireland, makes GI Genius, a program that marks suspected polyps with a box on the screen during colonoscopy. In the AID trial at three hospitals in Milan, Rome and Como, 700 patients were randomly assigned between September and November 2019 to colonoscopy with or without the program, performed by six endoscopists. FDA asked for an analysis of the 263 patients at low risk of colorectal cancer. In that group, FDA reviewers report an adenoma detection rate of 55.1% with GI Genius against 42.0% with standard colonoscopy, and 0.81 adenomas per colonoscopy against 0.57. The program caused no added adverse events. FDA granted the De Novo request on 9 April 2021. The reviewers note that race was not recorded, that most patients were presumed to be white, and that only six endoscopists in Italy took part. They write that a reduction in cancer risk would need confirmation in further trials.

Details

AI system

Workflow

Endoscopists with an adenoma detection rate of 25 to 40% used the program during white-light colonoscopy. A minimum withdrawal time of six minutes was required. Histology confirmed each lesion.

Measurement

Measurement

Randomized 1:1 multicenter trial with histology as reference; FDA reanalysis in low-risk patients. [2]

Setting and sample

700 patients at three Italian hospitals; 263 low-risk patients in the FDA analysis; six endoscopists. [2]

Measures

Adenoma detection rate and adenomas per colonoscopy. [2]

Results

ADR 55.1% against 42.0%; APC 0.81 against 0.57. [2]

Additional value from AI

Adjusted ADR difference of 13.1 percentage points. [2]

Limitations

Limited patient diversity; six endoscopists in Italy; cancer outcomes not measured. [2]

Denominators

One colonoscopy per patient. [2]

Materials for replication

ClinicalTrials.gov NCT04079478. [2]

Follow-up

Conditions for replication

The program runs with standard white-light endoscopy. Endoscopists use the marks as an aid and keep inspecting the whole colon.
