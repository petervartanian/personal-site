Short account

Recorded value

State what happened, for whom, and the main qualification.

Summary
Researchers used the Benevolent Platform to identify baricitinib as a candidate treatment for COVID-19.
Account
BenevolentAI researchers reported in February 2020 that their knowledge graph had helped them identify baricitinib, an arthritis drug, as a possible treatment for COVID-19. They proposed clinical testing after reviewing its effects on inflammation and proteins involved in viral entry. In ACTT-2, 1,033 hospitalized adults received remdesivir with either baricitinib or a placebo. Median recovery took 7 days with baricitinib and 8 days with the placebo. COV-BARRIER enrolled 1,525 adults. By day 28, 8% of patients receiving baricitinib had died, compared with 13% receiving a placebo. Both groups received standard care. Mortality was a secondary outcome. The trial did not meet its primary outcome of reduced disease progression, so its mortality result was not conclusive under the planned procedure for multiple comparisons. The subsequent RECOVERY trial enrolled 8,156 patients. Mortality, its primary outcome, was 12% with baricitinib and 14% with usual care alone.

Recorded value

State the particular claimed or observed change. Retain multiple outcomes without duplicating the deployment count.

Account
BenevolentAI researchers reported in February 2020 that their knowledge graph had helped them identify baricitinib, an arthritis drug, as a possible treatment for COVID-19. They proposed clinical testing after reviewing its effects on inflammation and proteins involved in viral entry. In ACTT-2, 1,033 hospitalized adults received remdesivir with either baricitinib or a placebo. Median recovery took 7 days with baricitinib and 8 days with the placebo. COV-BARRIER enrolled 1,525 adults. By day 28, 8% of patients receiving baricitinib had died, compared with 13% receiving a placebo. Both groups received standard care. Mortality was a secondary outcome. The trial did not meet its primary outcome of reduced disease progression, so its mortality result was not conclusive under the planned procedure for multiple comparisons. The subsequent RECOVERY trial enrolled 8,156 patients. Mortality, its primary outcome, was 12% with baricitinib and 14% with usual care alone.
