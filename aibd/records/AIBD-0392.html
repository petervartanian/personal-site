Northern Territory team maps ear disease and plans AI to target services

NT Health plans a Bayesian hierarchical model and Power BI AI features to guide ear-health services; by 25 May 2026 the mapping phase was complete and the AI tools were still being selected.

    NT Hearing Services intends the model to identify high-risk areas, and no source reports a resulting change in planning or services.

  • Not rated a person

    Better hearing or care through targeted services is intended, and no source reports a resulting patient outcome.

  • Not rated society

    Smaller health gaps for Indigenous people are an intended downstream effect, and no source reports one.

What happened

In its submission to the International Telecommunication Union (ITU), current as of June 2025, NT Health proposes an analysis that combines a Bayesian hierarchical model and Power BI AI features to infer ear-disease patterns from health and social data in the Northern Territory. The existing mapping and the seasonal rules written in DAX, the Power BI formula language, are conventional data processing. The intended AI contribution is better identification of the areas that need services. The submission does not fully specify the AI implementation. On 11 August 2025 the Northern Territory Health Minister announced that the tool covers 93 communities and outstations and that NT Hearing Services would next run the AI algorithms, with the map expected to be complete by 2026. In a poster at the World Congress of Audiology on 25 May 2026, the team reported that the mapping phase was complete and that it was still selecting AI tools for modeling. No source reports that the AI analysis was run or that it improved a service or a hearing result.

Details

AI system

Inputs and outputs

Community coordinates, de-identified ear-health records, census data, and environmental measures would be combined to map disease clusters and social conditions.

Workflow

The account does not establish conditions sufficient to reproduce the benefit.

Human oversight

Health staff audited manually entered records annually. Analysts and policymakers would interpret the maps and choose service priorities.

AI contribution

The team's submission names a Bayesian hierarchical model and Power BI AI features. In a poster of 25 May 2026, the team reported that it was still selecting AI tools for modeling. The completed work is a community-level map and data linkage, which are not AI functions. [1] [2] [3]

Benefits

Beneficiaries

Remote Northern Territory communities, especially Aboriginal children at risk of ear disease, are the intended beneficiaries. The immediate users are health planners and service providers.

Reach

The Northern Territory Health Minister said on 11 August 2025 that the tool covers 93 communities and outstations. That figure describes the reach of the map. No source reports a person who benefited. [1] [2] [3]

People and places

Dependencies

The proposed analysis used Azure and Power BI, community-level coordinates, and health and census datasets with recorded dates.
