Robots at a Dubai laboratory cut cement tests to eight minutes

The acting director of Dubai Central Laboratory said that its robotic X-ray fluorescence (XRF) system cut cement tests from four days to eight minutes.

What happened

On 16 August 2023 the Dubai Media Office announced that the Dubai Central Laboratory was using robots with X-ray and AI technology to test cement and other construction materials. The acting director of the laboratory said that tests that took four days now took eight minutes. The acting director also said that daily samples rose by 650 percent. In June 2025 Dubai Municipality reported that its robotic X-ray fluorescence (XRF) system was still active. The municipality reported that the system automates the preparation and analysis of cement samples and reduces manual work. It planned to extend automation to tests of concrete, asphalt and soil. No independent source verifies these gains. No source measures a gain in staff safety.
