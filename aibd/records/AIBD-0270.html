ChatGPT helps review MRI literature on intravascular lymphoma

Chinese neurologists used ChatGPT to help review published MRI findings of cerebral intravascular large B-cell lymphoma for a 59-year-old's case report; the diagnosis came from biopsy, not the model.

ⓘ

What happened

Chen et al. (Frontiers in Immunology, 14 November 2024) are at the Affiliated Yuebei People's Hospital, Shantou University Medical College, Shaoguan. They report a previously healthy 59-year-old woman who developed hemiparesis and altered mental status after SARS-CoV-2 reinfection. She was first treated for presumed infarction, then for suspected demyelination. Brain biopsy diagnosed intravascular large B-cell lymphoma. The authors say they reviewed the disease's brain magnetic resonance imaging (MRI) findings in the literature “with the assistance of ChatGPT”. They call their review the first to focus on MRI features of cerebral lesions. The model played no part in diagnosis or treatment. The authors declare no competing interests.

Purpose. Assist clinicians in reviewing published imaging literature for a case report

AI’s contribution

ChatGPT assisted the authors' literature review for the published case. The patient's diagnosis and care did not involve the model. Source is a non-vendor peer-reviewed case report.
