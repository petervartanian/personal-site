ChatGPT helps review MRI literature on intravascular lymphoma

off the scale

Chinese neurologists used ChatGPT to help review published MRI findings of cerebral intravascular large B-cell lymphoma for a 59-year-old's case report; the diagnosis came from biopsy, not the model.

What happened

Chen et al. (Frontiers in Immunology, 14 November 2024), from the Affiliated Yuebei People's Hospital of Shantou University Medical College in Shaoguan, report a previously healthy 59-year-old woman who developed hemiparesis and altered mental status after SARS-CoV-2 reinfection, was first treated for presumed infarction and then for suspected demyelination, and was diagnosed with intravascular large B-cell lymphoma by brain biopsy. The authors state that they reviewed the brain MRI findings of the disease in the literature 'with the assistance of ChatGPT' and describe their review as the first to focus on MRI features of cerebral lesions. The model played no part in the diagnosis or treatment; the earlier verifier set the case aside for that reason. The authors declare no competing interests. The date is the publication date.

What it was built to do. Assist clinicians in reviewing published imaging literature for a case report
