What happened

Chen et al. (Frontiers in Immunology, 14 November 2024) are at the Affiliated Yuebei People's Hospital, Shantou University Medical College, Shaoguan. They report a previously healthy 59-year-old woman who developed hemiparesis and altered mental status after SARS-CoV-2 reinfection. She was first treated for presumed infarction, then for suspected demyelination. Brain biopsy diagnosed intravascular large B-cell lymphoma. The authors say that they reviewed the disease's brain magnetic resonance imaging (MRI) findings in the literature “with the assistance of ChatGPT”. They call their review the first to focus on MRI features of cerebral lesions. Two radiologists selected 11 papers describing MRI findings in 57 patients, and the MRI descriptions were entered into ChatGPT-4o. The authors state that its conclusions agree with those of human experts. Their acknowledgments thank a student of a New York high school who ran ChatGPT-4o during the literature review. The model played no part in diagnosis or treatment. The authors declare no competing interests. The journal received the manuscript on 9 August 2024.
