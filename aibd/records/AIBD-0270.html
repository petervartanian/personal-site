Relation to AI

Two radiologists chose the papers, and their MRI descriptions were entered into ChatGPT-4o, which judged whether any features were specific to the disease. The authors state that the model's conclusions agree with those of human experts and that it found no specific features. A high school student ran ChatGPT-4o during the review, according to the acknowledgments. Biopsy made the diagnosis, and the model had no part in the patient's care.
