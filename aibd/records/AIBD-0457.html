HEART Engine uses AI to tailor training and match learners to jobs

The #BlackTechFutures Research Institute reports that HEART Engine uses recommendation models and conversational agents to tailor training and job matching but quantifies no gain.

  • Not rated a person

    No source reports a completed training or a job placement for any learner.

  • Not rated an organization

    No source reports that the platform trained more learners or placed more of them in jobs.

  • Not rated society

    The institute expects AI-assisted matching to reduce bias in hiring and to widen digital equity, and no source reports either result.

What happened

In the International Telecommunication Union report published on 29 October 2025, the #BlackTechFutures Research Institute submits the HEART Engine use case, with Dr. Fallon Wilson listed first among its contacts. HEART Engine uses adaptive prompts, supervised recommendation models and conversational agents to personalize training. It also uses them to connect learners with opportunities. The institute lists Spelman College and Ebenezer Baptist Church as testbeds. It names Spelman College, Meharry Medical College and Stillman College as post-secondary anchors. It names Microsoft, Omidyar Network and the Kapor Center among funders and infrastructure partners. The report does not quantify any gain in learning or job placement.
