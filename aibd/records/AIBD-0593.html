What happened

An undated submission attributed to Darkriderdesign proposes that social workers who help people experiencing homelessness use Copilot to synthesize health, shelter, social-service and other records. It names Milwaukee as the proposed setting. It classifies the idea as hypothetical and not yet built. The intended benefits are more tailored assistance and fewer interruptions to treatment. The submission supplies background statistics on prevalence. It reports no results from an intervention. A portfolio under the Darkriderdesign name does not describe the proposal or an implementation, and no other public account of either is known. The portfolio is not cited as evidence of a benefit. The date is the day on which the public submission was accessed. The submission gives no date for a launch or an outcome.
