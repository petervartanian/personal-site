What happened

StudentSphere is developing an AI journaling tool for students. The proposed tool analyzes journal entries, tracks mood, and offers personalized feedback. Its planned escalation workflow would involve counselors when high-risk content appears. An internal pilot at VIT is planned. Accuracy and response-time figures are development targets. No source reports an observed improvement in student well-being or a completed crisis intervention.
