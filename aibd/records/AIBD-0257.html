What happened

CNN.gr reported on Monday 18 May 2026 that Deputy Minister of Transport Konstantinos Kyranakis had presented an artificial-intelligence system the previous Saturday. The system converts in real time the dialogues between stationmasters and train drivers carried over the GSM-R radio system into text. It then uses AI to determine whether the text is within the strict traffic-regulation protocols, so that the control center knows when it must intervene. He described it as a serious safety valve that changes the lax mentality that used to exist on the railway. That was a reference to the dialogue patterns exposed after the Tempi disaster. The article gives no developer, start date or results, and the system is presented as planned. The OECD AI Incidents Monitor entry notes that no incidents had yet occurred. The date given is the publication date.

What it was built to do. The system is intended to monitor operational radio communications and flag departures from protocol for the control center.
