MiniMed 640G cuts hypoglycemia; mother delivers a 4,137 g boy

MiniMed 640G predictive low-glucose suspend cut hypoglycemia in a T1D woman after two miscarriages; term birth of a 4,137 g boy.

AI system

System

Medtronic MiniMed sensor-augmented pump / automated insulin delivery with predictive low glucose suspend (SmartGuard)

Release

Exact release not established.

Application developer

Medtronic

Role

The pump predicted low glucose and suspended insulin delivery.

Configuration

The initial event involved a MiniMed 640G. A later report involved a MiniMed 780G.

Workflow

A person with type 1 diabetes and frequent or unrecognized hypoglycemia uses a Medtronic pump with CGM and predictive low glucose suspend enabled. Sensor glucose is predicted to fall and the algorithm suspends basal insulin. Hypoglycemia frequency and variability fall and the clinical goal (pregnancy, restored awareness) is reached.

Relation to AI

The pump's predictive algorithm autonomously suspended insulin for about four hours a day. The authors link this to fewer hypoglycemic episodes and lower variability. Obstetric and diabetes care were delivered by clinicians. The causal link to carrying the pregnancy is inferred, not demonstrated.
