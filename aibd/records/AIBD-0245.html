Tesla Autopilot stops on I-75 shoulder; drunk driver charged

Ouest-France (3 May 2026, citing ABC 7): around 2 a.m. on 24 Apr 2026 police found a 37-year-old woman unconscious in her stopped Tesla on Interstate 75; blood alcohol over twice the limit; charged...

ⓘ

What happened

Alexandre Chauvel reported in Ouest-France on 3 May 2026, relaying the Sarasota station ABC 7, on a car stopped on Interstate 75. Officers were alerted at about 2 a.m. on Friday 24 April 2026 to a car on the emergency lane at Sarasota, Florida. They found a 37-year-old woman unconscious at the wheel of a Tesla. Local police, quoted without naming the agency or officer, said she should not have been driving. They said her blood alcohol was more than twice the legal limit. They said she had assumed the Tesla would bring her home safely on Autopilot. The article explains that cabin sensors check that the driver is looking ahead and warn them. If the driver does not reopen their eyes after the first warning, the vehicle stops. She was arrested and is being prosecuted for driving under the influence. The Tesla model is not stated.

Neither a police report nor the ABC 7 story is among the sources. The OECD AI Incidents Monitor lists the event as a protective response with French-language sources.

Purpose. Autopilot's driver-attention monitoring is built to warn an inattentive driver. It brings the car to a stop if the driver does not respond. It is not designed to drive an impaired person home.

AI’s contribution

Per the report, the attention-monitoring logic issued warnings and then stopped the vehicle on the emergency lane. The agency, the vehicle data and the duration of the stop are not given. The police framed the episode as misuse rather than a rescue.

Further evidence

Setting. Autopilot engaged by an impaired driver on a limited-access highway. Cabin camera attention monitoring with escalating warnings. A stop-in-lane or shoulder-stop fallback when warnings go unanswered.

Conditions. The driver does not respond to warnings. The road has a shoulder or lane where a stopped car is not struck. Police attend before another vehicle collides with the stationary car.
