Tesla halts on I-75 when its drunk driver falls asleep at the wheel

Troopers said that the eye detection feature of a Tesla on Autopilot halted the car on I-75 after its intoxicated driver fell asleep; no vehicle data confirm the sequence.

  • Not rated society

    The car came to rest in the middle lane of I-75, and no source documents a collision with other road users that the stop prevented.

What happened

On 25 April 2024 the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration closed an engineering analysis that found that Autopilot's driver engagement system was weak for the system's permissive operation. ABC 7's WWSB reported on 24 April 2026 that a Tesla had stopped in the middle lane of Interstate 75 near Sarasota. Florida Highway Patrol officers found 37-year-old Kimberly Brown asleep behind the wheel at about 2 a.m. The station quotes Trooper Kenn Watson and reports that she was arrested for driving under the influence. It reports that the car came to a halt in the middle lane when Brown stopped responding to the vehicle's alerts. The report says that she had expected Autopilot to take her home. Edmunds analyst Alistair Weaver explains the general driver-attention warning and stopping mechanism. The report does not include vehicle logs that establish the precise sequence in this car. Gray News, citing state police, reported that troopers found Brown at about 2 a.m. on Friday, 24 April 2026. It reported that the troopers credited the car's eye detection with bringing it to a halt. Ouest-France reported on 3 May 2026 that her blood alcohol level was more than twice the legal limit.

Details

AI system

Autonomy

The vehicle reportedly stopped after the driver failed to respond to alerts. That was a fallback response within driver assistance, not evidence that it could complete the trip safely without a driver.

Workflow

Autopilot engaged by an impaired driver on a limited-access highway. Cabin camera attention monitoring with escalating warnings. A stop-in-lane or shoulder-stop fallback when warnings go unanswered.

Human oversight

The driver failed to remain attentive, and Florida Highway Patrol investigated the stopped vehicle. The incident shows that driver monitoring did not ensure responsible supervision.

Follow-up

Conditions for replication

The driver does not respond to warnings. The road has a shoulder or lane where a stopped car is not struck. Police attend before another vehicle collides with the stationary car.
