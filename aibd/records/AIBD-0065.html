Viz HCM flags hypertrophic cardiomyopathy in a 61-year-old

AI-ECG (Viz HCM) scored >0.95 for HCM in a symptomatic 61-year-old; echo confirmed obstructive HCM; mavacamten cut gradients and symptoms.

ⓘ

What happened

A 61-year-old man, unaware of any cardiomyopathy, had lightheadedness, dizziness, fatigue and functional limitation on moderate exertion. He had a baseline electrocardiogram (ECG) at an executive physical at Cleveland Clinic, Cleveland, Ohio (no date given). Under an organization-wide initiative it was uploaded to a cloud platform running the Viz HCM convolutional neural network. The algorithm gave a probability above 0.95 for hypertrophic cardiomyopathy and he was referred to the Hypertrophic Cardiomyopathy Center. Echocardiography showed 2 cm basal septal hypertrophy, systolic anterior motion and a post-exercise outflow gradient of 72 mm Hg. After shared decision-making he started mavacamten 5 mg daily. By week 8 the resting gradient was 9 mm Hg, with less dizziness and dyspnea and no hospitalizations.

Purpose. Viz HCM is a cloud-based AI-ECG algorithm built to flag probable hypertrophic cardiomyopathy from 12-lead ECGs. This diagnosis is its intended function.

AI’s contribution

The AI-ECG probability (>0.95) was the trigger for referral and echocardiography in a patient not previously suspected of HCM. Imaging confirmed the diagnosis and clinicians chose the treatment. Disclosed relationships: Dr Desai is a consultant for and has research agreements with Viz-ai (among other companies). Mrs Rutkowski is a consultant for Viz-ai. Mrs Ospina has none. All three authors are listed under the Cleveland Clinic Hypertrophic Cardiomyopathy Center, with no Viz.ai affiliation.
