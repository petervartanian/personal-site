Viz HCM flags hypertrophic cardiomyopathy in a 61-year-old

depth 3 for a person

AI-ECG (Viz HCM) scored >0.95 for HCM in a symptomatic 61-year-old; echo confirmed obstructive HCM; mavacamten cut gradients and symptoms.

What happened

A 61-year-old man with lightheadedness, dizziness, fatigue and functional limitation on moderate exertion, unaware of any cardiomyopathy, had a baseline ECG as part of an executive physical at Cleveland Clinic, Cleveland, Ohio (the paper gives no date); under an organization-wide initiative the ECG was uploaded to a cloud-based platform running the Viz HCM convolutional neural network. The algorithm returned a probability above 0.95 for hypertrophic cardiomyopathy and he was referred to the Hypertrophic Cardiomyopathy Center. Echocardiography showed 2 cm basal septal hypertrophy, systolic anterior motion and a post-exercise outflow gradient of 72 mm Hg. After shared decision-making he started mavacamten 5 mg daily; by week 8 the resting gradient was 9 mm Hg, with less dizziness and dyspnoea and no hospitalizations.

What it was built to do. Viz HCM is a cloud-based AI-ECG algorithm built to flag probable hypertrophic cardiomyopathy from 12-lead ECGs; this diagnosis is its intended function.

The system's part

The AI-ECG probability (>0.95) was the trigger for referral and echocardiography in a patient not previously suspected of HCM; imaging confirmed the diagnosis and clinicians chose the treatment. Disclosed relationships: Dr Desai is a consultant for and has research agreements with Viz-ai (among other companies); Mrs Rutkowski is a consultant for Viz-ai; Mrs Ospina has none. All three authors are listed under the Cleveland Clinic Hypertrophic Cardiomyopathy Center, with no Viz.ai affiliation.

Replication

No attempt to repeat it has been recorded.

What produced it. Uploading routine 12-lead ECGs to the Viz HCM cloud platform as an institutional initiative. A referral pathway from AI flag to a specialist HCM center.

What must hold again. Echocardiographic confirmation before treatment. Access to mavacamten with REMS monitoring.
