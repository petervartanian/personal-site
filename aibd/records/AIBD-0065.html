What happened

A 61-year-old man, unaware of any cardiomyopathy, had lightheadedness, dizziness, fatigue and functional limitation on moderate exertion. He had a baseline electrocardiogram (ECG) at an executive physical at Cleveland Clinic, Cleveland, Ohio (no date given). Under an organization-wide initiative it was uploaded to a cloud platform running the Viz HCM convolutional neural network. The algorithm gave a probability above 0.95 for hypertrophic cardiomyopathy and he was referred to the Hypertrophic Cardiomyopathy Center. Echocardiography showed 2 cm basal septal hypertrophy, systolic anterior motion and a post-exercise outflow gradient of 72 mm Hg. After shared decision-making he started mavacamten 5 mg daily. By week 8 the resting gradient was 9 mm Hg, with less dizziness and dyspnea and no hospitalizations.

What it was built to do. Viz HCM is a cloud-based AI-ECG algorithm built to flag probable hypertrophic cardiomyopathy from 12-lead ECGs. This diagnosis is its intended function.

The AI-ECG probability (>0.95) was the trigger for referral and echocardiography in a patient not previously suspected of HCM. Imaging confirmed the diagnosis and clinicians chose the treatment. Replication

No attempt to repeat it has been recorded.

What produced it. Uploading routine 12-lead ECGs to the Viz HCM cloud platform as an institutional initiative. A referral pathway from AI flag to a specialist HCM center.

What must hold again. Echocardiographic confirmation before treatment. Access to mavacamten with REMS monitoring.
