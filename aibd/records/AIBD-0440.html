AI agents widen quality checks from 10% to 100% at China Telecom Cloud

China Telecom Cloud reports that CloudTop, built on China Telecom's TeleChat2 and Huize models, raised the coverage of quality inspection from 10% to 100% and cut night shifts by 30%.

What happened

China Telecom Cloud runs CloudTop in production across its service and marketing workflows. The system runs on China Telecom's TeleChat2 and Huize models with DeepSeek. China Telecom Cloud reported that its CloudTop marketing and service solution won a user's preferred brand award at the 23rd China IT User Satisfaction Conference in Beijing on 25 October 2024. In its submission of 2025, China Telecom Cloud reports that the coverage of knowledge compilation and quality inspection rose from 10% under manual work to 100%. It also reports 24% better service efficiency and 30% fewer night shifts. Staff from former service roles moved into data annotation. These operational and workforce figures are the company's own claims. No independent evaluation of the efficiency or night-shift figures had appeared by October 2026. No source measures wider effects on employment equity or economic growth.

Details

AI system

Model

CloudTop combines self-developed speech recognition with TeleChat2, Huize, DeepSeek, and BGE components for language and retrieval tasks.

Reported configuration

TeleChat2 and Huize (China Telecom), DeepSeek, BGE embeddings, and China Telecom Cloud's own speech recognition model

Workflow

The account does not establish conditions sufficient to reproduce the benefit.

Human oversight

The described service workflow combines AI assistance with human staff: speech recognition transcribes, NLP extracts intent, retrieval supplies answers, and generation produces summaries.
