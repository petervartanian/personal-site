What happened

The National Institute for Health and Care Excellence (NICE) assessed ten AI systems that highlight areas of concern for the endoscopist during colonoscopy. Its external assessment group reviewed 70 studies of live colonoscopy, most of them randomized controlled trials that compared AI-assisted colonoscopy with standard colonoscopy, and pooled the adenoma detection rate for each system. The pooled data showed a statistically significant improvement for most systems, and the UK NAIAD study added real-world NHS evidence for one of them. In guidance published on 19 March 2026, the committee concluded that using the AI technologies improves the adenoma detection rate overall. It allowed six systems in the NHS while more evidence is collected: CADDIE, CAD EYE, ENDO-AID, EndoScreener, GI Genius and MAGENTIQ-COLO. The gains were largest for small, non-advanced adenomas. For advanced adenomas and sessile serrated lesions the improvement was not statistically significant for any system, so NICE judged it uncertain whether the higher detection rate will lower colorectal cancer rates. Three systems with fewer trials showed no significant gain. NICE asked for more research on them and on ENDOANGEL, whose cost effectiveness could not be estimated.

Details

AI system

Workflow

The software ran during live colonoscopy and highlighted areas of concern for the endoscopist. Endoscopists made the decisions on what to remove.

Measurement

Measurement

Meta-analysis of the adenoma detection rate for each technology by the external assessment group. [1]

Setting and sample

70 studies of AI-assisted live colonoscopy, mostly randomized controlled trials. [1]

Measures

Adenoma detection rate. [1]

Results

Statistically significant improvement for most technologies; not significant for advanced adenomas or sessile serrated lesions. [1]

Additional value from AI

Higher adenoma detection rate overall. [1]

Limitations

Gains mostly in small non-advanced adenomas; effect on cancer rates uncertain; little data on false positives. [1]

Denominators

Colonoscopies with the software running. [1]

Materials for replication

External assessment group report on the NICE website. [1]

Follow-up

Conditions for replication

The extra detections include clinically significant polyps. Endoscopists keep their own inspection technique while using the software.
