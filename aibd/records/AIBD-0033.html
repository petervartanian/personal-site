Software flags a missing climber's helmet, and rescuers recover his body

Image-analysis software, which its developer TEXSAR identifies as ADIAT, flagged a red helmet in about 2,600 drone frames, and rescuers recovered Nicola Ivaldo's body on Monviso by 31 July 2025.

Workflow

Close-range high-resolution drone photography of steep faces (about 2,600 frames over 183 hectares). A target object (red helmet) with strong color contrast against rock and ice. Search area narrowed by mobile-phone cell data to the Monviso and Visolotto faces.

Relation to AI

The AI flagged a few red pixels, the helmet, a detail CAI describes as invisible to the naked eye. Ten months of helicopters, specialist teams and advanced technology (Quadricottero) had not resolved the search. Drone pilot Saverio Isola is quoted that it was the AI software that identified the differently colored pixels. CAI/CNSAS sources do not name the software. The ADIAT attribution comes from the developer TEXSAR's own page. So the tool identity rests on a vendor-authored source.

Benefit marks

Depth

The number and color show the depth of a benefit.

  1. 1Depth 1. A convenience, or time or money saved.
  2. 2Depth 2. A measurable improvement in a result or ability.
  3. 3Depth 3. A lasting change, an ability restored, or a risk removed.
  4. 4Depth 4. A serious harm averted or a serious condition resolved in time.
  5. 5Depth 5. A life or livelihood preserved, or a change documented across a population.

Beneficiary

  • Circle: a person
  • Square: an organization
  • Triangle: society

Evidence

  • Solid. A court or regulator found a benefit, or researchers independently studied it.

  • Hatched. Independent reporting.

  • Outline. Claimed by the parties. Only the people or organizations involved, or sources whose independence has not been assessed, support the benefit.

The fill changes with the evidence. The depth color stays the same.
