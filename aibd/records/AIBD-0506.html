What happened

Rein4ce Learning (U) Ltd., a company in Uganda, proposes personalized AI for learning, finance, nutrition and health. It describes the framework in its account in the International Telecommunication Union report of 2025. The company is seeking funding for a pilot. The account describes a plan and reports no pilot or result.
