PlantVillage Nuru out-diagnoses extension agents, 65% vs 40–58%

IITA-led evaluation in Kenya and Tanzania: the offline deep-learning app out-diagnosed extension officers (40–58%) and farmers (18–31%); six-leaf assessment raised accuracy to 74–88%.

ⓘ

AI system

PlantVillage Nuru cassava model (deep-learning object detection)

Not assessed

Application developer

PlantVillage, Pennsylvania State University, with FAO, IITA, CIMMYT and others

Role

Nuru detected signs of cassava disease in photographs.

The deployment used PlantVillage Nuru V2.6.0-39.

Not assessed

Workflow

A model trained on 2,756 cassava leaf images. Field plants and a 170-image CaSRAT set scored by consensus of cassava experts. Comparison groups of 30 extension agents and 50 farmers from Busia County.
