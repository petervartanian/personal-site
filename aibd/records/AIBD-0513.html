What happened

China Telecom Gansu Company planned Yi Jiang Jie, an interactive museum guide with contextual explanations and personalized routes. The project's account in the 2025 report of the International Telecommunication Union (ITU), current as of June 2025 names Zhongdian Wanwei Information Technology Co., Ltd. as the submitting organization. It states that the guide uses the DeepSeek-R1 model, and it lists the China Telecom Museum and the Gansu Provincial Museum as test sites. Visitor and institutional outcomes remain unmeasured. The 2026 interim edition of the ITU compendium does not list Yi Jiang Jie, and no later source reports visitor or museum outcomes as of 29 October 2026.

Sources

  1. Report, by the makers.

    Project account in International Telecommunication Union, AI for Good: Innovate for Impact, 2025. Information current as of June 2025. Outcomes are reported by the project team. The account provides no independent outcome evaluation.

  2. News report, by the makers.

    China National Radio (CNR), “翼讲解”，让文化之旅更“动听” (Yi Jiang Jie makes cultural trips sound better), CNR's account on Sohu, 24 January 2025. Gansu Telecom supplied the text and names China Telecom Wanwei Information Technology as the developer. It reports a result from the guide at the Gansu Provincial Museum.

    以甘肃省博物馆为例，使用“翼讲解”后，到馆游客的消费转化率显著提升，讲解服务体验时长平均延长了10%。

  3. Descriptive report, the authors' tie to the system is not established. Context or contrary evidence.

    National Data Administration of China, 2024年"数据要素×"大赛优秀项目案例集--文化旅游案例之六 | 数据要素×文博讲解大模型 (Outstanding projects of the 2024 Data Elements × competition, culture and tourism case 6: the museum guide large model), nda.gov.cn, 8 March 2025. The National Data Administration, which ran the competition, says that the model gives museum visitors tours and answers suited to each of them. It names no site or developer.

    “翼讲解”大模型通过采集多渠道数据，为文博场馆游客提供个性化导览、问题解析等服务。
