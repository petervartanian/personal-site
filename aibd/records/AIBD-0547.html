PlanAI summarizes Greater Cambridge consultation comments in minutes

Greater Cambridge Shared Planning reports that the PlanAI language model cut a consultation-summary task from 18.5 officer hours to 16 minutes while officers still read every response.

What happened

Greater Cambridge Shared Planning reports that a large language model summarizes consultation comments while planning officers still read every submission. The tool was used in three supplementary-planning-document consultations in winter 2024–25. A summary task reportedly fell from 18.5 officer hours to 16 minutes of model run time. The Local Government Association case study also reports savings of over 60% without defining that calculation. The developers report that in 2025 a planner and PlanAI processed the same 320 submissions in parallel. They report that a later quality check found no discernible difference between the summaries. In February 2026 UKAuthority reported that the tool was being tested in five more planning authorities.

Details

AI system

Role

The bespoke language model prepares draft summaries and thematic analysis of planning-consultation submissions within Opus Consult.

Workflow

The model was developed with 15 years of consultation material, including over 55,000 representations and planning-specific terminology. It was soft-launched in three supplementary-planning-document consultations in winter 2024–25.

Human oversight

Planning officers continued to read every consultation submission while the language model prepared draft summaries and thematic analysis. The accounts do not describe a check of every generated summary. The developers report one quality check that found no discernible difference from a planner's summaries, and they have not published its method. [1] [2] [3]

Benefits

Reach

The parallel test covered 320 submissions in three consultations. The sources give no count of the officers who saved time or the residents who took part. [1] [2] [3]

Duration

The tool was used in three consultations in winter 2024–25. The developers ran a parallel test in 2025. In February 2026 UKAuthority reported that the tool was being tested in five more authorities. The report gave no measured results from them. [1] [2] [3]

Measurement

Measurement

Officer hours spent on the same submissions were compared with PlanAI run time; summary quality was compared in a later qualitative assessment. [2]

Setting and sample

Three live public consultations in Greater Cambridge, 320 submissions, 2025. [2]

Measures

Time to summarize each submission; quality of the summaries. [2]

Results

About 18 hours of officer summary work against 16 minutes for PlanAI; no discernible difference in summary quality. [2]

Additional value from AI

The summary step fell from about 18 hours to 16 minutes. The officer's other work on the 320 submissions, about 42 hours, is not reported as reduced. [2]

Limitations

The developers ran and reported the test under a paid contract. One officer served as the comparison. The quality assessors and criteria are not described, and no peer-reviewed report exists. [2]

Denominators

320 submissions across three consultations. [2]

Follow-up

Conditions for replication

The reported workflow retains officer reading of every representation. The source does not establish which data or model conditions are necessary to reproduce the reported saving.
