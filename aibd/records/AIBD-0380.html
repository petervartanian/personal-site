Kunshan police AI flags gambling ring and freezes fraud transfers

Kunshan police say their “AI police” models led a 10 September 2024 bust of a mobile gambling ring (20-plus arrests) and froze 500,000 yuan of a 980,000-yuan telecom fraud within 10 minutes.

ⓘ

What happened

On 10 September 2024, the Kunshan Public Security Bureau in Jiangsu broke up a gang running a mobile open-air casino in Zhouzhuang town. It arrested over 20 suspects. Modern Express reported that the “AI police” had found the ring (NetEase reprint, 6 October 2024). These are models that detect outdoor gambling from behavioral patterns in bulk data. In a telecom fraud handled by the Lujia police station, a victim lost 980,000 yuan. The models traced three tiers of card transfers and froze 500,000 yuan within 10 minutes. They identified 87 suspect accounts within 8 hours. Nine suspects were arrested on the third day with 200,000 yuan recovered (Suzhou Daily via suzhou.gov.cn, 7 October 2024). The models were built by the “Kunpeng Squad”, 35 officers assembled in May 2024.

They report over 220 models and, over six months, 609 fraud cases solved and 32.47 million yuan recovered (both sources). All figures are the police's own.

Purpose. Models built by the Kunshan police to detect crime patterns and trace funds for investigators. The bust and the freeze were that purpose.

AI’s contribution

Both accounts are the police's. They credit the models with flagging the outdoor casino from behavioral features in bulk data and mapping the ring's structure “with one click”. They also traced card-tier fund flows in parallel, so a freeze came within 10 minutes. Officers made the arrests and freeze requests. Deputy criminal-investigation chief He Yongliang describes the gain as 24-hour, parallel rather than serial casework. Neither source separates the models' share from ordinary investigation.

Beneficiaries

  • a person

    The fraud victim who lost 980,000 yuan had 500,000 yuan frozen within 10 minutes and 200,000 yuan recovered after the arrests, per the police. Whether the frozen sum was returned is not reported.

  • an organization

    The Kunshan bureau reports a 20-plus-suspect bust, nine fraud arrests in three days and 609 fraud cases solved in six months, recovery rates up 62%. A measurable gain for the unit, on its own figures.

  • unrated
    society

    The six-month aggregate of 32.47 million yuan recovered is the deployer's own claim with no method or independent check, so no population effect is established.

Further evidence

Setting. Bulk police data on gambling and payment behavior that models can score for anomalies. A dedicated squad of officers who build and run models inside the bureau. Bank cooperation that lets a traced account be frozen within minutes.

Conditions. Officers verify each model flag and make the arrests and freeze requests themselves. Figures are the deployer's own; an outside audit would be needed to confirm them.
