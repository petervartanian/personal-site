Police models in Kunshan flag a gambling ring and trace fraud funds

Kunshan police say that their “AI police” models found a mobile gambling ring, broken up on 10 September 2024, and traced a telecom fraud so that 500,000 yuan was frozen within 10 minutes.

  • 2 a person

    The fraud victim who lost 980,000 yuan had 500,000 yuan frozen within 10 minutes, and 200,000 yuan was recovered after the arrests, according to the police. The sources do not report whether the frozen sum was returned.

  • 2 an organization

    The Kunshan bureau reports a bust of more than 20 suspects, nine fraud arrests on the third day and 609 fraud cases solved in six months, with recovery rates up 62%. The gain for the unit is measurable on the bureau's own figures.

  • 2 society

    The police report that 609 fraud cases were solved and 32.47 million yuan was recovered across Kunshan in six months, a measurable result for fraud victims in the city. No source reports a lasting change across the population.

The system’s part
Both accounts are the police's. They credit the models with flagging the outdoor casino from behavioral features in bulk data and mapping the ring's structure “with one click”. They also traced card-tier fund flows in parallel, so a freeze came within 10 minutes. Officers made the arrests and freeze requests. Deputy criminal-investigation chief He Yongliang describes the gain as 24-hour, parallel rather than serial casework. Neither source separates the models' share from ordinary investigation.
Limits
Frozen funds are not necessarily returned funds. The sources do not say how the 500,000 yuan freeze relates to the 200,000 yuan recovered. The only point of comparison is the police's own estimate that the work would once have taken five or six officers two weeks. No case files, false leads or model specifications separate the models' share from the work of investigators and payment controls. The aggregate results cannot be assigned to these two events.

What happened

The “Kunpeng Squad”, 35 officers assembled in May 2024, built the “AI police” models for the Kunshan Public Security Bureau in Jiangsu. On 10 September 2024, the bureau broke up a gang running a mobile open-air casino in Zhouzhuang town. It arrested over 20 suspects. Modern Express reported that the “AI police” had found the ring (NetEase reprint, 6 October 2024). These are models that detect outdoor gambling from behavioral patterns in bulk data. In a telecom fraud handled by the Lujia police station, a victim lost 980,000 yuan. Suzhou Daily reports that the fraud case came earlier in 2024. The models traced three tiers of card transfers and froze 500,000 yuan within 10 minutes. They identified 87 suspect accounts within 8 hours. Nine suspects were arrested on the third day with 200,000 yuan recovered (Suzhou Daily via suzhou.gov.cn, 7 October 2024). The police say that the same work would once have taken five or six officers two weeks before the first arrests. The squad reports over 220 models and, over six months, 609 fraud cases solved and 32.47 million yuan recovered (both sources). All figures are the police's own.

Details

AI system

Inputs and outputs

Behavioral patterns in large datasets generated suspected-gambling leads, while bank-card transfers were traced to identify connected accounts.

Workflow

Bulk police data on gambling and payment behavior that models can score for anomalies. A dedicated squad of officers who build and run models inside the bureau. Bank cooperation that lets a traced account be frozen within minutes.

Human oversight

Officers made the arrests and the freeze requests. The sources describe no other safeguard. Every figure comes from the police. [1] [2] [3]

Benefits

Reach

One fraud victim, who lost 980,000 yuan, had 500,000 yuan frozen. After the arrests, 200,000 yuan was recovered. The police also claim 609 fraud cases solved and 32.47 million yuan recovered over six months, with no independent check. [1] [2] [3]

Duration

The record reports two cases in 2024 and six-month aggregates from the police. It cites no source after 7 October 2024. [1] [2] [3]

Follow-up

Conditions for replication

Officers verify each model flag and make the arrests and freeze requests themselves. Figures are the deployer's own; an outside audit would be needed to confirm them.

Evidence

Original evidence

Both accounts come from the police: Modern Express, reprinted by NetEase on 6 October 2024, and Suzhou Daily, published on the Suzhou government website on 7 October 2024. The OECD Monitor entry lists Chinese news articles of 6 and 7 October 2024, one of which carries the Modern Express headline. No source checks the figures independently. [1] [2] [3]
