Brief from Peter

Julie Knichel, a 57-year-old nurse, suffered a ruptured brain aneurysm, with symptoms that began on 2 February 2024.1 At Baptist Health's Marcus Neuroscience Institute, according to her surgeon, software flagged the bleeding on her scan and alerted clinicians.2 They sealed the aneurysm with coils delivered through a catheter, and after about three weeks in the hospital she recovered and returned to full-time nursing.3 In a later interview, the surgeon named Viz.ai and described an unnamed intensive care unit nurse whose circumstances match hers, so the identification of the software remains an inference.4 No source isolates what the alert changed for her survival.
