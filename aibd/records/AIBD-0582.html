Doctors found a nurse’s brain bleed with help from AI

What happened

Julie Knichel, a 57-year-old nurse, suffered a ruptured brain aneurysm. At Baptist Health's Marcus Neuroscience Institute, AI reviewed her scan and alerted clinicians to the bleeding. They sealed the aneurysm with coils delivered through a catheter. After about three weeks in hospital, she recovered and returned to full-time nursing. Her symptoms began on 2 February 2024. Baptist Health gives the day and month, and Jan Engoren’s subsequent interviews with Knichel and Snelling establish the year. The surgeon names Viz.ai in a later interview and describes an unnamed ICU nurse whose circumstances match Knichel’s. The product attribution is therefore inferred. Neither account identifies the module or release.

What it was built to do. Detect brain hemorrhage and alert clinicians.

Beneficiaries

  • a person

    Knichel survived a life-threatening hemorrhage and resumed her work and activities.
