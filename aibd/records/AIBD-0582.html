Provider

Viz.ai, inferred from the surgeon’s separate interview.

Configuration

Software analyzed brain images and alerted the treating team before its review, according to the surgeon. [1] [2] [4]

Workflow

Software detected bleeding in brain images and alerted clinicians. The treating team investigated and treated the aneurysm. [1] [2] [3]

Relation to AI

AI flagged the bleeding and notified the stroke team, according to her treating surgeon. Clinicians treated the aneurysm. Viz.ai is an inferred identification from his separate interview. The sources do not isolate the software’s effect on survival. [1] [2] [3]

AI contribution

The surgeon reported that software detected the bleeding and alerted the stroke team. Clinicians treated the aneurysm. The sources do not separately measure the software’s effect on survival. [1] [2] [3]
