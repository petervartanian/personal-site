Zasocitinib clears psoriasis in more patients than deucravacitinib

Takeda reports 36.5% complete skin clearance with zasocitinib versus 13.9% with deucravacitinib at week 16 for a drug that Nimbus designed with Schrödinger's computational platform.

Brief from Peter

In the LATITUDE Atlas phase 3 trial, 36.5% of 301 participants on zasocitinib reached complete skin clearance at week 16, against 13.9% of 303 on deucravacitinib. Takeda reports these figures.1 Takeda also reports that the U.S. Food and Drug Administration has granted its application priority review. No approval had been reported by 29 October 2026.2 In a Cochrane meta-analysis published on 6 August 2025, Sbidian and colleagues included an earlier phase 2b trial of zasocitinib and rated the certainty of evidence as low for most comparisons that involve the drug. They declare no ties to Takeda or Nimbus.3 The attribution to AI is weaker. Nimbus and Schrödinger developed zasocitinib with computational structure-based design, and Schrödinger describes free-energy calculations and virtual screening. A 2025 Drug Target Review interview with a Takeda scientist describes the approach as AI-assisted.4 None of these accounts isolates the effect of AI from the rest of the discovery work. Takeda's upfront payment of approximately $4 billion for the program, announced when the acquisition closed in 2023, is no measure of that effect.5

Benefit marks

Depth

The number and color show the depth of a benefit.

  1. 1Depth 1. A convenience, or time or money saved.
  2. 2Depth 2. A measurable improvement in a result or ability.
  3. 3Depth 3. A lasting change, an ability restored, or a risk removed.
  4. 4Depth 4. A serious harm averted or a serious condition resolved in time.
  5. 5Depth 5. A life or livelihood preserved, or a change documented across a population.

Beneficiary

  • Circle: a person
  • Square: an organization
  • Triangle: society

Evidence

  • Solid. A court or regulator found a benefit, or researchers independently studied it.

  • Hatched. Independent reporting.

  • Outline. Claimed by the parties. Only the people or organizations involved, or sources whose independence has not been assessed, support the benefit.

The fill changes with the evidence. The depth color stays the same.
