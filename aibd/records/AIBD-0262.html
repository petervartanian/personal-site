Nimbus's zasocitinib beats deucravacitinib in phase 3 trial

Takeda reports 36.5% complete skin clearance with zasocitinib versus 13.9% with deucravacitinib at week 16. The contribution of AI to discovery is not separately measured.

ⓘ

What happened

Takeda reported that zasocitinib produced complete skin clearance in 36.5% of its LATITUDE Atlas group, versus 13.9% with deucravacitinib, at week 16. The September 30, 2026 announcement gives analysis groups of 301 and 303 participants. The sponsor’s registration lists 606 enrolled participants and has no posted results. The earlier Nimbus-funded phase 2b trial treated 259 participants and found a dose response against placebo, including complete clearance in 33% at the 30 mg dose. Nimbus and Schrödinger developed the molecule with computational structure-based design. Schrödinger describes free-energy calculations and virtual screening, while a Takeda scientist separately describes the approach as AI-assisted. These accounts do not isolate the effect of AI from the wider discovery process.

Takeda reported that the FDA granted priority review and that the EMA accepted its application in September 2026. These were application milestones, not approvals. Takeda’s February 2023 closing announcement states an approximately $4 billion upfront acquisition payment, with additional contingent milestones. That transaction is not a valuation of the benefit attributable to AI.

Purpose. Design a selective oral allosteric TYK2 inhibitor for plaque psoriasis and other immune-mediated diseases

AI’s contribution

The original developer accounts describe computational structure-based design, free-energy calculations, and virtual screening. Takeda’s Warren Winkelman calls the approach AI-assisted. Physics-based computation alone does not establish an AI contribution. The discovery paper and developer accounts do not provide a controlled estimate of AI’s contribution to clinical efficacy, discovery speed, or the acquisition price.

Beneficiaries

  • a person

    Trial participants achieved complete skin clearance at higher rates. PASI 100 was 36.5% vs 13.9% at week 16 in LATITUDE Atlas, and 33% at 30 mg in phase 2b.

  • an organization

    Takeda reported acceptance of its application for FDA priority review in September 2026 after acquiring the program for approximately $4 billion upfront. Application acceptance is not regulatory confirmation of efficacy or an estimate of AI-created value.

  • unrated
    society

    No approval or population-level outcome as of 20 October 2026.
