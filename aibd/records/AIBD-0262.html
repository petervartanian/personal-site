What happened

The earlier reviewer (20 October 2026) found only phase 2 reports in Europe PMC. Google News RSS surfaced the 2026 phase 3 record: HCPLive (30 September 2026, EADV) reported LATITUDE Atlas (NCT06973291), 606 adults randomized 301/303, PASI 100 36.5% vs 13.9%, PASI 90 62.5% vs 34.0% and sPGA 0 43.2% vs 18.5% at week 16 for zasocitinib versus deucravacitinib (p<.001), presented by Linda Stein Gold; pharmaphorum (15 September 2026) reported FDA acceptance of the marketing application under priority review with a decision expected mid-March 2027. The phase 2b trial (Armstrong et al., JAMA Dermatology 2024; 259 treated patients) reached PASI 75 in 67–68% at 15–30 mg versus 6% on placebo and was funded by Nimbus Discovery, with Nimbus and Takeda affiliations among the authors.

The discovery claim comes from the developers' side: Takeda's dermatology medical head told Drug Target Review (26 September 2025) that Nimbus chemists “used an AI-assisted approach of computationally guided structure-based drug design”; GEN (18 August 2023) reported a Nimbus-Schrodinger paper on “computational physics-based predictions” in identifying NDI-034858; BioPharma Dive (13 December 2022) reported Takeda's $4 billion upfront purchase. No regulatory approval had been granted as of 20 October 2026. The date is the EADV presentation reported by HCPLive.

What it was built to do. Design a selective oral allosteric TYK2 inhibitor for plaque psoriasis and other immune-mediated diseases

The system's part

Only developer-side accounts of the discovery method were read (Takeda's medical head in Drug Target Review; GEN citing a Nimbus-Schrodinger paper); they describe AI-assisted, physics-based structure-based design, not a pure ML discovery. The clinical results come from a Nimbus-funded RCT and Takeda-presented phase 3 data reported by HCPLive and pharmaphorum; no independent account of the computational platform's contribution was found.

Replication

What produced it. Physics-based free-energy and machine-learning modeling to design a selective allosteric TYK2 inhibitor. Company-funded randomized trials against placebo and against deucravacitinib.

What must hold again. Phase 3 efficacy and safety confirmed in regulatory review. Independent documentation of the computational platform's contribution to the molecule.
