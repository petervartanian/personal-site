What happened

World Bank Policy Research Working Paper 11125 (May 2025), by De Simone, Tiberti, Barron Rodriguez, Manolio, Mosuro and Dikoru, evaluates a six-week after-school program run between June and July 2024 in nine public schools in Benin City, Nigeria, chosen for their computer labs. First-year senior secondary students, typically 15 years old, who expressed interest were randomized: 657 to treatment and 671 to control, of whom 422 and 337 completed the final assessment. Treated students attended up to two 90-minute sessions a week (twelve in total) in which they used Microsoft Copilot, powered by GPT-4 at the time, as a virtual English tutor in curriculum-aligned activities guided by teachers.

The paper reports a 0.31 standard deviation improvement on an assessment covering English, AI knowledge and digital skills, 0.23 SD on English, gains equated to 1.5 to 2 years of business-as-usual schooling, and larger effects for female students and those with higher initial performance. The World Bank blog of 9 January 2025 describes the gain as about 0.3 SD. The date observed is the first day of the program's first month, used as a placeholder; the paper gives months, not days.

What it was built to do. Copilot was used as a virtual tutor to improve English learning outcomes; the measured gain is that purpose.

The system's part

Copilot was the tutor inside teacher-guided sessions, so the estimate is of the whole after-school program (twelve 90-minute sessions); the LLM's contribution is not isolated from teacher guidance and extra instructional time.

Replication

No attempt to repeat it has been recorded.

What produced it. A freely available LLM (Copilot on GPT-4) requiring only student registration. Curriculum-aligned prompts and activities guided by teachers in school computer labs. Student-level randomization among volunteers in nine schools with computer labs.

What must hold again. Working computer labs and internet access. Teacher guidance in every session. Attendance (the paper reports gains rising with days attended).
