Microsoft Copilot tutoring lifts Benin City scores by 0.31 SD

World Bank Policy Research Working Paper 11125: teacher-guided after-school Copilot (GPT-4) sessions, June-July 2024, Benin City; 0.31 SD overall, 0.23 SD in English; 657 treated, 671 control.

ⓘ

What happened

World Bank Policy Research Working Paper 11125 (May 2025) is by De Simone, Tiberti, Barron Rodriguez, Manolio, Mosuro and Dikoru. It evaluates a six-week after-school program run in June and July 2024 in nine public schools in Benin City, Nigeria, chosen for their computer labs. First-year senior secondary students, typically 15 years old, who expressed interest were randomized: 657 to treatment and 671 to control. Of these, 422 and 337 finished the final assessment. Treated students attended up to two 90-minute sessions a week (twelve in total). They used Microsoft Copilot, powered by GPT-4 at the time, as a virtual English tutor in curriculum-aligned activities guided by teachers. The paper reports a 0.31 standard deviation improvement on an assessment covering English, AI knowledge and digital skills, and 0.23 SD on English.

It equates the gains to 1.5 to 2 years of business-as-usual schooling. Effects were larger for female students and those with higher initial performance. The World Bank blog of 9 January 2025 describes the gain as about 0.3 SD. The paper gives months, not days.

Purpose. Copilot was used as a virtual tutor to improve English learning outcomes. The measured gain is that purpose.

AI’s contribution

Copilot tutored in teacher-guided sessions, so the estimate covers the whole after-school program (twelve 90-minute sessions). The large language model (LLM) share is not isolated from teacher guidance and extra teaching time.

Further evidence

Setting. A freely available LLM (Copilot on GPT-4) requiring only student registration. Curriculum-aligned prompts and activities guided by teachers in school computer labs. Student-level randomization among volunteers in nine schools with computer labs.

Conditions. Working computer labs and internet access. Teacher guidance in every session. Attendance (the paper reports gains rising with days attended).
