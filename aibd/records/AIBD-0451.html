IIT Hyderabad is implementing federated anomaly detection using network emulators, aiming to improve 5G security and service resilience. The entry supplies no measured detection latency, verified attack interception, or avoided service disruption. It proposes later emulator validation and additional protections against poisoned updates. Data isolation and federated architecture alone do not prove privacy, robustness, or prevention of cybercrime. The account does not isolate AI’s contribution from the surrounding workflow.
