What happened

SERA proposes a Smart Budget service that analyzes financial transactions and uses language and predictive models to personalize budgets, identify spending reductions, and suggest localized income opportunities. Its model plan includes fine-tuning and feedback on financial guidance. Changes in users’ finances attributable to the AI are unmeasured. A separate debt-offset method moves available funds against credit-card balances, without an identified AI contribution. In the ITU report published on 29 October 2025, SERA reports that it fine-tunes its models on data from early users and simulated scenarios, and that it was in talks with United for ALICE, Stand Together and Operation HOPE. Its website charges 10% of what SERA helps a user save or $10 a month, whichever is higher.
