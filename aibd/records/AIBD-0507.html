Dubai's Smart School Finder answers parents' questions

Dubai’s Smart School Finder pilot answers parents’ plain-language questions with KHDA school data; the makers say that it saves time and report no measured saving.

  • 1 a person

    The pilot provides natural-language search across current school data, which the source describes as saving parents time. Better school or educational outcomes are not established.

  • Not rated an organization

    Better educational planning is intended; no agency-level result is reported.

What happened

The ITU account names the Dubai Digital Authority and the Dubai Digital Government Establishment as the submitting organizations, with KHDA as partner, in the United Arab Emirates. The organizations describe an initial pilot inside the KHDA portal and the Dubai Now app. The pilot lets parents search KHDA school data in natural language. The organizations state that the system returns up-to-date school details and saves time. They report no measured time saving. Educational improvements are unmeasured. They state that the use case is part of a larger product in development.
