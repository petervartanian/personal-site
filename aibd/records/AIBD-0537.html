What happened

In June 2025 the CSIR-INSTI team described a TinyML weather-monitoring prototype that supports a proposed farm-advisory system for local farmers. The team said that it planned further development beyond the experimental phase. It did not report any effect on yield, income or resource use. The team later ran a 15-week field trial of a low-cost soil sensor at Sumbrungu Experimental Farm in Bolgatanga, Ghana, from 14 March to 26 June 2026, under the LSACROFT project. It published the trial data on 22 September 2026. The release reports sensor and crop measurements and no gain for farmers.

Details

AI system

Configuration

A TinyML weather-monitoring prototype is being developed as part of a local soil-analysis and farm-advisory system.

Inputs and outputs

The developing station collects rainfall, weather, and soil measurements for local forecasts and crop recommendations, with lightweight models intended to run on the sensor device.

Workflow

Conditions that could produce the benefit are unspecified.

Benefits

Duration

The work continued after the June 2025 account, with a 15-week field trial of a low-cost soil sensor at Sumbrungu Experimental Farm from 14 March to 26 June 2026. The trial release reports measurements only, so no benefit to farmers is shown to persist. [1] [2]
