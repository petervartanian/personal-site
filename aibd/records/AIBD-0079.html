RapidAI LVO alert gets Eric's artery reopened within 60 minutes

depth 3 for a person, depth 2 for an organization

thrombectomy restored flow under 60 minutes from arrival; no deficits

RapidAI's automated LVO alert mobilized the stroke team for Eric, 29; thrombectomy restored flow under 60 minutes from arrival; no deficits.

What happened

Eric, a 29-year-old firefighter and paramedic, felt unwell after a workout: he tried to send a text and could not move his left thumb, then his left leg went numb, and his trainer noticed that he was slurring and called 9-1-1. Emergency medical services took him to Memorial Hospital West's Comprehensive Stroke Center; under the Memorial stroke protocol the hospital had already been alerted by the paramedics and the neurointerventional surgery team was ready to perform a CT scan and treat in the catheterisation laboratory. As soon as the scan results were available Dr Brijesh Mehta received the RapidAI alert on his smartphone and smartwatch, showing the software's automated detection of a large vessel occlusion, which prompted him to mobilize the cath lab team and fast-track Eric for mechanical thrombectomy. The blocked artery was fully opened and blood flow restored in under 60 minutes from his arrival, and Eric described himself as alive with no deficits. The hospital published the account on 12 May 2022; the stroke date and the hospital's city are not given.

What it was built to do. RapidAI's platform is built to detect large vessel occlusion on imaging and push alerts to the stroke team so that thrombectomy can start sooner; this is its intended function.

The system's part

RapidAI's automated LVO detection delivered the alert that prompted Dr Mehta to mobilize the cath lab team and fast-track thrombectomy. Under the Memorial stroke protocol EMS had already pre-notified the hospital and the neurointerventional team was ready before imaging, and the occlusion would have been identified on the CT scan regardless, so the AI's contribution is speed at the point of care and its share of the time saved cannot be separated from the pre-notification. The only counterfactual offered is Dr Mehta's general description of the pre-RapidAI workflow.

Replication

No attempt to repeat it has been recorded.

What produced it. RapidAI LVO detection pushing alerts to the on-call neurointerventionalist's phone and watch. A comprehensive stroke center with an in-house thrombectomy team.

What must hold again. Rapid recognition of stroke signs and EMS transport to a thrombectomy-capable center. Team mobilization on the AI alert rather than after a formal radiology read.
