RapidAI LVO alert gets Eric's artery reopened within 60 minutes

RapidAI's automated LVO alert mobilized the stroke team for Eric, 29; thrombectomy restored flow under 60 minutes from arrival; no deficits.

ⓘ

What happened

Eric, a 29-year-old firefighter and paramedic, felt unwell after a workout. He tried to send a text and could not move his left thumb, then his left leg went numb. His trainer noticed he was slurring and called 9-1-1. Emergency medical services (EMS) took him to Memorial Hospital West's Comprehensive Stroke Center. Under the Memorial stroke protocol the paramedics had already alerted the hospital. The neurointerventional surgery team was ready to do a computed tomography (CT) scan and treat in the catheterization laboratory. As soon as scan results were available, Dr Brijesh Mehta received the RapidAI alert on his smartphone and smartwatch. It showed the software's automated detection of a large vessel occlusion (LVO). That prompted him to mobilize the cath lab team and fast-track Eric for mechanical thrombectomy.

The blocked artery was fully opened and blood flow restored under 60 minutes from his arrival. Eric described himself as alive with no deficits. The hospital published the account on 12 May 2022; the stroke date and its city are not given.

Purpose. RapidAI's platform is built to detect large vessel occlusion on imaging and push alerts to the stroke team so that thrombectomy can start sooner. This is its intended function.

AI’s contribution

RapidAI's automated LVO detection delivered the alert that prompted Dr Mehta to mobilize the cath lab team and fast-track thrombectomy. Under the Memorial stroke protocol EMS had already pre-notified the hospital and the neurointerventional team was ready before imaging. The occlusion would have been identified on the CT scan regardless. So the AI's contribution is speed at the point of care, and its share of the time saved cannot be separated from the pre-notification. The only counterfactual offered is Dr Mehta's general description of the pre-RapidAI workflow.
