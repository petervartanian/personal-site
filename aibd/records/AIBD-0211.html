o3-assisted reanalysis yields 11 new diagnoses and seven rediscoveries

AI-assisted reanalysis of 376 locally unresolved cases yielded 18 confirmed diagnoses. Eleven were new, while seven had already been established elsewhere.

ⓘ

AI system

System

OpenAI o3 Deep Research, used as the reasoning layer of an LLM-assisted genome reanalysis workflow (Human Phenotype Ontology terms, clinician notes and a filtered variant table in; explanation-linked candidate diagnoses out)

Model

Not assessed

Application developer

OpenAI

Role

The model proposed molecular explanations from clinical information and genetic variants.

Configuration

Researchers used o3 Deep Research to analyze de-identified clinical information and genetic variants.

Inputs and outputs

The model received clinical notes, phenotype terms, and filtered genetic variants. It proposed a molecular diagnosis with supporting evidence.

Autonomy

Not assessed

Workflow

Researchers gave the model de-identified symptoms, clinical notes, and genetic variants that earlier analyses had not resolved. They refined the prompt on known diagnoses, then asked for explanations of unresolved cases. At least two experts reviewed each candidate, and a certified laboratory confirmed diagnoses before clinicians returned results to families.

Human oversight

Not assessed

Relation to AI

o3 Deep Research read each de-identified case packet and proposed the most plausible molecular explanation with its supporting evidence. It diagnosed no one. Humans did the rest. At least two reviewers adjudicated each candidate under ACMG/AMP criteria, a CLIA-certified laboratory confirmed each variant, and the clinical team returned results to families. Its distinct contribution was surfacing candidates in cases that commercial and institutional analyses and multidisciplinary teams had already reviewed. In one early-psychosis case it inferred a 22q11.2 deletion absent from the input data, which genome sequencing then confirmed. Seven of the 18 were rediscoveries already established externally. The study was retrospective, and reviewers were not blinded to model confidence. The authors call for prospective evaluation with comparator baselines, so the gain over renewed human reanalysis is unmeasured.

Measurement

Setting and sample

A retrospective reanalysis of 376 rare-disease records at Boston Children’s Hospital, mostly involving a child and both parents.

Comparison

Results

Reference: 376 cases unresolved in the local research record, including some diagnosed elsewhere. Result: 18 confirmed local diagnoses: 11 new and seven rediscoveries; 4.8% overall.

Limitations

There was no simultaneous expert reanalysis using the same updated evidence. Some findings relied on later gene discoveries or revised variant classifications. The study did not measure treatment improvements, savings, or clinician time, and involved OpenAI employees and funding.

Field

Clinical genomics / rare disease diagnosis. Hospital rare-disease research center; retrospective reanalysis study.

Purpose

OpenAI o3 Deep Research is a general-purpose reasoning and research model, not a diagnostic device. The Manton Center team built an explanation-first reanalysis workflow around it to propose candidate molecular diagnoses for unsolved cases. These diagnoses were the workflow's intended output. OpenAI states that the study does not describe or endorse a customer use of o3 for diagnosis. Brownstein said that she was shocked that a commercial system could find new diagnoses in genomes analyzed many times.
