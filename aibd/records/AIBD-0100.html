PROMPTS raises knowledge and care-seeking in Kenyan cluster RCT

raised knowledge and care-seeking indices by 0.06–0.09 SD

Kenyan cluster RCT (Nov 2021–Aug 2022): PROMPTS with NLP-triaged helpdesk raised knowledge and care-seeking indices by 0.06–0.09 SD

What happened

PROMPTS is an SMS platform for pregnant and postpartum women in Kenya run by Jacaranda Health, combining informational messages, appointment reminders and a two-way clinical helpdesk in which a natural language processing pipeline assigns each incoming message an intent and priority level, after which roughly 86% receive automated guidance and the rest are escalated to clinical agents. In an unmasked cluster randomised trial across 40 health facilities in eight counties, 6,139 pregnant women enrolled between 8 November and 10 December 2021 and were followed to 24 August 2022. Treatment-facility participants had higher knowledge (0.08 SD), birth preparedness (0.08 SD), routine care-seeking (0.07 SD), newborn care (0.09 SD) and postpartum care content (0.06 SD) indices; danger-sign care-seeking did not differ significantly. Effects are for the whole platform, not the AI component alone.

What it was built to do. Deliver pregnancy and postpartum information by SMS and triage incoming questions to a clinical helpdesk by intent and priority.

The system's part

NLP triage decided the priority of helpdesk messages, but the trial randomised the whole platform, so the AI's separate contribution to the measured effects is not identified. The trial was led by Harvard researchers (the first author and the two joint senior authors) with Innovations for Poverty Action researchers (data curation, investigation, supervision) and five Jacaranda Health employees among the co-authors; the USAID DIV and Gates Foundation grants were awarded to Rajasekharan and the Jacaranda Health team.

Replication

No attempt to repeat it has been recorded.

What produced it

  • SMS message schedule plus NLP-triaged two-way helpdesk
  • Facility-based enrolment of pregnant women
  • County health system partnership

What must hold again

  • Mobile-phone ownership among enrolled women
  • Helpdesk staffing to answer prioritised messages
  • Facility participation in enrolment
