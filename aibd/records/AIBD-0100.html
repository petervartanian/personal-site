What happened

PROMPTS is an SMS platform for pregnant and postpartum women in Kenya run by Jacaranda Health. It combines informational messages, appointment reminders and a two-way clinical helpdesk. In the helpdesk, natural language processing (NLP) assigns each message an intent and priority level. Roughly 86% then get automated guidance; the rest are escalated to clinical agents. In an unmasked cluster randomized trial across 40 health facilities in eight counties, 6,139 pregnant women enrolled between 8 November and 10 December 2021. Follow-up ran to 24 August 2022. Treatment-facility participants had higher knowledge (0.08 SD), birth preparedness (0.08 SD) and routine care-seeking (0.07 SD) indices. Also higher: newborn care (0.09 SD) and postpartum care content (0.06 SD). Danger-sign care-seeking did not differ significantly. Effects are for the whole platform, not the AI component alone.

What it was built to do. Deliver pregnancy and postpartum information by SMS and triage incoming questions to a clinical helpdesk by intent and priority.

Since then
Scaled on 30 September 2025: By the third quarter of 2025 Jacaranda Health reported 3,580,095 mothers reached cumulatively through PROMPTS across 23 Kenyan counties and 1,500 facilities, with over 200,000 added in the quarter and Ghana signing up for national scale-up; the trial paper itself had already noted over 2 million users added after November 2021.In Q3 alone, we reached over 200,000 additional mothers, the highest of any quarter to-date (p2), and received the green light in Ghana to scale nationally (p5). source

The system's part

NLP triage decided the priority of helpdesk messages. The trial randomized the whole platform, so the AI's separate contribution to the measured effects is not identified. The trial was led by Harvard researchers (the first author and the two joint senior authors). Innovations for Poverty Action researchers (data curation, investigation, supervision) and five Jacaranda Health employees were among the co-authors. The USAID DIV and Gates Foundation grants were awarded to Rajasekharan and the Jacaranda Health team.

Replication

No attempt to repeat it has been recorded.

What produced it. SMS message schedule plus NLP-triaged two-way helpdesk. Facility-based enrollment of pregnant women. County health system partnership.

What must hold again. Mobile-phone ownership among enrolled women. Helpdesk staffing to answer prioritized messages. Facility participation in enrollment.
