German tests check IdentiFlight cameras that detect eagles near turbines

IdentiFlight V5 classified lesser spotted eagles correctly in 97.8% of checked images at a German test site in 2025; no German source measures a drop in bird deaths.

The system’s part
The camera software detects and classifies birds and sends the shutdown signal. Validation reports measure that step against ornithologists' observations, with 97.8% correct classification of lesser spotted eagles in 2025. No German source measures whether the shutdowns reduced bird deaths.
Limits
No German source counts bird deaths before and after installation, so no reduction in fatalities is measured. The authors of the 2026 evaluation measure detection, classification and shutdown signals. The Bavarian state environment office reported 31 dangerous red kite flights and no observed collisions at one forest site. The 82% Wyoming estimate cannot show protection of German lesser spotted eagles. Two U.S. Geological Survey scientists disputed that estimate in 2023.

What happened

OekoFor and ARSU compare camera detections with observations by ornithologists at German test sites. OekoFor's dated updates report that the red-kite validation was completed in October 2021. The OECD AI Incidents entry summarizes press coverage of 20 and 21 September 2022 (The Guardian, Mother Jones, The Register and others, not among the sources). It says that Germany is deploying AI-powered camera systems developed by IdentiFlight to detect endangered lesser spotted eagles near wind turbines and slow the blades to prevent collisions. The monitor's rationale cites the 82% reduction reported from the Wyoming study. It cites no German measurement. OekoFor's updates report the publication of a white-tailed eagle assessment in May 2023. These updates give no estimate of any reduction in bird deaths in Germany. They also give no start date for the lesser spotted eagle tests. Two U.S. Geological Survey scientists argued in 2023 that the Wyoming data show little evidence of fewer eagle deaths. The Bavarian state environment office reported 31 dangerous red kite flights through rotors at a forest site since spring 2024 and no observed collisions. On 27 August 2026, OekoFor and ARSU reported for Boulder Imaging that IdentiFlight V5 classified lesser spotted eagles correctly in 97.8% of checked images at Kublank in 2025. On 27 October 2026, the competence center KNE (Kompetenzzentrum Naturschutz und Energiewende) listed basic recognition of V5 for red kite, black kite, white-tailed eagle and lesser spotted eagle classification.

Details

AI system

Inputs and outputs

IdentiFlight camera systems were used at German test sites to detect and classify birds near wind turbines for potential curtailment.

Workflow

Wind-farm expansion into lesser spotted eagle habitat creating a regulatory need for mitigation. Availability of the IdentiFlight system already evaluated in the United States.

Human oversight

OekoFor and ARSU supervised validation, comparing system detections with reference observations made by ornithologists.

Benefits

Duration

On 27 October 2026, the competence center KNE listed basic recognition for IdentiFlight V5 for red kite, black kite and white-tailed eagle and for lesser spotted eagle classification. Recognition under the stricter Schleswig-Holstein test framework is still in progress. No German source measures a change in bird deaths over time. [1] [2] [3] [4] [5] [6]

Measurement

Measurement

Validation against KNE criteria: system tracks compared with ornithologists' laser range finder tracks and manually checked images. [4]

Setting and sample

Three test sites (Arpsdorf, Bendorf, Kublank) in Schleswig-Holstein and Mecklenburg-Vorpommern in 2025; lesser spotted eagles occurred only at Kublank. [4]

Measures

Classification rate per species; share of inner-zone entries with an active shutdown signal. [4]

Results

Correct classification of 99.7% to 99.9% for red kite, black kite and white-tailed eagle and 97.8% for lesser spotted eagle; shutdown signal active in 90.5% of 1,054 inner-zone entries, 99.4% after 1.5 seconds. [4]

Additional value from AI

Not measured as fewer deaths; the study measures detection and signaling only. [4]

Limitations

Commissioned by the maker; turbines at Kublank were virtual; for lesser spotted eagle only the classification rate was tested. [4]

Denominators

Test sites in 2025, including virtual turbines. [4]

Materials for replication

Methods and site descriptions are in the report; raw data are not published. [4]

Follow-up

Conditions for replication

Site-specific monitoring of eagle fatalities before and after installation. Operators accepting curtailment losses.
