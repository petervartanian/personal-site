510's ML forecast triggers PRC shelter kits before Storm Nalgae

510 ML impact forecast hit the trigger on 27 October 2022; PRC installed shelter kits for 78 Aurora households before Storm Nalgae

ⓘ

What happened

The Philippine Red Cross (PRC) approved its Early Action Protocol (EAP) for typhoons in December 2019. Its impact-based forecasting (IBF) model, developed with 510 (Netherlands Red Cross), applies machine learning (ML) to predict housing damage per municipality from typhoon forecasts. As Tropical Storm Paeng (Nalgae) approached on 27 October 2022, the 510/IBF forecast projected up to 10 percent shelter damage in priority municipalities. That hit the trigger at 56 hours' lead time. With IFRC anticipatory DREF funds, PRC activated it in Aurora Province. On 27 to 29 October, 40 workers distributed and installed shelter strengthening kits for 78 of 100 planned households in Dipaculao before landfall. PRC's post-storm survey reports all 78 kits installed, 390 people reached. CHF 7,002 of the CHF 117,555 budget was spent.

Purpose. Forecast typhoon housing damage per municipality to trigger pre-agreed early actions before landfall.

AI’s contribution

The ML impact forecast was the trigger. Under the EAP, early actions fire on the forecast wind impact on housing 72 hours before landfall. The trigger: at least 10% of houses totally damaged in more than three municipalities. The notification records the Aurora municipalities as “Met (More than 10%)”. The 72-hour lead-time condition was not met. The final report says the 56-hour lead time was exceptionally granted. The notification says the projection came from the 510/IBF forecast incorporating PAGASA data. PRC staff confirmed chapter capacity and chose municipalities. The outcome claim is PRC's own post-storm survey. The NHESS paper and EGU abstract on the model have 510 staff as co-authors, not lead authors.

Beneficiaries

  • a person

    78 households had roofs and walls reinforced before landfall. PRC reports they mitigated wind impact (implementer survey, no counterfactual).

  • an organization

    First completed activation of the Philippine typhoon EAP. Lessons on lead time and weather constraints documented in the final report.

  • society

    Small scale: 390 people reached in one municipality.

Since then
The IFRC final report of 30 June 2023 records all 78 households served and houses reinforced before impact, but a PRC lessons-learned workshop judged the trigger high and recommended reviewing the typhoon EAP.In addition, the trigger was considered high according to the solicited lessons from Paeng Lesson learned Workshop conducted by PRC with the support IFRC. Thus, one of the recommendations gathered is to review and revisit the Typhoon EAP. source

Further evidence

Setting. 510 IBF damage model fed by PAGASA typhoon forecasts. Pre-approved EAP with a 10% projected-damage trigger. IFRC anticipatory DREF funds released on trigger. Pre-positioned shelter kits, trained carpenters and pre-identified households.

Conditions. Lead time near 72 hours. Forecast track and intensity accuracy. Weather permitting installation before landfall. Local government authorization in no-build zones.
