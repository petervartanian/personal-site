Damage forecast leads the Philippine Red Cross to reinforce 78 homes

A machine-learning damage forecast from 510 met the early-action trigger on 27 October 2022, and the Philippine Red Cross installed shelter kits for 78 Aurora households before Storm Nalgae.

  • 2 a person

    The Philippine Red Cross reports that 78 households had their roofs and walls reinforced before landfall and that the kits mitigated the wind's impact. The claim rests on its own post-storm survey, which has no comparison group.

  • 1 an organization

    This was the first completed activation of the Philippine typhoon protocol. The final report documents lessons on lead time and weather constraints.

  • Not rated society

    The 390 people reached are the households counted under the personal rating, and no source reports an outcome beyond them.

The ML impact forecast was the trigger. Under the EAP, early actions fire on the forecast wind impact on housing 72 hours before landfall. The trigger: at least 10% of houses totally damaged in more than three municipalities. The notification records the Aurora municipalities as “Met (More than 10%)”. What happened

The Philippine Red Cross approved its Early Action Protocol for typhoons in December 2019. Its impact-based forecasting model, developed with 510, an initiative of the Netherlands Red Cross, applies machine learning to predict housing damage per municipality from typhoon forecasts. In 2021 the 510 model also became the trigger model for the anticipatory action pilot of the UN Office for the Coordination of Humanitarian Affairs (OCHA) in the Philippines. The Central Emergency Response Fund funded the OCHA pilot with $7.5 million for the 2023 typhoon season. Under the protocol, early actions start when the forecast shows at least 10 percent of houses totally damaged in more than three municipalities 72 hours before landfall. As Tropical Storm Paeng (Nalgae) approached on 27 October 2022, the 510 forecast projected up to 10 percent shelter damage in priority municipalities. That met the trigger at 56 hours' lead time. The final report says that the 56-hour lead time was exceptionally granted. With anticipatory funds from the Disaster Response Emergency Fund of the International Federation of Red Cross and Red Crescent Societies, the Philippine Red Cross activated the protocol in Aurora Province. From 27 to 29 October, 40 workers distributed and installed shelter strengthening kits for 78 of 100 planned households in Dipaculao before landfall. The final report says that strong winds and the onset of rain limited the distribution to 78 households. The Philippine Red Cross's post-storm survey reports that all 78 kits were installed and that 390 people were reached. The operation spent CHF 7,002 of its CHF 117,555 budget. The final report of 30 June 2023 records that a lessons-learned workshop judged the trigger high and recommended a review of the protocol. The Philippine Red Cross renewed its typhoon protocol and first activated the renewed protocol in November 2025, when it installed shelter strengthening kits in Quirino, Isabela and Quezon-Lucena. The annual report on that activation does not name the trigger model.

Details

AI system

Model

XGBoost.

Role

The model forecast damage to housing before a typhoon.

Inputs and outputs

The impact model combined PAGASA typhoon forecasts with housing-damage prediction to identify municipalities expected to cross the early-action threshold.

Workflow

510 IBF damage model fed by PAGASA typhoon forecasts. Pre-approved EAP with a 10% projected-damage trigger. IFRC anticipatory DREF funds released on trigger. Pre-positioned shelter kits, trained carpenters and pre-identified households.

Human oversight

PRC staff confirmed chapter capacity, chose municipalities, and organized workers to install strengthening kits under the preapproved protocol.

AI contribution

The 510 model forecast that damage to housing would exceed the threshold for early action in Aurora. Its forecasts incorporated data from PAGASA.

Benefits

Reach

The operation installed kits at 78 households, reaching 390 people according to the post-storm survey.

Duration

Workers distributed and installed the kits from 27 to 29 October 2022 before landfall. A later lessons-learned workshop judged the trigger high and recommended a review of the protocol. The Philippine Red Cross renewed its typhoon protocol and first activated it in November 2025. The annual report on that activation does not name the trigger model. [1] [2] [3] [4]
