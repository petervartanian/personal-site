What happened

The Philippine Red Cross's Early Action Protocol for typhoons, approved in December 2019, uses an impact-based forecasting model developed with 510 (Netherlands Red Cross), which applies machine learning to predict housing damage per municipality from typhoon forecasts. On 27 October 2022, as Tropical Storm Paeng (Nalgae) approached, the 510/IBF forecast projected shelter damage up to 10 percent in priority municipalities, reaching the trigger with 56 hours' lead time. Using IFRC anticipatory DREF funds, PRC activated the protocol in Aurora Province; between 27 and 29 October, 40 workers distributed and installed shelter strengthening kits for 78 of 100 planned households in Dipaculao before landfall. PRC's post-storm survey reports all 78 kits installed and 390 people reached; CHF 7,002 of the CHF 117,555 budget was spent.

What it was built to do. Forecast typhoon housing damage per municipality to trigger pre-agreed early actions before landfall.

The system's part

The ML impact forecast was the trigger: under the EAP, early actions fire when the forecast wind impact on housing, 72 hours before landfall, is at least 10% of houses totally damaged in more than three municipalities; the notification records the Aurora municipalities as 'Met (More than 10%)' with the 72-hour lead-time condition not met, and the final report says the 56-hour lead time was exceptionally granted. The notification states the projection came from the 510/IBF forecast incorporating PAGASA data. PRC staff confirmed chapter capacity and chose municipalities; the outcome claim is PRC's own post-storm survey. The NHESS paper and EGU abstract describing the model have 510 staff as co-authors, not lead authors.

Replication

What produced it. 510 IBF damage model fed by PAGASA typhoon forecasts. Pre-approved EAP with a 10% projected-damage trigger. IFRC anticipatory DREF funds released on trigger. Pre-positioned shelter kits, trained carpenters and pre-identified households.

What must hold again. Lead time near 72 hours. Forecast track and intensity accuracy. Weather permitting installation before landfall. Local government authorization in no-build zones.
