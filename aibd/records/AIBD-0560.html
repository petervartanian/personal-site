Somerset staff report time saved on meeting notes in a Copilot pilot

Somerset Council reports that Microsoft Copilot saved pilot users two to four hours a week and that 75% reported better focus and 53% found work more enjoyable.

  • 1 an organization

    Staff report less time spent on meeting notes and other tasks.

  • 2 a person

    Staff report improved focus, productivity, and work experience. The duration of those improvements is unknown.

The source attributes transcription, note creation, and related task support to Copilot. Staff surveys, interviews, and usage data underpin reported time and work-experience improvements, without an independent causal evaluation.
Limits
The results rely heavily on user feedback and lack a randomized comparison or reported response denominator. Returned licenses and acknowledged usage-dashboard inaccuracies qualify the positive reports.

What happened

Somerset offered 300 Copilot licenses to staff and assessed their experiences after three months. Staff reportedly reduced meeting-minute work from 72 to 17 minutes and saved two to four hours weekly. Productivity and enjoyment results are self-reported. The stated 4–10x ROI is project-side valuation, not independently validated cash savings. The council launched the pilot in November 2023 and took its results from a review in February 2024. It also reported that some volunteers returned their licenses and that Microsoft's usage dashboards were inaccurate. In a later pilot with a control group, the Department for Business and Trade found that Copilot saved small amounts of time and helped neurodiverse staff, but it found no evidence that the savings improved productivity.

Details

AI system

Role

Microsoft 365 Copilot drafted meeting notes and assisted with documents in the council’s pilot. [1]

Configuration

The pilot began in November 2023 and provided 300 Copilot licenses to staff volunteers for their everyday work. [1]

Workflow

The discovery pilot provided 300 licenses to staff volunteers and used regular review points. Participants received peer support through a Teams community and regular contact sessions.

Benefits

Reach

The council offered 300 Copilot licenses to staff volunteers, and some volunteers returned their licenses. No source gives the number of staff who answered the surveys behind the reported percentages. [1] [2]

Duration

The council took its results from a review in February 2024, three months into the pilot. In March 2025, a Microsoft customer story quoted council staff who said that Copilot users saved 10 hours a month. No source reports a measured saving after that date. [1] [2]
