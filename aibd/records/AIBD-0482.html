IndustryGPT beats manual training in an industrial inspection test

SmartMore reports that, in one classification test, the training that IndustryGPT chose reached 100% accuracy in 5:27, compared with 50% in 9:31 for manual training.

What happened

SmartMore released IndustryGPT in 2023, according to the company's Hong Kong listing document. In its submission to the ITU AI for Good report of 2025, SmartMore reports that an IndustryGPT model-training example reached 100% accuracy in 5:27, compared with 50% in 9:31 for manual training. Recall was 100% with IndustryGPT and 66.7% with manual training. The submission also describes commercial production-line applications, but customer financial returns remain unmeasured. SmartMore's Hong Kong listing document, filed on 16 March 2026, says that the company had served over 730 customers and had delivered about 140,000 industrial AI agents built on IndustryGPT by 31 December 2025. These are company figures for all its products. The same document claims that ViMo annotation runs more than eight times faster than manual labeling. No published independent test of the model is known.

AI system

Role

IndustryGPT interprets natural-language or voice instructions and configures SMore ViMo model training, including data preparation, model choice, augmentation, and training parameters.
