AI predicts new archaeological zones at Dubai's Saruq Al-Hadid site

Dubai Culture and Khalifa University report that machine learning on remote-sensing data detected known excavation areas at Saruq Al-Hadid and predicted new zones that await excavation.

What happened

Khalifa University researchers published the results of a remote-sensing and machine-learning pilot at Saruq Al-Hadid in Geosciences on 15 June 2023. The authors report that the method detected areas already under excavation and predicted new ones. They validated the results against earlier excavations and field surveys. On 27 September 2024 Artnet News reported that Dubai Culture planned to excavate the predicted areas the next month. The story relayed a caution from Hugh Thomas, an archaeology lecturer at the University of Sydney. On 26 February 2025 Dubai Culture said that follow-up radar and magnetic surveys had cut excavation time and costs by 30%, without giving a baseline. That release does not mention machine learning. In a project account current as of June 2025, Dubai Culture and Khalifa University report that the completed pilot identified known excavation areas and predicted new zones. On 7 November 2025 Diana Francis of Khalifa University told The National that the structures found by the follow-up surveys still awaited manual excavation. No source reports that excavation has confirmed a predicted zone.
