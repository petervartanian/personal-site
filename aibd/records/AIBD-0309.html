ProRail uses Genetec analytics on 7,000 cameras to flag trespass

off the scale

SpoorPro: ProRail swaps 288 lineside cameras for smart cameras in 2026, tests drones and runs Genetec video analytics on 7,000 cameras to flag trespassers to dispatchers; no results are given.

What happened

SpoorPro.nl reported on 2 February 2026 that ProRail, the Dutch rail infrastructure manager, is replacing 288 lineside cameras in 2026 with new 'smart' cameras and testing drones that fly autonomously and analyze images, quoting ProRail that these forms of camera surveillance help it see and prevent incidents sooner, making the track safer with fewer disruptions and delays; the article's photo caption says the AI cameras' images are monitored 24/7, and the rest of the text is behind a paywall. SpoorPro reported on 3 March 2026 that ProRail is bringing the 7,000 security cameras it manages into one Genetec platform monitored from its Smart Monitoring Room, that Genetec Mission Control filters and analyzes alerts and forwards relevant images to an operator with suggested actions, and that video analysis is used among other things to flag spoorlopers (people on the track), in which case the train dispatcher can warn drivers; the article quotes ProRail project lead Kristian Ruiter and Genetec's Bob van Keulen. No source gives a detection count, a stopped train or a trespass reduction attributable to the analytics, and the UIC trespass-detection trial the earlier verifier mentioned could not be identified. The date observed is the first article's publication date.

What it was built to do. Camera upgrades and video analytics intended to detect incidents such as trespassers sooner so that dispatchers can warn drivers; no detection event is documented.

Replication

No attempt to repeat it has been recorded.

What produced it. Thousands of lineside and station security cameras brought into one Genetec video-management platform. Operators in ProRail's Smart Monitoring Room receiving filtered alerts with suggested actions. Video analytics configured to flag people on the track.

What must hold again. A dispatcher able to warn drivers once a trespasser is flagged. Privacy masking and blurring applied to passenger images, as the article states ProRail does.
