What happened

SpoorPro.nl reported on 2 February 2026 that ProRail, the Dutch rail infrastructure manager, is replacing 288 lineside cameras in 2026 with new “smart” cameras. ProRail is also testing drones that fly autonomously and analyze images. ProRail is quoted that these forms of camera surveillance help it see and prevent incidents sooner, making the track safer with fewer disruptions and delays. The article's photo caption says the AI cameras' images are monitored 24/7. The rest of the text is behind a paywall. SpoorPro reported on 3 March 2026 that ProRail is bringing the 7,000 security cameras it manages into one Genetec platform. It is monitored from ProRail's Smart Monitoring Room. Genetec Mission Control filters and analyzes alerts and forwards relevant images to an operator with suggested actions. Video analysis is used among other things to flag spoorlopers (people on the track).

In that case the train dispatcher can warn drivers. The article quotes ProRail project lead Kristian Ruiter and Genetec's Bob van Keulen. No source gives a detection count, a stopped train or a trespass reduction attributable to the analytics. No source on record identifies a UIC trespass-detection trial. The date observed is the first article's publication date.

What it was built to do. Camera upgrades and video analytics intended to detect incidents such as trespassers sooner so that dispatchers can warn drivers. No detection event is documented.

The system's part

Not assessed. No incident is documented. The AI character of the cameras rests on SpoorPro's caption and ProRail's “smart cameras”. The trespasser-flagging function is described by Genetec, the platform vendor. Whether the analytics changed any outcome is not reported. The full 2 February 2026 article is paywalled.

Replication

No attempt to repeat it has been recorded.

What produced it. Thousands of lineside and station security cameras brought into one Genetec video-management platform. Operators in ProRail's Smart Monitoring Room receiving filtered alerts with suggested actions. Video analytics configured to flag people on the track.

What must hold again. A dispatcher able to warn drivers once a trespasser is flagged. Privacy masking and blurring applied to passenger images, as the article states ProRail does.
