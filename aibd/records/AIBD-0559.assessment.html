Summary
Peterborough and Datnexa are developing OSCAR to connect council services. The council reports that OSCAR Workforce is live, but has not supplied measured results.
Account
Peterborough’s May 13, 2026 LGA case study describes OSCAR as a proof of concept for connecting council assistants through a shared service interface. Datnexa identifies itself as the development partner and describes AI that interprets requests and routes them to specialist services. On October 21, the council reported that OSCAR Workforce was live and helped staff find emails and documents and query data faster. It did not publish timings, user counts, or a comparative evaluation. The resident-facing accessibility benefits remain intended. A separate council contract identifies Datnexa as the supplier for Workforce design and scoping. That contract establishes procurement, not a measured benefit.

Account

Datnexa identifies itself as the development partner and describes AI that interprets requests and routes them to specialist services. On October 21, the council reported that OSCAR Workforce was live and helped staff find emails and documents and query data faster. It did not publish timings, user counts, or a comparative evaluation. The resident-facing accessibility benefits remain intended. A separate council contract identifies Datnexa as the supplier for Workforce design and scoping. That contract establishes procurement, not a measured benefit.
Replication
Produced by
  • The framework is designed for web, WhatsApp, and voice channels and for connection to existing services.
  • The reported design includes centralized permissions, redaction, guardrails, and audit logging.
Must hold
  • Specialist services and information sources must be available for the routing system to use
  • Human staff must retain responsibility for the decisions that the system is not authorized to make
  • Access controls and records of system actions must work in the deployment, rather than merely being described in the design
