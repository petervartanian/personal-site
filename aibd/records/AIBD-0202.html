Gyeonggi AI pig-weighing pilot lifts income per head 18%

2025 provincial pilot on six farms in Anseong, Pocheon and Yeoju: AI non-contact weighing cut shipping age 182.3 to 176.7 days, lifted 1+ grade share 35.3 to 37.7% and income per head 18%.

AI system

System

Fattening-pig shipping selection system (AI non-contact weighing) and sow farrowing management system (ICT monitoring)

Application developer

Not named in the sources

Workflow

A provincial extension pilot funded in 2025. Six commercial pig farms adopting non-contact AI weighing. A grading system that pays a premium for grade 1+ pigs.

Relation to AI

The AI's contribution is the weight estimate and shipping-date advice. The production gains are the agency's uncontrolled before-and-after comparisons. The deployer is a public body that reports its own pilot. The developer is not named. Two independent newspapers carried the release with a named official.

Organizations. People.

Depth

Organizations: 2. People: Not assessed.

Reasons

Organizations: On six farms, shipping age fell from 182.3 to 176.7 days and the grade 1+ share rose from 35.3% to 37.7%. Income per head rose from 100,000 to 118,000 won, up 18%. On Pocheon farms, sow accidents fell from 3.1% to 2.7% and piglets per sow per year rose from 18.3 to 19.2. These are the agency's own before-and-after figures. People: Reduced need for farmers to stay in the barn is mentioned but not measured.
