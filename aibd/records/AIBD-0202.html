Gyeonggi AI pig-weighing pilot lifts income per head 18%

2025 provincial pilot on six farms in Anseong, Pocheon and Yeoju: AI non-contact weighing cut shipping age 182.3 to 176.7 days, lifted 1+ grade share 35.3 to 37.7% and income per head 18%.

ⓘ

What happened

Gyeonggi-do Agricultural Research and Extension Services announced the results of its 2025 livestock AI pilot on 6 March 2026. The Fact reported it with a Suwon dateline, and Joongboo Ilbo on 8 March. A fattening-pig shipping selection system uses AI to measure pigs' weight without contact and advise the optimal shipping date. It was introduced on six farms in Anseong, Pocheon and Yeoju. Shipping age fell from 182.3 to 176.7 days, a drop of 5.6 days. The share shipped at grade 1+ rose from 35.3% to 37.7%, up 2.4 percentage points. Income per head rose from 100,000 to 118,000 won, up 18%. A sow farrowing management system using ICT equipment for real-time monitoring was introduced on Pocheon farms. It cut the sow accident rate from 3.1% to 2.7%; those accidents are mastitis, fever, loss of appetite and crushing of piglets. Piglets shipped per sow per year rose from 18.3 to 19.2, up 4.9%.

Real-time alerts reduced the need to stay in the barn. Lee Jun-bae, head of the agency's technology dissemination division, said the results confirmed AI as a way to raise productivity and income. The agency plans to extend the weighing system to Yeoncheon in 2026. The vendor of the systems is not named. The pilot is listed in the OECD AI Incidents Monitor.

Purpose. Measure fattening pigs' weight without contact using AI and advise the optimal shipping date. Monitor sows in real time around farrowing.

AI’s contribution

The AI's contribution is the weight estimate and shipping-date advice. The production gains are the agency's uncontrolled before-and-after comparisons. The deployer is a public body that reports its own pilot. The developer is not named. Two independent newspapers carried the release with a named official.

Further evidence

Setting. A provincial extension pilot funded in 2025. Six commercial pig farms adopting non-contact AI weighing. A grading system that pays a premium for grade 1+ pigs.

Conditions. Farm staff acting on the shipping-date advice. Real-time alerts reaching farmers from the sow system.
