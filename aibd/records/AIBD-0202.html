What happened

Gyeonggi-do Agricultural Research and Extension Services announced on 6 March 2026 (reported by The Fact, dateline Suwon, and by Joongboo Ilbo on 8 March) the results of its 2025 livestock AI pilot. A fattening-pig shipping selection system, in which AI measures pigs' weight without contact and advises the optimal shipping date, was introduced on six farms in Anseong, Pocheon and Yeoju: shipping age fell from 182.3 to 176.7 days (5.6 days), the share shipped at grade 1+ rose from 35.3% to 37.7% (2.4 percentage points) and income per head rose from 100,000 to 118,000 won (18%).

A sow farrowing management system using ICT equipment for real-time monitoring, introduced on Pocheon farms, cut the sow accident rate (mastitis, fever, loss of appetite, crushing of piglets) from 3.1% to 2.7% and raised piglets shipped per sow per year from 18.3 to 19.2 (4.9%), with real-time alerts reducing the need to stay in the barn. Lee Jun-bae, head of the agency's technology dissemination division, said the results confirmed AI as a way to raise productivity and income, and the agency plans to extend the weighing system to Yeoncheon in 2026. The vendor of the systems is not named. Listed in the OECD AI Incidents Monitor (2026-03-05-1ac3).

What it was built to do. Measure fattening pigs' weight without contact using AI and advise the optimal shipping date; monitor sows in real time around farrowing.

The system's part

The AI's contribution is the weight estimate and shipping-date advice; the production gains are the agency's uncontrolled before/after comparisons. The deployer is a public body that reports its own pilot; the developer is not named. Two independent newspapers carried the release with a named official.

Replication

What produced it. A provincial extension pilot funded in 2025. Six commercial pig farms adopting non-contact AI weighing. A grading system that pays a premium for grade 1+ pigs.

What must hold again. Farm staff acting on the shipping-date advice. Real-time alerts reaching farmers from the sow system.
