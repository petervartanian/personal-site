Alibaba's Baguan model forecasts regional weather with less error

Alibaba DAMO Academy reports that its Baguan weather model had 57.8% lower regional forecast error on average than ECMWF-IFS, the European Centre for Medium-Range Weather Forecasts' system.

What happened

Alibaba's news site, Alizila, reported on 6 November 2024 that Baguan forecast a 20% drop in electricity demand in Shandong with 98.1% load-forecast accuracy. The article gives no date for the forecast and no measure of grid savings. The makers' paper, posted on 20 May 2025, reports a test on the last half of 2023 against the Integrated Forecasting System of the European Centre for Medium-Range Weather Forecasts (ECMWF-IFS). In that test, Baguan-Regional achieved an average improvement of 57.8% on key regional variables. The paper states that the system serves the power sector in an eastern Chinese province. The Baguan submission to the International Telecommunication Union, current as of June 2025, reports improvements over ECMWF-IFS on selected forecast variables in regional and global comparisons. It names State Grid Shandong among the test settings. A version of the paper appeared in the proceedings of the 31st ACM SIGKDD Conference on 3 August 2025. Better forecasting of renewable power and better grid operations are intended applications. No source quantifies a resulting change in dispatch, reliability or emissions.

Sources

  1. Report, by the makers.

    International Telecommunication Union, AI for Good: Innovate for Impact (2025). Project-submitted account, current as of June 2025. ITU provides no independent outcome evaluation.

    In terms of the global model alone, it consistently outperforms IFS, achieving an average improvement of 20.5%.

  2. Preprint, by the makers.

    Peisong Niu, Ziqing Ma, Tian Zhou, Weiqi Chen, Lefei Shen, Rong Jin and Liang Sun, Utilizing Strategic Pre-training to Reduce Overfitting: Baguan - A Pre-trained Weather Forecasting Model, arXiv 2505.13873, 20 May 2025; a version appeared in the Proceedings of the 31st ACM SIGKDD Conference on 3 August 2025 (doi 10.1145/3711896.3737178). Six of the seven authors work at Alibaba's DAMO Academy, which built Baguan; the paper reports retrospective test results against ECMWF-IFS and states that the system was deployed for the power sector in an eastern Chinese province.

    Baguan-Regional demonstrates significantly superior performance compared to EC-IFS, achieving an average improvement of 57.8% on key variables in regional forecasting.

  3. News report, by the makers. Context or contrary evidence.

    Xinhua, 达摩院发布八观气象大模型：精度达1小时1公里，率先落地新能源场景 (DAMO Academy releases the Baguan weather model, with a precision of 1 hour and 1 kilometer, first in use for renewable energy), news.cn, 6 November 2024. DAMO Academy supplied this account of the launch, which China Daily's site also ran as promotional content. DAMO quotes State Grid Shandong's dispatch center on a load forecast from 25 to 28 August 2024.

    据国网山东省电力公司电力调度控制中心介绍，今年8月25日至8月28日，受降水影响，山东省气温出现大幅度变化，3日内用电负荷总量下降20%，八观气象大模型通过构筑每小时更新一次的高精度区域天气预报网络，及时捕捉到温度变化，推动下游新能源发电功率、电力负荷预测准确率分别提升至96.5%和98.1%

  4. Press release, by the makers.

    Crystal Liu, Alibaba's New AI Model Enhances Weather Forecasting Precision Amid Rising Climate Threats, Alizila, 6 November 2024. Alizila is Alibaba's own news site; the article announces Baguan and reports a load forecast in Shandong without method or date.

    The model's precision was demonstrated during a sudden temperature drop in Shandong Province in China, where Baguan accurately anticipated a 20% drop in electricity demand, achieving a high accuracy-98.1% in load forecasting.

Details

AI system

Reported configuration

Baguan-Regional (regional fine-tune of the Baguan foundation model, KDD 2025 paper)

Role

Baguan produces weather forecasts that can inform renewable-power forecasting and grid operations.

Workflow

The account does not establish conditions sufficient to reproduce the benefit.

Measurement

Measurement

Retrospective forecast test on held-out data: root mean square error of forecasts compared with gridded analysis data. [2]

Setting and sample

China Meteorological Administration Land Data Assimilation System data at 0.05 degree spacing over China for 2022 and 2023; the last half year held out for testing. [2]

Measures

Average reduction in root mean square error on key variables such as 2-meter temperature and 10-meter wind. [2]

Results

57.8% lower average error than ECMWF-IFS and 47.0% lower than interpolation. [2]

Additional value from AI

57.8% average error reduction against the physics-based ECMWF-IFS forecast. [2]

Limitations

Run by the developers on one half-year test period; no confidence intervals; no independent replication; no measure of downstream grid effects. [2]

Denominators

Forecasts at 6-hour and 24-hour lead times over China. [2]

Materials for replication

Regional observation and analysis data are proprietary according to the ITU submission. [2]

Conditions for replication

The account does not establish requirements for reproducing the benefit in other settings.
