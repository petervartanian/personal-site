UNDP hands Mines Eye to SESU after a pilot scanning over 360 ha

Mines Eye (magnetometry, visual/infrared imaging, AI detection of explosive remnants) scanned over 360 ha in a five-month SESU pilot; handed over 21 June 2025. No detection or land-release counts.

ⓘ

What happened

UNDP Ukraine's press release of 21 June 2025 is datelined Mykolaiv. It says UNDP “officially handed over the Mines Eye aerial detection system to the State Emergency Service of Ukraine (SESU)”. Financial support came from the Government of Luxembourg. The base of SESU's Main Department in Mykolaiv oblast hosted it, with UNDP Acting Administrator Haoliang Xu present. The release calls the system “Developed in Ukraine” but does not name the developer. It combines magnetometry with visual and infrared aerial imaging. It states that “Using artificial intelligence, it detects explosive remnants of war (ERW), including anti-tank mines, even beneath vegetation”. It produces maps and anomaly reports for operators before manual or mechanical clearance. UNDP-trained SESU teams ran a five-month pilot.

They “used the system to scan over 360 hectares of confirmed and suspected hazardous areas in Kharkiv and Mykolaiv oblasts”. SESU First Deputy Head Vitalii Myroniuk is quoted that the technology “enables us to detect threats in areas that are too dangerous for manual inspection”. The release gives no number of items detected or confirmed, no hectares released, and no accuracy figure. UNDP's 26 June 2025 release concerned a testing exhibition with mock devices. No source independent of UNDP or SESU was found.

Purpose. Built to survey contaminated land remotely and flag explosive remnants of war, including anti-tank mines under vegetation. This lets clearance teams plan safely. The pilot was that intended use.

AI’s contribution

UNDP's release attributes the detection of explosive remnants beneath vegetation to the system's artificial intelligence working on magnetometry and visual and infrared imagery. It reports only area scanned. No detection, false-alarm or confirmation figure is given. No source independent of UNDP and SESU was found. The developer is unnamed, so the AI component cannot be characterized further.
