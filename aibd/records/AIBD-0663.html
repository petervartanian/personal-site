What happened

The Kangaroo Island Landscape Board in South Australia runs a program to eradicate feral cats from the 384 square kilometer Dudley Peninsula, with 200 fixed monitoring cameras. Board staff and their co-authors uploaded 101,586 images from 42 cameras, taken between 11 November 2020 and 9 September 2021 and already sorted by field staff, to eVorta, an Australian camera-trap classification service. eVorta correctly classified 482 of 492 cat images (97.9%) against 469 (95.3%) for staff, and missed one of 68 cat sequences where staff missed 11. It also produced 2,751 false cat alerts, against five by staff. Using program costs, the authors estimate that eVorta alone saved A$0.27 million (10%) over three years and that eVorta with 4G-connected cameras saved A$2.15 million (81%) and 115,838 kg of carbon emissions. Scientific Reports published the study on 24 June 2024. The cost figures come from a decision tool with program inputs and are estimates.

Details

AI system

Workflow

Field staff had already classified the same images, which allowed a direct comparison. 4G cameras sent images to the cloud without site visits.

Relation to AI

EVorta and staff classified the same 101,586 images, so the authors measure the detection of the AI against human review directly; the cost savings are modeled.

Measurement

Measurement

Image and sequence classification compared between eVorta and staff; cost and emissions from a decision tool with program inputs. [1]

Setting and sample

101,586 images from 42 cameras, 11 November 2020 to 9 September 2021; 492 cat and 155 echidna images. [1]

Measures

Correct classifications, false negatives, false positives; cost over three years. [1]

Results

97.9% against 95.3% of cat images; one against 11 missed sequences; A$2.15 million estimated saving with 4G. [1]

Additional value from AI

Fewer missed cats; 81% lower estimated monitoring cost with 4G cameras. [1]

Limitations

2,751 false cat alerts against five by staff; costs modeled; one program; written by the deployer, with no eVorta author or funding. [1]

Denominators

Program monitoring cameras. [1]

Materials for replication

Decision tool in the supplement. [1]

Follow-up

Conditions for replication

4G coverage must reach the camera sites. Staff must screen the many false positives.
