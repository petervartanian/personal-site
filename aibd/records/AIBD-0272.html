Nephew uses ChatGPT during FOLFIRINOX; liver metastases regress

off the scale

11 liver metastases regressed

A dentist nephew in Baku used ChatGPT, later GPT-5, to summarize literature and weigh off-label add-ons for a 65-year-old on FOLFIRINOX; 11 liver metastases regressed; the author cannot attribute it.

What happened

Khalil (International Medical Case Reports Journal, 2026), a dentist and the patient's nephew writing as sole author, reports a 65-year-old with metastatic pancreatic adenosquamous carcinoma and 11 hepatic metastases treated with FOLFIRINOX; the patient and author added high-dose intravenous vitamin C, doxycycline and later azithromycin as off-label home measures. After three cycles MRI showed complete radiological regression of the hepatic lesions, confirmed around the sixth cycle, and chemotherapy stopped after six cycles; ursodeoxycholic acid in late September 2025 resolved cholestasis. The author and patient used ChatGPT, initially an earlier model and later GPT-5, to search and summarize literature, interpret CA 19-9 during cholestasis and appraise data on adjunctive interventions, and the author writes that GPT 'materially influenced how we navigated information' while stating that the single case cannot determine the contribution of those interventions to the response. The earlier verifier set the case aside because no attributable change in diagnosis or treatment could be established. The date is the publication date; the clinical course ran through 2025.

What it was built to do. Patient-side literature navigation and summarization during cancer treatment

The system's part

ChatGPT's role, per the author, was literature navigation and summarization that shaped which supportive options the family considered; the author reports no efficacy claim and warns that outputs may be incorrect. The outcome is not attributable to the model.
