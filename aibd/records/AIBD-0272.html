Nephew uses ChatGPT to sum up research while a relative has chemotherapy

A nephew in Baku who is a dentist used ChatGPT, later GPT-5, to sum up studies and weigh off-label add-ons for a 65-year-old on FOLFIRINOX; 11 liver metastases regressed, cause unattributed.

  • 1 a person

    The author and the patient report that ChatGPT helped them find and summarize articles quickly, and that they relied on those summaries when deciding on additional support. The author states that the case cannot show what the chatbot-informed additions contributed to the regression, so only the help with information is rated.

What happened

Khalil (International Medical Case Reports Journal, 2026) is a dentist and the patient's nephew, writing as sole author. He reports a 65-year-old with metastatic pancreatic adenosquamous carcinoma and 11 hepatic metastases treated with FOLFIRINOX. The report's timeline dates the start of FOLFIRINOX to 7 July 2025. The patient and author added high-dose intravenous vitamin C, doxycycline and later azithromycin as off-label home measures. After three cycles, magnetic resonance imaging (MRI) showed complete radiological regression of the hepatic lesions. The timeline dates the first MRI without visible liver lesions to 21 August 2025. The regression was confirmed around the sixth cycle. Chemotherapy stopped after six cycles. Cholestasis and a rise in CA 19-9 in late September 2025 initially suggested recurrence. After ursodeoxycholic acid, CA 19-9 normalized by 10 January 2026. Follow-up MRI on 20 February 2026 showed no visible hepatic lesions. The author and patient used ChatGPT, initially an earlier model and later GPT-5. They used it to search and summarize literature, interpret CA 19-9 during cholestasis and appraise data on adjunctive interventions. The author writes that GPT “materially influenced how we navigated information”. He also states that the single case cannot determine the contribution of those adjunctive interventions to the response. The cited sources establish no change in diagnosis or treatment that can be attributed to ChatGPT. The journal published the case report online on 3 October 2026.
