What happened

Khalil (International Medical Case Reports Journal, 2026) is a dentist and the patient's nephew writing as sole author. He reports a 65-year-old with metastatic pancreatic adenosquamous carcinoma and 11 hepatic metastases treated with FOLFIRINOX. The patient and author added high-dose intravenous vitamin C, doxycycline and later azithromycin as off-label home measures. After three cycles magnetic resonance imaging (MRI) showed complete radiological regression of the hepatic lesions, confirmed around the sixth cycle. Chemotherapy stopped after six cycles. Ursodeoxycholic acid in late September 2025 resolved cholestasis. The author and patient used ChatGPT, initially an earlier model and later GPT-5. They used it to search and summarize literature, interpret CA 19–9 during cholestasis and appraise data on adjunctive interventions.

The author writes that GPT “materially influenced how we navigated information”. He also states that the single case cannot determine the contribution of those interventions to the response. No attributable change in diagnosis or treatment can be established from the sources on record. The date is the publication date. The clinical course ran through 2025.
