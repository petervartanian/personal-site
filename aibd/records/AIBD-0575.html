Manchester residents shape a proposed AI advice service

A Manchester residents' panel proposed safeguards and a fourth design for a public-facing version of Caddy, an AI advice tool from Citizens Advice; no source reports that the service launched.

What happened

In July 2024 Citizens Advice Stockport, Oldham, Rochdale and Trafford presented to the People's Panel for AI, according to an Open University interview with Stuart Pearson of that organization. The Local Government Association account of 28 January 2025 states that Manchester City Council set up the panel with Manchester Metropolitan University. The council's account reports changes in learning and confidence from its wider participation program. It reports no outcome from Caddy. In a post of 2 September 2026, Pearson describes a panel meeting in January 2026. The panel considered three proposals for a public-facing Caddy and proposed a fourth. Residents called for client choice, human escalation for red flags and transparency. They also noted that members of the public would lack the prompting skills of trained advisers. Pearson describes the People's Panel for AI as a Manchester Metropolitan University panel. None of these accounts establishes that the proposed public service was deployed or that it benefited its clients.
