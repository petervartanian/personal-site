Flint uses a prediction model to find homes with hazardous water pipes

ActiveRemediation, a model built at the University of Michigan, ranked Flint homes for inspection and replacement of lead or galvanized-steel service lines, with 91.6% held-out accuracy.

AI contribution

The model ranked homes for inspection and replacement. The developers wrote that their modeling had less influence on the choice of replacement homes, because those choices faced political and logistical limits. In 2023 an independent comparison by Pierre Goovaerts found that the program's excavation order found lead lines fastest in its first eight months, after which geostatistical rankings would have done better. He concluded that the 2018 decision to stop using the model does not explain this weaker relative result. The field data do not isolate the model's effect on health or spending, and the cost saving came from simulation. [1] [2] [3] [4] [5] [6] [7] [8]
