What happened

On 4 February 2020 BenevolentAI staff published a Lancet correspondence. It reported that the company's machine-learning-built knowledge graph identified baricitinib, a rheumatoid-arthritis JAK inhibitor, as a candidate against 2019-nCoV because it binds AAK1. AAK1 is a regulator of viral endocytosis. Randomized trials tested it. ACTT-2 (1,033 patients, enrolled 8 May to 1 July 2020) reported median recovery of 7 days versus 8 (rate ratio 1.16, 95% CI 1.01–1.32). On high-flow oxygen or non-invasive ventilation, 10 versus 18 days. COV-BARRIER (1,525 patients) missed its primary endpoint of disease progression (27.8% versus 30.5%, odds ratio 0.85, p=0.18). Its 28-day all-cause mortality, a secondary endpoint, was 8% versus 13% (hazard ratio 0.57, nominal p=0.0018).

Because the primary endpoint failed, the prespecified procedure did not establish statistical significance for the secondary endpoints after adjustment for multiple testing. The Food and Drug Administration (FDA) authorized it with remdesivir on 19 November 2020. WHO strongly recommended it on 14 January 2022.

What it was built to do. Drug repurposing: mine a machine-learning-built biomedical knowledge graph for approved drugs that might block a new coronavirus.

Beneficiaries

    COV-BARRIER reported 28-day all-cause mortality of 8% (62 patients) on baricitinib versus 13% (100 patients) on placebo, hazard ratio 0.57. The randomized comparison supports a life-preserving treatment benefit.

  • 2
    an organization

    ACTT-2 showed median recovery falling from 8 to 7 days overall and from 18 to 10 days on high-flow oxygen or non-invasive ventilation. That materially eased hospital burden.

  • 4
    society

    WHO strongly recommended baricitinib for severe or critical COVID-19 on 14 January 2022. That made the AI-proposed drug part of standard pandemic care at population scale.

The system's part

The AI's role was hypothesis generation only: BenevolentAI's knowledge graph ranked baricitinib among six high-affinity AAK1-binding approved drugs and the authors published the prediction. It did not design, run or analyze the trials. ACTT-2 was led by NIAID, with input from the drug suppliers. COV-BARRIER was designed with Eli Lilly, which also analyzed the data. These trials tested the drug, not the additional benefit of using AI to identify it. FDA and WHO subsequently assessed the clinical evidence.

Replication

What produced it. A machine-learning-built knowledge graph queried for approved drugs likely to block viral endocytosis through AAK1. A new pathogen without a treatment and trial investigators willing to test a repurposing hypothesis. Hospitalized patients requiring oxygen, in whom the effect was measured in randomized trials.

What must hold again. Hospitalized COVID-19 patients needing supplemental oxygen or more. Baricitinib given per protocol alongside remdesivir or corticosteroids. SARS-CoV-2 epidemiology and standard of care of 2020–2021.
