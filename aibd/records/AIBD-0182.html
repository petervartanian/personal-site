Italian tax agency sets up Ve.R.A. to flag taxpayers at risk of evasion

Ve.R.A., an algorithm of Italy's tax agency, cross-matches financial and tax data under pseudonyms to flag taxpayers at higher risk of evasion; no source reports a result.

The system’s part
Not isolated. Agenda Digitale calls the application AI-based and Fiscomania describes risk analysis over cross-matched data. The material on record describes legal authorization, operational risk lists, and numerical targets. It does not report tax receipts caused by VeRa, achieved reductions in false positives, or an evaluated AI technique. The targets cannot be counted as realized benefits.
Costs and harms
Financial-data matching is subject to pseudonymization, data minimization, restricted access, and retention limits. The decree also temporarily limits some taxpayer data rights.

What happened

Law 160/2019, article 1, paragraphs 681–686, provides the legal basis for VeRa, “verifica dei rapporti finanziari”. In its operating guidelines of 20 June 2022, the Agenzia delle Entrate wrote that Ve.R.A. would deliver new selective lists to every regional and provincial office for ordinary audits. Offices were to report the outcome of each flagged case on a feedback form so that the Agenzia could check the selection model. A model for partnerships and companies that had already been piloted was to be refined. Fiscomania (Elisa Migliorini, 15 May 2026) states that the algorithm became operative under the ministerial decree of 28 June 2022. The decree was adopted after hearing the data protection authority (Garante) and the Agenzia. On 29 July 2022 Denaura Bordandini and Massimo Borgobello described VeRa in Agenda Digitale. They called it a new AI-based application to help Agenzia delle Entrate officials fight tax evasion. It cross-matches the Archivio dei Rapporti Finanziari with other tax-registry information. Preliminary analysis uses pseudonymized data. The data are not irreversibly anonymized. Under the decree, analysis datasets are kept until the second year after tax-assessment powers expire and, where relevant, until litigation is resolved. According to Fiscomania, the Settore Analisi del rischio of the Divisione Contribuenti runs the system. It produces selective lists of taxpayers at higher fiscal risk by crossing data on financial relationships, declarations, assets and electronic payments. Taxpayers are handled under a pseudonymous code during the preliminary phase. Fiscomania gives targets for 2022 under Italy's national recovery and resilience plan (PNRR): 2,581,090 communications, 2.45 billion euro in expected receipts and 5% fewer false positives than in 2019. It reports no results. No source through October 2026 reports the results of the feedback forms or any audit or evaluation of Ve.R.A.

Details

AI system

Inputs and outputs

VeRa cross-matched financial relationships, declarations, assets, and electronic payments to generate lists of taxpayers with higher estimated fiscal risk.

Workflow

A legal basis (Law 160/2019 and the decree of 28 June 2022) permitting cross-matching of the financial-relationships archive with tax data under Garante safeguards.

Human oversight

The decree requires human involvement in preparing analysis and control datasets. Authorized staff must check representative samples before and after the risk lists are formed.
