Agenzia delle Entrate activates VeRa to list high-risk taxpayers

off the scale

VeRa (Verifica dei Rapporti Finanziari) cross-matches the financial-relationships archive with tax data under pseudonymisation to build selective lists of high-risk taxpayers; no realized outcome f...

What happened

Agenda Digitale (Denaura Bordandini and Massimo Borgobello, 29 July 2022) described VeRa, 'verifica dei rapporti finanziari', as a new AI-based application to assist Agenzia delle Entrate officials in fighting tax evasion by cross-matching the Archivio dei Rapporti Finanziari with other tax-registry information, with the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali requiring anonymized preliminary analysis, data minimization and deletion after the second year following assessment. Fiscomania (Elisa Migliorini, 15 May 2026) states that the algorithm became operative under the ministerial decree of 28 June 2022, adopted after hearing the Garante and the Agenzia, with a legal basis in Law 160/2019 article 1 paragraphs 681-686, that it is run by the Settore Analisi del rischio of the Divisione Contribuenti, that it produces selective lists of taxpayers at higher fiscal risk by crossing financial-relationship, declaration, asset and electronic-payment data, and that taxpayers are handled under an anonymous code during the preliminary phase; it gives only PNRR targets for 2022 (2,581,090 communications, 2.45 billion euro expected receipts, 5% fewer false positives than 2019), not results. The date used is the decree date.

What it was built to do. Build selective lists of taxpayers at higher fiscal risk by cross-matching financial-relationship data with tax declarations.

The system's part

not assessed Agenda Digitale calls the application AI-based and Fiscomania describes risk analysis over cross-matched data; neither gives the technique or any result, so the algorithm's contribution cannot be assessed. The Agenzia both runs and, as far as the sources show, operates the system in-house.

Replication

No attempt to repeat it has been recorded.

What produced it. A legal basis (Law 160/2019 and the decree of 28 June 2022) permitting cross-matching of the financial-relationships archive with tax data under Garante safeguards.

What must hold again. Access to the Archivio dei Rapporti Finanziari. Pseudonymisation during preliminary analysis and retention limits set by the Garante.
