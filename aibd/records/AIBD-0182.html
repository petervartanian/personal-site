What happened

Agenda Digitale (Denaura Bordandini and Massimo Borgobello, 29 July 2022) described VeRa, “verifica dei rapporti finanziari”. It called it a new AI-based application to help Agenzia delle Entrate officials fight tax evasion. It cross-matches the Archivio dei Rapporti Finanziari with other tax-registry information. The Garante per la protezione dei dati personali required anonymized preliminary analysis, data minimization and deletion after the second year following assessment. Fiscomania (Elisa Migliorini, 15 May 2026) states that the algorithm became operative under the ministerial decree of 28 June 2022. The decree was adopted after hearing the Garante and the Agenzia. Its legal basis is Law 160/2019 article 1 paragraphs 681–686. It is run by the Settore Analisi del rischio of the Divisione Contribuenti.

It produces selective lists of taxpayers at higher fiscal risk by crossing financial-relationship, declaration, asset and electronic-payment data. Taxpayers are handled under an anonymous code during the preliminary phase. Fiscomania gives only PNRR targets for 2022 (2,581,090 communications, 2.45 billion euro expected receipts, 5% fewer false positives than 2019), not results.

What it was built to do. Build selective lists of taxpayers at higher fiscal risk by cross-matching financial-relationship data with tax declarations.

The system's part

Not assessed. Agenda Digitale calls the application AI-based and Fiscomania describes risk analysis over cross-matched data. Neither gives the technique or any result, so the algorithm's contribution cannot be assessed. The Agenzia both runs and, as far as the sources show, operates the system in-house.

Replication

No replication cited.

What produced it. A legal basis (Law 160/2019 and the decree of 28 June 2022) permitting cross-matching of the financial-relationships archive with tax data under Garante safeguards.

What must hold again. Access to the Archivio dei Rapporti Finanziari. Pseudonymization during preliminary analysis and retention limits set by the Garante.
