Archaeologists confirm 303 new Nazca geoglyphs in a survey guided by AI

A deep-learning model from IBM Research and Yamagata University flagged likely sites in aerial photographs, and fieldwork in Peru confirmed 303 new Nazca geoglyphs.

  • 2 an organization

    Yamagata University's Institute of Nasca reports a sixteen-fold increase in the discovery rate. The German Aerospace Center says that the survey achieved in six months what had previously taken decades.

  • 3 society

    The known corpus of figurative geoglyphs at a UNESCO World Heritage site nearly doubled, with 303 confirmed sites added to Peru's archaeological record.

What happened

Between September 2022 and February 2023, archaeologists surveyed the Nazca Pampa in Peru. The sources give only the months of the survey. Masato Sakai of Yamagata University's Institute of Nasca led the team. They used candidate locations proposed by a deep-learning model developed with IBM Research. The model screened aerial photographs at 10-centimeter resolution over more than 600 square kilometers. Field verification confirmed 303 previously unknown figurative relief-type geoglyphs, almost doubling the known total. The model individually suggested 178 of them. The other 125 were found during the AI-guided survey without an individual suggestion. The team screened about 36 AI suggestions per promising candidate. With high-resolution imagery but without AI, discoveries had run at 18.7 a year from 2004 to 2020. The authors report a sixteen-fold increase in the discovery rate. The results appeared in the Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences on 23 September 2024. Johny Isla, Peru's chief archaeologist for the Nazca Lines, told The Guardian on 26 September 2024 that the new figures were too small for past overflights to have detected. Amina Jambajantsan of the Max Planck Institute of Geoanthropology, who was not involved, told CNN that doubling the known geoglyphs with limited training data was amazing. She added that AI models are not yet perfectly accurate. On 14 August 2025, The Art Newspaper reported that the same Yamagata University and IBM team had announced 248 further AI-assisted geoglyph finds at Expo 2025 Osaka. This brought the total to 893, of which 781 came from AI and aerial-image analysis.

Details

AI system

Model

ResNet50 with a two-layer classifier, adapted from ImageNet training. [1]

Provider

IBM Research and Yamagata University, with the German Aerospace Center. [1]

Role

The classifier estimated where relief-type geoglyphs might appear in aerial images. [1]

Configuration

The model analyzed 112 × 112-pixel images at 5-meter intervals. Candidate selection used probability threshold 0.55 and at least two adjoining image patches. [1]

Inputs and outputs

The classifier analyzed aerial photographs and returned a map of probabilities and candidate locations. [1]

Autonomy

Archaeologists screened the candidates and visited selected locations to confirm the geoglyphs. [1]

Workflow

High-resolution aerial imagery of the whole survey area. A detector trained with limited labeled examples of relief-type geoglyphs. Archaeologist screening of candidates followed by ground verification.

Human oversight

Archaeologists screened model suggestions and verified candidate geoglyphs on the ground before counting discoveries.

Benefits

Reach

The sources give no count of people who benefit. The survey confirmed 303 new geoglyphs, and the same team later announced 248 more, as The Art Newspaper reported on 14 August 2025. [1] [2] [3] [4] [5] [6] [7]

Duration

The field survey ran from September 2022 to February 2023. On 14 August 2025, The Art Newspaper reported that the same team had announced 248 further geoglyphs found with AI help, which brought the total to 893. [1] [2] [3] [4] [5] [6] [7]

Measurement

Measurement

Archaeologists screened AI-ranked candidate boxes on 10 cm aerial images and confirmed geoglyphs by survey on foot and with drones. [1]

Setting and sample

The Nazca Pampa and surrounding desert, more than 600 square kilometers; 341 candidate locations visited in the 2022/23 season. [1]

Measures

New figurative geoglyphs confirmed per year. [1]

Results

303 new figurative geoglyphs in one six-month season, which the authors call a 16-fold acceleration. [1]

Additional value from AI

178 of the 303 were individually suggested by the model; 66 more were found in AI-discovered groups, and 59 were found without AI help or outside the AI-suggested boxes. [1]

Limitations

The study is the developers' own; the comparison is with earlier periods and has no concurrent control; 968 promising candidates were not yet surveyed. [1]

Denominators

One field season, September 2022 to February 2023, 1,440 labor hours of survey. [1]

Materials for replication

Data are held by the Peruvian Ministry of Culture and are available on request with its permission. [1]

Follow-up

Conditions for replication

Targets must be visible at the imagery resolution (about 10 cm). Each candidate must be confirmed on the ground before being counted.
