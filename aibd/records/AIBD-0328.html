Ghost Murmur may have located a downed F-15E officer in Iran

Two anonymous sources credit the CIA's Ghost Murmur, quantum sensing paired with AI, with locating a downed F-15E officer in Iran; CBS reports that CIA technology pinpointed him and names no tool.

  • 5 a person

    The weapons systems officer was found alive in April 2026 and brought out with no US casualties, according to Ratcliffe and Trump. Whether Ghost Murmur produced the find rests on the Post's anonymous sources and Trump's “very important”. Physicists dispute the described mechanism, and a beacon and a long-range camera were also in play.

  • Not rated an organization

    No source quantifies the tool's contribution to the search beyond Trump's remark. The mission also used human assets, deception, a camera at 40 miles and the airman's beacon.

Brief from Peter

The April 6 White House briefing documents the rescue of a downed American airman in Iran.1 The claim that an AI-assisted quantum sensor located him comes from two anonymous sources in the New York Post. President Trump subsequently told the Post that the tool was important, without explaining how it worked.2 Physicists interviewed by the Post and Scientific American questioned the described capability.3 The rescue is established; the contribution attributed to Ghost Murmur remains unverified.

Details

AI system

Role

The reported AI role was separating a possible heartbeat signature from background sensor noise. That function remains an anonymous-source claim.

Workflow

A rescue operation that involved personnel, aircraft, intelligence, and camera observation. An alleged AI-assisted sensing contribution whose specifications and performance are not public.

AI contribution

The Post's anonymous sources describe AI that isolates a heartbeat signal from sensor noise. No public technical evidence establishes that this system located the airman. 60 Minutes reports that top-secret CIA technology pinpointed the officer, without naming the tool or saying that it used AI. The officer carried communication equipment, and messages from him reached US forces. [1] [2] [3] [4] [5] [6]
