Novissi targeting pays 138,531 rural beneficiaries in Togo

ML on satellite imagery and phone metadata set eligibility for Novissi rural cash; 138,531 beneficiaries paid, Nov 2020 to Aug 2021

ⓘ

What happened

The Government of Togo launched the Novissi emergency cash program on 8 April 2020. On 2 November 2020 it extended it to rural areas using a machine-learning pipeline built with UC Berkeley and IPA researchers. Satellite imagery produced poverty estimates for every canton to rank the poorest. Within eligible cantons a model trained on mobile-phone metadata predicted each subscriber's consumption to decide who was paid. Beneficiaries self-registered by USSD and received mobile-money installments of 8,170 CFA (women) or 7,000 CFA (men). The World Bank and Government of Togo report 138,531 rural beneficiaries from November 2020 to August 2021, funded by GiveDirectly (US$10 million). The Nature evaluation found phone-based targeting reduced exclusion errors by 4–21% relative to geographic alternatives.

Purpose. Prioritize the poorest cantons and the poorest mobile subscribers within them for COVID-19 emergency cash transfers in the absence of a social registry.

AI’s contribution

Decisive for who was paid: canton ranking and individual eligibility were both set by machine-learning predictions, while humans fixed the budget, thresholds and registration rules. The Nature evaluation is co-authored by the team that built the targeting method. The World Bank/Government of Togo case study is independent of the developers.

Beneficiaries

  • a person

    Five mobile-money installments of 8,170 CFA (women) or 7,000 CFA (men) to each of 138,531 rural beneficiaries during COVID-19 restrictions (World Bank study, Table 1).

  • an organization

    The Government of Togo delivered shock-responsive rural assistance without a social registry. The World Bank documents the model as a reusable case study.

  • society

Further evidence

Setting. Satellite-imagery poverty map ranking cantons. Consumption model trained on mobile-phone metadata with a recent phone survey as ground truth. USSD self-registration and mobile-money payment rails. Government and GiveDirectly funding decision.

Conditions. Target population owns and uses mobile phones (non-subscribers are excluded by design). Operators provide anonymized call detail records. Recent ground-truth survey available for training. Mobile-money infrastructure in place.
