Novissi targeting pays 138,531 rural beneficiaries in Togo

reduced exclusion errors by 4–21% relative to geographic alternatives

ML on satellite imagery and phone metadata set eligibility for Novissi rural cash; 138,531 beneficiaries paid, Nov 2020 to Aug 2021

What happened

The Government of Togo launched the Novissi emergency cash program on 8 April 2020 and on 2 November 2020 extended it to rural areas using a machine-learning pipeline built with UC Berkeley and IPA researchers. Satellite imagery produced poverty estimates for every canton to rank the poorest; within eligible cantons a model trained on mobile-phone metadata predicted each subscriber's consumption to decide who was paid. Beneficiaries self-registered by USSD and received mobile-money installments of 8,170 CFA (women) or 7,000 CFA (men). The World Bank and Government of Togo report 138,531 rural beneficiaries from November 2020 to August 2021, funded by GiveDirectly (US$10 million). The Nature evaluation found phone-based targeting reduced exclusion errors by 4–21% relative to geographic alternatives.

What it was built to do. Prioritise the poorest cantons and the poorest mobile subscribers within them for COVID-19 emergency cash transfers in the absence of a social registry.

The system's part

Decisive for who was paid: canton ranking and individual eligibility were both set by machine-learning predictions, while humans fixed the budget, thresholds and registration rules. The Nature evaluation is co-authored by the team that built the targeting method; the World Bank/Government of Togo case study is independent of the developers.

Replication

What produced it
  • Satellite-imagery poverty map ranking cantons
  • Consumption model trained on mobile-phone metadata with a recent phone survey as ground truth
  • USSD self-registration and mobile-money payment rails
  • Government and GiveDirectly funding decision
What must hold again
  • Target population owns and uses mobile phones (non-subscribers are excluded by design)
  • Operators provide anonymized call detail records
  • Recent ground-truth survey available for training
  • Mobile-money infrastructure in place
