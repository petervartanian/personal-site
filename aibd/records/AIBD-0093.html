Togo uses machine learning to send emergency cash to its rural poor

Machine-learning models using satellite imagery and phone metadata set eligibility for Togo's Novissi rural cash; 138,531 beneficiaries were paid from November 2020 to August 2021.

Reasons

People: Each of 138,531 rural beneficiaries received five mobile-money installments of 8,170 CFA (women) or 7,000 CFA (men), according to Table 1 of the World Bank study. The developers' randomized trial reports gains in food security, mental health and self-perceived economic status. A beneficiary told NPR that he paid school fees with the money. Organizations: The Government of Togo delivered shock-responsive rural assistance without a social registry. The World Bank documents the model as a reusable case study. Society: The program reached 138,531 rural beneficiaries. The authors of the Nature evaluation report that exclusion errors were 4 to 21 percent lower than with geographic targeting.
