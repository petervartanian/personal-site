Imagene AI predicts a tumor mutation, and treatment starts in two days

Imagene's algorithm predicted an EGFR mutation from a stained biopsy slide; after a rapid PCR test confirmed it, the patient began osimertinib two days after biopsy.

  • 3 a person

    Targeted therapy for leptomeningeal disease started a week earlier and whole-neuraxis radiotherapy was avoided, with MRI improvement at two months. No source reports a survival benefit.

  • 1 an organization

    The slide-based prediction let the team choose a single rapid PCR test on limited tissue without waiting for sequencing.

What happened

In July 2022 a previously healthy woman in her early 40s was admitted to Tel Aviv Sourasky Medical Center, Israel. She had a month of worsening headaches. Imaging showed a 2.6 cm left apical lung adenocarcinoma with leptomeningeal spread, and biopsy tissue was scarce. To guide gene testing without exhausting tissue, the team ran Imagene's deep-learning algorithm on the slide stained with hematoxylin and eosin (H&E). Within 48 hours of biopsy it inferred that the tumor was positive for a mutation in the epidermal growth factor receptor (EGFR) gene. A rapid Idylla polymerase chain reaction (PCR) assay confirmed the L858R exon 21 mutation, and osimertinib started two days after biopsy. The authors say that this was one week earlier than routine practice and avoided whole-neuraxis radiotherapy. Magnetic resonance imaging (MRI) at two months showed the enhancement gone. In February 2023 a second woman in her early 40s with EGFR-mutant lung cancer at the same center followed the same path, and her MRI also improved at two months. The case report was first published online on 18 July 2023. In January 2026 Imagene staff and partner pathologists reported that the company's later EGFR classifier reached an area under the curve of 0.87 on 968 slides from two centers. Of the slides that it called likely positive for EGFR, 79.3% carried the mutation on conventional testing.

Details

AI system

Inputs and outputs

Imagene analyzed the digitized H&E slide and inferred EGFR positivity, helping the team choose a rapid confirmatory PCR test.

Workflow

Scanning the diagnostic H&E slide and running the inference within 48 hours of biopsy. A rapid single-gene PCR assay available for confirmation.

Human oversight

Clinicians used the prediction to prioritize testing and began osimertinib only after PCR confirmed the mutation.

Benefits

Duration

Treatment began two days after biopsy, and MRI at two months showed that the meningeal enhancement had resolved.

Follow-up

Conditions for replication

PCR or sequencing confirmation before treatment. A tumor with a targetable driver the model can infer.
