Fine-tuned Aurora beats pretrained Aurora on past South African weather

Ishango's Project Umoya plans to fine-tune GraphCast and Aurora on African data; in a partner master's study, a fine-tuned small Aurora beat pretrained Aurora on past South African weather.

What happened

In June 2025, Ishango proposed fine-tuning GraphCast and Aurora with African weather datasets, including reanalysis and station observations. The intended benefit is more locally relevant forecasting for agriculture, energy, and weather services. At that time, data acquisition and model validation were in progress. Community training and networking supported development. On 17 August 2025, the authors of an AIMS South Africa master's study done with Ishango reported that fine-tuning the small Aurora model on South African data beat both pretrained Aurora models over South Africa on 2022 data. They report that the fine-tuned model predicted almost all the considered variables better, especially for forecasts up to 48 hours, with exceptions such as specific humidity. As of 29 October 2026, Ishango reports that it supervised three master's projects with AIMS South Africa and that this research phase is complete. Ishango reports that its proof of concept is still in progress. It plans deployment as the next phase and seeks funders and partners for it.

Details

AI system

Model

GraphCast and Aurora are the proposed base models for locally adapted forecasts.

Workflow

The account does not establish conditions sufficient to reproduce the benefit.

Measurement

Measurement

Hindcast scorecards comparing forecast error of fine-tuned and pretrained Aurora models by variable and lead time. [3]

Setting and sample

South Africa region at 0.25-degree resolution, HRES T0 data for 2022; Europe and the USA as reference regions. [3]

Measures

Forecast error per variable and lead time, shown as scorecards. [3]

Results

Fine-tuned AuroraSmall improved on almost all variables, most of all up to 48 hours, and beat pretrained AuroraLarge modestly over South Africa; specific humidity did not improve. [3]

Limitations

MSc study described in a blog post without peer review; the authors name the lack of high-resolution local data and limited compute as limits, and accuracy over South Africa stays below Europe and the USA. [3]

Materials for replication

Https://github.com/koomited/AIMS-PROJECT. [3]

People and places

The project needs access to African weather datasets, including reanalysis and station observations, before it can validate locally adapted models.
