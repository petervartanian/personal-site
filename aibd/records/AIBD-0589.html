What happened

In an interview on 18 June 2024, XR Guild founder Avi Bar-Zeev described plans for an AI librarian. He said that it would return relevant papers and postmortems in response to ethical dilemmas. He made the plan conditional on reliable retrieval. A public entry in the Guild's library visibly attributes its keywords to ChatGPT. The 2022 date on that entry belongs to the underlying video and does not date the keywords. The Guild's 2025-2026 annual report credits more than a dozen volunteers with a 2025 refresh of the library, which held 220 articles when the report appeared. The report does not mention an AI librarian. These sources establish a proposed assistant and some metadata work associated with AI. They do not establish that the proposed librarian was deployed or that AI improved retrieval, learning, or ethical decisions.
