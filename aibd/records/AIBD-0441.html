Hubei Telecom says that Di Ting cut new agents' training to one week

Hubei Telecom reports that its Di Ting assistant shortened training for new agents from three months to one week and cut average handling time by 10%.

What happened

Di Ting assists agents with intent recognition, information retrieval, and service actions in Hubei Telecom’s customer-service center. The company says that the agent recognizes intent with a TeleChat 32B or Qwen 8B model fine-tuned on about 300,000 dialogues between callers and agents. It reports that the training period for newly hired agents fell from three months to one week. It reports that the agent cut average handling time per operation by 10% and the time spent sorting and confirming information by 20%. The company also reports faster workflow changes and favorable user surveys. As of 3 June 2025, a voice version was handling calls in 18 scenarios, and fewer than 10% of complex calls were passed to human agents. No independent source validates these claims.
