Intelligent Anti-bacterial Platform flags resistance in an hour

AI read of a MALDI-TOF spectrum flagged ceftazidime resistance in 1 hour; 78-year-old septic woman switched to levofloxacin and recovered.

ⓘ

AI system

System

智抗菌平台 (Intelligent Anti-bacterial Platform) of China Medical University Hospital, in hospital-wide use since February 2022; its core 智能抗藥性細菌快速預測系統 applies machine learning to routine MALDI-TOF mass spectra linked to susceptibility records, covering nine bacterial species including Stenotrophomonas maltophilia

Workflow

The hospital's own laboratory identifies isolates by MALDI-TOF, so a spectrum exists as soon as a culture grows. A machine-learning classifier trained on the hospital's spectra and susceptibility records for Stenotrophomonas maltophilia and ceftazidime, one of nine species the platform covers. The platform returns the resistance prediction and a suggested alternative to the treating team within an hour.

Relation to AI

harm prevented

AI contribution

The platform's model read the isolate's routine MALDI-TOF spectrum and predicted ceftazidime resistance within one hour. That was about two days ahead of the conventional result, which the hospital says takes two to three days. It suggested levofloxacin; the clinicians made the switch and the antibiotic did the curing. The hospital puts the model's accuracy at 92% and argues each hour of delayed effective therapy in sepsis raises mortality by 7.6%. The case is reported only by the treating hospital, without independent review, and the counterfactual is earlier rather than different treatment.

Field

Healthcare - clinical microbiology and antibiotic therapy. Hospital laboratory AI (antibiotic-resistance prediction from MALDI-TOF spectra).

Purpose

The platform's core, the 智能抗藥性細菌快速預測系統 (intelligent rapid prediction system for antibiotic-resistant bacteria), was built to predict resistance from routine MALDI-TOF spectra within an hour. That replaces the two to three days the hospital gives for conventional susceptibility testing, so an effective antibiotic is given earlier. This case is that intended use.
