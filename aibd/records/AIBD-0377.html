Waymo pulls a car over; police find a loaded ghost gun with two minors

After Waymo detected a firearm violation, one of its driverless cars pulled over in San Francisco on 3 September 2026; police found a loaded ghost gun and booked two minors.

What happened

Shortly before 4 a.m. on Thursday 3 September 2026, Waymo pulled one of its driverless cars over in San Francisco's Richmond District. Waymo had detected a “violation of our terms of service involving a firearm”. The company alerted emergency services and “cooperated fully” with police, a Waymo spokesperson told the Los Angeles Times (12 September). The San Francisco Police Department said that officers made a “high-risk vehicle stop” and detained the two juvenile passengers. Officers found a loaded AR-style assault rifle, suspected marijuana and mace spray, and the two were taken to juvenile hall. The Independent, citing SFGATE, quotes police spokesperson Allison Maxie calling the weapon an “AR-styled assault rifle also known as a ghost gun”. It says that the boy and girl were booked into the Juvenile Justice Center on firearm-possession charges. The California Post puts the stop at 40th Avenue and Cabrillo Street. The Independent and Xataka both say that it is unclear how the firearm was detected. On 17 September 2026, Waymo spokesperson Julia Ilina told The Verge that the company uses automated technology to help its teams identify potential violations such as firearms inside the vehicle. She said that Waymo may contact local police when it detects a firearm. Waymo did not say whether software or staff flagged the weapon in this ride. It did not explain what role humans play between an automated alert and a call to police. No report through 29 October 2026 gives the outcome of the firearm charges against the two minors.
