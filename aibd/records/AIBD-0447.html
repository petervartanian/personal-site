AI and 5G-A widen the area monitored in a Xiamen dolphin reserve

China Mobile reports that its AI and 5G-A monitoring system widened the monitored area of a Xiamen reserve from 120 to 330 square kilometers and cut collision-related animal deaths by 97%.

What happened

The system at the Xiamen Chinese White Dolphin Amphioxus Nature Reserve combines vessel monitoring, analysis of wildlife data and management supported by AI. The ITU entry dates commercial deployment to 2024. In the same year TM Forum named the makers' Catalyst project its Outstanding Catalyst in Tech for Good. In the ITU entry, current to June 2025, China Mobile reports that the monitored area grew from 120 square kilometers under traditional methods to 330 square kilometers. It reports that wildlife distribution increased by 60% and that collision-related animal deaths fell by 97%. It also reports that detection and enforcement efficiency improved by 45% and that coastal enforcement deployments fell by 50%. The entry gives no underlying counts and no controlled comparison for these figures. On 11 November 2025 Huawei, a project partner, reported that a 5G-A network of 10 base stations still covered 330 square kilometers of Xiamen Bay. Huawei also reported that 12 vessels had been investigated for potentially endangering the dolphins.

AI system

Human oversight

Huawei reported on 11 November 2025 that the system sends alerts for violations such as boundary trespass or speeding and that law enforcement personnel verify violations through shore-based monitoring.
