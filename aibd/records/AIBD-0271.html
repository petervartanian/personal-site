GPT-5 proposes oncocytoma first in two retrospective cases

Sassari ophthalmologists gave ChatGPT (GPT-5) slit-lamp photos and brief summaries of two surgically confirmed caruncular oncocytomas; it proposed oncocytoma first in both. Retrospective proof only.

ⓘ

What happened

Sacchi et al. (Frontiers in Ophthalmology, 24 April 2026) retrospectively analyzed two cases of caruncular oncocytoma. Both had been excised and confirmed histopathologically. ChatGPT (GPT-5, 2025 version) received only slit-lamp photographs and a brief summary (age, sex, site). It proposed oncocytoma as the primary hypothesis in both. Differentials included papilloma, nevus, squamous cell carcinoma and melanoma. The authors call it a proof of concept that a general-purpose multimodal model can recognize a rare ocular surface tumor, limited by the sample size. The analysis was run on 8 September 2025, months after confirmation, with no effect on care. The authors declare no competing interests. Publication was 24 April 2026.

Purpose. Test whether a multimodal large language model can generate diagnostic hypotheses from slit-lamp images

AI’s contribution

GPT-5 produced the correct primary hypothesis in two retrospective cases when given images and minimal clinical data. The result is a proof of concept reported by non-vendor clinicians. It had no role in the patients' management.
