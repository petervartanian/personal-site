GPT-5 proposes oncocytoma first in two retrospective cases

off the scale

Sassari ophthalmologists gave ChatGPT (GPT-5) slit-lamp photos and brief summaries of two surgically confirmed caruncular oncocytomas; it proposed oncocytoma first in both. Retrospective proof only.

What happened

Sacchi et al. (Frontiers in Ophthalmology, 24 April 2026) retrospectively analyzed two cases of caruncular oncocytoma that had been excised and confirmed histopathologically. ChatGPT (GPT-5, 2025 version) received only slit-lamp photographs and a brief summary (age, sex, site) and proposed oncocytoma as the primary hypothesis in both, with differentials including papilloma, nevus, squamous cell carcinoma and melanoma. The authors call it a proof of concept that a general-purpose multimodal model can recognize a rare ocular surface tumor, limited by the sample size; the earlier verifier noted that the analysis was run on 8 September 2025, months after confirmation, with no effect on care. The authors declare no competing interests. The date is the analysis date given in the earlier reason; publication was 24 April 2026.

What it was built to do. Test whether a multimodal large language model can generate diagnostic hypotheses from slit-lamp images

The system's part

GPT-5 produced the correct primary hypothesis in two retrospective cases when given images and minimal clinical data; the result is a proof of concept reported by non-vendor clinicians and had no role in the patients' management.

Replication

No attempt to repeat it has been recorded.

What produced it. Slit-lamp photographs plus a brief clinical summary submitted to a multimodal model.

What must hold again. Prospective testing before any clinical use. Larger samples than two cases.
