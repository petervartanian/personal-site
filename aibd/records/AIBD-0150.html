Tenant may have overturned her eviction with ChatGPT and Perplexity

NBC News reported that Lynn White used ChatGPT and Perplexity to overturn her eviction on appeal; court records show that her later petitions in the case were denied in November 2025.

What happened

Lynn White, a small music production business owner living in a Long Beach, California, trailer park, was allegedly behind on rent and facing eviction. Represented by a lawyer from a local tenant advocacy network, she lost a jury trial. To appeal, she argued that Los Angeles Covid-era rules allowed a 12-month rent deferral. She gave her documents to ChatGPT and the AI search engine Perplexity. She used them to find procedural errors by the judge, to research the law and to draft her court responses. NBC News reported on 8 October 2025 that, after several months of litigation, she overturned the eviction. It said that she avoided roughly $55,000 in penalties and over $18,000 in overdue rent. NBC News gives no court, docket or ruling date for that result. Court of Appeal records show that on 5 November 2025 White, representing herself, petitioned the Court of Appeal, Second District, for a writ of mandate and a stay against the Los Angeles Superior Court and Judge Ronald F. Frank of the Long Beach courthouse. The petition concerned the same eviction case, 23LBUD01928, and the appellate division case 24APLC00165, with Best Trailer Park, LLC as the opposing party. The Court of Appeal denied the petition on 14 November 2025 and rejected a motion to vacate the denial on 17 November. The California Supreme Court allowed a late petition for review with a request for a stay and denied both on 26 November 2025 (S294091). A Community Note on an X post of 4 April 2026 cites these dockets.

Details

AI system

Role

White reported that the tools researched legal questions and drafted responses for her appeal.

Configuration

White used a paid ChatGPT subscription and Perplexity Pro. The report does not identify the models selected within those services.

Inputs and outputs

White supplied case documents and details. ChatGPT and Perplexity helped her identify possible procedural errors, research rules, and draft responses to the court.

Workflow

A litigant with time, her case documents and $40 a month for paid ChatGPT and Perplexity Pro. She fed them the record and detailed case facts. A substantive ground to find, here Los Angeles Covid-era rules allowing a 12-month rent deferral. Procedural errors at trial that an appeal could reach. A court system in which a self-represented tenant can appeal an eviction judgment.
