Later findings

On 5 November 2025 White, representing herself, petitioned the Court of Appeal, Second District, for a writ of mandate against the Los Angeles Superior Court and Judge Ronald F. Frank of the Long Beach courthouse, with a request for a stay. Best Trailer Park, LLC was the real party in interest. The trial court cases are the eviction case 23LBUD01928 and the appellate division case 24APLC00165. The court denied the petition on 14 November 2025 and rejected a motion to vacate the denial on 17 November. The California Supreme Court allowed a late petition for review with a stay request and denied both on 26 November 2025 (S294091).
