What happened

Newham Council and the University of East London propose an AI model that would use existing data to forecast housing demand and homelessness trends for temporary-accommodation planning. Development is planned, with deployment and housing improvements unverified. Council expenditure of more than £20 million is baseline spending. A separate procurement and allocation application is under consideration.

What it was built to do. Forecast housing demand and homelessness trends to support temporary-accommodation planning.
