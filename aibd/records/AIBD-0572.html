Newham plans AI forecasts of homelessness to guide temporary housing

Newham Council and the University of East London planned an unnamed AI model to forecast housing demand and homelessness; a partner later said that the models inform allocations, giving no results.

What happened

On 28 January 2025 the Local Government Association published an account in which Newham Council and the University of East London propose an AI model. The model would use existing data to forecast housing demand and homelessness trends for the planning of temporary accommodation. The account describes development as planned. It reports no deployment and no improvement in housing. It gives the council's spending on temporary housing in the past year as more than £20 million. A separate application for procurement and allocation is under consideration. On 23 April 2025 the London Office of Technology and Innovation, which the Local Government Association account names among the venture's leads, published a blog post by Piers Kelly. Kelly wrote that Newham launched the center at the 2024 DigiGov Expo and that its predictive models now inform housing allocations and social care interventions. Kelly gave no results, and no independent source confirms that use.
