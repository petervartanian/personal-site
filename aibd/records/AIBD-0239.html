OpenAI flag leads Brazilian police to hold a father a day early

AI system

System

ChatGPT with OpenAI's safety-monitoring pipeline (automated classifiers plus human review and law-enforcement referral)

Application developer

OpenAI

Workflow

The user typed explicit, dated threats against an identifiable child into ChatGPT over about two months, naming means and a hired killer. OpenAI's moderation process, automated classifiers plus human review, flagged the account. The company chose to refer it to the FBI under its policy on imminent threats to others. A standing FBI-to-Brazil channel through the Ministry of Justice CyberLab relayed the file to the state police within days. The Polícia Civil do Espírito Santo prioritized the case, identified the user, confirmed the child existed and obtained warrants before the stated date.

Relation to AI

OpenAI's automated classifiers and human reviewers flagged the user's messages, and the company chose to refer the account to the FBI. The system did not predict a crime or act on its own. The warrants, the arrest and the evidence-gathering were police and court actions. OpenAI sent only the user's prompts, not ChatGPT's replies. What the assistant said to the user, and whether it helped or hindered him, is unknown.

Depth

People: 3. Organizations: 2.

Reasons

People: The 8-year-old, and his mother who was placed under a no-contact order, were shielded from a plan the father had dated to 20 June. Police say the platform's information was corroborated when they arrived. He denies intent and no charge has been filed, so a life-saving effect is not established. Organizations: The Polícia Civil do Espírito Santo obtained a lead through the FBI and the Ministry of Justice CyberLab. It says it had no other way to get it. Its cybercrime chief called the alert another path for police work. The Ministry rated it the gravest of three such alerts in Brazil.

Entry details

Field

Public safety. Law enforcement and child protection.

Purpose

ChatGPT is a general-purpose conversational assistant. OpenAI's moderation layer is built to route conversations that indicate plans to harm others to human reviewers. Those reviewers may refer an imminent threat to law enforcement, under a policy published 26 August 2025. The referral that triggered the arrest was that layer doing what it was built to do, not an emergent use of the assistant.
