Workflow

The user typed explicit, dated threats against an identifiable child into ChatGPT over about two months, naming means and a hired killer. OpenAI's moderation process, automated classifiers plus human review, flagged the account. The company chose to refer it to the FBI under its policy on imminent threats to others. A standing FBI-to-Brazil channel through the Ministry of Justice CyberLab relayed the file to the state police within days. The Polícia Civil do Espírito Santo prioritized the case, identified the user, confirmed the child existed and obtained warrants before the stated date.

Purpose

ChatGPT is a general-purpose conversational assistant. OpenAI's moderation layer is built to route conversations that indicate plans to harm others to human reviewers. Those reviewers may refer an imminent threat to law enforcement, under a policy published 26 August 2025. The referral that triggered the arrest was that layer doing what it was built to do, not an emergent use of the assistant.
