OpenAI flag leads Brazilian police to hold a father a day early

OpenAI flagged a user's plan to kill his 8-year-old son; relayed via the FBI, Brazilian police held him a day before his stated date.

ⓘ

What happened

In 2026 a 36-year-old farmer in São Gabriel da Palha, Espírito Santo, Brazil, told ChatGPT about a plan to kill his 8-year-old son. He said he had offered R$50,000 to a hitman and set 20 June as the date. OpenAI's safety systems flagged the account, and the company referred it to the FBI. The FBI passed it to the Ministry of Justice's CyberLab. The Polícia Civil received it on 16 June and, under warrants, arrested him on 19 June. Officers seized his phone, four bottles of an unidentified substance and a knotted rope. He admitted the searches but denied intent. He was freed on habeas corpus on 13 August 2026 after 54 days, under an ankle monitor and a no-contact order. The inquiry continues.

Purpose. ChatGPT is a general-purpose conversational assistant. OpenAI's moderation layer is built to route conversations that indicate plans to harm others to human reviewers. Those reviewers may refer an imminent threat to law enforcement, under a policy published 26 August 2025. The referral that triggered the arrest was that layer doing what it was built to do, not an emergent use of the assistant.

AI’s contribution

OpenAI's automated classifiers and human reviewers flagged the user's messages, and the company chose to refer the account to the FBI. The system did not predict a crime or act on its own. The warrants, the arrest and the evidence-gathering were police and court actions. OpenAI sent only the user's prompts, not ChatGPT's replies. What the assistant said to the user, and whether it helped or hindered him, is unknown.

Beneficiaries

  • a person

    The 8-year-old, and his mother who was placed under a no-contact order, were shielded from a plan the father had dated to 20 June. Police say the platform's information was corroborated when they arrived. He denies intent and no charge has been filed, so a life-saving effect is not established.

  • an organization

    The Polícia Civil do Espírito Santo obtained a lead through the FBI and the Ministry of Justice CyberLab. It says it had no other way to get it. Its cybercrime chief called the alert another path for police work. The Ministry rated it the gravest of three such alerts in Brazil.

Further evidence

Setting. The user typed explicit, dated threats against an identifiable child into ChatGPT over about two months, naming means and a hired killer. OpenAI's moderation process, automated classifiers plus human review, flagged the account. The company chose to refer it to the FBI under its policy on imminent threats to others. A standing FBI-to-Brazil channel through the Ministry of Justice CyberLab relayed the file to the state police within days. The Polícia Civil do Espírito Santo prioritized the case, identified the user, confirmed the child existed and obtained warrants before the stated date.

Conditions. The user discloses the plan to the assistant rather than keeping it offline. The vendor's reviewers judge the threat credible and imminent and choose referral rather than only an account ban. A cross-border law-enforcement channel exists and acts within days. Local police can identify the user from the vendor's data and secure judicial warrants before the stated date.
