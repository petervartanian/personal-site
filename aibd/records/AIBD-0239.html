OpenAI flag leads Brazilian police to hold a father a day early

OpenAI's safety systems flagged a ChatGPT user's plan to kill his 8-year-old son; alerted through the FBI, Brazilian police held him a day before his stated date.

  • 3 a person

    The 8-year-old, and his mother who was placed under a no-contact order, were shielded from a plan that the father had dated to 20 June. Police say that the platform's information was corroborated when they arrived. He denies intent and no charge has been filed, so a life-saving effect is not established.

  • 2 an organization

    The Polícia Civil do Espírito Santo obtained a lead through the FBI and the Ministry of Justice CyberLab. It says that it had no other way to get it. Its cybercrime chief called the alert another path for police work. The Ministry rated it the gravest of three such alerts in Brazil.

What happened

In 2026 a 36-year-old farmer in São Gabriel da Palha, Espírito Santo, Brazil, told ChatGPT about a plan to kill his 8-year-old son. He said that he had offered R$50,000 to a hitman and that he had set 20 June as the date. OpenAI's safety systems flagged the account, and the company referred it to the FBI. The FBI passed it to the Ministry of Justice's CyberLab. The Polícia Civil do Espírito Santo received it on 16 June and arrested him under warrants on 19 June. Officers seized his phone, four bottles of an unidentified substance and a knotted rope. He admitted the searches but denied intent. He was freed on habeas corpus on 13 August 2026 after 54 days, under an ankle monitor and a no-contact order. The inquiry continues. BBC News Brasil reported on 14 August 2026 that the court freed him because the inquiry had run too long without a charge. Legal experts whom BBC News Brasil interviewed said that the messages justified pretrial detention. They said that the messages alone would not support a charge. The lead investigator told BBC News Brasil that OpenAI sent the user's prompts without ChatGPT's replies. In early October 2026 OpenAI closed the account of a user who said that he wanted to shoot President Lula after seeing him at a campaign event, and the company passed the data to the Federal Police.

Details

AI system

Inputs and outputs

The referral supplied the user’s threatening messages and information that let investigators identify him. Police compared those messages with evidence from his seized phone.

Workflow

The user typed explicit, dated threats against an identifiable child into ChatGPT over about two months, naming means and a hired killer. OpenAI's moderation process, automated classifiers plus human review, flagged the account. The company chose to refer it to the FBI under its policy on imminent threats to others. A standing FBI-to-Brazil channel through the Ministry of Justice CyberLab relayed the file to the state police within days. The Polícia Civil do Espírito Santo prioritized the case, identified the user, confirmed the child existed and obtained warrants before the stated date.

Human oversight

OpenAI's automated classifiers and human reviewers flagged the messages, and the company chose to refer the account to the FBI. The FBI and the Ministry of Justice's CyberLab passed it to the state police, who obtained warrants before the arrest. After the arrest the police examined whether he had taken concrete steps toward the plans. A court freed him on habeas corpus on 13 August 2026. [1] [2] [3] [4] [5] [6] [7]

Follow-up

Conditions for replication

The user discloses the plan to the assistant rather than keeping it offline. The vendor's reviewers judge the threat credible and imminent and choose referral rather than only an account ban. A cross-border law-enforcement channel exists and acts within days. Local police can identify the user from the vendor's data and secure judicial warrants before the stated date.

Purpose

ChatGPT is a general-purpose conversational assistant. OpenAI's moderation layer is built to route conversations that indicate plans to harm others to human reviewers. Those reviewers may refer an imminent threat to law enforcement, under a policy published 26 August 2025. The referral that triggered the arrest came from that layer working as it was built to work.
