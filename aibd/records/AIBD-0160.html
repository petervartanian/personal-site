Istanbul police's AVCI detains 325 and closes 69 dealer groups

depth 2 for an organization, depth 2 for society

325 detained and remanded, 69 groups closed

Istanbul police's AI-based AVCI system, first used 10 Feb 2026 in a 14-province drug operation: 325 detained and remanded, 69 groups closed.

What happened

AVCI (Açık Kaynak İstihbaratı Analiz Sistemi, Open Source Intelligence Analysis System) is an AI-supported program built in-house by the Istanbul Police Department's narcotics and cybercrime divisions with a special software team, on the instruction of police chief Selami Yıldız. It infiltrates closed groups and channels on end-to-end encrypted messaging apps, analyzes messages, voice notes, photos and location data, decodes dealer slang, recovers deleted messages, links fake profiles and maps group hierarchies. Anadolu Agency reported on 6 April 2026 that AVCI was first used on 10 February 2026 in a drug operation centered on Istanbul across 14 provinces: 325 suspects were detained, all 325 referred to court were remanded in custody, and 69 messaging-app groups were closed.

What it was built to do. AVCI was built by Istanbul police to infiltrate and analyze encrypted messaging groups used for drug dealing and to identify suspects, fake profiles and network hierarchies; the 10 February operation was that intended use.

The system's part

By the police account relayed by Anadolu Agency, AVCI infiltrated closed messaging groups, decoded slang and coded language, recovered deleted messages, linked fake profiles through IP analysis and mapped group hierarchies, producing the suspects and groups targeted on 10 February. Detentions, referrals and remands were carried out by police and courts. No independent evaluation of the system exists in the sources, no official is quoted on how many of the 325 were identified by AVCI rather than by conventional work, and the sources name no developer beyond the police's own software team.

Replication

No attempt to repeat it has been recorded.

What produced it. In-house police software that infiltrates closed groups on encrypted messaging apps and analyzes messages, media and location data with natural language processing. A coordinated same-day operation across 14 provinces in which the identified suspects were detained and referred to court.

What must hold again. Police have access to the closed groups and to the server-side data the sources say the system uses to recover deleted messages and link profiles. Courts remand the suspects; the sources report all 325 remanded but no later charging or conviction outcomes. The attribution of the suspects to AVCI rather than to conventional investigation is the police's own; no independent evaluation exists.
