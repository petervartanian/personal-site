Istanbul police's AVCI detains 325 and closes 69 dealer groups

Istanbul police's AI-based AVCI system, first used 10 February 2026 in a 14-province drug operation: 325 detained and remanded, 69 groups closed.

ⓘ

What happened

AVCI (Açık Kaynak İstihbaratı Analiz Sistemi, Open Source Intelligence Analysis System) is an AI-supported program. The Istanbul Police Department's narcotics and cybercrime divisions built it in-house with a special software team. Police chief Selami Yıldız ordered the work. It infiltrates closed groups and channels on end-to-end encrypted messaging apps. It analyzes messages, voice notes, photos and location data. It decodes dealer slang, recovers deleted messages, links fake profiles and maps group hierarchies. Anadolu Agency reported on 6 April 2026 that AVCI was first used on 10 February 2026. The drug operation was centered on Istanbul and ran across 14 provinces. 325 suspects were detained. All 325 referred to court were remanded in custody. 69 messaging-app groups were closed.

Purpose. Istanbul police built AVCI to infiltrate and analyze encrypted messaging groups used for drug dealing. It also identifies suspects, fake profiles and network hierarchies. The 10 February operation was that intended use.

AI’s contribution

By the police account relayed by Anadolu Agency, AVCI infiltrated closed messaging groups and decoded slang and coded language. It recovered deleted messages, linked fake profiles through IP analysis and mapped group hierarchies. That work produced the suspects and groups targeted on 10 February. Police and courts carried out the detentions, referrals and remands. The sources include no independent evaluation of the system. No official is quoted on how many of the 325 were identified by AVCI rather than by conventional work. The sources name no developer beyond the police's own software team.

Beneficiaries

  • an organization

    In one operation the force detained 325 suspects across 14 provinces and closed 69 dealer groups. All 325 were remanded. The sources give no comparison with operations before AVCI, so the gain to the force is not measured.

  • society

    The police say the operation disrupted drug sales networks run through encrypted groups and social media across 14 provinces. No source measures any effect on drug supply or on the public. A population-level benefit is not established.

Further evidence

Setting. In-house police software that infiltrates closed groups on encrypted messaging apps and analyzes messages, media and location data with natural language processing. A coordinated same-day operation across 14 provinces in which the identified suspects were detained and referred to court.

Conditions. Police have access to the closed groups and to the server-side data the sources say the system uses to recover deleted messages and link profiles. Courts remand the suspects. The sources report all 325 remanded, but no later charging or conviction outcomes. The attribution of the suspects to AVCI, rather than to conventional investigation, is the police's own. No independent evaluation exists.
