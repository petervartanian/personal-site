What happened

AVCI (Açık Kaynak İstihbaratı Analiz Sistemi, Open Source Intelligence Analysis System) is an AI-supported program. The Istanbul Police Department's narcotics and cybercrime divisions built it in-house with a special software team. Police chief Selami Yıldız ordered the work. It infiltrates closed groups and channels on end-to-end encrypted messaging apps. It analyzes messages, voice notes, photos and location data. It decodes dealer slang, recovers deleted messages, links fake profiles and maps group hierarchies. Anadolu Agency reported on 6 April 2026 that AVCI was first used on 10 February 2026. The drug operation was centered on Istanbul and ran across 14 provinces. 325 suspects were detained. All 325 referred to court were remanded in custody. 69 messaging-app groups were closed.

What it was built to do. Istanbul police built AVCI to infiltrate and analyze encrypted messaging groups used for drug dealing. It also identifies suspects, fake profiles and network hierarchies. The 10 February operation was that intended use.

The system's part

By the police account relayed by Anadolu Agency, AVCI infiltrated closed messaging groups and decoded slang and coded language. It recovered deleted messages, linked fake profiles through IP analysis and mapped group hierarchies. That work produced the suspects and groups targeted on 10 February. Police and courts carried out the detentions, referrals and remands. The sources include no independent evaluation of the system. No official is quoted on how many of the 325 were identified by AVCI rather than by conventional work. The sources name no developer beyond the police's own software team.

Replication

No replication cited.

What produced it. In-house police software that infiltrates closed groups on encrypted messaging apps and analyzes messages, media and location data with natural language processing. A coordinated same-day operation across 14 provinces in which the identified suspects were detained and referred to court.

What must hold again. Police have access to the closed groups and to the server-side data the sources say the system uses to recover deleted messages and link profiles. Courts remand the suspects. The sources report all 325 remanded, but no later charging or conviction outcomes. The attribution of the suspects to AVCI, rather than to conventional investigation, is the police's own. No independent evaluation exists.
