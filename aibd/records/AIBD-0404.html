What happened

The proposed augmentative and alternative communication tool would transform difficult-to-understand speech into clearer output. Limi Hospital is named as a testbed, while the technical development is described as future work. The intended benefit is greater communication independence. The source does not document restored communication for a user.

What it was built to do. Support communication for people with speech disabilities.
