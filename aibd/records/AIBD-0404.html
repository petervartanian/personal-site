What happened

The AI4Africa team in Nigeria, with Emmanuella Sule and Dr. Houda Chihi among its contacts, proposes an augmentative and alternative communication tool that would transform difficult-to-understand speech into clearer output. The team describes the technical development as future work. It plans to train a dysarthric speech encoder on the UASpeech dataset and later fine-tune it on speech collected from Nigerian hospitals. The report states that an affiliation with Limi Hospital or other Nigerian hospitals will be pursued to test the tool, so the hospital was not yet a confirmed testbed. The intended benefit is greater independence in communication. No later report of a test or a user result had been published by October 2026. No source documents restored communication for a user.

Details

AI system

Model

The proposed speech-conversion design is based on Tacotron, with a speech encoder trained through knowledge distillation from a text encoder.

Inputs and outputs

The proposed tool takes difficult-to-understand speech and produces clearer speech for the user’s communication partner.
