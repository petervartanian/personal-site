Culturalink connects cultural knowledge across countries

Culturalink organizes heritage information and generates cultural narratives, and it uses the DeepSeek language model to link heritages; no source measures a learning or preservation result.

  • Not rated a person

    Cultural literacy and understanding are intended, but no learning or access improvement is measured.

  • Not rated an organization

    Heritage organizations are intended to gain analysis and narrative tools; no operational improvement is reported.

  • Not rated society

    Cultural preservation and representation are intended benefits; no preservation result is demonstrated.

What happened

The Culturalink website names Yi Zeng of the Institute of Automation, Chinese Academy of Sciences, as principal investigator. On 30 June 2024, Xinhua Silk Road reported that the project, then called the Cultural Interactions Engine, had users in 84 countries. In the 2025 report of the International Telecommunication Union (ITU), current as of June 2025, the project team describes a research platform that organizes heritage information and generates cultural narratives. The final ITU report names DeepSeek as the language model behind the cross-heritage links. The team describes benefits for learning, representation and preservation and measures none of them. The site was still online in October 2026, and it reports no learning results.

Details

AI system

Reported configuration

DeepSeek (large language model used for cross-heritage reasoning)

Role

Culturalink combines multimodal classification, a cultural knowledge graph, and semantic mapping to connect heritage material and generate cultural content.
