Letrus essay feedback lifts Brazilian students' exam essay scores

In a 2019 trial in 178 public high schools in Espírito Santo, Brazil, Letrus automated essay scoring and feedback raised ENEM essay scores by 0.094 standard deviations.

Schools were randomly assigned to the automated system alone, to the system with human graders, or to no system. What happened

Bruno Ferman, Lycia Lima and Flavio Riva of the São Paulo schools of economics and business administration at FGV ran a randomized trial from March to November 2019 with the Espírito Santo state education department and an education technology company. Exame reports that the company was Letrus and that J-PAL funded the trial. The trial covered 178 public high schools with about 19,000 senior students. In 55 schools, students wrote practice essays for the national college entrance exam (ENEM) on a platform whose automated writing evaluation system gave instant feedback and a predicted score. Another 55 schools received the same system plus grades and comments from human graders, and 68 schools served as the comparison group. Both versions raised essay scores by 0.094 standard deviations, pooled across the official 2019 ENEM essay and an independently administered essay of the same format. The human graders added no further gain. The authors report that teachers shifted time from routine grading toward individual discussions with students about their essays. The paper is a working paper that the authors posted in February 2021, and it does not name the company.

Details

AI system

Workflow

Students wrote practice essays for the ENEM on a platform that scored them at once and flagged errors. Teachers used the time saved on grading for individual feedback. The state gave the schools laptops before the program began.

Measurement

Measurement

ENEM essay scores from the official 2019 exam and an independently administered essay with the same structure, graded on the ENEM's five competencies. [2]

Setting and sample

178 public high schools in Espírito Santo, Brazil, with about 19,000 senior students; 55 schools per treatment arm and 68 comparison schools. [2]

Measures

Full ENEM essay score in standard deviations. [2]

Results

0.094 standard deviations for both the automated-only and the automated-plus-human-grader versions. [2]

Additional value from AI

The automated-only arm matched the arm with human graders, so the gain does not depend on human grading. [2]

Limitations

Working paper; pooled outcome across two essays; the company is not named in the paper. [2]

Denominators

Five practice essays over the 2019 school year. [2]

Materials for replication

AEA RCT Registry AEARCTR-0003729. [2]

Follow-up

Conditions for replication

Schools need working computers and an internet connection. Teachers must assign the practice essays and discuss the results with students.

1 follow-up entry. Outcome unresolved: 1.

The system was built to grade practice essays for the ENEM and give students feedback, so that teachers could spend less time on routine grading. Higher essay scores were that purpose.
