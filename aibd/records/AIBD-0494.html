Researchers test adaptive credit-score cutoffs in simulation

Researchers at the Vellore Institute of Technology propose a model that adjusts credit-scoring thresholds for subprime lending and have tested it in synthetic simulations.

What happened

Parth Khairnar and Geetha S at the Vellore Institute of Technology, Chennai, propose a model that adjusts credit-scoring thresholds for subprime lending. The team says that it used the public Home Credit Default Risk dataset. The team reports that initial testing used synthetic simulations in Google Colab. The team says that a rural implementation and a peer-reviewed paper were still to come. Borrowing and lending benefits in practice are unreported.

Details

AI system

Model

The proposed scoring method uses LightGBM with cross-validation, Bayesian hyperparameter optimization, and MCMC estimates of uncertainty.

Role

The proposed model adjusts the credit-score threshold used to accept or reject subprime lending applications.

Workflow

Conditions that could produce the benefit are unspecified.
