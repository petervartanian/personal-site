Crash warnings and automatic braking cut rear-end crash rates up to 50%

In an Insurance Institute for Highway Safety study, forward collision warning alone, low-speed emergency braking and warning with braking cut rear-end striking crash rates by 27%, 43% and 50%.

Relation to AI

Front crash prevention systems are automated perception-and-braking systems. The within-model comparisons (FCW alone, FCW with AEB) and Volvo S60/XC60 comparison with other luxury midsize cars and SUVs (low-speed AEB) control for other factors. Later findings

NHTSA adopted FMVSS No. 127 on 9 May 2024 after citing this study in its proposed rule. The rule requires AEB on new light vehicles from 1 September 2029. Auto Innovators asked the D.C. Circuit to review the rule on 17 January 2025, and the case has been in abeyance since February 2025. On 23 March 2026 the Department of Transportation told the court that it is preparing a proposal to amend the rule. The National Law Review reports that the proposal would extend the compliance lead time by two years. [4]
