What happened

Crowdworks AI and an anonymized South Korean construction firm, Company S, developed Safety Talk, a translation app for construction and shipbuilding worksites. They built it with specialized terminology and worker feedback. General translation apps such as Google Translate and Papago had mistranslated construction terms, according to the developer. In its account for the International Telecommunication Union, Crowdworks AI states that Safety Talk is a fine-tuned Gemma-2-9B-IT model. It reports that real-world tests using 1.05 million data samples confirmed over 90% translation accuracy. It also reports field testing in more than 20 languages, with over 90% user satisfaction. In a post of 28 July 2025, Crowdworks describes the 1.05 million figure as user tests on real sites. Injury prevention and wider productivity gains remain intended and have not been separately measured.
