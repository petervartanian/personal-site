Metalesa AI pilot lights wild-boar warning signs on the CV-600

Europa Press (12 Feb 2026): a solar-powered system combining cameras, computer vision and detection algorithms lights warning signs only when animals are present; the provincial council will evalua...

What happened

Europa Press reported on 12 Feb 2026, from a statement by the Diputació de València, that the provincial council had begun pilot tests on the CV-600 road, in the area of Simat de la Valldigna and Benifairó de la Valldigna, of an intelligent signalling system designed to detect wild boar near the carriageway and automatically activate luminous warning signs. The system, carried out by the Valencian road-safety company Metalesa with civil works by Grupo Bertolín, combines cameras with computer vision and automatic detection algorithms that identify animals passing and activate dynamic luminous signs only when a real risk exists, and is powered by photovoltaic energy. Council president Vicent Mompó, who visited the site with roads deputy Reme Mazzolari, said that after some weeks the council would evaluate the results and, if effective, extend the model to the most sensitive stretches of the provincial network. No detection counts or accident figures are given; the article was read through the Wayback Machine because the publisher blocks automated fetching. The OECD AI Incidents Monitor lists the pilot (2026-02-12-aa5d) as an aggregate benefit.

What it was built to do. The system is built to detect wild boar near the road and warn drivers only when animals are present.
