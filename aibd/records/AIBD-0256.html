What happened

Europa Press reported on 12 Feb 2026, from a statement by the Diputació de València, on a set of pilot tests. The provincial council had begun them on the CV-600 road, in the area of Simat de la Valldigna and Benifairó de la Valldigna. The intelligent signaling system is designed to detect wild boar near the carriageway and automatically activate luminous warning signs. The Valencian road-safety company Metalesa carried out the work, with civil works by Grupo Bertolín. The system combines cameras with computer vision and automatic detection algorithms that identify animals passing. It activates dynamic luminous signs only when a real risk exists, and it is powered by photovoltaic energy. Council president Vicent Mompó visited the site with roads deputy Reme Mazzolari. He said that after some weeks the council would evaluate the results.

If the system is effective, the council would extend the model to the most sensitive stretches of the provincial network. No detection counts or accident figures are given. The OECD AI Incidents Monitor lists the pilot as an aggregate benefit.

What it was built to do. The system is built to detect wild boar near the road and warn drivers only when animals are present.

Replication

What produced it. A road stretch with frequent wildlife crossings. Camera detection tied to dynamic signs powered by solar panels. A road authority committed to evaluating before extending.

What must hold again. Detection works at night when boar move. Drivers slow in response to the lights.
