What happened

Dr. Dhananjay Kumar of Anna University, Chennai, is the first contact for the project. The team designs the rehabilitation system to assess movement and give immediate corrective feedback. The system pairs a camera and a Raspberry Pi with a cloud model that scores each exercise. The team reports that the project was at its inception stage and that it intended to seek technical input from the National Institute of Information and Communications Technology in Tokyo. The team proposes laboratory testing followed by testing in a home setting, alongside future 3D sensing and language support. The team intends the system to improve exercise performance, recovery, and remote access to rehabilitation. No source reports these outcomes. No later report of a test or a patient result had been published by October 2026.
