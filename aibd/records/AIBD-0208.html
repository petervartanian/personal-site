IMIE intelligent retina restores partial vision to a blind woman

Blind over a decade, a 61-year-old woman read E optotypes and walked indoors unaided after a 256-channel IMIE retinal implant in Changsha.

ⓘ

AI system

System

IMIE intelligent retina (IMIE智能视网膜): 256-channel flexible epiretinal electrode array fixed on the macula, with camera glasses and an external video processing unit transmitting wirelessly to the implant

Workflow

Surviving retinal ganglion cells and optic nerve in end-stage retinitis pigmentosa, so that epiretinal stimulation can reach the visual cortex. A 256-channel flexible array fixed on the macula with 250-micrometer tacks and per-patient fitting of stimulation currents. Camera glasses and an external processor converting live video into stimulation patterns in real time. More than a month of structured visual rehabilitation after implantation. A surgical team with prior IMIE 256 experience (five implants reported in TVST 2021) and on-site guidance from Mark Humayun.

Human oversight

Not assessed

Relation to AI

The system is the whole substitute visual pathway. Camera glasses capture the scene. An external processor converts each frame in real time into stimulation patterns across 256 electrodes. The implant stimulates surviving ganglion cells whose signals reach the visual cortex. The patient had no vision before, so the benefit is attributable to the system together with structured rehabilitation. The 2026 reports describe the conversion only as an algorithm inside an “intelligent” retina. The 2021 IMIE 256 paper describes a video processing unit with a per-electrode threshold map and 15 gray levels and no learned model. How much of that processing is AI in a machine-learning sense is not established by these sources.

AI contribution

The IMIE system converted images from camera glasses into electrical stimulation through an implanted array. The patient also received rehabilitation to learn to use the resulting visual signals.

Follow-up

Conditions for replication

The implant stays positioned on the macula without serious complications. The 2021 series saw one array movement and one hypotony. Rehabilitation continues; the hospital's one-year observation period is still running. The reported acuity (0.03, peak 0.1) is phosphene vision, not natural sight. The hospital says the patient lacks full self-care ability.
