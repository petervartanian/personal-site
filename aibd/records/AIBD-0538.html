Zimbabwe pilots AI phone advice for farmers; gains are only projected

Hurudza AI, a contact center that uses a GPT-2 model trained on Shona, gives farmers advice by voice and text in pilots in three wards of Zimbabwe; its gains are projections.

  • Not rated a person

    Zimbabwean farmers are intended to receive accessible advice and increase income; the 25% income gain and 40% loss reduction are projections.

  • Not rated an organization

    Agricultural extension organizations are intended to expand service coverage.

  • Not rated society

    Food security and climate resilience are intended, with no attributable public outcome.

What happened

In April 2024 the resident representative of the United Nations Development Programme (UNDP) in Zimbabwe praised the Hurudza AI idea at the Innovative Forum of the Zimbabwe International Trade Fair (ZITF). Hurudza AI is an AI contact center that assists farmers with climate-smart agriculture. In its June 2025 account to the International Telecommunication Union, the Postal and Telecommunications Regulatory Authority of Zimbabwe (POTRAZ) describes pilots in three wards that give agricultural advice by voice and text message. Development was continuing at that time. The account's figures for income, loss reduction and adoption are projections, including a 40% reduction in post-harvest losses. In March 2026 Hurudza AI and Paltech Africa announced solar-powered Farmers' Bench units for the service. Developing Telecoms reported that the joint project was at the pilot phase, with plans to expand across provinces. No source reports how many farmers used the service or what they gained.

Details

AI system

Model

The submission describes GPT-2 adapted to Shona, local-language speech models, and CNN-based crop-disease prediction.

Reported configuration

GPT-2 custom-trained on Shona data, with fine-tuned speech models

Workflow

Conditions that could produce the benefit are unspecified.

Benefits

Reach

The June 2025 account targets more than 10,000 rural farmers by 2026, which is an intended reach. No source reports how many farmers used the service or what they gained. [1] [2] [3]

Duration

The joint project of Hurudza AI and Paltech Africa was at the pilot phase in March 2026, when the two announced solar-powered Farmers' Bench units for the service. No later report gives user numbers or results, so no benefit is shown to persist. [1] [2] [3]
