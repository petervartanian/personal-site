FOX matches or beats manual cochlear-implant fitting in RCT

In a Brussels crossover RCT, newly implanted patients fitted with FOX had better speech intelligibility at 3 and 12 months; all manual-arm patients kept the FOX map; 47 experienced users improved.

Relation to AI

The software proposes MAP settings from measured outcomes. The trials compare it with manual fitting and attribute the measured gains to the FOX maps. All three papers share co-author P. J. Govaerts.
