FOX matches or beats manual cochlear-implant fitting in RCT

In a Brussels crossover RCT, newly implanted patients fitted with FOX had better speech intelligibility at 3 and 12 months; all manual-arm patients kept the FOX map; 47 experienced users improved.

ⓘ

What happened

Wathour et al. (Ear and Hearing, published online 21 December 2022) ran a one-year randomized crossover trial. It had 24 newly implanted cochlear-implant patients at Cliniques universitaires Saint-Luc, Brussels. It compared manual fitting with the Fitting to Outcome eXpert (FOX). From 3 months the FOX group showed less variability and better speech intelligibility in quiet and noise. Median phoneme scores were 50%, 70% and 50% at 55, 70 and 85 dB SPL versus 45%, 50% and 40%. After crossover, 100% of the manual group kept the FOX map and 82% performed better with it. 63% of the FOX group preferred their manual map for comfort. A companion study (Otology & Neurotology, 18 January 2023) gave 47 experienced users a FOX map. It found median improvements of 10 dB at 6,000 Hz and 10% in phonemic discrimination and speech scores after 15 days of take-home use.

Two 2019 case studies from the same group reported improved word recognition with AI suggestions in one of two adults. Otoconsult's site describes FOX as an outcome-based application assisting audiologists and AI-FOX as the successor of a deterministic first-generation tool. P. J. Govaerts co-authored all three papers. Whether any author is affiliated with Otoconsult could not be verified from the records read.

Purpose. Recommend cochlear-implant MAP settings to audiologists from measured auditory outcomes

AI’s contribution

The software proposes MAP settings from measured outcomes. The trials compare it with manual fitting and attribute the measured gains to the FOX maps. All three papers share co-author P. J. Govaerts. The developer's identity is taken from Otoconsult's own site. No record independent of the authors' group is among the sources.

Beneficiaries

  • a person

    Better speech intelligibility at 3 and 12 months in newly implanted patients. Median 10% gains in speech scores for experienced users.

  • an organization

    Authors report the FOX approach equivalent or superior in “hearing performance, comfort, and resources”.

  • unrated
    society

    Single-center studies. No population-level outcome.

Further evidence

Setting. Outcome measurements (thresholds, loudness scaling, speech scores) fed to the FOX recommender. Audiologists applying the recommended MAPs.

Conditions. Clinicians accept and apply FOX recommendations. Patients tolerate the map (63% of FOX-arm patients preferred manual maps for comfort).
