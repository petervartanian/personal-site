Workflow

A model trained on historical fraud and error data in advance claims. Referrals above a threshold are reviewed by caseworkers alongside control-group referrals with scores removed.

Not assessed

Relation to AI

The NAO describes a model trained on historical fraud and error data that scores advance requests and refers those above a threshold. Every payment decision is made by a caseworker, so the model's contribution is prioritization. Developer and deployer are the same public body (DWP). The NAO, an independent auditor, wrote the report.

Conditions for replication

A human decision on every advance. Regular fairness assessments (age is the only protected characteristic in the model). Feedback of review outcomes into the model.

Replication attempts

Later findings

9 July 2026. DWP's effectiveness assessment for April 2025 to March 2026 found the retrained model 2.5 times more effective than a random control group and concluded it should keep operating.
