The watch classified a user-initiated 30-second ECG as atrial fibrillation. Holycross says that he would not have gone to the ER without that result and that an ER doctor confirmed the rhythm. The recording was prompted by his wife's observation, not by a passive irregular-rhythm notification. CyberGuy's original interview records the couple's account.
