A father went to the ER after checking his heart with a smartwatch

Mr. Joseph Holycross reported that doctors confirmed the atrial fibrillation that Samsung Health Monitor detected on his Galaxy Watch4 Classic.

Workflow

A Galaxy Watch with the ECG feature enabled through Samsung Health Monitor on a paired Galaxy phone. A symptomatic user prompted to record an ECG. An ER able to confirm the rhythm on a 12-lead ECG.

Human oversight

Not assessed

Relation to AI

The watch classified a user-initiated 30-second ECG as atrial fibrillation. Holycross says that he would not have gone to the ER without that result and that an ER doctor confirmed the rhythm. The recording was prompted by his wife's observation, not by a passive irregular-rhythm notification. CyberGuy's original interview records the couple's account. No clinical document or independent clinical verification is cited. Samsung's instructions distinguish the ECG app from its compatible watch hardware. The interview does not disclose the installed app, operating-system, or classifier version. [1]

AI contribution

The watch classified a 30-second ECG as atrial fibrillation after the patient’s wife prompted him to take a reading. He said that an emergency physician confirmed the rhythm. The original interview provides their testimony without clinical records. [1]
