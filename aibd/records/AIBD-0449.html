China Telecom reports energy savings from AI control of its network

China Telecom reports that its AI-powered control and operations platform saves more than 900 million kWh a year and has cut CO₂ emissions by more than 500,000 tons.

What happened

China Telecom describes an AI-powered control and operations platform that manages 5.13 million 4G and 5G sectors, 3,800 equipment rooms and 46,000 servers. In the ITU entry, current to June 2025, it reports more than 900 million kWh in annual energy savings and a cut in CO₂ emissions of more than 500,000 tons. It also reports that deployment and operating costs for the energy system fell by 90%. These are aggregate claims by China Telecom. The entry does not provide the underlying accounting data or isolate the contribution of each technical component. China Telecom set targets of more than 99% base station coverage by 2025 and 1.5 billion kWh in annual savings. It planned to rebuild the platform with large language models from 2025 to 2027. No later source reports whether it met these targets, and the 2026 interim edition of the ITU report does not update this use case.

Sources

    International Telecommunication Union, AI for Good: Innovate for Impact (2025). Project-submitted account, current as of June 2025. ITU provides no independent outcome evaluation.

    Achieving over 900 million kWh in annual energy savings and reducing CO₂ emissions by more than 500,000 tons.

  2. Report, by the makers.

    China Telecom Corporation Limited, 2024 Sustainability Report (ESG Report), filed with the Stock Exchange of Hong Kong, 28 April 2025. China Telecom says that it built the energy-saving platform itself and that the platform had saved more than 900 million kWh by the end of 2024.

    By the end of 2024, the Company had more than 5 million 4/5G base station sectors, over 3,200 facility rooms, and above 6,000 cloud hosts under management, saved more than 900 million kWh of electricity and reduced over 500,000 tons of greenhouse gas emissions.

Details

AI system

Role

AI is part of a network-wide control and operations platform that manages energy use across mobile sectors, equipment rooms, and servers.

Autonomy

A central decision engine and edge controls are reported to adjust base stations, servers, and cooling equipment automatically.

Workflow

The account does not establish conditions sufficient to reproduce the benefit.

Benefits

Duration

China Telecom reports the energy savings on an annual basis and does not say for how many years they lasted. No later source reports whether it met its targets for 2025. [1]
