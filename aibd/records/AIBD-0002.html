UC Davis neuroprosthesis restores conversation for Casey Harrell

Four 64-electrode arrays plus RNN decoder let Casey Harrell (ALS) converse at 97.5% accuracy; later 1.96 million words at home

ⓘ

What happened

At the University of California Davis, a 45-year-old man with amyotrophic lateral sclerosis and severe dysarthria took part in the BrainGate2 trial (NCT00912041). Press identify him as Casey Harrell. Four 64-electrode arrays were implanted in his left precentral gyrus in July 2023. From August 2023, a decoder predicting phonemes every 80 ms, with language models over a 125,000-word vocabulary, converted his attempted speech to text. Accuracy was 99.6% with a 50-word vocabulary on day one and 90.2% with the full vocabulary on day two. It was 97.5% sustained over 248 hours of self-paced conversation with family, friends and colleagues (Card et al., NEJM, August 2024). The paper gives only the month for the first session, so 1 August 2023 is used.

Purpose. Built to restore communication by decoding attempted speech from intracortical activity into text. Conversational communication was the intended benefit and is what occurred.

AI’s contribution

A neural-network decoder (phoneme prediction every 80 ms) combined with language models is the mechanism converting attempted speech into text. The benefit is attributable to the decoding system, with surgery and arrays as enabling hardware.

Beneficiaries

  • a person

    Life-changing benefit to one identifiable person: conversational communication with family and colleagues was restored. The 2026 follow-up documents over 3,800 hours of unsupervised home use, nearly two million words and continued full-time employment.

Further evidence

Setting. Preserved speech-motor cortical activity during attempted speech despite severe dysarthria. 256-electrode intracortical recording in the ventral precentral gyrus. Phoneme decoder plus large-vocabulary language models calibrated on the participant's own data within minutes. Regulated trial setting (BrainGate2, NCT00912041) with ongoing research support.

Conditions. Array signal quality remains stable over months to years. Participant retains the ability to attempt speech and the cognition to operate the system. Decoder recalibration available as neural signals drift.
