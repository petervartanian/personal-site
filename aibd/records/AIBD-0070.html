AlphaProof and AlphaGeometry 2 score silver on 2024 olympiad problems

DeepMind's AlphaProof and AlphaGeometry 2 were awarded 28 of 42 points, a silver-medal score, on the 2024 International Mathematical Olympiad problems, with some solutions taking up to three days.

  • Not rated an organization

    DeepMind evaluated its systems on the 2024 olympiad problems. The sources describe grading and scores and report no deployment or observed change in an organization.

  • Not rated society

    Two graders marked the proofs of contest problems that already had solutions, and the sources identify no beneficiary beyond the research community. Scientific American notes that around 60 students scored better in 2024.

The system’s part
The AI systems produced the proofs. Humans manually formalized the 2024 problem statements into Lean (Scientific American) and graded the output. In 2024 the solutions were graded by two mathematicians at DeepMind's request (one of them, Myers, chaired the IMO 2024 Problem Selection Committee). The result was not officially certified by the IMO. DeepMind states the 2025 result was graded and certified by IMO coordinators, with the IMO president's confirmation appearing in the vendor blog. The cited sources do not state whether OpenAI's 2025 claim was certified by the IMO.
Limits
The 2024 systems used human formalization and up to three days, so their silver-level score is not an equivalent timed contest performance. The 2025 gold result concerns a successor system, and the separate OpenAI claim needs its own certification evidence.

What happened

Google DeepMind's AlphaProof (reinforcement learning over the Lean proof language) and AlphaGeometry 2 produced solutions to four of the six 2024 International Mathematical Olympiad (IMO) problems. Because the systems work in formal language, the problems were first translated manually. One problem was solved within minutes and others took up to three days, longer than the contest allows. Two former IMO gold medalists, Sir Timothy Gowers and Joseph Myers, graded the solutions under IMO rules. They awarded 28 of 42 points, a silver-medal score. DeepMind announced the result on 25 July 2024 and did not give the grading date. In July 2025 a Gemini Deep Think model working in natural language within the time limit scored 35 of 42. IMO president Gregor Dolinar confirmed this as a gold-medal score. Nature reported that OpenAI also claimed a gold-level result. DeepMind's AlphaProof team published the peer-reviewed account of the IMO 2024 run in Nature on 12 November 2025. The authors report that experts formalized the five non-geometry problems in Lean right after the problems were released and that the Lean kernel verified each AlphaProof proof. They state that the 28 points fell one point below the gold-medal threshold of 29, which 58 of 609 contestants reached.

Details

AI system

Model

AlphaProof and AlphaGeometry 2 in the 2024 work.

Role

The systems generated mathematical proofs.

Configuration

The 2024 work combined AlphaProof and AlphaGeometry 2. A later report concerned Gemini Deep Think.

Inputs and outputs

Human-formalized mathematical problems entered the systems, which generated proofs for four of the six 2024IMO problems.

Workflow

Manual translation of the 2024 problem statements into Lean. AlphaProof reinforcement-learning proof search and AlphaGeometry 2 for the geometry problem. Grading under IMO rules by two former IMO gold medalists.

Human oversight

Lean verified AlphaProof's formal proof steps, and Gowers and Myers graded the resulting solutions using IMO scoring rules.

Benefits

Reach

The sources identify no person, organization or population that gained from the result. The result is a score of 28 of 42 points on the six 2024 problems. [1] [2] [3] [4] [5] [6] [7]

Duration

The 2024 score is a single contest result. A year later a successor system, an advanced version of Gemini Deep Think, scored 35 of 42 at IMO 2025 within the time limit, which the IMO president confirmed as a gold-medal score. [1] [2] [3] [4] [5] [6] [7]

Measurement

Measurement

The Lean kernel checked the AlphaProof proofs, and two former IMO gold medalists engaged by DeepMind scored all solutions under IMO point rules. [7]

Setting and sample

The six IMO 2024 problems; five formalized in Lean by experts and one geometry problem handled by AlphaGeometry 2. [7]

Measures

IMO points out of 42. [7]

Results

28 of 42 points from four fully solved problems, one point below the gold threshold of 29. [7]

Limitations

Solutions took up to three days against the contest's two sessions of 4.5 hours, people translated the problems into Lean, the IMO did not certify the score, and the graders were engaged by the developer. [7]

Denominators

Up to three days of computation per problem. [7]

Materials for replication

DeepMind's 2024 blog links to the system's IMO 2024 solutions, and the Nature paper shows the AlphaProof proofs in its Extended Data figures. [7]

Follow-up

Conditions for replication

Each proof is machine-checked in Lean or graded by qualified IMO graders. Problems are unseen by the system before the contest. Grading criteria identical to those for human contestants.
