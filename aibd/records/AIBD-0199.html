See & Spray reduces herbicide use in soybean trials

John Deere's See & Spray machine-vision sprayer gave weed control within 1% of broadcast spraying in soybean trials, with 28.4% early-postemergence and 36.5% mid-postemergence herbicide savings.

  • 1 an organization

    The researchers measured mean herbicide savings of 28.4% to 62.4% across the three application timings, with weed control comparable to broadcast spraying and no yield increase. These savings lower the grower's input costs.

  • Not rated society

    Less herbicide was sprayed in the trials, but no source reports a measured environmental outcome off the plots.

The system’s part
The machine-vision sprayer decided where to spray, and the randomized trial held herbicides and rates constant, so the savings come from targeting. Savings depended on nozzle angle and the sensitivity setting chosen by the operator. A three-year follow-up by the same group found no seedbank increase at the highest sensitivity, while at the lowest setting weed density rose, which the authors attribute to Palmer amaranth escapes. The maker's agronomists co-wrote the trial paper, and the maker funded both studies.
Limits
The measured herbicide savings apply to particular crops, herbicides and settings at two research locations. They do not demonstrate higher yield, reduced health harm or net commercial profit after equipment costs. Blue River agronomists co-wrote the trial paper, and Blue River funded both Arkansas studies.
Costs and harms
Herbicide use fell without a yield increase, and the savings varied with nozzle angle and the sensitivity setting. At the lowest sensitivity, weed density rose from 867 to 11,300 plants per hectare over three years.
Later
Scaled up, 4 November 2025. John Deere reported that customers ran See & Spray on more than five million acres in the 2025 season, cutting non-residual herbicide use by nearly half.

What happened

University of Arkansas weed scientists and Blue River Technology agronomists ran four site-years of randomized complete block trials in 2021 and 2022. Sites were the Northeast Research and Extension Center in Keiser, Arkansas, and Stoneville R&D in Greenville, Mississippi. They compared John Deere See & Spray machine-vision targeted applications with traditional broadcast programs in glyphosate/glufosinate/dicamba-resistant soybean. They used a preemergence residual followed by early-postemergence dicamba and mid-postemergence glufosinate. Targeted applications gave control comparable to broadcast, with at most 1% less efficacy. Overall control was 93% or more for Palmer amaranth, broadleaf signalgrass, morningglory and purslane species. Soybean injury fell by at most 5 percentage points without a yield increase. The results section of the paper assigns mean savings of 62.4% to preemergence, 28.4% to early-postemergence, and 36.5% to mid-postemergence applications, with narrower nozzle angles spraying less area. The paper appeared online on 15 November 2024. The work was a research trial on two research sites, and it reports no commercial farm deployment. Grainews reported on 19 December 2024 that an Iowa State University field-scale trial of See & Spray Ultimate on five fields and 415 acres cut herbicide use by 43.9 to 87.2 percent in 2024, 76 percent on average. In a three-year study published on 4 November 2025 and funded by Blue River, the same Arkansas group found no seedbank increase at the highest sensitivity. At the lowest sensitivity, weed density rose from 867 to 11,300 plants per hectare. On the same day John Deere reported that customers ran See & Spray on more than five million acres in the 2025 season, cutting non-residual herbicide use by nearly half.

Details

AI system

Role

The system identified weeds and directed herbicide spraying to those locations.

Configuration

The deployment used the 2022 See & Spray system at detection sensitivity level 3 with a small coverage buffer.

Inputs and outputs

Cameras identified weeds while the sprayer selectively activated nozzles over the detected patches.

Workflow

Herbicide-resistant soybean with a PRE residual, EPOST dicamba and MPOST glufosinate. Four site-years (2021–2022) in a randomized complete block design.

Human oversight

The operator chose the detection sensitivity, and the savings depended on that setting and on the nozzle angle. The trials used a See & Spray machine of model year 2022 at level 3 detection sensitivity with a small coverage buffer. [1] [2] [3]
