Peterborough staff find documents faster with OSCAR Workforce

Peterborough City Council reports that staff find emails and documents faster with OSCAR Workforce, a Datnexa AI tool; it gives no figures, and resident benefits remain intended.

  • 1 an organization

    The council reports that the live Workforce tool lets staff find emails and documents and query data faster than by hand. This is a time saving with no figures and no independent check.

  • Not rated a person

    The resident-facing front door is intended to simplify access to council services. No source reports a realized resident outcome.

What happened

Peterborough City Council’s case study for the Local Government Association, published on 13 May 2026, describes OSCAR as a proof of concept for connecting council assistants through a shared service interface. In an article of 9 July 2026, Datnexa identifies itself as the development partner and describes AI that interprets requests and routes them to specialist services. A council contract register entry identifies Datnexa as the supplier for the design and scoping of OSCAR Workforce, with an award dated 5 October 2026. That entry records a procurement and reports no measured benefit. On 21 October 2026, the council reported that OSCAR Workforce was live and helped staff find emails and documents and query data faster. It published no timings, user counts or comparative evaluation. On its undated OSCAR product page, Datnexa states that OSCAR is going live in 2026 with Peterborough and that it seeks other councils to join in early 2027. The resident-facing accessibility benefits remain intended.

Details

AI system

Role

OSCAR is intended to route a resident’s request to the relevant service-specific agent. [1]

Workflow

The framework is designed for web, WhatsApp, and voice channels and for connection to existing services. The reported design includes centralized permissions, redaction, guardrails, and audit logging.

Human oversight

Datnexa states that OSCAR will not independently diagnose, decide eligibility or make safeguarding judgments. No source describes how staff check the results of OSCAR Workforce. [1] [2] [3] [4] [5]

People and places

Participants

Datnexa and Peterborough City Council developed the system. The council operates it. [1] [2] [3] [4]

Follow-up

Conditions for replication

Specialist services and information sources must be available for the routing system to use. Human staff must retain responsibility for the decisions that the system is not authorized to make. Access controls and records of system actions must work in the deployment, rather than merely being described in the design. [1] [2] [3] [4]

Availability

OSCAR had not yet reached its planned public website launch in the May 2026 account. [1]
