Bradford AI calls the de Brecy Tondo a Raphael; scholars dispute

unrated a person1 an organizationunrated society

97% facial similarity with the Sistine Madonna

Ugail's deep-learning comparison found 97% facial similarity with the Sistine Madonna; Cartwright Hall showed the Tondo July 2023-Jan 2024; Raphael scholars call it a copy; Art Recognition says not.

What happened

In January 2023 (Artforum, 26 January 2023, title seen in RSS) a facial-recognition deep-learning comparison led by Hassan Ugail (University of Bradford) with University of Nottingham colleagues reported 97% similarity between the Madonna of the de Brecy Tondo and Raphael's Sistine Madonna and 86% for the child; Ugail said the two 'are undoubtedly by the same artist' (BBC, 25 July 2023). The painting, owned by the de Brecy Trust, went on public display at Cartwright Hall Art Gallery, Bradford, from July 2023, extended 'due to popular demand' to 3 January 2024 according to Bradford Museums' own page, which calls it 'a mystery painting found to be undoubtedly by Raphael'. The Art Newspaper (26 July 2023) quoted Raphael scholars rejecting the attribution: an anonymous specialist said 'The picture is clearly a copy from the 19th century' and Angelamaria Aceto (Ashmolean) would not support an AI-based assessment. Artnet (18 September 2023) reported that Art Recognition's model found with 85% probability that the Tondo was not painted by Raphael, and that the same company had earlier asserted with 92% certainty that the National Gallery's Samson and Delilah might not be by Rubens. The earlier verifier found no museum or auction house accepting an AI attribution. The date is the BBC report of the display; the AI finding was reported on 26 January 2023.

What it was built to do. Test whether facial features in a disputed painting match those in an authenticated Raphael

The system's part

not assessed Bradford's model produced the similarity figures that underpinned the 'undoubtedly Raphael' claim and the exhibition; Raphael scholars reject the attribution and Art Recognition's model gives 85% probability against Raphael. The evidence is press reporting and the deployer's own page; no peer-reviewed report of the Tondo analysis and no institutional acceptance was found.
