What happened

Prenosis of Chicago developed the Sepsis ImmunoScore, machine learning software that uses up to 22 inputs from the electronic health record to place a patient in one of four sepsis risk categories. The validation study used prospectively collected data and blood samples from patients with suspected sepsis at Beth Israel Deaconess Medical Center in Boston, the Jesse Brown VA in Chicago and Beaumont in Royal Oak, Michigan. Physicians blinded to the score judged whether each patient had sepsis. FDA reviewers report an area under the ROC curve of 0.81 against the majority judgment, above a goal of 0.75. Sepsis was present in 3.02% of the low risk group and 69.70% of the very high risk group. In-hospital death rose from 0% in the low group to 18.18% in the very high group. FDA granted the De Novo request on 2 April 2024. The study was retrospective, so the score did not guide care, and adjudicators worked at the same hospitals as the patients.

Details

AI system

Workflow

The score used up to 22 inputs from the electronic health record, including a blood sample drawn within three hours of the first blood culture order. Risk thresholds were fixed before the validation study.

Measurement

Measurement

Retrospective study of prospectively collected data and samples; sepsis adjudicated by three physicians blinded to the score. [1]

Setting and sample

746 patients with suspected sepsis at three US hospitals. [1]

Measures

AUROC and sepsis rate by risk category. [1]

Results

AUROC 0.81; sepsis 3.02% low to 69.70% very high. [1]

Limitations

Retrospective; same-site adjudicators; mechanical ventilation endpoint not met. [1]

Denominators

One score per patient. [1]

Materials for replication

None public. [1]

Follow-up

Conditions for replication

The patient is an adult with suspected sepsis and a blood culture ordered. Clinicians use the score with other findings and not as the sole basis for a decision.

Grades

Observed or intended

Intended. The validation applied the score retrospectively to stored data, and the score guided no care, so the sources report no realized benefit. [1]
