Orca AI may have cut high-severity close encounters by 52%

Riviera and Hellenic Shipping News (16 Apr 2026): the P&I club and Orca AI analyzed 10.8 million nautical miles; high-severity close encounters fell from 2.7 to 1.3 per vessel-month between months ...

ⓘ

What happened

Trade reports published on 15 and 16 Apr 2026 describe a study by the protection and indemnity insurer NorthStandard with Orca AI. The reports are MarineLink, Riviera Maritime Media's Martyn Wingrove, and Hellenic Shipping News reproducing Orca AI's release. The study covered a globally operating cohort of 139 container vessels fitted with Orca AI's navigation platform. Across more than 10.8 million nautical miles, it compared the first three months after installation with months 10 to 12. It reported a 52% reduction in high-severity close encounters, with 22% within the first six months. The reduction was 36% in the North and Baltic Seas and 18% in the seas around China and Japan. Encounters were defined by closest point of approach, time to closest point of approach, COLREGs interactions and traffic density.

Orca AI's report page states that such encounters declined from 2.7 per vessel per month early after implementation to 1.3 by months 10 to 12. NorthStandard's head of loss prevention Colin Gillespie and Orca AI's chief executive Yarden Gross are quoted. The design is a before-and-after comparison on the same vessels with no control fleet, and the report is hosted by the vendor.

Purpose. Orca AI's platform is built to give bridge teams AI-assisted situational awareness and earlier detection of risky targets.

AI’s contribution

The platform's detections feed bridge decisions. The reported reduction compares the same vessels early and late in their first year. Crew familiarization, seasonality and route changes are not separated from the system's effect.

Further evidence

Setting. Fleet-wide installation of a sensor-and-software watchkeeping platform. Twelve months of anonymized operational data per vessel. An insurer willing to analyze member-fleet data.

Conditions. Crews keep using the system after the adaptation phase. The close-encounter definition stays constant across the comparison. Routes and traffic density are comparable between periods.
