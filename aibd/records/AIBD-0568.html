West Oxfordshire and Cotswold report less planning admin

West Oxfordshire and Cotswold report 85% less repetitive planning-administration time with AI tagging and summaries of consultation feedback.

ⓘ

What happened

West Oxfordshire and Cotswold councils use Konveio and Go Vocal to tag and summarize planning-consultation feedback. The councils report an estimated 85% reduction in repetitive planning-administration time, and teams are actively using the systems.

Purpose. Tag and summarize consultation responses to reduce repetitive planning-administration work.

AI’s contribution

Machine learning tags comments and generative AI summarizes individual or grouped responses. The councils report less repetitive administration. Earlier growth in public participation came from digital-engagement platforms and is not established as an AI effect.

Beneficiaries

  • an organization

    Active use of tagging and summary tools reportedly reduces repetitive administrative time.

  • a person

    Less manual consultation processing saves staff time. Public participation increased during earlier digital-engagement work, before these AI tools.

Further evidence

Setting. The councils used PropTech funding and their existing partnership to develop tools with two vendors. Officers collaborated in defining needs, and teams were actively using the systems after the pilot.

Conditions. The source states that AI use was permitted case by case and prohibited for personal data. The conditions needed to reproduce the estimated time saving are unknown.
