What happened

West Oxfordshire and Cotswold councils use Konveio and Go Vocal to tag and summarize planning-consultation feedback. The councils report an estimated 85% reduction in repetitive planning-administration time, and teams are actively using the systems.

What it was built to do. Tag and summarize consultation responses to reduce repetitive planning-administration work.

Beneficiaries

  • 1
    an organization

    Active use of tagging and summary tools reportedly reduces repetitive administrative time.

  • 1
    a person

    Less manual consultation processing saves staff time. Increased public participation belongs to the earlier digital-engagement work and is not credited to AI here.

The system's part

Machine learning tags comments and generative AI summarizes individual or grouped responses. The councils report less repetitive administration. Earlier growth in public participation came from digital-engagement platforms and is not established as an AI effect.

Replication

No attempt to repeat it has been recorded.

What produced it. The councils used PropTech funding and their existing partnership to develop tools with two vendors. Officers collaborated in defining needs, and teams were actively using the systems after the pilot.

What must hold again. The source states that AI use was permitted case by case and prohibited for personal data. The source does not establish causal requirements for reproducing the estimated time reduction.
