AI summaries of planning consultations save council officers time

West Oxfordshire and Cotswold councils report that AI tagging and summaries in Konveio and Go Vocal cut planning officers' repetitive administrative time by an estimated 85%.

  2. Report Authorship unassessed

The ministry funded the pilot through its PropTech Innovation Fund and does not name the author; the case study gives the test and baseline consultations behind the 85 per cent figure.

    In terms of reporting, the test-case consultation conducted in West Oxfordshire in August - October 2023 was compared to the Cotswolds consultation in February 2022 as the baseline. It was shown that the AI interventions could offer an average saving in officer time of up to 85%.
