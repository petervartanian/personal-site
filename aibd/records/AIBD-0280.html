AI may have helped Abu Dhabi surgeons separate twins joined at the head

XRlabs says that its Surgical Intelligence platform designed skull implants for conjoined twins, and the hospital credits AI planning in their successful separation in four staged procedures.

  • 4 a person

    Two children joined at the head were separated, reconstructed and discharged home to Nigeria, per the hospital and press. The surgical team produced this outcome, and no source quantifies the AI's share of it.

  • Not rated an organization

    XRlabs claims that implant design took seconds, but supplies no measured comparison with manual design or independently verified planning-time saving.

Brief from Peter

On 5 June 2026 PureHealth reported that surgeons at Sheikh Khalifa Medical City in Abu Dhabi had separated the Nigerian twins Mercy and Goodness. More than 60 clinicians took part in four staged procedures, led by Noor ul Owase Jeelani of Gemini Untwined. PureHealth credited AI-enhanced surgical planning in general terms.1 On 19 June 2026 XRlabs named its Surgical Intelligence platform and said that its AI model generated patient-specific skull implants from computed tomography data. The company also reported that the twins had returned home.2 The separation is a benefit to the two children. The AI's share of it is unmeasured: One account comes from the hospital group and the other from the technology supplier, and neither gives a comparison without AI. In Rio de Janeiro in 2022, Gemini Untwined helped separate twins joined at the head after rehearsal in virtual reality, with no stated AI component.

Details

AI system

Inputs and outputs

CT-derived models supported surgical rehearsal and patient-specific skull-implant design. The outputs were plans and physical implants for the surgical team to use.

Autonomy

The reported AI supported planning and implant design. It did not perform the separation or make clinical decisions independently.

Workflow

A multidisciplinary surgical team performing four staged procedures. CT-derived 3D models, mixed-reality rehearsal, and patient-specific skull implants. Specialist coordination by Gemini Untwined and the treating hospital.

Human oversight

Surgeons and the multidisciplinary team planned and performed the staged procedures. They remained responsible for judging the models, implant designs, and operative anatomy.

AI contribution

The hospital describes AI-assisted planning, and XRlabs describes AI-designed skull implants. Both took part in the procedure. Neither measures the AI’s contribution separately from the surgical team’s work. [1] [2]

Benefits

Reach

Two children, the twins Mercy and Goodness, were separated and returned home to Nigeria. [1] [2]

Follow-up

Conditions for replication

A surgical team able to act on AI-assisted plans and patient-specific implants. Imaging of sufficient quality to model the twins' shared vasculature and skull. The vendor's claims about the implant-design AI are not independently documented, so the AI's share cannot be assumed to transfer.
