AI planning may have helped SKMC separate craniopagus twins

PureHealth/SKMC statement (5 June 2026) cites “AI-enhanced surgical planning”; XRlabs' own release claims AI-designed implants. The twins were separated in four operations and returned to Nigeria.

AI system

System

XRlabs 'Surgical Intelligence' platform for AI-designed skull implants (identified only by the vendor) and unnamed 'AI-enhanced surgical planning' (PureHealth statement)

Application developer

XRlabs (implant design, per its own release); the hospital names no developer for the planning tools

Workflow

Specialists used CT images to make three-dimensional models, rehearse the surgery in mixed reality, and design skull implants for the twins. Gemini Untwined and the hospital coordinated four staged procedures. [1] [2]

Relation to AI

The treating institution identifies AI-assisted planning, and XRlabs describes an AI model for patient-specific skull-implant design. Both participated in the procedure. Neither provides a comparison without AI or a clinical evaluation that separates the model’s contribution from the surgical team’s work. [1] [2]

AI contribution

The hospital describes AI-assisted planning, and XRlabs describes AI-designed skull implants. Both took part in the procedure. Neither measures the AI’s contribution separately from the surgical team’s work. [1] [2]

Follow-up

Conditions for replication

Specialists need imaging that shows the shared blood vessels and skull, together with the ability to use the plans and implants safely. The reported benefit does not establish how much the AI would contribute in another operation. [1] [2]

Later findings

19 June 2026. XRlabs reported that the twins had recovered and returned home. The release gives no clinical follow-up interval or independent assessment of the AI contribution. [1] [2]
