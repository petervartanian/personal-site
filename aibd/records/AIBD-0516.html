Dubai housing agency says approval times fell from 12 months to 15 days

The Mohammed Bin Rashid Housing Establishment reports that its Smart Housing Experience, which uses AI to screen applicants, cut application approvals from 12 months to 15 days.

  • 1 an organization

    The deployer reports a 183% rise in productivity and AED 246 million in annual budget savings as achieved. These are time and cost savings, and the article ties the budget savings to Emirates Islamic Bank taking over the funding of housing loans. No independent source confirms them.

  • 2 a person

    The deployer reports that application approvals fell from 12 months to 15 days, a measurable improvement in access to housing services for applicants. The figure is the deployer's own, and the 84-to-32-month service timeline in the ITU chapter is not reported as achieved.

What happened

The Mohammed Bin Rashid Housing Establishment in Dubai combines AI with financing, digital processing, shared data and pre-approved designs in its Smart Housing Experience. On 5 October 2023, the Government of Dubai Media Office stated that the establishment's AI-based Housing Intelligence Matrix, which screens housing applicants, had received a 6-star rating at the International Best Practices Competition. The office stated that the establishment's solutions had "minimized human intervention in data processing, reducing it from days to just a few minutes." The establishment told the International Telecommunication Union (ITU) that a pilot ended in 2023 and that full deployment began in 2024. Its ITU account gives a reduction in the housing-service timeline from 84 to 32 months and faster approvals. The account describes these figures both as delivered features and as expected benefits. In a partner article of 20 June 2025, the establishment presented the platform results as achieved. It stated that application approvals fell from 12 months to 15 days. It also reported a 183% rise in productivity and AED 246 million in annual budget savings. The article ties the budget savings to Emirates Islamic Bank taking over the funding of housing loans. No independent source has checked these figures. No later source reports outcomes of the Smart Housing Experience.
