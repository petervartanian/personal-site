Fiscal Heredia may have identified a 10-member robbery gang

depth 3 for society, depth 2 for an organization

In Operación Influencer (11 May 2023) prosecutors and PDI detained a gang of 10 aged 15–22 behind eight accredited violent robberies; the Fiscalía credits the university-built AI model Fiscal Heredia.

What happened

The Fiscalía de Chile's regional news release describes a joint investigation by Focos Occidente and the PDI's Brigada de Investigación Criminal. In the early hours of Thursday 11 May 2023 it led to 30 search warrants across Maipú, Pudahuel, Cerro Navia and other comunas. There were 17 detentions, ten linked to “encerronas” (robbery with intimidation or violence) whose perpetrators posted their robberies on TikTok. The release says they were identified through the software Fiscal Heredia (Herramienta para el Estudio del Delito Usando Inteligencia Artificial). It is an AI model developed by several universities and incorporated into the Ministerio Público in March 2023. It proposes probable participants from prosecution and police records for prosecutors and police to work up. The Noveno Juzgado de Garantía accredited the eight robberies.

Four adults went to preventive detention and four adolescents remained in provisional internment. T13 quotes Fiscal Nacional Ángel Valencia saying the algorithm accelerates criminal analysis. It reports that after three members were arrested the AI searched their links to raise persons of interest later identified by victims. The OECD AI Incidents Monitor records the case.

What it was built to do. Build networks of links among people with criminal records to propose probable members of a group tied to a specific crime. This reduces the case files prosecutors must review.

The system's part

The deploying public body credits the AI with the identification of probable participants, complemented by prosecutors' and police work and victim identification. T13 adds the mechanism (link search from three arrested members). No court document or independent evaluation of the model's accuracy is among the sources. The Fiscalía is both the source and the beneficiary.

Replication

No attempt to repeat it has been recorded.

What produced it. Linked prosecution, prison and police records (SAF, Gendarmería, Carabineros). A few confirmed arrests seeding a link search. Victim identification to confirm proposed persons of interest.

What must hold again. Suspects must already appear in the records the model draws on. Human verification before charging. Legal basis for cross-agency data linkage.
