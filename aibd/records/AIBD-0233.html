Chilean prosecutors credit Fiscal Heredia with finding a robbery gang

In Operación Influencer (11 May 2023) prosecutors and police detained a gang of 10 aged 15–22 behind eight accredited violent robberies; prosecutors credit the AI model Fiscal Heredia.

  • 3 society

  • 2 an organization

    Prosecutors state that the tool replaces review of hundreds of case files with a short list of proposed links.

What happened

In the early hours of Thursday 11 May 2023, a joint investigation by Focos Occidente and the Brigada de Investigación Criminal of Chile's Policía de Investigaciones led to 30 search warrants across Maipú, Pudahuel, Cerro Navia and other comunas. There were 17 detentions. Ten of them were linked to “encerronas” (robbery with intimidation or violence) whose perpetrators posted their robberies on TikTok. On 12 May 2023 the television channel T13 quoted Fiscal Nacional Ángel Valencia saying that the algorithm accelerates criminal analysis. T13 reports that after three members were arrested, the model searched their links and raised persons of interest whom victims later identified. The Fiscalía de Chile's regional news release of 17 May 2023 says that the ten were identified through the software Fiscal Heredia (Herramienta para el Estudio del Delito Usando Inteligencia Artificial). The release describes it as an AI model developed by several universities and incorporated into the Ministerio Público in March 2023. The model proposes probable participants from prosecution and police records for prosecutors and police to work up. The Noveno Juzgado de Garantía accredited the eight robberies. Four adults went to preventive detention, and four adolescents remained in provisional internment. The OECD AI Incidents Monitor records the case. On 25 August 2026 the Fiscalía named the developers as the Universidad de Chile, the Universidad del Biobío and the Universidad de los Andes. They worked with the Ministerio Público in a project, funded by the Fondo de Fomento al Desarrollo Científico y Tecnológico (Fondef), that began in 2020. The Fiscalía reported that the tool, now called Fiscal HeredIA, runs in 15 regional prosecutor's offices with more than 1,000 prosecutors and other users. It also reported that the tool won second place in the IFORS Prize for OR in Development 2026.

Details

AI system

Application developer

Several universities (not named in the sources)

Autonomy

The model proposed probable participants; police, prosecutors and victims supplied the verification that supported legal action.

Workflow

After initial arrests, investigators searched links to those suspects, reviewed people whom Fiscal Heredia proposed, and sought victim identification before further action.

Human oversight

The model proposes probable participants, and prosecutors and police work up those proposals. In this case victims later identified the persons of interest that the model raised. The Noveno Juzgado de Garantía accredited the eight robberies. The sources describe no check of the model's accuracy. [1] [2] [3] [4]

Duration

No source reports a verdict in the Operación Influencer case. By 25 August 2026 the Fiscalía said that the tool ran in 15 regional prosecutor's offices with more than 1,000 prosecutors and other users. [1] [2] [3] [4]

Follow-up

Conditions for replication

Suspects must already appear in the records the model draws on. Human verification before charging. Legal basis for cross-agency data linkage.
