Xuanwu Hospital implants China's first responsive neurostimulator

Xuanwu Hospital implanted the Epilcure responsive neurostimulator in a man with epilepsy in March 2022; its trial team reports a 61.1% mean seizure reduction in seven patients and no result for him.

Autonomy

Workflow

Intracranial electrodes at the epileptic focus feeding continuous EEG to a chip implanted in the skull. Clinician-configured EEG detection algorithms that trigger stimulation when signal measures cross set thresholds, as described in the later clinical paper. Enrollment in the hospital-led NMPA registration trial for patients whose epilepsy is drug-resistant and unsuited to open surgery.

Awaiting review

Human oversight

In the later clinical protocol, clinicians used recorded EEG to select detection algorithms and thresholds, then adjusted stimulation settings. Patients or family members could switch the device on or off and monitor its battery.
