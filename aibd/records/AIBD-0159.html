29 Mar 2026: Meta alert at 8:58 pm; Raebareli police reached an 18-year-old in 12 minutes after rat poison; hospital treatment, stable

What happened

On 29 March 2026 an 18-year-old class 12 student from the Mill Area police station limits of Raebareli, Uttar Pradesh, texted his girlfriend 'Alvida' and posted a suicide video on Instagram with rat poison and messages of intent after a breakup. At about 8:58 pm Meta flagged the content and emailed an alert to the Social Media Center at Uttar Pradesh Police headquarters, which traced his mobile number and location and alerted Raebareli police. A police team reached his home within 12 minutes and found him in severe distress with an empty rat-poison packet nearby; police and family took him to a nearby hospital, where he was treated, and on 1 April 2026 police said he was stable.

What it was built to do. Meta's pipeline is built to find posts and live videos indicating suicide or self-injury risk, route them to trained reviewers and, in imminent cases, contact local authorities; the Uttar Pradesh Police Social Media Center exists to act on those alerts. This rescue is that purpose.

The system's part

Police say Meta flagged the Instagram post at about 8:58 pm on 29 March and emailed the Uttar Pradesh Police Social Media Center; no source says whether Meta's classifier or a user report started the review, and Decode reports that UP alerts arise from 'artificial intelligence systems or user reports' reviewed by Meta staff. Everything after the email was human work: the center traced the phone number and location, Raebareli police reached the home in 12 minutes on the DGP's instructions, and a hospital treated him.

Replication

No attempt to repeat it has been recorded.

What produced it. An Instagram post with rat poison and messages of intent, after a breakup, that Meta's detection-and-review pipeline flagged as a suicide risk at about 8:58 pm on 29 March 2026. A standing arrangement, in place since 2022 per Decode, under which Meta emails such alerts to the Uttar Pradesh Police Social Media Center, which traces the account's phone number and location and forwards them to district police. A Raebareli police team able to reach the Mill Area address within 12 minutes and take him to a nearby hospital with his family.

What must hold again. The post is flagged and reviewed while the person can still be treated; here the alert came at about 8:58 pm and police arrived within 12 minutes. The account resolves to a phone number and a reachable address; Decode reports that UP police usually receive a phone number and trace the SIM and live location. Officers and a hospital are available at once; officials said even a slight delay could have been fatal.
