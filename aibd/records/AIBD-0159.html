Meta flags a suicide post; Raebareli police arrive in 12 minutes

29 March 2026: Meta alert at 8:58 pm; Raebareli police reached an 18-year-old in 12 minutes after rat poison; hospital treatment, stable

ⓘ

What happened

On 29 March 2026 an 18-year-old class 12 student from Raebareli, Uttar Pradesh, texted his girlfriend “Alvida” after a breakup. He lived within the Mill Area police station limits. He also posted a suicide video on Instagram with rat poison and messages of intent. At about 8:58 pm Meta flagged the content and emailed an alert to the Social Media Center at Uttar Pradesh Police headquarters. The center traced his mobile number and location and alerted Raebareli police. A police team reached his home within 12 minutes and found him in severe distress, with an empty rat-poison packet nearby. Police and family took him to a nearby hospital, where he was treated. On 1 April 2026 police said he was stable.

Purpose. Meta's detection and review system is built to find posts and live videos that indicate suicide or self-injury risk. It routes them to trained reviewers and, in imminent cases, contacts local authorities. The Uttar Pradesh Police Social Media Center exists to act on those alerts. This rescue is that purpose.

AI’s contribution

Police say Meta flagged the Instagram post at about 8:58 pm on 29 March and emailed the Uttar Pradesh Police Social Media Center. No source says whether Meta's classifier or a user report started the review. Decode reports that UP alerts arise from “artificial intelligence systems or user reports” reviewed by Meta staff. Everything after the email was human work. The center traced the phone number and location. Raebareli police reached the home in 12 minutes on the DGP's instructions, and a hospital treated him.

Further evidence

Setting. An Instagram post with rat poison and messages of intent, after a breakup. Meta's detection and review system flagged it as a suicide risk at about 8:58 pm on 29 March 2026. A standing arrangement, in place since 2022 per Decode, under which Meta emails such alerts to the Uttar Pradesh Police Social Media Center. The center traces the account's phone number and location and forwards them to district police. A Raebareli police team able to reach the Mill Area address within 12 minutes and take him to a nearby hospital with his family.

Conditions. The post is flagged and reviewed while the person can still be treated. Here the alert came at about 8:58 pm and police arrived within 12 minutes. The account leads to a phone number and a reachable address. Decode reports that UP police usually receive a phone number and trace the SIM and live location. Officers and a hospital are available at once. Officials said even a slight delay could have been fatal.
