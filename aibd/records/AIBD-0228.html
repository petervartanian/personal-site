INEC's ABIS invalidates 2,780,756 ineligible voter registrations

INEC chairman Mahmood Yakubu reported on 26 Oct 2022 that ABIS cleaning of 2021–22 registrations identified 2,780,756 (22.6%) double, underage or fake entries, leaving 9,518,188 valid new registrants.

ⓘ

What happened

The date is that of Professor Mahmood Yakubu's address to political parties in Abuja, reproduced by the Nigerian Democratic Report. He said the Continuous Voter Registration data (28 June 2021 to 31 July 2022) was cleaned with the Automated Biometric Identification System (ABIS). This identified 2,780,756 registrations (22.6%) as ineligible and invalidated them. Among them were double or multiple registrants, underage persons and fake registrations failing INEC's business rules. That left 9,518,188 valid post-ABIS registrations; 23 registration officers were identified for sanctions. On 12 September 2022, INEC commissioner Festus Okoye said 1,126,359 of 2,523,458 registrants (28 June 2021 to 14 January 2022) were found invalid and delisted. ABIS processing of later registrations was ongoing (Punch).

BusinessDay later argued that some fake and underage registrants evaded ABIS and that invalidation rates were regionally lopsided. The vendor of ABIS is not named in the sources.

Purpose. De-duplicate and validate biometric voter registrations (fingerprints and faces) to remove double, multiple, underage and fake entries from the national register.

AI’s contribution

The figures come from INEC's own statements (the deployer) relayed by two Nigerian outlets, not from an audit. INEC's website could not be searched. ABIS's matching method, error rates and the share of invalidations due to biometrics versus business rules are not stated. BusinessDay's critique (registrants evading ABIS; 49.3% of invalidations from two regions) is included as a limit.

Beneficiaries

  • society

    2,780,756 ineligible entries (22.6% of new registrations) were removed from the register used for the 2023 general election, per INEC's chairman. BusinessDay disputes completeness and regional balance.

  • an organization

    INEC obtained a cleaned register and identified 23 of its own officers for sanctions over multiple registrations.

Further evidence

Setting. Biometric capture (fingerprints, face) at registration with a central de-duplication pass. Business rules defining ineligible entries applied after matching. Public display of the register for claims and objections.

Conditions. Biometric capture quality adequate for matching. Independent audit of matching accuracy (absent here). Officers unable to bypass capture protocols.
