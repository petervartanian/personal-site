Face2Gene suggests MRD7 after an 18-year misdiagnosis

Face2Gene suggested MRD7 for a 22-year-old misdiagnosed with Cornelia de Lange at age 4; exome sequencing confirmed a DYRK1A variant.

ⓘ

What happened

Obara, Abe and Toyoshima of the National Hospital Organization Akita National Hospital, Japan, reported a 22-year-old man in Cureus on 5 January 2023. He had been diagnosed with Cornelia de Lange syndrome at age four based on facial dysmorphism, microcephaly, short stature and intellectual disability. At 22 his Cornelia de Lange clinical diagnostic score was 5 of 19. His frontal facial photograph and clinical features were uploaded to Face2Gene. This “introduced the following candidate diseases: Angelman syndrome (AS, OMIM 105833), Witteveen-Kolk syndrome (WKOS; MIM 613406), Rubinstein-Taybi syndrome (RTS; OMIM 180849), and MRD7”. The paper gives no ranking or scores for these candidates. Whole-exome sequencing then identified a novel heterozygous DYRK1A variant, c.848dup, p.(Asn283LysfsTer6), confirming MRD7.

The 18-year span in the title derives from the ages the paper gives, diagnosed at four and re-evaluated at 22. The paper does not state a duration. The consultation date is not given.

Purpose. Face2Gene is built to suggest candidate syndromes from facial images and clinical features. This suggestion was that purpose.

AI’s contribution

Face2Gene listed MRD7 among four candidates. The paper gives no ranking. Whole-exome sequencing made the diagnosis. The AI redirected the differential after the clinical score undermined the prior diagnosis.

Beneficiaries

  • a person

    A correct molecular diagnosis replaced a misdiagnosis held since age four. The report documents no treatment change, so material rather than life-changing.

Further evidence

Setting. Frontal facial photograph plus clinical features uploaded to Face2Gene. A prior clinical diagnosis that no longer fit the clinical score. Access to whole-exome sequencing to confirm.

Conditions. The true syndrome is represented in Face2Gene's training data (authors note MRD7 recommendations improve as cases are registered). Clinicians pursue a candidate that is one of several suggestions.
