Face2Gene lists a rare disorder that sequencing confirms after 18 years

Face2Gene listed a DYRK1A-related intellectual developmental disorder among four candidates for a man diagnosed with Cornelia de Lange syndrome at age four, and exome sequencing confirmed it.

What happened

A man had been diagnosed with Cornelia de Lange syndrome at age four based on facial dysmorphism, microcephaly, short stature and intellectual disability. At 22 his Cornelia de Lange clinical diagnostic score was 5 of 19. His frontal facial photograph and clinical features were uploaded to Face2Gene. This “introduced the following candidate diseases: Angelman syndrome (AS, OMIM 105833), Witteveen-Kolk syndrome (WKOS; MIM 613406), Rubinstein-Taybi syndrome (RTS; OMIM 180849), and MRD7”. The paper gives no ranking or scores for these candidates. The consultation date is not given. Whole-exome sequencing then identified a novel heterozygous DYRK1A variant, c.848dup, p.(Asn283LysfsTer6), which confirmed MRD7, intellectual developmental disorder, autosomal dominant 7. According to the hospital laboratory's ClinVar submission, the sequencing analysis firm cBioinformatics reported the variant on 28 January 2022, which dates the corrected diagnosis. The laboratory submitted the variant to ClinVar as pathogenic and de novo in one affected man. The submission first appeared in ClinVar on 11 December 2022. On 5 January 2023 Obara, Abe and Toyoshima of the National Hospital Organization Akita National Hospital, Japan, reported the case of the 22-year-old man in Cureus. The 18-year span in the headline derives from the ages that the paper gives, diagnosed at four and re-evaluated at 22. The paper does not state a duration. In October 2026 ClinVar still lists the variant as pathogenic. No source reports later on the patient's care or course.

Details

AI system

Inputs and outputs

Face2Gene received a frontal facial photograph and clinical features and returned four candidate syndromes, including MRD7.

Workflow

Frontal facial photograph plus clinical features uploaded to Face2Gene. A prior clinical diagnosis that no longer fit the clinical score. Access to whole-exome sequencing to confirm.

Human oversight

Clinicians reassessed the earlier diagnosis and pursued whole-exome sequencing, which identified the DYRK1A variant supporting MRD7.

Duration

ClinVar still lists the variant as pathogenic in October 2026. No source reports later on the patient's care or course. [1] [2]

Follow-up

Conditions for replication

The true syndrome is represented in Face2Gene's training data (authors note MRD7 recommendations improve as cases are registered). Clinicians pursue a candidate that is one of several suggestions.
