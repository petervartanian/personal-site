What happened

Wigan and Agilisys developed QuickAction workflows for care assessments, minutes, audits, and analysis of free-text surveys. The council reports 107 staff days saved in minute-taking and analysis of 4,500 tenant responses that could not previously be processed in aggregate. Reduced sickness, recruitment, and overtime remain projections. No independent evaluation was extracted.

What it was built to do. Reduce social-care administration and make qualitative feedback usable for service teams.

The system's part

The tools transcribe, summarize, and analyze material that staff previously processed manually or could not analyze at scale. The council attributes time and analytical-capability gains to these workflows, while other workforce outcomes remain projected.

Replication

No attempt to repeat it has been recorded.

What produced it. Council staff and Agilisys co-developed several tools around specific adult-social-care administrative tasks. The reported deployments include meeting minutes and analysis of care and tenant feedback, with further refinement during pilots.
