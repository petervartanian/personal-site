Wigan reports time savings and newly usable survey responses

Wigan reports 107 staff days saved on minutes and new analysis of 4,500 tenant responses through QuickAction tools.

ⓘ

What happened

Wigan and Agilisys developed QuickAction workflows for care assessments, minutes, audits, and analysis of free-text surveys. The council reports 107 staff days saved in minute-taking and analysis of 4,500 tenant responses that could not previously be processed in aggregate. Reduced sickness, recruitment, and overtime remain projections. The account provides no independent outcome evaluation.

Purpose. Reduce social-care administration and make qualitative feedback usable for service teams.

AI’s contribution

The tools transcribe, summarize, and analyze material that staff previously processed manually or could not analyze at scale. The council attributes time and analytical-capability gains to these workflows, while other workforce outcomes remain projected.

Beneficiaries

  • an organization

    The council can analyze qualitative responses that it previously could not process in aggregate.

  • a person

    Reduced note-taking and processing effort saves staff time. Reductions in sickness, overtime, and recruitment remain projected.

Further evidence

Setting. Council staff and Agilisys co-developed several tools around specific adult-social-care administrative tasks. The reported deployments include meeting minutes and analysis of care and tenant feedback, with further refinement during pilots.

Conditions. The conditions needed to reproduce the benefit are unspecified.
