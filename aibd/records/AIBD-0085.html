What happened

Brazil's Supremo Tribunal Federal (STF) uses VitorIA, operational since 2023, to compare the texts of incoming appeals. It groups cases raising similar legal questions for the staff who screen candidates for repercussão geral themes. The court says the tool does not judge cases. On 28 September 2026 the STF described the controversy behind Tema 1.261 (leading case ARE 1441934, on municipal teachers' personal pay advantages). VitorIA identified at least 247 appeals with similar legal questions by grouping on content similarity. The STF added that finding so many similar cases did not determine the result. The theme record shows the Plenary decided unanimously on 22 August 2023 that there is no general repercussion because the question is infraconstitutional. It became final on 23 September 2023. No cited source says what then happened to the 247 appeals.

The STF credits VitorIA with four themes in 2023, 19 in 2024 and 11 in 2025. It says these figures do not refer to decisions taken with AI help and do not allow calculating accuracy or time saved.

What it was built to do. Identify clusters of textually similar appeals so the court can treat them jointly or open new general-repercussion themes

The system's part

The STF says its function precedes judgment: “identificar grupos de casos parecidos, que depois passam pela análise das equipes responsáveis e, quando necessário, dos ministros.” The identification of at least 247 similar appeals is attributed to VitorIA by the court itself. The court adds that finding them “não determinou o resultado”. The theme decision was made by the Plenary. Developer and deployer are the same public body (STF). The STF news items are the court documenting its own tool. No cited source names an external developer or links VitorIA to the court's earlier Victor project (STF with UnB). The STF said in August 2021 that Victor's classifier was not yet in production.

Replication

What produced it. Electronic appeals in machine-readable text for VitorIA to compare. The STF's 2021 Victor item documents same-day OCR of all electronic appeals since December 2020. No cited source ties that layer to VitorIA, so the dependency is inferred. Textual-similarity clustering by VitorIA proposing groups of similar appeals. Human screening teams and ministers deciding whether to open a theme and how to rule.

What must hold again. Appeals filed electronically with machine-readable text. A procedural device (repercussão geral themes) that lets one decision govern many appeals. Staff capacity to review proposed clusters.
