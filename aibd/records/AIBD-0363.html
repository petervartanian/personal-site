Whale Safe grades ship cooperation with 10-knot slowdowns

Whale Safe rates whale presence from acoustic buoys, sightings and a habitat model and grades ship cooperation with California's voluntary 10-knot zones; no avoided strike is documented.

Workflow

Moored acoustic buoy with onboard call detection and classification. Human analyst review of detections. Sightings and habitat-model data streams. AIS vessel tracking and public cooperation grading. NOAA voluntary speed reduction zones.

Human oversight

Not assessed

Relation to AI

The automated component is the buoy's onboard detection and classification of whale calls, the Baumgartner et al. 2019 system. Whale Safe's own methodology page says a human analyst then reviews it before it contributes to the Whale Presence Rating. Cooperation grades are computed from AIS data, not AI. Only the maker's pages and the developers' buoy paper are among the sources, and none records a dated outcome attributable to the detections.
