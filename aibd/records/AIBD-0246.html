What happened

ITmedia's 岡田有花 reported on 22 Jun 2026 that Odakyu Electric Railway would introduce an “AI踏切画像解析システム” from 24 June 2026. It covers four crossings on its Odawara Line. They are Minami-Shinjuku Nos. 2, 4 and 5 between Minami-Shinjuku and Sangubashi stations, and Mukogaoka-Yuen No. 9 between Mukogaoka-Yuen and Ikuta stations. The AI analyzes footage from the existing safety-confirmation cameras in real time. It identifies and tracks pedestrians, wheelchair users, bicycles and motorcycles. When it detects someone left inside after the barriers come down, it sends a signal through the signaling equipment. That applies the brakes of approaching trains and lights the crossing's special signal lamps to alert crews. Both are released automatically once the crossing is clear. Images are used only for hazard detection, not to identify individuals, and analysis data are destroyed within a year.

The system was developed by Meitetsu EI Engineer, Toyota Systems and Toho Electric Industries. It was trialed from January 2023, with work on detection accuracy at night and in rain. railf.jp carried the same announcement on 25 Jun 2026. No detection events or outcomes are reported, and the date given is the operational start date.

What it was built to do. The system is built to detect people left inside a closed crossing and stop approaching trains automatically.

The system's part

Not assessed. The sources describe the intended detection-to-brake chain and the trial period; no detections, false alarms or accuracy figures are reported.

Replication

What produced it. Existing crossing cameras with sufficient coverage of the enclosed area. Integration with signaling equipment able to command train braking. Three years of trials covering night and rain.

What must hold again. Detection accuracy holds in poor weather and lighting. False detections do not stop trains so often that the function is disabled. Signaling interlocks remain in place.
