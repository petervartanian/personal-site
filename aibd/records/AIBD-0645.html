Brief from Peter

The Veterans Health Administration tested computer-aided polyp detection in routine colonoscopy at scale.1 It assigned facilities at random to receive a polyp-detection device, which the trial registry names as Medtronic's GI Genius. At 42 equipped facilities adenoma detection rose from 50.7% to 54.9%. At 97 control facilities it fell from 51.8% to 51.1%. Withdrawal time did not change. The gain was 4.2 percentage points on a baseline that was already about 51%. The rate of 54.9% rests on 35,399 colonoscopies. The Veterans Health Administration program that installed the devices also ran and reported the study.2 The authors state that the effect on cancer outcomes remains unknown, as does the risk that endoscopists lose skill. Elsewhere, researchers found a gain in a randomized trial at two Seoul hospitals and no gain in another at a Japanese community hospital.

Details

AI system

Workflow

The VA installed the devices at facilities chosen at random. Endoscopists used the device during routine colonoscopy. Baseline adenoma detection at the facilities was already about 51%.

Measurement

Measurement

Adenoma detection from VA colonoscopy and pathology records before and after deployment; mixed effects logistic regression. [2]

Setting and sample

269 endoscopists at 42 equipped facilities and 547 at 97 control facilities; 334,200 colonoscopies. [2]

Measures

Adenoma detection rate. [2]

Results

+4.2 percentage points (50.7% to 54.9%) at equipped facilities against -0.7 points at control facilities; odds ratio 1.22. [2]

Additional value from AI

Odds ratio 1.22 (95% CI 1.15 to 1.28). [2]

Limitations

Facility-level randomization; no data on cancer outcomes; deskilling not assessed. [2]

Denominators

Device available for all colonoscopies at equipped facilities. [2]

Materials for replication

Registered as NCT05888623. [2]

Follow-up

Conditions for replication

Endoscopists must keep using the device once it is installed. The gain must not fade as endoscopists grow used to the alerts.

Replication attempts

2 follow-up entries. Benefit recurred: 1. Benefit did not recur: 1.
