St Helens uses AI to match care needs and draft care plans

St Helens built AI tools that match care assessments to a technology catalog and draft personalized care plans. Improved resident independence remains an intended benefit.

ⓘ

What happened

St Helens Borough Council built an AI-integrated technology-enabled-care catalog using Microsoft Copilot Studio. Care professionals co-curated the catalog. Predictive matching connects care-assessment information to suitable technology options, and generative AI drafts personalized care plans and costings. The workflow is intended to help residents return home and live independently. Improvements in care and independent living remain unevaluated. Reported medication-dispenser savings and in-house development costs concern other parts of the service.

Purpose. Match care needs to technology-enabled support and generate personalized care plans.

AI’s contribution

Predictive matching links care-assessment information to catalog options, and generative AI drafts personalized care plans. The source identifies this functionality but does not establish improved resident outcomes or AI-produced financial savings.
