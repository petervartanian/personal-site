Pindrop traces the Biden New Hampshire robocall to ElevenLabs

the result came back well north of 99 percent for ElevenLabs

After a deepfake Biden robocall targeted New Hampshire's Jan 2024 primary, Pindrop's analysis against 120+ voice engines pointed to ElevenLabs; Hany Farid's Berkeley team reached the same conclusion.

What happened

The date is WIRED's publication; Pindrop's blog post appeared the day before. Voters in New Hampshire received an AI-generated robocall impersonating President Biden before the 23 January 2024 primary. Pindrop, a company that builds synthetic-audio detection tools, analyzed a 39-second clip, checking its patterns against more than 120 voice synthesis engines, and its CEO Vijay Balasubramaniyan told WIRED the result came back well north of 99 percent for ElevenLabs. Hany Farid's UC Berkeley team, initially skeptical, ran its own model on the same sample and concluded with high confidence that it was AI-generated and likely ElevenLabs; ElevenLabs' own detector returned 84 percent, a result WIRED reproduced. In May 2024 the New Hampshire Attorney General announced 26 charges against Steven Kramer and the FCC proposed a $6 million fine (NHPR); the fetched sources do not connect those actions to Pindrop's analysis.

What it was built to do. Identify synthetic (deepfake) audio and attribute it to the generating system.

The system's part

The attribution is the vendor's own analysis, reported by WIRED with named sources and independently replicated by Farid's team; ElevenLabs did not comment on the incident. The sources document no harm stopped and do not link the later charges against Kramer or the FCC fine to Pindrop's finding.

Replication

No attempt to repeat it has been recorded.

What produced it

  • A clean audio sample of the deepfake
  • A library of fingerprints for many commercial voice-synthesis engines
  • A second, independent model run on the same sample

What must hold again

  • The generating engine must be among those fingerprinted
  • Sample length and quality sufficient for attribution
