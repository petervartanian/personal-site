Android Earthquake Alerts warns over 16 million users of an earthquake

Google's Android Earthquake Alerts detected the Marmara Ereğlisi earthquake of 23 April 2025 and alerted over 16 million users, but its stronger alert missed inland areas of moderate shaking.

  • 1 a person

    Google's feedback data say that recipients were likely to drop, cover and hold on or to warn others. No individual injury avoided is documented, and Google collected the data.

  • Not rated an organization

    No organization's decision or operation is documented as changed by the alerts.

  • 2 society

    More than 16 million users were alerted, with a median warning of 56 seconds for weak shaking. The “Take Action” alerts did not reach inland areas of moderate shaking (MMI V) in time. No reduction in injuries is demonstrated, and Google employees wrote the evidence.

What happened

On 23 April 2025 the Marmara Ereğlisi earthquake, of moment magnitude (Mw) 6.2, struck offshore in the Sea of Marmara, Türkiye. Initial reports cited by the evaluation put shaking in Istanbul at intensity V on the Modified Mercalli Intensity (MMI) scale. At least 359 people were injured, mostly from panic. Google's Android Earthquake Alerts (AEA) system uses the accelerometers of online Android phones, and Google launched it in Türkiye in June 2021. An on-phone detection algorithm flags sudden changes in acceleration. The system detected the event 5.31 seconds after its origin. It issued a first “Be Aware” alert 0.43 seconds later on an initial magnitude estimate of Mw 4.83. “Take Action” alerts followed as the estimate rose. Alerts reached more than 16 million Android users in Türkiye, Greece, Romania and Bulgaria. The modeled warning distribution for residents in correctly alerted geographic areas of weak shaking (MMI III) reached up to 150 seconds, with a median of 56 seconds. The figures of 90% true positives and 99% precision also count people by geographic area. Neither figure counts alerted devices. In a separate voluntary survey of 67,056 recipients, 50.5% reported receiving a warning before they felt shaking. The final magnitude estimate saturated at Mw 6.04, so the “Take Action” polygon was too small. Inland populations that felt MMI V shaking received only the “Be Aware” warning before stronger shaking arrived. Google reports that more than 116 million alerts had been issued in Türkiye for 203 detected events between June 2021 and 16 September 2025. Google employees wrote the system description (Science, 17 July 2025) and the evaluation (Nature Communications, 11 August 2026), and the user-feedback data also come from Google. The same system underestimated the Türkiye earthquakes of 6 February 2023. The BBC reported on 27 July 2025 that only 469 “Take Action” alerts were sent for the magnitude 7.8 event, when 10 million people could have received one. The AI Incident Database records that underestimation as incident 1546.

Details

AI system

Inputs and outputs

Acceleration changes from many online Android phones produced an earthquake location and magnitude estimate, followed by geographically targeted warning messages.

Autonomy

The service automatically detected the event and issued updated warnings directly to phones; recipients chose how to respond.

Workflow

Dense network of online Android phones (about 66,000 used for characterization) around an offshore epicentre. On-phone trigger on P-wave acceleration and server-side magnitude estimation updated over 12 s. Two-tier alerting (Be Aware, Take Action) delivered through the operating system. A population with high earthquake awareness after the 2023 Kahramanmaraş disaster, per the authors.

Human oversight

The system generated the detection and the alerts itself. The sources describe no human review before the alerts went out. [1] [2] [3] [4]

AI contribution

Android Earthquake Alerts detected the earthquake from phone accelerometers and issued warnings. The authors report detection 5.31 seconds after the earthquake began and an alert within a further 0.43 seconds. The papers describe an on-phone detection algorithm and do not use the terms machine learning or artificial intelligence. The sources do not establish whether the system uses AI. [1] [2] [3] [4]

Benefits

Reach

Alerts reached more than 16 million Android users in Türkiye, Greece, Romania and Bulgaria. In a voluntary survey of 67,056 recipients, 50.5% reported receiving a warning before they felt shaking. [1] [2] [3] [4]

Distribution and access

Warning time depended on distance and shaking level. The 56-second median represents population-weighted geographic coverage for weak MMI III shaking, not a measured median for all alerted phones.

Duration

The alerts covered a single earthquake on 23 April 2025. Google reports that the system was still operating in Türkiye on 16 September 2025, with more than 116 million alerts issued for 203 detected events since June 2021. [1] [2] [3] [4]

People and places

Dependencies

The service needed enough connected phones to detect the first waves and a communication path that delivered warnings before stronger waves arrived.

Follow-up

Conditions for replication

Enough stationary, charging phones near the epicentre to trigger early. Magnitude estimates that do not saturate below the true magnitude. This failed here at Mw 6.04 and failed badly in 2023. Alerts that outrun S-wave arrival at the populated distance. Users who recognize and act on the alert.
