What happened

In June 2025 AI4Africa proposed an AI-enabled 6G vehicle-to-everything (V2X) network for connected transport. The team reported that the work was in the model training and simulation phase and that it had produced no results. Its deep reinforcement learning method allocates vehicles to roadside units. The team takes integer linear programming in its conventional use as the benchmark for that method. The system has not been deployed. No paper, code release or ITU update on this system has appeared since June 2025. Mobility benefits remain prospective.
