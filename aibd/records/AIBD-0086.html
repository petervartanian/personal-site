SUVAS translated 36,271 Supreme Court judgments into Hindi and 17,142 into 16 languages by 5 Aug 2024; High Courts report own totals

What happened

SUVAS (Supreme Court Vidhik Anuvaad Software) is the Supreme Court of India's AI-assisted tool for translating English judgments into Indian languages. A 2025 peer-reviewed article citing the Press Information Bureau reports that as on 5 August 2024, 36,271 Supreme Court judgments had been translated into Hindi and 17,142 into 16 other regional languages. The Supreme Court's Annual Report 2024-25 summarizes a judge's address crediting SUVAS with translating over 100,000 judgments into 18 regional languages, and records High Court translation work: Jammu & Kashmir and Ladakh, which the report says uses SUVAS, had translated 4,426 Supreme Court judgments into Urdu, and Manipur's AI-Assisted Legal Translation Committee had translated 433 Supreme Court judgments into Meetei Mayek, 259 uploaded and 174 under review, which the report describes as 'AI-assisted translation' without naming the tool. These are aggregates; no single translated judgment with a dated delivery was found.

What it was built to do. Translate Supreme Court and High Court judgments from English into Indian languages to widen access

Replication

What produced it
  • Machine translation of English judgments by SUVAS
  • Review by High Court AI-assisted translation committees before upload
  • Publication on the e-SCR portal and High Court websites
What must hold again
  • Digitised English judgments as input
  • Committees with capacity to verify output in each language
  • Hosting portals for the translated texts
