SUVAS translates 36,271 Supreme Court judgments into Hindi

SUVAS translated 36,271 Supreme Court judgments into Hindi and 17,142 into 16 languages by 5 August 2024; High Courts report own totals

ⓘ

What happened

SUVAS (Supreme Court Vidhik Anuvaad Software) is the Supreme Court of India's AI-assisted tool for translating English judgments into Indian languages. A 2025 peer-reviewed article cites Press Information Bureau figures as on 5 August 2024. By then 36,271 Supreme Court judgments were translated into Hindi and 17,142 into 16 other regional languages. The Supreme Court's Annual Report 2024–25 summarizes a judge's address crediting SUVAS with translating over 100,000 judgments into 18 regional languages. It also records High Court translation work. Jammu & Kashmir and Ladakh, which the report says uses SUVAS, translated 4,426 Supreme Court judgments into Urdu. Manipur's AI-Assisted Legal Translation Committee translated 433 Supreme Court judgments into Meetei Mayek, 259 uploaded and 174 under review. The report calls this “AI-assisted translation” without naming the tool.

These are aggregates. No single translated judgment with a dated delivery was found.

Purpose. Translate Supreme Court and High Court judgments from English into Indian languages to widen access

AI’s contribution

Not assessed. Court documents attribute the translations to SUVAS with human review by translation committees. The sources do not evaluate accuracy or separate machine from human contribution. The PIB release behind the 36,271/17,142 figures is not itself among the sources. So it is cited via the peer-reviewed article that quotes it.

Further evidence

Setting. Machine translation of English judgments by SUVAS. Review by High Court AI-assisted translation committees before upload. Publication on the e-SCR portal and High Court websites.

Conditions. Digitized English judgments as input. Committees with capacity to verify output in each language. Hosting portals for the translated texts.
