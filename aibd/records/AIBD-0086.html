What happened

SUVAS (Supreme Court Vidhik Anuvaad Software) is the Supreme Court of India's AI-assisted tool for translating English judgments into Indian languages. A 2025 peer-reviewed article citing the Press Information Bureau reports that as on 5 August 2024, 36,271 Supreme Court judgments had been translated into Hindi and 17,142 into 16 other regional languages.

The Supreme Court's Annual Report 2024–25 summarizes a judge's address crediting SUVAS with translating over 100,000 judgments into 18 regional languages, and records High Court translation work: Jammu & Kashmir and Ladakh, which the report says uses SUVAS, had translated 4,426 Supreme Court judgments into Urdu, and Manipur's AI-Assisted Legal Translation Committee had translated 433 Supreme Court judgments into Meetei Mayek, 259 uploaded and 174 under review, which the report describes as “AI-assisted translation” without naming the tool. These are aggregates; no single translated judgment with a dated delivery was found.

What it was built to do. Translate Supreme Court and High Court judgments from English into Indian languages to widen access

The system's part

Not assessed. Court documents attribute the translations to SUVAS with human review by translation committees; the sources do not evaluate accuracy or separate machine from human contribution. The PIB release behind the 36,271/17,142 figures could not be read directly (its URL was not recoverable with the search tools available), so it is cited via the peer-reviewed article that quotes it.

Replication

What produced it. Machine translation of English judgments by SUVAS. Review by High Court AI-assisted translation committees before upload. Publication on the e-SCR portal and High Court websites.

What must hold again. Digitized English judgments as input. Committees with capacity to verify output in each language. Hosting portals for the translated texts.
