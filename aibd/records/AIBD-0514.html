OneAstronomy model scores higher than GPT-4o on two astronomy tests

Zhejiang Lab and the National Astronomical Observatories of China report that OneAstronomy, built on Qwen2.5-72B, scored higher than comparison models on two astronomy test sets.

What happened

Zhejiang Lab and the National Astronomical Observatories of China built the OneAstronomy research platform on Qwen2.5-72B. They report that it scored higher than the base model, GPT-4o and AstroLLaMA 70B on two astronomy test sets of knowledge and reasoning: GPQA-astro, with 447 questions, and the NAOC-TianYi test set. They give the scores only in charts and name no outside group that ran the tests. Discoveries and public benefits remain unmeasured.
