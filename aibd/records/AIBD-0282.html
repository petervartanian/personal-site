AresGW finds eight new gravitational-wave candidates in LIGO O3

The deep-learning pipeline AresGW found eight new O3 candidates with p_astro > 0.5 (cumulative 5.94); an independent burst search detected one of them, and a later search recovered none.

Later findings

A University of Western Australia team published an independent deep-learning search of O3 in Physical Review D on 22 July 2026. Their pipeline did not recover any of the eight AresGW-only candidates, and the authors attribute most missed events to its lower sensitivity at low chirp mass. They also note that GW190607_083827 is supported by AresGW and by an independent burst search. [4]
