What happened

The International Fund for Animal Welfare (IFAW) and Baidu launched AI Guardian, an image-recognition tool that supports monitoring of online wildlife trade, on 22 April 2020. In a five-month test it flagged 3,348 images of illegal wildlife products among about 250,000 picture messages, at a reported accuracy of 75%. IFAW reports that by June 2024 the tool had screened over 360,000 images and detected 21,271 images of targeted species. The screening led to the removal of 7,853 illegal postings. Version 2.0, a broader species-detection version, followed on 21 November 2024. The number of prevented transactions or animals protected is not established.
