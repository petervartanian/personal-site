UDK fraud models may have yielded 135, 36 and 72 cases

UDK/ATP ML fraud models (about 60 since 2019): 2023 figures of 135, 36 and 72 cases with revenue from three models; Amnesty (Nov 2024) calls their effectiveness questionable.

ⓘ

Udbetaling Danmark / ATP Den Faelles Dataenhed fraud-control models (about 60 profiling and machine-learning models as of 2019, including 'Really Single', 'Model Abroad' and 'Fictitious Employment')

Workflow

Models examine linked public registers and flag recipients for review. A UDK/ATP team that handles about 5,000 to 6,000 cases a year screens the results and investigates selected cases. Around 1,800 of its annual cases come from the models.

Human oversight

Staff investigated the files and decided whether benefits should be reduced or stopped.

Relation to AI

The models select “requested cases” that UDK's HOK control team screens and investigates. Decisions to stop or reduce benefits are taken by humans after investigation. UDK measures model quality by the share of cases the team accepts for manual processing. Every outcome figure is a UDK statement (FOI responses and a letter) as reproduced verbatim in Amnesty International's report. No official publication of them is among the sources. Amnesty contests effectiveness and alleges discrimination risks, and UDK rejected parts of Amnesty's assessment. NNIT built the models to ATP's specifications. No developer-authored source is on record. The dm.dk report and ATP's page are not among the sources.

AI contribution

Udbetaling Danmark’s models selected benefit files for investigation. Staff screened the selections before deciding whether to investigate further.

Results

The three controls reported 135, 36, and 72 cases with payments stopped or reduced in 2023. The same controls requested 412, 511, and 491 cases, but the agency cautions that the annual totals do not necessarily refer to the same cases.

Limitations

The outcome figures come from the agency’s letter reproduced by Amnesty. No monetary amounts or matched case cohorts are given. Amnesty disputes the controls’ effectiveness and necessity, while the agency rejected parts of that assessment.
