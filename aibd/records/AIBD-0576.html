Milton Keynes offers hands-on AI learning and interactive demonstrations

Milton Keynes offered a practical AI workshop and interactive demonstrations in 2024. Better understanding, confidence, and feedback remain intended benefits without measured changes.

ⓘ

What happened

The festival included Code For Her's October 29, 2024 workshop, where participants could use and build AI on their own laptops. The Smart City Experience Centre opened on November 1 with interactive AI applications and robots, including Pepper for visitor information and entertainment. The host aimed to improve public understanding and confidence and gather feedback for technology providers. The accounts provide no measurements of changes in learning, anxiety, or product quality. Civic outcomes from the separate hackathon are unspecified.

Purpose. Improve understanding of AI through practical use and interactive demonstrations, and obtain user feedback on technology applications.

AI’s contribution

Not assessed. Participants could use and build AI, and visitors could interact with AI applications. Effects on learning and feedback are not separated from human instruction and the wider event.

Intended beneficiaries

  • unrated
    a person

    Workshop participants and visitors are intended to understand AI better through practical use and interactive demonstrations. The extent of any learning or confidence improvement is unmeasured.

  • unrated
    an organization

    Technology providers are intended to receive useful feedback from public interaction with their applications. The sources do not establish the extent or consequences of that feedback.

Further evidence

Setting. Participants are offered practical AI exercises, while visitors can interact with AI applications in an accessible shopping-center setting.

Conditions. Likely requirements include suitable AI tools, practical instruction, usable demonstrations, and visitor feedback. These have not been validated as sufficient for a beneficial outcome.
