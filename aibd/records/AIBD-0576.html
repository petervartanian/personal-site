Milton Keynes offers hands-on AI learning and interactive demonstrations

Milton Keynes's 2024 AI festival offered a hands-on AI workshop and an experience center with interactive AI applications and robots, including Pepper; no source measures what visitors learned.

  • Not rated an organization

What happened

The council's account states that the Milton Keynes AI Festival ran from 28 October to 1 November 2024. On 29 October 2024 the festival included a Code For Her workshop, where participants could use and build AI on their own laptops. The Smart City Experience Centre opened on 1 November 2024 with interactive AI applications and robots. They included Pepper, a robot that gives visitors information and entertainment. The host aimed to improve public understanding and confidence and to gather feedback for technology providers. The festival also included a separate hackathon, and its civic outcomes are unspecified. The council's account of 25 March 2025 states that the festival drew over 2,300 attendees. The accounts provide no measurements of changes in learning, anxiety or product quality. On 28 October 2025, Smart City Consultancy reported that robots from a new government-funded licensing trial would be on show to the public in the Experience Centre.

Conditions for replication
