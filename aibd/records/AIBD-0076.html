AlphaEvolve finds 48-multiplication 4x4 complex matrix algorithm

depth 1 for an organization, depth 1 for society

4x4 complex-valued matrices with 48 scalar multiplications, improving on the 49

AlphaEvolve found a 48-multiplication 4x4 complex matrix algorithm (May 2025); Dumas, Pernet and Sedoglavic derived rational variants from its decomposition

What happened

On 14 May 2025 Google DeepMind announced AlphaEvolve, a Gemini-powered evolutionary coding agent, and reported that it had found an algorithm multiplying two 4x4 complex-valued matrices with 48 scalar multiplications, improving on the 49 obtained from two recursion levels of Strassen's 1969 algorithm. On 16 June 2025 Jean-Guillaume Dumas, Clément Pernet and Alexandre Sedoglavic (CASC and CRIStAL, France), researchers independent of DeepMind, posted a preprint presenting a 48-multiplication algorithm with only rational coefficients, removing the need for complex arithmetic; they state that it was derived from AlphaEvolve's algorithm under the action of an isotropy that projects the decomposition onto the field of rational numbers, so the derivation is not independent of the AI result and the preprint contains no explicit verification statement. On 19 March 2026 the same authors posted a more accurate variant of that rational algorithm, valid over any ring containing an inverse of 2. These derived variants corroborate and extend the rank-48 bound; the preprints are not yet recorded as journal-published.

The system's part

AlphaEvolve discovered the 48-multiplication scheme over the complex numbers; researchers independent of DeepMind corroborated the rank-48 bound by deriving rational and more accurate variants from the AI's decomposition, through an isotropy that projects it onto the rational numbers, which is the community acceptance relied on here. The derivation is not independent of the AI result and the 2025 preprint does not state an explicit verification of it. Both sources are preprints by authors independent of DeepMind.

Replication

What produced it. Evolutionary search over candidate programs generated by Gemini with automatic evaluation. A rank-48 decomposition target for the 4x4 matrix multiplication tensor.

What must hold again. An automatic verifier scores every candidate algorithm. Independent researchers can reproduce and extend the decomposition.
