AlphaEvolve finds a 48-multiplication method for 4x4 complex matrices

In May 2025 DeepMind reported that its Gemini-powered coding agent AlphaEvolve had found a 48-multiplication algorithm for 4x4 complex matrices, a rank that Igor Kaporin had reported in 2024.

  • 1 an organization

    Small: a research result for the developer. DeepMind's blog reports improvements on about 20% of the open problems tested.

  • 1 society

    Small: a one-multiplication improvement for 4x4 matrices. Independent researchers have since derived rational and faster variants, and a 2024 paper by Kaporin had already reported a 48-multiplication design.

Depth runs from 1, a convenience or time saved, to 5, a life or a livelihood preserved.

The system’s part
AlphaEvolve found the 48-multiplication scheme over the complex numbers. Researchers outside DeepMind then derived rational and faster variants from that decomposition by isotropies. A peer-reviewed ISSAC 2026 paper by Moran, Schwartz and Yuan recovers the rational form by a general method. These derivations depend on the AI result. They corroborate it without discovering the scheme independently. Kaporin had reported a 48-multiplication design with complex coefficients, found by numerical search, in 2024. AlphaEvolve was therefore not the first to reach a rank of 48 for 4x4 complex matrices.
Limits
The rational variants derive from AlphaEvolve's decomposition, so they are not independent rediscoveries. Kaporin reported a 48-multiplication design in 2024, before AlphaEvolve. The sources do not report wall-clock speed or a broad economic benefit.

What happened

In a Doklady Mathematics paper published online on 20 October 2024, Igor Kaporin of the Russian Academy of Sciences reported a 48-multiplication design for 4x4 matrices that he found by numerical search. On 14 May 2025 Google DeepMind announced AlphaEvolve, a Gemini-powered evolutionary coding agent. DeepMind reported that it had found an algorithm multiplying two 4x4 complex-valued matrices with 48 scalar multiplications, improving on the 49 obtained from two recursion levels of Strassen's 1969 algorithm. The makers' technical paper, posted on 16 June 2025, states that AlphaEvolve is the first method to find a rank-48 algorithm for these matrices. The AlphaEvolve paper does not cite Kaporin's paper. On 16 June 2025 Jean-Guillaume Dumas, Clément Pernet and Alexandre Sedoglavic (CASC and CRIStAL, France), researchers independent of DeepMind, posted a preprint. It presents a 48-multiplication algorithm with only rational coefficients, removing the need for complex arithmetic. They state that it was derived from AlphaEvolve's algorithm under the action of an isotropy that projects the decomposition onto the field of rational numbers. The derivation is therefore not independent of the AI result. The first version of the preprint contains no explicit verification statement. On 19 March 2026 the same authors posted a more accurate variant of that rational algorithm, valid over any ring containing an inverse of 2. In a peer-reviewed ISSAC 2026 paper of 12 July 2026, Yoav Moran, Oded Schwartz and Shuncheng Yuan recover the rational form of AlphaEvolve's scheme by a general method and prove that the scheme has no integer-coefficient equivalent. On 16 July 2026 Li, Wang and Hu, of Zhejiang University of Technology and the National University of Defense Technology, posted a preprint in which they find infinitely many inequivalent rational 48-multiplication algorithms. Version 7 of the Dumas, Pernet and Sedoglavic preprint (27 July 2026) embeds a Maple program that checks their rational algorithm. On 10 September 2026 Dumas, Pernet and Sedoglavic, with Joshua Stapleton and Petr Tichavský, posted a practical 48-multiplication algorithm in the same isotropy orbit that needs 204 other operations. These derived variants corroborate and extend the rank-48 bound. Journal publication of the Dumas, Pernet and Sedoglavic preprints is unconfirmed.

Details

AI system

Role

AlphaEvolve generated a scheme for multiplying matrices with fewer multiplications.

Configuration

AlphaEvolve used Gemini models in the system announced in May 2025.

Inputs and outputs

AlphaEvolve generated candidate algorithms and used automated evaluators to select promising matrix-multiplication schemes.

Workflow

Evolutionary search over candidate programs generated by Gemini with automatic evaluation. A rank-48 decomposition target for the 4x4 matrix multiplication tensor.

Human oversight

Researchers defined the mathematical target and evaluation criteria. Other mathematicians then derived rational variants from the published decomposition.
