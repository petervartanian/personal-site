AlphaEvolve finds a 48-multiplication method for 4x4 complex matrices

In May 2025 DeepMind reported that its Gemini-powered coding agent AlphaEvolve had found a 48-multiplication algorithm for 4x4 complex matrices, a rank that Igor Kaporin had reported in 2024.

Relation to AI

AlphaEvolve found the 48-multiplication scheme over the complex numbers. Researchers outside DeepMind then derived rational and faster variants from that decomposition by isotropies. A peer-reviewed ISSAC 2026 paper by Moran, Schwartz and Yuan recovers the rational form by a general method. These derivations depend on the AI result. They corroborate it without discovering the scheme independently. Kaporin had reported a 48-multiplication design with complex coefficients, found by numerical search, in 2024. AlphaEvolve was therefore not the first to reach a rank of 48 for 4x4 complex matrices.

    Original developer announcement. Gemini generation is paired with automated evaluation and evolutionary selection. May 14 announcement, evaluators description

    Original independent derivation. Authors construct a rational variant through isotropy of the original decomposition. A rational point in the orbit of AlphaEvolve's tensor decomposition

    Original independent derivation

    AlphaEvolve's procedure found an algorithm to multiply 4x4 complex-valued matrices using 48 scalar multiplications, improving upon Strassen's 1969 algorithm that was previously known as the best in this setting.

  2. Preprint Independent author

    Dumas, J.-G., Pernet, C., Sedoglavic, A. (2025). A non-commutative algorithm for multiplying 4x4 matrices using 48 non-complex multiplications. arXiv:2506.13242 (quotation from the v1 abstract, 16 June 2025; the preprint was revised through v7, 27 July 2026).

    We propose an algorithm in 48 multiplications with only rational coefficients, hence removing the complex number requirement. It was derived from the latter one, under the action of an isotropy which happen to project the algorithm on the field of rational numbers.

  3. Preprint Independent author

    Dumas, J.-G., Pernet, C., Sedoglavic, A. (2026). A more accurate rational non-commutative algorithm for multiplying 4x4 matrices using 48 multiplications. arXiv:2603.18699.

    We propose a more accurate variant of an algorithm for multiplying 4x4 matrices using 48 multiplications over any ring containing an inverse of 2.

  4. Peer-reviewed study Independent author

    Moran, Y., Schwartz, O., Yuan, S., Complex to Rational Fast Matrix Multiplication, Proceedings of ISSAC 2026, pp. 297-304, 12 July 2026 (arXiv:2602.13171, 13 February 2026). The authors work at the Hebrew University of Jerusalem and UC San Diego and have no tie to DeepMind; they treat AlphaEvolve's complex scheme as a valid algorithm, recover its rational equivalent by a general method, and prove that AlphaEvolve's schemes have no integer-coefficient equivalents.

    This method recovers the previously known ad hoc results of Dumas, Pernet, and Sedoglavic (2025) and extends them to more general settings

  5. Peer-reviewed study Independent author Context or contrary evidence

    Kaporin, I. E., Semi-analytical solution of Brent equations, Doklady Mathematics 110(1), published online 20 October 2024 (Russian edition: Doklady Rossijskoj akademii nauk 518(1), 2024). Kaporin works at the Federal Research Center Computer Science and Control of the Russian Academy of Sciences and has no tie to DeepMind; he reports that a numerical search found a (4,4,4;48) design, which challenges DeepMind's claim to be first.

    for the resulting fast matrix multiplication algorithms many known values of rank are reproduced and even improved, in particular, the designs (4,4,4;48) and (2,4,5;32) are found

  6. Preprint Independent author

    Li, X., Wang, Y., Hu, S., Substitution and quotient of the isotropy group action, arXiv:2607.15069, 16 July 2026 (version 2, 15 August 2026). The authors work at Zhejiang University of Technology and the National University of Defense Technology and have no tie to DeepMind; they parameterize the rational solution derived from AlphaEvolve's scheme and find infinitely many inequivalent rational 48-multiplication algorithms.

    In the parameterized solution set, we can find infinitely many inequivalent 48-multiplication algorithms with only rational coefficients.

  7. Preprint Developer

    Novikov, A. and colleagues (Google DeepMind), AlphaEvolve: A coding agent for scientific and algorithmic discovery, arXiv:2506.13131, 16 June 2025. The makers' technical paper states the priority claim that Kaporin's 2024 paper contradicts.

    AlphaEvolve is the first method to find a rank-48 algorithm to multiply two 4 × 4 complex-valued matrices.
