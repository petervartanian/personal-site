What happened

On 14 January 2025 the Controladoria-Geral da União (CGU) reported on ALICE (Analisador de Licitações, Contratos e Editais). ALICE, its in-house tool, reads procurement documents with artificial-intelligence techniques and issues risk alerts. It had flagged three 2024 tenders of federal hospitals in Rio de Janeiro state. At the Hospital Federal de Ipanema, tender 90016/2024 for laminated gowns (estimated R$16,726,635.12) was suspended after auditors confirmed an overpricing risk of R$10,925,029.92. Tender 90017/2024 for nursing supplies (estimated R$15,450,714.80) had an overpricing risk of R$5,994,645.60. It was re-estimated at R$12,386,939.96, a saving of R$3,063,774.84. At the Hospital Federal dos Servidores do Estado, tender 90002/2024 (estimated R$26,578,591.02) was revoked after a confirmed risk of R$2,475,054.36. The release gives no total.

The three confirmed risks sum to R$19,394,729.88, the R$19.4 million of the headline and metric. The release gives no dates for the suspension and revocation.

What it was built to do. Continuous automated audit of federal procurement notices to detect risks such as inflated price estimates before contracts are awarded

The system's part

The CGU release states that each tender “teve alerta do Robô ALICE” and that auditors then confirmed the overpricing risk. The suspension, revocation and re-estimation are described as results of that audit action. Human auditors and hospital managers made the decisions. ALICE's role was detection and triage. Developer and deployer are the same public body, so this is the body documenting its own tool. The CGU page is on record as an archived copy, because the live page was restricted during Brazil's 2026 electoral period.

Replication

What produced it. ALICE audit trails that flag price surveys with abnormally high estimates in documents published on Compras.gov.br and bank procurement systems. CGU preventive audit teams that examine flagged tenders before the auction concludes and recommend corrections. Managers' response to audit recommendations (suspension, revocation or new price estimate).

What must hold again. Procurement planning documents and notices must be machine-readable in the monitored systems. Audit capacity must exist to act on alerts before award. Reference prices must be available to confirm overpricing.
