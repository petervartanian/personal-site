CGU's ALICE flags R$19.4m overpricing risk in three Rio tenders

The CGU's ALICE tool flagged three 2024 federal hospital tenders in Rio de Janeiro state; auditors confirmed R$19.4m of overpricing risk, and one was suspended, one revoked and one re-estimated

The system’s part
The CGU release states that each tender “teve alerta do Robô ALICE” and that auditors then confirmed the overpricing risk. The suspension, revocation and re-estimation are described as results of that audit action. Human auditors and hospital managers made the decisions. ALICE's role was detection and triage. Developer and deployer are the same public body, so this is the body documenting its own tool. The CGU page is on record as an archived copy, because the live page was restricted during Brazil's 2026 electoral period.
Limits
The roughly R$19.4 million total is identified overpricing risk across three tenders, not demonstrated cash savings. The nursing tender’s revised estimate is a separate reduction of about R$3.1 million. Suspension and revocation do not establish final expenditure avoided, and the result depends on human audit and procurement action.

What happened

In March 2023 Fernanda Odilla of the University of Bologna reported in an independent study that Alice has helped auditors stop irregular tenders across Brazil. She did not examine the tenders in this case. ALICE (Analisador de Licitações, Contratos e Editais) is the in-house tool of the Controladoria-Geral da União (CGU). It reads procurement documents with artificial-intelligence techniques and issues risk alerts. The national procurement portal shows that tender 90017/2024 was published on 14 August 2024. On 14 January 2025 the CGU reported that ALICE had flagged three 2024 tenders of federal hospitals in Rio de Janeiro state. At the Hospital Federal de Ipanema, tender 90016/2024 for laminated gowns (estimated R$16,726,635.12) was suspended after auditors confirmed an overpricing risk of R$10,925,029.92. Tender 90017/2024 for nursing supplies (estimated R$15,450,714.80) had an overpricing risk of R$5,994,645.60. It was re-estimated at R$12,386,939.96, a saving of R$3,063,774.84. At the Hospital Federal dos Servidores do Estado, tender 90002/2024 (estimated R$26,578,591.02) was revoked after a confirmed risk of R$2,475,054.36. The release gives no total. The three confirmed risks sum to R$19,394,729.88, the R$19.4 million of the headline and summary. The release gives no dates for the suspension and revocation. The portal shows that the hospital bought surgical gloves under tender 90017/2024 in May 2025, after the re-estimate. That record gives no final prices, so it does not show the size of the saving at purchase.

Details

AI system

Inputs and outputs

ALICE read procurement documents and produced risk alerts for auditors to investigate.

Workflow

ALICE audit trails that flag price surveys with abnormally high estimates in documents published on Compras.gov.br and bank procurement systems. CGU preventive audit teams that examine flagged tenders before the auction concludes and recommend corrections. Managers' response to audit recommendations (suspension, revocation or new price estimate).

Human oversight

Auditors checked the flagged prices, and hospital managers suspended, revoked, or revised the tenders.
