Relation to AI

No cited source documents an AI or machine-learning role. INTERPOL's release says it tracked the vessel with support from OceanMind and that hourly updates were aided by contributions from Global Fishing Watch. GFW's own article credits named analysts who tracked AIS data, predicted the course and identified rendezvous points. It does not mention algorithms, machine learning or automated detection. OceanMind's homepage states only that it uses satellites, AI and human expertise in general. The AI role is thus undocumented rather than established. The account comes from INTERPOL, GFW, and the Blue Belt blog. The court outcome (Supreme Court verdict of 27 February 2020, conviction, Rp300,000,000 fine, forfeiture) comes from the Blue Belt blog. The Blue Belt post of 5 June 2021 is no longer available, and no archived copy is cited.
