AI contribution

The tutor provided adaptive exercises, hints and mastery-based progression within a complete algebra curriculum. The trial did not separate its contribution from classroom teaching, materials or training. Later analyses of the trial's software logs by Adam Sales, John Pane and Anita Israni gave conflicting answers on whether hint use helped. They also found that students often moved on without mastery, and more often in the second year, when the effect was larger. [1] [2] [3] [4] [5] [6] [7] [8]

Evidence

  • Solid. A court or regulator found a benefit, or researchers independently studied it.

  • Hatched. Independent reporting.

  • Outline. Claimed by the parties. Only the people or organizations involved, or sources whose independence has not been assessed, support the benefit.
