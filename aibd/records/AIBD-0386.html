What happened

The Centre for Development of Advanced Computing (C-DAC) in Pune built a virtual instructor that uses AI to recognize yoga poses and give feedback. The C-DAC team described the system in a paper at the International Telecommunication Union (ITU) Kaleidoscope conference in New Delhi in October 2024. The authors report that their MoveNet-based classifier identified ten yoga poses with an accuracy of 0.86 on test data. They report no trial with practitioners. In its 2025 entry in an ITU report, C-DAC lists an active demonstration and plans a wider pose dataset and a mobile application. Its proposed benefits include accessible exercise guidance and preventive health education. No source reports a fitness or health improvement for any practitioner.

Details

AI system

Model

MoveNet supplied body-pose estimation, with a classifier using the resulting landmarks to assess yoga poses.

Inputs and outputs

Video of the practitioner supplied body landmarks. The proposed interface compared the pose with learned patterns and provided visual or audible feedback and a holding-time counter.

The account does not establish conditions sufficient to reproduce the benefit.
