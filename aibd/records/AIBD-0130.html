Google's system alerts phones in Crete seconds before an earthquake

Google's Android Earthquake Alerts System warned Android users in Crete and the Dodecanese seconds before a magnitude 6.0 quake near Kasos on 14 May 2025.

  • 1 a person

    Android users in Crete, the Dodecanese and farther away received a message and an audible signal a few seconds before the shaking of a deep magnitude 6.0 quake. The reports do not state their number or name a recipient, and they give no lead time in seconds. They report no damage, injuries or protective action.

Depth runs from 1, a convenience or time saved, to 5, a life or a livelihood preserved.

The system’s part
If the reports are right, the alert was produced end to end by Google's system. Accelerometer triggers from many phones were combined on Google's servers to detect the quake. Alerts were then pushed ahead of the slower waves, as Google's 2020 description and the 2025 Science paper set out. Google's own pages do not use the words AI or machine learning. The Science abstract speaks of smartphone-based earthquake detection algorithms. The OECD Monitor classifies the system as AI doing event detection. That alerts arrived seconds before shaking rests on unattributed statements in four Greek outlets published within a day of the quake. None quotes a recipient, a seismologist or Google. No Google per-event confirmation was found.
Limits
The reports give no named recipient or measured lead time for the Kasos earthquake. USGS confirms the event, not alert delivery. General Android system documentation does not establish that particular users received a warning early enough to act or that injuries were prevented.

What happened

Google switched on its Android Earthquake Alerts System in Greece on 28 April 2021. In Europe the system detects quakes with the accelerometers in phones and uses no seismographs. On 23 April 2025 the magnitude 6.2 Marmara Ereğlisi earthquake struck Türkiye. Google's team reports that the system detected it 5.31 seconds after it began and alerted more than 16 million users. Three weeks later, at 01:51 local time on Wednesday 14 May 2025 (22:51 UTC on 13 May), an earthquake struck between Kasos and Karpathos. The U.S. Geological Survey gives its magnitude as 6.0, and Greek outlets give 6.1. It was centered 20 km south-south-east of Fry on Kasos, at 76 km depth. The U.S. Geological Survey logged 931 felt reports and a green alert level. On 14 and 15 May Newsbomb, GizChina Greece, in.gr and Alphafreepress reported that the system notified users in Crete, the Dodecanese and farther away seconds before the shaking. Notice came by message and an audible signal similar to the 112 alert. None of the reports names a recipient or gives a lead time in seconds. None mentions damage or injuries. On 15 October 2026 Google extended the service to more than 200 regions.

Details

AI system

Inputs and outputs

The system combined motion signals and approximate locations from Android phones, then sent alerts to nearby users.

Workflow

A magnitude 6.0 (USGS) quake above the system's 4.5 alert threshold, centered offshore south of Kasos at about 76 km depth. A push alert could therefore outrun the slower waves to Crete and the Dodecanese. Enough Android phones near the epicentre with the Earthquake Alerts setting on to trigger Google's server-side detection. In Europe the system uses the accelerometers in phones instead of seismographs (in.gr). Receiving phones with location (GPS) enabled and an internet connection, which Alphafreepress lists as requirements for the alerts.

Human oversight

The record documents no human review before the alerts went out. Google's servers combined the signals from many phones and pushed alerts ahead of the slower seismic waves. Alerts reach only phones with the Earthquake Alerts setting enabled. [1] [2] [3] [4] [5] [6] [7] [8]

AI contribution

Android Earthquake Alerts combined signals from phone accelerometers and sent warnings. Greek news reports said that some alerts arrived before the shaking.

Benefits

Reach

No source counts the Android users in Crete, the Dodecanese and farther away who received the alert. The reports mention no damage or injuries and no protective action. [1] [2] [3] [4] [5] [6] [7] [8]

Duration

The alert was a single warning before one quake. Google's team reports that the same system alerted more than 16 million users for the Marmara Ereğlisi quake of 23 April 2025. On 15 October 2026 Google extended the system to more than 200 regions, up from 98. [1] [2] [3] [4] [5] [6] [7] [8]

Follow-up

Conditions for replication

The server's magnitude and location estimate must be right and early. The 2025 Science paper reports that only 36% of alert recipients got the alert before shaking. Recipients must know what to do with seconds of notice. The Greek reports document no protective action by anyone who received the alert. The phone must have the Earthquake Alerts setting enabled (Alphafreepress: Settings > Safety & Emergency > Earthquake Alerts). The feature must be supported in the region.
