What happened

Mynavi News reported on 12 Aug 2020, on the basis of a press release, that Optage, Sanyo Electric Railway and the Sanyo Electric Railway Information Center had begun a field trial, from early August to mid-October 2020 at three crossings on Sanyo Electric Railway lines, of AI image analysis of crossing surveillance cameras. Edge devices analyze the video in real time to detect people, including those with prams or wheelchairs, and aim to stop trains safely by sending a signal to the driver through a special signal lamp and notifying the operations center over an IP-VPN; accuracy in rain and at night was to be verified with K4Digital. Full deployment was targeted for spring 2021. No results are reported and the OECD AI Incidents Monitor entry (2020-08-11-4fb3) notes that no incidents had been reported; the date given is the publication date.

What it was built to do. The trial was built to detect people in crossings in real time and alert train drivers.
