Benefits

Benefit

VoteMate announced the nationwide launch of its free AI ballot assistant on October 16, 2025. Its original release described conversational, multilingual access to candidate and ballot-measure information and reported that the soft launch had reached thousands of voters in 30 states. In May 2026, Ballotpedia confirmed an agreement for direct integration of its election data. VoteMate’s October 2026 transparency page describes several model providers, human review of AI-written candidate profiles, and connections to official and civic data sources. These publications establish the service and its intended accessibility benefits, but do not measure changes in voter knowledge, accuracy, participation, or turnout. The AI Opportunity Inventory submission reports 7,652 beta users and acknowledges the absence of measured effects on voters. That usage count is not a benefit estimate. [1] [2] [3] [4]

Benefit marks

Depth

The number and color show the depth of a benefit.

  1. 1Depth 1. A convenience, or time or money saved.
  2. 2Depth 2. A measurable improvement in a result or ability.
  3. 3Depth 3. A lasting change, an ability restored, or a risk removed.
  4. 4Depth 4. A serious harm averted or a serious condition resolved in time.
  5. 5Depth 5. A life or livelihood preserved, or a change documented across a population.

Beneficiary

  • Circle: a person
  • Square: an organization
  • Triangle: society

Evidence

  • Solid. A court or regulator found a benefit, or researchers independently studied it.

  • Hatched. Independent reporting.

  • Outline. The report comes from an involved party, or its independence is unassessed.

The fill changes with the evidence. The depth color stays the same.
