Tens of thousands of voters use an AI assistant to research ballots

VoteMate’s free AI ballot assistant answers voters’ questions about candidates and ballot measures; tens of thousands used it before the 2026 midterms, and no source measures what they learned.

What happened

VoteMate announced the nationwide launch of its free AI ballot assistant on 16 October 2025. Its original release described conversational, multilingual access to candidate and ballot-measure information. The release reported that the soft launch had reached thousands of voters in 30 states. The undated AI Opportunity Inventory submission reports 7,652 beta users and acknowledges the absence of measured effects on voters. That usage count is not a benefit estimate. In May 2026, Ballotpedia confirmed an agreement for direct integration of its election data. VoteMate’s October 2026 transparency page describes several model providers, human review of AI-written candidate profiles, and connections to official and civic data sources. On 13 October 2026 VoteMate announced a connector to USAFacts government data. On 22 October 2026 NBC News reported that tens of thousands of voters had used VoteMate before the midterm elections and that its use was growing fast. These publications establish the service and its intended accessibility benefits. None of them measures a change in voter knowledge, accuracy, participation, or turnout. No source reports whether the answers were accurate.

Details

AI system

Release

The October 2026 disclosure names Anthropic, OpenAI, and Microsoft among its providers, without exact model versions. [3]

Configuration

VoteMate retrieves election information and supplements unanswered questions with live web search. [3]

Workflow

The service combines language models with ballot data and retrieval from election-information sources.

Human oversight

Staff review AI-written candidate profiles. Users receive links to the underlying sources. [3]

Benefits

Reach

The soft launch reached thousands of voters in 30 states, and the submission reports 7,652 beta users. NBC News reports that tens of thousands of voters used the service. These figures count users and do not count voters who benefited. [1] [2] [3] [4] [5] [6]

Duration

VoteMate launched nationwide on 16 October 2025, and NBC News reported on 22 October 2026 that its use was growing fast before the midterm elections. No source follows a benefit to voters over time. [1] [2] [3] [4] [5] [6]

Follow-up

Conditions for replication

Reliable and current election data, source links, and appropriate human review are needed. Outcomes need to be measured separately from usage and favorable feedback.
