Transpara helps Swedish breast screening find more cancers

In the MASAI trial of 105,934 women, Transpara-supported screening found 6.4 cancers per 1,000 against 5.0 with standard double reading and cut screen reading by 44.2%.

  • 4 a person

    Women in the AI group had 338 cancers found at screening against 262 in the control group, and the extra invasive cancers were mainly small and node-negative.

  • 2 an organization

    The screening service needed 61,248 screen readings in the AI group against 109,692 in the control group, a 44.2% reduction.

Brief from Peter

Lund University researchers ran the MASAI trial within the Swedish national screening program. Between 12 April 2021 and 7 December 2022, they randomly assigned 105,934 women in southwest Sweden to Transpara-supported screening or to standard double reading by two radiologists without AI. The AI group had 6.4 screen-detected cancers per 1,000 women against 5.0 in the control group, and it needed 44.2% fewer screen readings.1 The primary outcome, published in The Lancet in 2026, was the interval cancer rate: 1.55 per 1,000 with AI and 1.76 per 1,000 without, which met the trial's non-inferiority margin. That difference was not statistically significant.2 The trial tested one product in one region, and its design does not separate the effect of the triage by risk score from that of the marks on suspicious areas.3

  1. 1 The extra invasive cancers were mainly small and had not spread to the lymph nodes. Recall and false-positive rates were not significantly higher in the AI group. V. Hernström and colleagues, The Lancet Digital Health, February 3, 2025. pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov
  2. 2 The ratio of interval cancer rates was 0.88 (95% confidence interval 0.65 to 1.18). J. Gommers and colleagues, The Lancet, January 2026. pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov
  3. 3 Transpara version 1.7.0 from ScreenPoint Medical gave each examination a risk score from 1 to 10. In the AI group, examinations scored 10 went to two readers and all others to one. The risk score of every examination, and the marks for those scored 8 to 10, were available to the readers. K. Lång and colleagues, The Lancet Oncology, August 2023. pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov

Details

AI system

Reported configuration

1.7.0

Workflow

Transpara 1.7.0 scored every screening examination on a scale from 1 to 10. Examinations scored 10 went to double reading and all others to single reading. Readers saw the AI risk scores and detection marks while reading. The trial ran inside an organized population screening program with experienced breast radiologists.

Measurement

Measurement

Study 1: Interval cancers from registry follow-up; sensitivity and specificity. Study 2: Screen-detected cancers, recalls and screen readings. [1] [2]

Setting and sample

Study 1: 105,934 women screened at four sites in southwest Sweden, 12 April 2021 to 7 December 2022. Study 2: 105,915 women analyzed. [1] [2]

Measures

Study 1: Interval cancer rate per 1,000 participants. Study 2: Cancer detection rate per 1,000 and screen-reading workload. [1] [2]

Results

Study 1: 1.55 vs 1.76 per 1,000; proportion ratio 0.88 (95% CI 0.65 to 1.18), non-inferior; sensitivity 80.5% vs 73.8%. Study 2: 6.4 vs 5.0 per 1,000 (ratio 1.29); 44.2% fewer screen readings. [1] [2]

Additional value from AI

Study 1: Sensitivity 6.7 percentage points higher with AI. Study 2: 1.4 more cancers per 1,000 women. [1] [2]

Limitations

Study 1: Interval cancer difference not statistically significant; one AI product; mortality not measured. Study 2: Some extra cancers may be overdiagnosed in situ disease. [1] [2]

Denominators

Study 1: AI triage plus detection support for all examinations in the intervention group. Study 2: AI triage plus detection support. [1] [2]

Materials for replication

Study 1: Trial registered as NCT04838756. Study 2: Trial registered as NCT04838756. [1] [2]

Follow-up

Conditions for replication

Readers must have the AI scores and marks in front of them when they read. The AI must perform on the local equipment and population as it did in the trial. The program must use double reading that the AI triage can replace for low-risk examinations.

Replication attempts

3 follow-up entries. Benefit recurred: 3.

Entry details

Sector

Healthcare.

Purpose

Transpara was built to detect breast cancer on screening mammograms and to score examinations by risk. In MASAI it triaged examinations to single or double reading and marked suspicious findings; the higher detection and the saved reading time were that purpose.
