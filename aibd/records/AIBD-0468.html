A woman with ALS introduces Lenovo's CEO on stage through an AI avatar

The Scott-Morgan Foundation reports that its AI communication platform enabled Erin Taylor, who has ALS, to introduce Lenovo's CEO at Tech World on 15 October 2024.

What happened

Lenovo's release of 15 October 2024 reports that Erin Taylor, a disability advocate with ALS, used the technology to introduce herself and welcome Lenovo Chairman and CEO Yuanqing Yang to the stage at Tech World. She spoke through an avatar in a recreation of her voice as it sounded before her diagnosis. Lenovo co-developed the system and calls it a proof of concept in testing. On 18 March 2025 ALS Network, a patient charity, confirmed that Erin Taylor spoke at Tech World with eye gaze and her AI avatar. In the ITU entry, current to June 2025, the Scott-Morgan Foundation reports that its communication platform enabled Erin Taylor to introduce Lenovo's CEO at Tech World. The chapter also describes an Austrian former parliamentarian and a father who use related communication tools. The sources do not establish a lasting restoration of independent communication. On 15 December 2025 Observer reported that the foundation had launched a successor platform, VoXAI, at the AI Summit New York. The foundation built it with D-ID, ElevenLabs, Irisbond, Lenovo and Nvidia. Observer reported that VoXAI is free at the basic tier, with advanced features available for a $30 monthly subscription. Observer interviewed users with ALS who communicate through it.

Details

AI system

Role

The platform combines IrisBond eye tracking, language-model prediction, ElevenLabs voice synthesis, and D-ID avatars through a Lenovo interface and hardware platform.

Workflow

The account does not separately establish the conditions responsible for the benefit.

Benefits

Beneficiaries

Erin Taylor, who has ALS, is the named beneficiary of the reported Tech World introduction. The account describes other users but does not name them here.

Duration

The use established for Erin Taylor is a prepared introduction at Tech World on 15 October 2024. Observer reported on 15 December 2025 that other users with ALS communicate through the successor platform VoXAI. No source reports her use over a longer period. [1] [2] [3] [4]

Follow-up

Conditions for replication

Eye-tracking input compatible with the participant's abilities. Personalized voice material and an integrated hardware/software platform.

Evidence

The ITU entry is the foundation's own account, and Lenovo, which co-developed the system, issued the release of 15 October 2024. ALS Network, a patient charity with no stake in the platform, and Observer reported independently. Observer does not mention Erin Taylor. [1] [2] [3] [4]
