AI voice tools help people with ALS communicate

The Scott-Morgan Foundation reports that its communication platform enabled Erin Taylor, who has ALS, to introduce Lenovo's CEO at Tech World.

ⓘ

What happened

The Scott-Morgan Foundation reports that its communication platform enabled Erin Taylor, who has ALS, to introduce Lenovo's CEO at Tech World. The chapter also describes an Austrian former parliamentarian and a father using related communication tools. Lasting restoration of independent communication remains unestablished.

Purpose. Enable people with severe communication impairments to express themselves.

AI’s contribution

Eye tracking, language-model prediction, personalized voice synthesis, and avatars combine to enable communication. Their individual contributions have not been separated.

Beneficiaries

  • a person

    Erin Taylor reportedly used the system to introduce Lenovo’s CEO. Lasting restoration of independent communication remains unestablished.

Further evidence

Setting. The account does not separately establish the conditions responsible for the benefit.

Conditions. Eye-tracking input compatible with the participant's abilities. Personalized voice material and an integrated hardware/software platform.
