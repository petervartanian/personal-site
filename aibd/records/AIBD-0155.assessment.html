Short account

Recorded; review pending

Summary
AI camera analytics flagged smoke in an empty temple office a minute before a battery fire; held to 10 m², no injuries, main hall intact.
Account
At about 05:30 on 5 February 2026 the AI門神 (AI door god) system flagged smoke in a first-floor office and machine room, empty after hours. The system is run by 立達軟體 (LEADERG Inc.) over the closed-circuit television (CCTV) of Beigang Wude Temple, Yunlin County, Taiwan. It messaged the front desk, the general manager and chairman 林安樂, in Japan, by LINE. The night-duty clerk reached the room with an extinguisher within a minute. At 05:33 overheated lithium batteries beside a tool charger burst and flames spread. Staff called 119 at 05:36. Yunlin County Fire Bureau crews arrived at 05:44, controlled it at 06:10 and put it out at 06:14. Ten square meters burned. Nobody was hurt. The main hall and statues were untouched.

Account
At about 05:30 on 5 February 2026 the AI門神 (AI door god) system flagged smoke in a first-floor office and machine room, empty after hours. The system is run by 立達軟體 (LEADERG Inc.) over the closed-circuit television (CCTV) of Beigang Wude Temple, Yunlin County, Taiwan. It messaged the front desk, the general manager and chairman 林安樂, in Japan, by LINE. The night-duty clerk reached the room with an extinguisher within a minute. At 05:33 overheated lithium batteries beside a tool charger burst and flames spread. Staff called 119 at 05:36. Yunlin County Fire Bureau crews arrived at 05:44, controlled it at 06:10 and put it out at 06:14. Ten square meters burned. Nobody was hurt. The main hall and statues were untouched.
Replication
Produced by
  • A camera-analytics system (AI門神) already watching the office over the temple's existing CCTV. It was set to alert on small amounts of smoke and to message a named chain of people (front desk, general manager, chairman) over LINE.
  • A staffed night watch. The temple has kept someone on duty every night since it moved to the site in 1980 (民國69年). So the alert had a clerk a minute away with an extinguisher.
  • A slow start to the fire. A tool charger and lithium batteries smoked before the batteries burst at 05:33, which gave the alert a minute's lead.
  • A fire brigade eight minutes away and a reinforced-concrete annex that held the fire to one room, as the Third Brigade commander said.
Must hold
  • The alert reaches a person who can act within minutes. At an unstaffed site the lead time is worth little.
  • The camera covers the rooms where fires start (here an office and machine room with chargers, beside stored joss paper), not only the shrine.
  • The AI alert complements conventional detectors rather than replacing them. A residential smoke alarm sounded here as well.

Replication
Produced by
  • A camera-analytics system (AI門神) already watching the office over the temple's existing CCTV. It was set to alert on small amounts of smoke and to message a named chain of people (front desk, general manager, chairman) over LINE.
  • A staffed night watch. The temple has kept someone on duty every night since it moved to the site in 1980 (民國69年). So the alert had a clerk a minute away with an extinguisher.
  • A slow start to the fire. A tool charger and lithium batteries smoked before the batteries burst at 05:33, which gave the alert a minute's lead.
  • A fire brigade eight minutes away and a reinforced-concrete annex that held the fire to one room, as the Third Brigade commander said.
Must hold
  • The alert reaches a person who can act within minutes. At an unstaffed site the lead time is worth little.
  • The camera covers the rooms where fires start (here an office and machine room with chargers, beside stored joss paper), not only the shrine.
  • The AI alert complements conventional detectors rather than replacing them. A residential smoke alarm sounded here as well.
