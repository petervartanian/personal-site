ChatGPT narrows a mother's search; surgeon finds her son's tethered cord

ChatGPT listed tethered cord syndrome among three possible diagnoses after a mother entered her son's scan notes and symptoms, and a neurosurgeon found the tethered cord and released it.

  • 3 a person

    A boy who had chronic pain for three years received a diagnosis and corrective spinal surgery. He had also come to drag his left foot and had stopped growing taller. In November 2024 his mother said that he is a new kid now. No source shows that the surgery saved his life.

The system’s part
ChatGPT returned three possible diagnoses from the mother's combined records and symptom list. The mother then read parent stories on social media and concluded that tethered cord syndrome was the likely one, which led her to Dr Holly Gilmer. Gilmer says that the mother did not mention tethered cord to her and that she found it herself on a lumbar MRI that she had ordered for his back pain. What happened

Alex's chronic pain began at age 4 during the COVID-19 lockdown. He went on to grind his teeth, stop growing taller and drag his left foot. Over three years, 17 physicians had found no diagnosis. In 2023 his mother, Courtney Hofmann, entered his magnetic resonance imaging (MRI) notes and symptom observations line by line into ChatGPT. ChatGPT suggested tethered cord syndrome. On the NEJM AI Grand Rounds podcast of 20 November 2024, she said that ChatGPT gave three possible diagnoses and that parent stories on social media led her to settle on tethered cord syndrome. She booked a new neurosurgeon, Dr. Holly Gilmer of the Michigan Head & Spine Institute. TODAY.com reports that Gilmer reviewed the MRI, found spina bifida occulta with a tethered cord and operated to release it. On the podcast Gilmer said that the mother had not named the diagnosis to her and that she found the tethered cord on a lumbar MRI herself. The surgery took place a few weeks before the TODAY.com report of 11 September 2023, updated 12 September 2023. Alex was still recovering at the time of that report. Neither source gives the month of the ChatGPT query or the date of the surgery. On the podcast the mother said that her son had surgery six weeks after her ChatGPT query and that he is a new kid now.

Details

AI system

Inputs and outputs

Courtney entered MRI report text and detailed observations about Alex's symptoms. ChatGPT proposed tethered cord syndrome as a possible explanation.

Workflow

A caregiver entering detailed clinical records (MRI report text, symptom observations) in a general-purpose language model. A clinician willing to re-review existing imaging against the proposed diagnosis.

Human oversight

Dr. Holly Gilmer, a neurosurgeon, found spina bifida occulta with a tethered cord and operated to release it. On the podcast she said that the mother had not named the diagnosis to her and that she found the tethered cord on a lumbar MRI herself. [1] [2]

Purpose

ChatGPT is a general-purpose conversational assistant built to answer user prompts. Producing a diagnostic hypothesis from entered clinical notes is an instance of that general function. It is not a medical capability that has been designed or validated.
