26 Indian banks use MuleHunter.AI to flag likely mule accounts

MuleHunter.AI, from the Reserve Bank Innovation Hub, flags likely mule accounts at 26 Indian banks; the Reserve Bank of India has withheld the number of accounts found.

    The Reserve Bank of India declined a Right to Information request for the number of mule accounts identified, and the only monthly figure is one that a news summary attributes to the central bank's governor.

  • Not rated society

What happened

The Reserve Bank Innovation Hub (RBIH) developed MuleHunter.AI. It analyzes transaction and account patterns within each participating bank’s infrastructure to identify likely mule accounts. RBIH’s technical documentation says that personally identifiable information is not exchanged with the hub. On 20 November 2025 the Economic Times reported that the Governor of the Reserve Bank of India (RBI), Sanjay Malhotra, said that the tool showed a good success rate. Its summary attributed to him a figure of about 20,000 mule accounts detected each month. On 30 December 2025 Prabhanu Kumar Das reported in MediaNama on a Right to Information response. It showed that 23 banks had implemented MuleHunter.AI as of 10 December 2025. That was up from at least 15 banks in August 2025 and nearly 20 in November 2025. The report named Canara Bank, Punjab National Bank, Bank of India, Bank of Baroda, AU Small Finance Bank and Federal Bank. The RBI said that it cannot provide data on the number of mule accounts identified or acted upon through MuleHunter.AI. It said that it holds such information in a fiduciary capacity with banks and that disclosure could harm competitive interests. On 30 April 2026 Financial Services Secretary M Nagaraju asked banks to adopt the tool at the earliest. The Ministry of Home Affairs announced on 12 May 2026 that its Indian Cyber Crime Coordination Centre (I4C) and RBIH had signed a memorandum to use suspect identifiers and mule-account intelligence for model improvement. By August 2026 the RBI had told an inter-departmental committee in Supreme Court proceedings on digital-arrest frauds that 26 banks were using the tool. MediaNama reported that other banks used their own systems.

Details

AI system

Role

MuleHunter.AI identifies suspicious account behavior for banks to investigate. Adoption by 23 banks establishes deployment, but the available RTI response does not establish how many mule accounts it detected or stopped.

Workflow

Supervised analysis of account and transaction patterns within each participating bank’s infrastructure.

Human oversight

The banks operate MuleHunter.AI and decide what action to take on the accounts that it flags. The sources do not describe how bank staff review those flags. [1] [2] [3] [4] [5]

Benefits

Duration

Twenty-three banks had implemented the tool by 10 December 2025, and the Reserve Bank reported 26 by August 2026. The sources give no dated series of detections. [1] [2] [3] [4] [5]
