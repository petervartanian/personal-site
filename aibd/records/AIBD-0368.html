Instagram self-harm alert brings Satna police to a 17-year-old

3 November 2022, about 5 pm: Instagram emailed MP cyber police a self-harm alert; Bhopal and Indore teams traced a 17-year-old to Satna in about an hour; she was counseled and sent home.

ⓘ

What happened

On 3 November 2022 at about 5 pm, Madhya Pradesh's state cyber police received an alert emailed by Instagram's US office. It said a user in the state had suffered a body injury. The Indore cyber-crime unit shared the “self-harm” alert with the cyber headquarters in Bhopal (ANI, 5 November 2022; Times of India, 4 November 2022). The girl, 17, had uploaded two videos. In one, a packet of poison lay beside her as she acted as if drunk. In the other she appeared to cut a vein (ANI). The Times of India notes a small cut on her wrist. State Cyber SP Vaibhav Shrivastava said: “After an effort of one hour, we traced the location of the girl”. She was in Satna district, whose police were informed (ANI). Satna SP Ashutosh Gupta said officers located her and called her in with her mother. She said she only wanted to scare her boyfriend and was sent home after counseling (Times of India).

Purpose. Instagram's self-harm detection was built to flag content indicating self-harm or suicide risk and route alerts to agencies that can intervene. An alert that brought police to a girl posting self-harm videos was that purpose.

AI’s contribution

Police say the alert came by email from Instagram's US office after the videos were posted. An ASP described the platform's AI algorithms as flagging non-graphic self-harm content such as scars, search words and hashtags. No source says whether a classifier or a user report started the alert, or whether Instagram staff reviewed it. Everything after the email was human work. Bhopal and Indore technical teams traced the location in about an hour. Satna police found and counseled her.

Beneficiaries

  • a person

    A 17-year-old had posted videos with poison and an apparent cut. She was reached and counseled within about an hour of the alert and sent home with a small cut on her wrist. By her own account she wanted to scare her boyfriend. An averted suicide rests on the police's framing and is not established.

Further evidence

Setting. Instagram's self-harm detection and alert routing, which per the police flags non-graphic self-harm content and emails state cyber police with account information. A state cyber police unit (Bhopal headquarters and Indore unit) able to trace the account's location from the emailed information, here in about an hour. District police able to reach the person and hold a counseling session with a parent present.

Conditions. The account's IP or other details resolve to a reachable district. The person is found before any act. Here she was found with a small cut on her wrist. A standing channel exists between Instagram and the state cyber police. This was the unit's second such alert.
