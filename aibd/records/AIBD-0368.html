Instagram self-harm alert brings Satna police to a 17-year-old

An alert from Instagram's self-harm detection led Madhya Pradesh police to a 17-year-old in Satna within about an hour on 3 November 2022, and she was counseled and sent home.

  • 2 a person

    A 17-year-old had posted videos with poison and an apparent cut. She was reached and counseled within about an hour of the alert and sent home with a small cut on her wrist. By her own account she wanted to scare her boyfriend. An averted suicide rests on the police's framing and is not established.

Depth runs from 1, a convenience or time saved, to 5, a life or a livelihood preserved.

Status
Reported benefit. Police report that they found the girl and provided counseling. A suicide prevented by AI remains uncertain.

The system’s part
Police say that the alert came by email from Instagram's US office after the videos were posted. An ASP described the platform's AI algorithms as flagging non-graphic self-harm content such as scars, search words and hashtags. Decode reports that posts reach police after AI systems or user reports flag them and Meta's staff review them, so the alert likely combined a classifier or a report with human review. No source says which started this alert. Everything after the email was police work. Bhopal and Indore technical teams traced the location in about an hour, and Satna police found and counseled her.
Limits
The alert led to locating and counseling the girl, but the account does not establish imminent lethal danger or an AI-initiated flag. General descriptions of platform algorithms cannot identify the trigger for her alert. Police reporting that she wanted to frighten her boyfriend limits the life-saved claim.
Costs and harms
Decode reports that some alerts in Uttar Pradesh were false alarms. In one, a student in Kanpur Dehat said that she had posted a video with tablets only to gain views. Decode reports that Meta does not publish state- or country-level data on false positives in suicide-risk alerts shared with police.

What happened

On 3 November 2022 at about 5 pm, Madhya Pradesh's state cyber police received an alert emailed by Instagram's US office. It said that a user in the state had suffered a body injury. The Indore cyber-crime unit shared the “self-harm” alert with the cyber headquarters in Bhopal (ANI, 5 November 2022; Times of India, 4 November 2022). The girl, 17, had uploaded two videos. In one, a packet of poison lay beside her as she acted as if drunk. In the other she appeared to cut a vein (ANI). The Times of India notes a small cut on her wrist. Vaibhav Shrivastava, superintendent of police with the state cyber police, said: “After an effort of one hour, we traced the location of the girl”. She was in Satna district, whose police were informed (ANI). Ashutosh Gupta, the Satna superintendent of police, said that officers located her and called her in with her mother. She said that she only wanted to scare her boyfriend, and she was sent home after counseling (Times of India). In January 2026 Decode reported that Meta reviews flagged posts before it alerts police and that Meta publishes no data on false alarms. From its own interviews with Uttar Pradesh police, Decode also described a false alarm in Kanpur Dehat, where a student said that she had posted a video with tablets only to gain views.

Details

AI system

Workflow

Instagram emailed a self-harm warning to cyber police, technical teams traced the account, and local officers brought the girl and her mother in for counseling.

Human oversight

Decode reports that Meta staff review flagged posts before Meta alerts police. No source says how this alert was reviewed. State cyber police traced the girl's location, and Satna officers called her in with her mother and counseled her. [1] [2] [3] [4] [5]

Benefits

Beneficiaries

The girl received an in-person welfare response and counseling. The evidence does not establish that she would otherwise have died.

Reach

One 17-year-old girl was reached in this case. Uttar Pradesh police say that such alerts prevented 1,597 suicide attempts between January 2023 and November 2025, a figure that concerns another state. [1] [2] [3] [4] [5]

Follow-up

Conditions for replication

The account's IP or other details resolve to a reachable district. The person is found before any act. Here she was found with a small cut on her wrist. A standing channel exists between Instagram and the state cyber police. This was the unit's second such alert.
