UCSF neuroprosthesis lets Ann Johnson speak through an avatar

depth 2 for a person

decoded text at a median 78 words per minute with 25% median word error rate

253-electrode ECoG and deep-learning decoder let Ann Johnson, mute since a 2005 stroke, produce text, synthesized speech and an avatar

What happened

At UCSF Medical Center, a 47-year-old woman with quadriplegia and anarthria from a pontine infarct in 2005 (named in news reports as Ann Johnson) had a 253-channel electrocorticography array implanted over speech cortical areas in September 2022 under the BRAVO trial (NCT03698149). Deep-learning recurrent neural networks trained on her attempted silent speech decoded text at a median 78 words per minute with 25% median word error rate over a 1,024-word vocabulary, synthesized speech personalized to her pre-injury voice, and animated a facial avatar (Metzger et al., Nature, 23 August 2023). She had earlier communicated by letterboard. The paper anchors the text-decoding result to 14 days of data collection shortly after the September 2022 implant and gives no calendar session dates, so the implant month (1 September 2022) is used as the observation date.

What it was built to do. Built to restore communication by decoding attempted speech from cortical surface recordings into text, synthesized speech and avatar animation; that was the intended benefit and is what occurred.

The system's part

Deep-learning decoders are the mechanism: they map cortical activity during attempted speech to phonemes, speech units and articulatory gestures, and every output (text, voice, avatar) depends on them.

Replication

What produced it. Preserved articulatory representations in speech sensorimotor cortex 18 years after paralysis. High-density ECoG coverage of speech cortex. Deep-learning decoders trained on the participant's attempted speech in under two weeks.

What must hold again. Participant can attempt speech and retains cortical speech representations. Array remains stable with adequate signal quality. Research infrastructure available for training and real-time decoding.
