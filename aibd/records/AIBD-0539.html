China Telecom's Divine Farmer answers farm questions and detects pests

China Telecom reports that its Divine Farmer model answers farming questions with over 90% accuracy and recognizes pests and diseases with over 85% accuracy.

  • Not rated society

What happened

China Telecom Wanwei Information Technology built the Divine Farmer model to give farming advice, identify pests and forecast agricultural markets. Its project account for the International Telecommunication Union, current as of June 2025, states that the question-answering assistant runs on Qwen2.5-32B and that the pest module uses YOLO models. China Telecom reports an accuracy above 90% for answers to agricultural questions and above 85% for recognition of pests and diseases. It reports an accuracy of over 46% for predictions of price trends. No source measures changes in crop yield, farmer income, pesticide use or environmental conditions. As of 29 October 2026 no independent source has described the model's use or results.
