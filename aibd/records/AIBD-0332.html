Alice audit at TCE-MG corrects notices worth R$91.7 million

TCE-MG audit with Alice of 2,297 Aug 2022 tenders: 60 irregularities (R$113,713,128.26); 53 alerts answered; notices corrected worth R$91,723,011.31 (TCE-MG, 27 June 2023).

ⓘ

What happened

The Tribunal de Contas do Estado de Minas Gerais (TCE-MG) is one of more than ten Brazilian courts of accounts that adopted Alice. Alice is the Análise de Licitações e Editais system created by the CGU and the TCU. In December 2022 it published an audit of 2,297 municipal and state tenders held in a single month, August 2022. The exact day is not given. The court's own news item of 27 June 2023, reproduced by Atricon on 28 June 2023, gives the results. The work detected 60 irregularities involving R$113,713,128.26. 53 of the 60 alerts sent to the audited bodies received answers that allowed control verification. Irregularities were fixed in republished notices or measures adopted for future tenders. The audit achieved corrected notices worth R$91,723,011.31.

The figures are aggregates presented by analysts Maria Aparecida Aiko Ikemura and Leonardo Alves Mateus to about 60 people from courts of accounts across Brazil. No individual tender is named.

Purpose. Automated identification of tender notices with possible inconsistencies or irregularities so that the court can oversee more tenders. This use was its intended purpose.

AI’s contribution

Alice produced the alerts on the 2,297 tenders. TCE-MG analysts forwarded 60 alerts and the audited bodies made the corrections. The AI role was detection and triage. The only sources are the deployer's own news item (TCE-MG, 27 June 2023) and Atricon's reproduction of it the next day. No audit report, tender or court decision is among the sources. TCE-MG did not build the tool (CGU/TCU did), so the statement is not vendor-authored. It is the user describing its own results.

Further evidence

Setting. Adoption of the CGU/TCU Alice system by a state court of accounts, with its own data acquisition and infrastructure. Selection of a typology of irregularities and a designed oversight process. Alerts sent to the audited bodies with a request for response. Audited bodies republishing corrected notices or adopting measures for future tenders.

Conditions. Access to state and municipal tender notices in machine-readable form. Internal acceptance of the tool by auditors (the court cites internal resistance as a challenge). Audited bodies answering alerts (53 of 60 did). Typologies kept current so that alerts remain relevant.
