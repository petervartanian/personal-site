What happened

Researchers at the University of Stirling's Institute of Aquaculture applied Observe's algorithm to feed-camera video at two commercial Atlantic salmon marine farms in Scotland. Co-authors were from Observe Technologies (Richmond, UK), with Innovate UK funding in partnership with Observe and AKVA Group. The algorithm, a patented convolutional neural network (EP3644717), ran on video in every cage, with 5 and 4 extra cameras in one cage per farm. It outputs “activity” (%), combining fish abundance, speed and shoal cohesion. Gill health, operational welfare indicators, mortality and specific feeding rate were scored weekly. Data were collected from 10 May to 10 September 2023 at Farm A and 22 June to 13 October 2023 at Farm B. Gill health issues arose at both farms in summer 2023. Mean activity in the two months before was 25.6 ± 10.5% (A) and 24.9 ± 7.0% (B).

It rose for two months after to 43.6 ± 15.1% and 32.6 ± 9.6%, with fish moving to the cage center. A mixed model found proliferative gill disease the main driver. The paper proposes behavior as a non-invasive welfare indicator but reports no alert preceding diagnosis or changed treatment. The date is the end of Farm B's data collection.

What it was built to do. Extract group behavior (abundance, speed, shoal cohesion) from feed-camera video as a non-invasive indicator of fish health and welfare.

The system's part

The vendor's neural network quantified a behavioral response that coincided with gill disease already identified by weekly scoring. The study shows correlation, not an alert that changed what happened. Vendor staff co-authored the peer-reviewed paper.

Replication

No attempt to repeat it has been recorded.

What produced it. Feed cameras in all cages plus 4–5 extra cameras in one cage per farm. Weekly gill scoring, welfare indicators, mortality and feeding-rate records. A summer 2023 gill-health episode at both farms.

What must hold again. Gill disease severe enough to alter shoaling and distribution. Camera coverage of the cage volume.
