Lynxight alerts lifeguards to an unconscious swimmer

depth 5 for a person

Independently reported, 3 sources.

A swimmer recovered fully after a medical episode in Inglewood Pool. Lynxight alerted lifeguards while another swimmer brought him to the surface.

What happened

On June 10, 2025, a man suffered a medical episode and sank in the outdoor pool at Stirling Leisure - Inglewood, Perth. Lynxight sent lifeguard Cameron Menzies a smartwatch alert with the swimmer's location and images. Another swimmer, Ofer Lefelman, independently noticed the emergency and lifted the man to the surface. Menzies and fellow lifeguards brought him out of the water. The City of Stirling reports that he was conscious when paramedics took him away and later recovered fully. ABC subsequently interviewed the city's operations coordinator, who credited the alert with accelerating the lifeguard's response.

What it was built to do. Detect swimmers in distress and alert lifeguards.

The system's part

Lynxight detected distress and alerted the lifeguard, who had not seen the man sink. Human rescuers brought him to safety. Whether he would have survived without the alert is unknown.

Replication

No replication cited.

What produced it. Camera analysis identified distress and sent a location alert. A nearby swimmer and trained lifeguards performed the rescue.

What must hold again. The system must detect distress and deliver an alert that lifeguards can act on promptly.
