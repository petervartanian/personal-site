Lynxight alerts a Perth lifeguard to a sinking swimmer, who is rescued

Lynxight, a camera system at a Perth pool, alerted a lifeguard's smartwatch to a man who had sunk unconscious; lifeguards and a swimmer rescued him, and he recovered fully.

Brief from Peter

On 10 June 2025, a man suffered a medical episode and sank in the outdoor pool at Stirling Leisure - Inglewood in Perth. Lynxight, a camera system built to detect swimmers in distress, sent lifeguard Cameron Menzies a smartwatch alert with the swimmer's location and images. Another swimmer, Ofer Lefelman, noticed the emergency on his own and lifted the man to the surface.1 Menzies and fellow lifeguards brought him out of the water. The City of Stirling reports that he was conscious when paramedics took him away and that he later recovered fully.2 Help came by two routes: The alert reached a lifeguard who had not seen the man sink, and a fellow swimmer noticed him independently.3 The sources do not show whether he would have survived without the alert.

Details

AI system

Configuration

Lynxight analyzed images from the pool’s existing cameras and sent a smartwatch alert with the swimmer’s location and camera images. [1] [2] [3]

Inputs and outputs

Inputs: Images from the pool’s existing CCTV cameras. Outputs: A smartwatch alert with the swimmer’s location and camera images.

Workflow

Camera analysis identified distress and sent a location alert. A nearby swimmer and trained lifeguards performed the rescue.

Human oversight

A lifeguard responded to the watch alert and helped complete the rescue after a bystander lifted the swimmer. [1] [3]

Benefits

Reach

One swimmer benefited in this rescue. ABC News also reports a rescue at a Sydney pool, on a date that it does not give. [1] [2] [3] [4]

Duration

The man recovered fully, according to the City of Stirling. ABC News reported on 31 December 2025 that the system was still in use at the Inglewood pool more than a year after it started. [1] [2] [3] [4]

Follow-up

The system must detect distress and deliver an alert that lifeguards can act on promptly.
