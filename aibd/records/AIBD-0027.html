Fitbit Alta HR heart-rate rise leads to vasculitis diagnosis

Fitbit Alta HR resting-heart-rate rise from 55 to 75 bpm led to early diagnosis and treatment of large vessel vasculitis (Zurich).

Workflow

The wearer tracks resting heart rate over months with a Fitbit. A systemic inflammatory illness raises resting heart rate persistently. The wearer brings the trend to a clinician who pursues imaging.

Human oversight

Not assessed

Relation to AI

The device's resting-heart-rate algorithm produced the trend the patient noticed; there was no alert, and the patient and clinicians drew the inference. The authors credit the elevated resting heart rate with an earlier diagnosis.
