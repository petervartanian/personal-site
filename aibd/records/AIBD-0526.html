What happened

A team at Addis Ababa Science and Technology University in Ethiopia is developing computer control through vision models and eye tracking for people with hand impairments. A GitHub repository created on 4 June 2025, which the team's submission cites for the project, holds a one-line description and five screenshots. Three screenshots show a webcam prototype that marks the eyes and mouth. Two show rows of the developer's own table of test cases with pass and fail results. The repository holds no code or data and reports no test with people who have hand impairments. In its project account for the International Telecommunication Union, current as of June 2025, the team states that "This use case proposes a solution for those who have disabilities to use a computer system with ease". Functional benefits for users remain intended.

Details

AI system

Model

The submission describes YOLOv5 face and eye detection, Dlib facial landmarks, and eye-aspect-ratio calculations for blink detection.

Inputs and outputs

The proposed interface uses camera frames to locate the face and eyes, tracks facial landmarks and blinks, and maps the resulting gestures to cursor movement and clicks.

Workflow

Conditions that could produce the benefit are unspecified.

Follow-up

Conditions for replication

Camera-based eye and facial tracking. Mappings from recognized gestures to interface actions.
