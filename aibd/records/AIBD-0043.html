AlphaFold models cover over 90% of the human NPC scaffold

Researchers combined AI predictions with electron-microscopy maps to model more than 90% of the human nuclear pore scaffold.

AI system

System

AlphaFold, RoseTTAfold, and ColabFold. [1]

Model

AlphaFold and RoseTTAfold. [1]

Release

The 2021 research configuration. Exact builds remain unverified. [1] [2] [3] [4]

Provider

DeepMind (AlphaFold); University of Washington and Harvard University researchers (RoseTTAFold) [3] [4]

Role

The models predicted protein structures and complexes for comparison with microscopy data. [1]

Configuration

Researchers used AlphaFold and RoseTTAfold for protein structures, ColabFold for complexes, and Assembline to fit the models to microscopy data. [1]

Inputs and outputs

Protein sequences supplied the basis for predictions of individual structures and complexes. Researchers fitted those predictions to electron-microscopy maps. [1]

Workflow

Cryo-electron tomographic maps of the human nuclear pore complex in two states. AI structure predictions for nucleoporins and subcomplexes, benchmarked against experimental structures. Integrative modeling by groups holding prior structural data on the complex.

Relation to AI

AI structure prediction generated the nucleoporin and subcomplex models that formed the input to integrative modeling. The authors state that the predictions were benchmarked against existing and new experimental structures. The resulting model was validated against earlier and new experimental data, so the AI output was independently checked rather than accepted as given.

Comparison

Results

Reference: 16 Nups and 35 MDa (46% of the scaffold) of the previous model. Result: 25 out of ~30 Nups, and account for 70 MDa of the molecular weight of the NPC (>90% of the scaffold molecular weight).

Reasons

Organizations: The collaborating laboratories obtained a model that roughly doubles structural coverage of the human NPC scaffold. That is a material advance for those groups' research programs. Society: A 70-MDa atomically resolved model covering over 90% of the scaffold is a durable public resource for nuclear transport research. The source reports no direct patient or population benefit.
