What happened

VIT University in India is building a prototype that uses AI to optimize the space in floor plans for low-income housing. The International Telecommunication Union account, current as of June 2025, lists two student authors and a faculty mentor, Dr. Geetha S. The team describes current practice in these terms: "Traditional space planning is manual, static, and lacks customization based on user needs." The account states that the prototype was trained and evaluated on synthetic floor plans and that pilots with nongovernmental organizations in Tamil Nadu and with housing development boards were planned. Improved living space and construction outcomes remain prospective. The 2026 interim edition of the compendium does not list the project. No paper or pilot report from the team appears as of 29 October 2026.

Details

AI system

Model

The prototype combines CNN layout analysis, PPO or DQN reinforcement learning, and OR-Tools constraints that reject overlapping furniture or blocked paths.

Inputs and outputs

The prototype tests housing-layout optimization using synthetic and reference floor plans.
