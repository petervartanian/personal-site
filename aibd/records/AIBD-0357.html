What happened

Citizen Digital reported on 21 September 2024 that the Kenya Wildlife Service (KWS) had installed FLIR thermal cameras at three black rhino sites. The US Department of State Bureau of International Narcotics and Law Enforcement Affairs and WWF supported it. They are Solio Game Reserve, Lake Nakuru National Park and Ol Pejeta Conservancy, with Solio the latest and Meru National Park named next. Geoffrey Chege (WWF-Kenya) said the cameras “have inbuilt analytics that send out automated alerts every time they detect an intrusion from humans, vehicles or wildlife”. Colby Loucks of WWF-US said the cameras “can tell you when a human is in the picture or an animal” and alert rangers. KWS Director General Dr Erustus Kanga said: “I am aware that over the last six years, we have not lost a single black rhino in this sanctuary”. That predates the Solio installation.

The article reports no arrest, interception or prevented poaching event attributable to the cameras. The date used is the article's publication date.

What it was built to do. Automated night-time detection of human and vehicle intrusion at rhino sanctuaries to alert rangers. The claimed benefit is the intended one.

The system's part

The sole source read is one news report (Citizen Digital, 21 September 2024). It describes AI analytics that classify humans, vehicles and animals in thermal video and alert rangers automatically. The statements come from WWF and KWS officials at the handover ceremony. No alert, arrest or prevented poaching event is attributed to the system in any source on file. So the AI's contribution to rhino protection is unassessed in outcome terms.

Replication

What produced it. Thermal cameras with on-board human/vehicle/animal analytics at sanctuary perimeters. Ranger teams receiving automated alerts. Donor funding (US State Department INL via WWF) for installation and upgrades.

What must hold again. Camera coverage of intrusion routes. Ranger response capacity at night. Maintenance and power at remote sites. Evaluation that records alerts and responses, absent so far.
