Kota police act on a Meta self-harm flag within a week of tie-up

depth unassessed PTI reports only that police “prevented a possible suicide by a student hailing from the Jhunjhunu district” within a week of the launch. The sources give no account of what the student posted, their condition when reached or what officers did. So the outcome is not established.

What happened

PTI reported from Kota on 28 May 2024 that city police under Superintendent Amrita Duhan had tied up with Meta. It aims to identify students exhibiting suicidal tendencies on Facebook and Instagram. When a student posts suicide or self-harm content, a red-flag tag appears on Meta's system and account information is shared with Kota police. For students outside Kota it goes to Abhay Command Centre or the district control room. There a dedicated team works round the clock in eight-hour shifts. Within a week of the launch, police said they had prevented a possible suicide by a student from Jhunjhunu district. No red-flag tags had yet been reported in the city. PTI counted nine coaching-student suicides in 2024 to date and 26 in 2023. Duhan planned to extend the arrangement to Jaipur (PTI via LatestLY).

Meta's newsroom describes machine-learning detection with human review before authorities are contacted (Card, 2018). The intervention date is not given.

What it was built to do. Meta's pipeline is built to find posts indicating suicide or self-injury risk and route them to trained reviewers. In imminent cases it contacts local authorities (Meta Newsroom). The Kota arrangement routes those flags to police to intervene. This case is that purpose.

The system's part

PTI's report says the chain starts with a red-flag tag on Meta's system when a student posts suicide or self-harm content. Account information is then shared with police. It does not say whether Meta's classifier or a user report raised the flag in the Jhunjhunu case, nor what happened on arrival. Meta's own description (2018, updated 2021) says machine-learning detection is followed by human review before authorities are contacted. The intervention itself was police work. PTI also notes that no red-flag tags had been reported in Kota city since the launch.

Replication

No replication cited.

What produced it. Meta's detection-and-review pipeline flagging suicide or self-harm posts by students on Facebook and Instagram. A standing arrangement under which Meta shares account information with Kota police. For students outside Kota it goes to Abhay Command Centre or a district control room. A police team monitoring the flags round the clock in eight-hour shifts.

What must hold again. The student must post on Facebook or Instagram in a way the pipeline recognizes. Account details must resolve to a reachable location in time. Police must be able to reach the student quickly.
