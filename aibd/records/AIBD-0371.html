Kota police act on a Meta self-harm flag within a week of tie-up

Kota police receive Meta's red-flag tags for suicide and self-harm posts on Facebook and Instagram; within a week, police said that they had prevented a possible suicide by a Jhunjhunu student.

  • Not rated a person

    PTI reports only that police “prevented a possible suicide by a student hailing from the Jhunjhunu district” within a week of the launch. The sources give no account of what the student posted, their condition when reached or what officers did. So the outcome is not established.

The system’s part
PTI's report says the chain starts with a red-flag tag on Meta's system when a student posts suicide or self-harm content. Account information is then shared with police. It does not say whether Meta's classifier or a user report raised the flag in the Jhunjhunu case, nor what happened on arrival. Meta's own description (2018, updated 2021) says machine-learning detection is followed by human review before authorities are contacted. The intervention itself was police work. PTI also notes that no red-flag tags had been reported in Kota city since the launch.
Limits
The individual flag could have originated from user reporting rather than automated detection. The police account gives no intervention details, imminent-risk assessment, or later outcome. Meta’s generic workflow cannot establish which mechanism acted in Jhunjhunu.

What happened

Meta's newsroom describes machine-learning detection of suicide and self-injury posts, with human review before authorities are contacted (Card, 2018). PTI reported from Kota on 28 May 2024 that city police under Superintendent Amrita Duhan had tied up with Meta. The tie-up aims to identify students exhibiting suicidal tendencies on Facebook and Instagram. When a student posts suicide or self-harm content, a red-flag tag appears on Meta's system and account information is shared with Kota police. For students outside Kota it goes to Abhay Command Centre or the district control room. There a dedicated team works round the clock in eight-hour shifts. PTI's report places the start of the tie-up about a week before 28 May 2024. Within a week of the launch, police said that they had prevented a possible suicide by a student from Jhunjhunu district. The intervention therefore took place in May 2024, on a date that PTI does not give. No red-flag tags had yet been reported in the city. PTI counted nine suicides among coaching students in 2024 to date and 26 in 2023. Duhan planned to extend the arrangement to Jaipur (PTI via LatestLY). No later source reports on the Kota arrangement or its planned extension to Jaipur. The same chain of a Meta alert routed to police has been reported in Uttar Pradesh, in Raebareli on 29 March 2026 and in Meerut on 7 June 2026.

Details

AI system

Inputs and outputs

A flag on Facebook or Instagram led to account information being passed to a police control center for location tracing and intervention.

Workflow

Meta's detection-and-review pipeline flagging suicide or self-harm posts by students on Facebook and Instagram. A standing arrangement under which Meta shares account information with Kota police. For students outside Kota it goes to Abhay Command Centre or a district control room. A police team monitoring the flags round the clock in eight-hour shifts.
