What happened

On 14 November 2024 GCC Business News relayed a statement by the UAE Ministry of Finance that named its AI platform, called Tax Information Exchange Projects Inquiries. The platform answers questions on the Foreign Account Tax Compliance Act (FATCA), the Common Reporting Standard (CRS) and related reporting rules. In a project account current as of June 2025, the ministry reports that its website includes an AI conversational assistant that answers FATCA and CRS inquiries and provides real-time guidance. The main reporting platform uses rules, without model training. The counts of reporting institutions and international exchange partners in that account describe this wider infrastructure. No source measures the effect of the assistant on how inquiries are resolved, on the time of staff or on the experience of users. In October 2026 the FATCA and CRS page of the ministry still offers the assistant and warns that its answers are general guidance only.

Details

AI system

Workflow

A website conversational AI assistant provides FATCA/CRS guidance.

Human oversight

The ministry's web page on automatic exchange of tax information warns that the answers of the assistant are general guidance only and should not be taken as final or definitive advice. No source describes staff checking the answers. [1] [2] [3]

AI contribution

The AI contribution is the website inquiry assistant. The FATCA/CRS reporting platform itself uses rules and does not train a model.
