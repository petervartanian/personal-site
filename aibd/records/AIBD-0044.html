AlphaFold-Multimer predicts Tmem81 as a fertilization factor

depth 2 for an organization, depth 2 for society

Claimed by the parties, 1 source.

An AlphaFold-Multimer screen predicted a trimeric sperm complex; the lab then showed Tmem81 is essential for male fertility in zebrafish and mice.

What happened

In a paper published in Cell on 17 October 2024, the Pauli laboratory and collaborators reported that an AlphaFold-Multimer screen identified Tmem81 as part of a conserved trimeric sperm complex with the known essential fertilization factors Izumo1 and Spaca6. The prediction was then tested at the bench: the authors demonstrated that Tmem81 is essential for male fertility in zebrafish and mice, showed that Izumo1, Spaca6 and Tmem81 interact in zebrafish sperm and that the human orthologues interact in vitro, and found that complex formation creates the binding site for the egg factor Bouncer in zebrafish. The screen and the validation were performed independently of AlphaFold's developer.

What it was built to do. Predict protein-protein complexes in order to find missing components of the vertebrate sperm-egg interaction machinery.

The system's part

AlphaFold-Multimer generated the hypothesis that Tmem81 belongs to a trimeric complex with Izumo1 and Spaca6. The functional claim rests on wet-lab work by the same laboratory: knockouts in zebrafish and mice, interaction assays in zebrafish sperm, and in vitro interaction of the human orthologues.

Replication

No attempt to repeat it has been recorded.

What produced it. An AlphaFold-Multimer screen of candidate sperm protein complexes. Knockout models in zebrafish and mice to test the prediction. Biochemical interaction assays, including the human orthologues.

What must hold again. Candidate complexes predicted from vertebrate sperm protein sequences. Zebrafish and mouse knockout models available. Biochemical interaction assays for orthologue validation.
