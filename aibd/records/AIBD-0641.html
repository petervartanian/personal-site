A phone camera coach improved fitness and blood pressure in hypertension

In a 62-person Guangzhou trial, 8 weeks of AI pose-guided home exercise added 62.77 m to 6-minute walk distance and lowered systolic pressure more than usual care.

What happened

Researchers at Zhujiang Hospital of Southern Medical University, Guangdong Provincial People's Hospital and the Hong Kong Polytechnic University tested home exercise guided by an AI app for adults with hypertension. They recruited 62 patients in Guangzhou from November 2023 to October 2024 and randomized them to the app or to conventional offline exercise guidance, with health education in both groups. The app, from RaphAI Health Technologies Limited, used OpenPose pose estimation on the phone camera to track 17 body points and give corrective feedback during sessions. Patients trained at least three times a week for eight weeks. The app group gained 62.77 meters more in the 6-minute walk test than the control group. Its systolic blood pressure fell by 4.11 mmHg more, and diastolic pressure did not differ. No serious adverse events related to the program occurred. The trial was small and open-label, and it used convenience sampling.

Sources

    Yao Q, Qiu B, He L, et al. Effects of Artificial Intelligence Recognition-Based Telerehabilitation on Exercise Capacity in Patients With Hypertension: Randomized Controlled Trial. Journal of Medical Internet Research 2026;28:e81400.

    This study demonstrated that AI-assisted training based on 3D skeletal pose recognition significantly improved exercise capacity and BP and increased exercise self-efficacy in patients with hypertension, superior to usual care.

Details

AI system

Workflow

The app pushed an exercise prescription with warm-up, endurance, resistance, balance and flexibility work. The phone camera tracked 17 body points and gave real-time correction. Patients trained 30 to 50 minutes at least three times a week for eight weeks.

Measurement

Measurement

6-minute walk test, cardiopulmonary exercise testing, blood pressure and questionnaires at baseline and 8 weeks. [1]

Setting and sample

62 adults aged 18 to 75 with hypertension, Guangzhou. [1]

Measures

Change in 6-minute walk distance; systolic blood pressure. [1]

Results

6MWD +62.77 m (95% CI 26.33-99.22); systolic BP 4.11 mmHg greater reduction (95% CI 0.11-8.28). [1]

Additional value from AI

Isolated against conventional guidance. [1]

Limitations

Small, open-label, convenience sample, eight weeks. [1]

Denominators

At least three sessions a week for eight weeks. [1]

Materials for replication

CONSORT-eHEALTH checklist and supplementary tables. [1]

Follow-up

Conditions for replication

Patients have stable hypertension and are cleared for exercise. Patients have a smartphone and keep training at home.
