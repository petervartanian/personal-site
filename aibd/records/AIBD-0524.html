What happened

On 30 August 2024 the project team published a Hugging Face model card for whisper-small-Kurdish-Sorani-10, a Kurdish speech recognition model. The card reports a word error rate on an evaluation set that it does not describe, and it reports no use with humanitarian interviews. In a project account for the International Telecommunication Union, current as of June 2025, the team at the United Nations High Commissioner for Refugees (UNHCR) described work to fine-tune Kurdish speech recognition for humanitarian interviews and to integrate it into KoboToolbox. The team wrote that "Using this tool, colleagues can have more time to work on analyzing the data instead of spending time on transcription." Faster processing and more inclusive decisions remain intended benefits. A KoboToolbox help page, last updated on 6 October 2026, states that automatic transcription in KoboToolbox comes from Google Cloud. The page does not mention a UNHCR Kurdish model.

Details

AI system

Model

The project references Kurdish adaptations of Whisper small and w2v-BERT 2.0 as speech-recognition starting points. A completed humanitarian deployment is not established.

Inputs and outputs

The developing Kurdish speech-recognition system transcribes humanitarian interviews and is intended to integrate with KoboToolbox.
