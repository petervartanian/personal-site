Short account

Partially assessed

Summary
Milton Keynes reports faster validation and decisions during its Valon pilot, with receipt-to-validation time falling from 15.8 to 7.6 days.
Account
Milton Keynes piloted Valon for planning work from October to December 2025. The council reports that officers validated applications 60–80% faster. Its analysis of the planning register put average receipt-to-validation time at 15.8 days before and 7.6 days during the pilot, and validation-to-decision time at 53.1 and 43.2 days. The estimated 1,360 officer hours per year is an extrapolation from an assumed 80% saving. Valon’s own account describes major-application checks falling from about 65 to 10 minutes and non-major checks from 15 to 5 minutes. It reports 7.7 days for early-November validation turnaround and a separate 18% decision-time difference against a non-pilot group. The two accounts do not publish the underlying timesheets, selected application records, sample sizes, or rules for constructing the comparisons. These are reported efficiency gains, with uncertainty about how much AI caused. The council purchased a one-year license after the pilot and reports continued use.
