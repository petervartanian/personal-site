ContaCT alerts came sooner than usual care in a test on past scans

In a retrospective test that FDA reviewed, Viz.ai ContaCT sent its stroke alert in 7.32 minutes on average, against 58.72 minutes for usual-care notification of the specialist.

What happened

Viz.ai of San Francisco submitted its ContaCT software to FDA as a tool that reads CT angiograms of the brain and alerts a neurovascular specialist when it suspects a large vessel occlusion. In a retrospective standalone test on 300 CT angiograms from two US clinical sites, the software had a sensitivity of 87.8% and a specificity of 89.6%. For 44 cases with a true occlusion and a recorded usual-care time, the study compared the time from scan to specialist notification. The mean was 58.72 minutes in usual care and 7.32 minutes for the software, a mean difference of 51.40 minutes. FDA granted the De Novo request on 13 February 2018. The reviewers note that the time comparison could only use the subset of cases with usual-care times and may hold some bias. The test used past scans, so the alerts did not change care, and the review did not measure patient outcomes.
