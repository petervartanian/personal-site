GraphCast matches best operational cyclone track models in 2023

CIRA/Colorado State evaluation: GraphCast and three AI models match top operational cyclone track models in 2023; adding all four cut consensus error 11% at 120 h.

ⓘ

What happened

Google DeepMind's GraphCast was run live on ECMWF's public charts from 2023. DeepMind's blog of 14 November 2023 says the live model predicted Hurricane Lee's landfall in Nova Scotia in September 2023 about nine days in advance. The blog says only “In September”, so the year is implied by its 14 November 2023 post date. The ECMWF publications read do not document the case. MIT Technology Review quoted ECMWF's Peter Dueben saying the models are so good they cannot be avoided. An independent CIRA/Colorado State evaluation applied NHC verification to May-November 2023 cyclones. It found GraphCast-operational and three other AI models' track errors comparable to the best operational models. Adding all four improved the NHC track consensus by 11% at 120 h.

Purpose. Medium-range global weather forecasting by machine learning, including severe-event prediction such as tropical cyclone tracks.

AI’s contribution

The CIRA preprint isolates the AI models' contribution via NHC verification and consensus experiments. The nine-day Hurricane Lee landfall forecast rests on vendor statements and press quotes of ECMWF staff. ECMWF documents read (Newsletters 177 and 178, science blog, Severe Event Catalog) do not record it.

Basis
Measured.The consensus track errors are reduced by up to 11% at the longer time periods. The five-day NHC official track forecasts have improved by about 2% per year since 2001, so this represents more than a five-year gain in accuracy. source
Since then
By the 2025 season NHC had brought AI-based models, including a newer Google DeepMind ensemble, into real-time operations, per its verification report.The 2025 season marked the first time that NHC incorporated AI-based models into real-time operations. source

Further evidence

Setting. AI models initialized from operational NWP analyses and run alongside physics-based models. Verification using the NHC procedure, including early-model conversion and consensus experiments.

Conditions. Track skill does not extend to intensity: the CIRA preprint reports a substantial low intensity bias. Independent case evaluation (Storm Ciaran) found peak winds underestimated by all AI models tested.
