University says AI lip-reading practice helped 343 deaf people speak

Huazhong University of Science and Technology reports that 343 deaf users regained speech capabilities through practice with its unnamed AI lip-reading system.

What happened

Huazhong University of Science and Technology developed an AI system that locates points on a learner's lips, compares the lip shapes with standard shapes, scores them and gives feedback. Xinhua photographed a deaf child using the system at the Hubei Provincial Rehabilitation Center for Deaf Children on 19 September 2024. It reported that the university's Jinchanzi volunteer team had served more than 300 hearing-impaired people since 2021, and it did not report restored speech. In its account for the International Telecommunication Union (ITU), with information current as of June 2025, the university reports that 343 deaf users regained speech capabilities with AI-guided lip-reading practice and claims threefold learning efficiency. The ITU account provides no independent confirmation of those aggregate results or of how long the restored speech lasted. Hubei Daily reported on 10 July 2025 that team volunteers recall two deaf brothers in Zhaotong, Yunnan, who said their first words after volunteers spent an afternoon teaching them with the system. No source separates the system's share of any speech gains from the volunteers' teaching.

Details

AI system

Role

The system gives AI-guided feedback during lip-reading and speech-development practice in schools and rehabilitation centers.

Configuration

The submission describes cloud inference through client applications, with free access through schools and rehabilitation centers. Local processing is a planned extension.

Workflow

Conditions that could produce the benefit are unspecified.

Human oversight

Xinhua reports that volunteers explain and practice alongside learners. Hubei Daily reports that volunteers spent an afternoon teaching two deaf brothers with the system. [1] [2] [3]

Benefits

Reach

The university reports that 343 deaf users regained speech capabilities. Xinhua reports that the volunteer team had served more than 300 hearing-impaired people since 2021. No source confirms the 343 figure independently. [1] [2] [3]

Follow-up

Conditions for replication

Client applications with video input and cloud inference. Practice materials and corrective feedback during articulation training.
