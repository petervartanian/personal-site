What happened

Kyunghyang Shinmun's 최미랑 reported on 11 Feb 2026 that the Ministry of Land, Infrastructure and Transport had drawn up a comprehensive railway-crossing accident-prevention plan. It was to take effect from March 2026. It includes an AI-based intelligent closed-circuit television (CCTV) system, to be installed in railway protection zones nationwide. The system immediately detects a vehicle or pedestrian trapped inside a crossing. It then transmits the information to the driver of an approaching train. Pilot installations were planned within the first quarter at Nonsan in Chungnam and at Boseong, where accidents occurred in 2025. Installation at the 543 national crossings would follow in sequence, depending on the results. The plan also adds fines of up to 70,000 won, after a six-month grace period. They apply to vehicles that fail to stop or that enter while barriers operate.

The ministry cited 36 crossing accidents over five years with 14 deaths and 7 injuries, 27 of them due to driver carelessness. No developer is named and no results exist. The OECD AI Incidents Monitor classified the announcement as an AI hazard rather than an incident.

What it was built to do. The AI CCTV is intended to detect vehicles or pedestrians trapped in crossings and warn approaching train drivers in time to stop.
