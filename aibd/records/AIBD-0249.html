MOLIT announces AI crossing CCTV pilots in Nonsan and Boseong

Kyunghyang Shinmun (11 Feb 2026): MOLIT's crossing-accident plan from March 2026 includes AI intelligent CCTV piloted in Q1 at two crossings where 2025 accidents occurred; 36 crossing accidents in ...

What happened

Kyunghyang Shinmun's 최미랑 reported on 11 Feb 2026 that the Ministry of Land, Infrastructure and Transport had drawn up a comprehensive railway-crossing accident-prevention plan to take effect from March 2026. It includes an AI-based intelligent CCTV system, to be installed in railway protection zones nationwide, that immediately detects a vehicle or pedestrian trapped inside a crossing and transmits the information to the driver of an approaching train; pilot installations were planned within the first quarter at Nonsan in Chungnam and at Boseong, where accidents occurred in 2025, with sequential installation at the 543 national crossings depending on the results. The plan also adds fines of up to 70,000 won, after a six-month grace period, for vehicles that fail to stop or enter while barriers operate. The ministry cited 36 crossing accidents over five years with 14 deaths and 7 injuries, 27 of them due to driver carelessness. No developer is named and no results exist; the OECD AI Incidents Monitor classified the announcement (2026-02-11-4404) as an AI hazard rather than an incident.

What it was built to do. The AI CCTV is intended to detect vehicles or pedestrians trapped in crossings and warn approaching train drivers in time to stop.
