What happened

AInnovation completed a minimum viable product of ChatCAD integrated with Bentley software. On 26 August 2025, AInnovation and Bentley Systems released iPID as a commercial product at a Bentley summit in Beijing. iPID converts piping and instrument diagrams stored as images into interactive digital diagrams, and AInnovation describes it as an application of ChatCAD's Image-to-CAD function. AInnovation gives a preliminary estimate that iPID raises design efficiency more than tenfold. It reports no measured result from users. Faster design and shorter product-development cycles therefore remain intended benefits.
