Citable summary

HSE reports an operational pipeline that extracted a 38,000-event natural-hazard database from eight million Russian-language articles. The counts of processed articles and event entries do not establish extraction precision, event recall, correct geocoding, or deduplication. Media coverage biases may shape the resource. No emergency decision or avoided loss is attributed to its use. The account does not isolate AI’s contribution from the surrounding workflow.
