HSE University reports that a language-model pipeline turned eight million Russian-language articles into a database of 38,000 natural-hazard events.

What happened

HSE University uses a language model to identify, structure, and geocode natural-hazard reports. The team registered the database with Rospatent in 2024. In its submission of June 2025, the team reports that the first phase processed eight million articles and produced 38,000 event entries covering Russia in 2018–2024. The team's own page reports that the language model filled the database fields with an average accuracy of 0.65 to 0.97. Specialists checked the records against other public hazard databases, and the user version holds 11.7 thousand messages on 8.3 thousand events. The page notes that the database maps where people write about hazards more than where hazards occur. The database exists as a research resource. Transfer to additional users and any resulting emergency-management benefits remain prospective.
