Nara proposes AI voice control for an accessible metaverse

depth unassessed

Claimed by the parties, 3 sources.

Nara's patent specifies AI voice control for an accessible metaverse. The Blind Metaverse demo uses speech synthesis; deployment of the patented speech recognition is unverified.

What happened

Nara is developing audio and tactile interfaces to help blind users navigate virtual spaces. A Nara patent describes deep-learning speech recognition that interprets spoken input from a Braille terminal and controls the computing device. Its connection to Blind Metaverse is inferred from the assignee, inventor, purpose, and interface design. The patent does not name the product. The public demo integrates Google Cloud speech synthesis, although the exact model behind its Korean Standard voice is unspecified. Nara reports exhibition use of the accessibility platform. The sources do not confirm deployment of the patented speech-recognition function or measure its effect on users.

What it was built to do. Use AI speech recognition to provide blind users with voice control of an accessible virtual environment.
