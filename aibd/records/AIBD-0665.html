Consultants with GPT-4 finish more tasks in less time

In a preregistered experiment with 758 BCG consultants, GPT-4 users completed 12.2% more tasks 25.1% faster with higher quality, but erred more on a task beyond its reach.

What happened

Researchers from Harvard Business School, Wharton, Warwick Business School, MIT Sloan and the Boston Consulting Group Henderson Institute ran a preregistered experiment with 758 BCG consultants, about 7% of the firm's individual contributor-level consultants. After a baseline task, the consultants were randomly assigned to work without AI, with GPT-4, or with GPT-4 and an overview of prompt engineering; all used GPT-4 as available at the end of April 2023 through a company platform built on the OpenAI API. On 18 realistic consulting tasks that the authors judged within the reach of the model, the consultants using AI completed 12.2% more tasks, finished 25.1% more quickly and produced work that human graders rated more than 40% higher in quality. Consultants below the average baseline score improved by 43% and those above it by 17%. On a business problem chosen to lie outside the model's reach, consultants using AI were 19 percentage points less likely to reach the correct answer. Harvard Business School released the working paper on 22 September 2023, and Organization Science published the study in March 2026. The tasks were designed for the experiment. Three of the nine authors worked for BCG, another advises BCG, and Harvard Business School paid for part of the research.

Details

AI system

Reported configuration

GPT-4 as available at the end of April 2023

Workflow

Consultants used GPT-4 on tasks that experts had chosen as within its reach. Some consultants also received a short overview of prompt engineering. Human graders scored the quality of the work.

Measurement

Measurement

Subtasks completed, time to completion and graded quality of outputs. [2]

Setting and sample

758 BCG consultants, two experiments, 18 tasks inside the frontier and one outside. [2]

Measures

Tasks completed, speed and quality score. [2]

Results

12.2% more tasks, 25.1% faster, more than 40% higher quality inside the frontier; 19 points lower correctness outside it. [2]

Additional value from AI

Gains of 43% for below-average and 17% for above-average consultants. [2]

Limitations

Designed tasks; one firm; one model version; BCG co-authors; lower correctness on the task outside the frontier. [2]

Denominators

GPT-4 access during the experimental task, with or without a prompt overview. [2]

Materials for replication

Preregistered on OSF; data not public. [2]

Follow-up

Conditions for replication

The task must lie within what the model can do well. Workers must check output on tasks where the model is weak.
