Relation to AI

The notification feature's deep-learning model, described in Apple's submission to FDA, flagged elevated breathing disturbance and brought the patient to the clinic. His own morning ECG recordings, read by the authors, showed the AF paroxysms. The paper does not state separately what the ECG app's classifier reported. Clinicians chose CPAP and deferred ablation. The treating authors report the clinical episode. Apple supplies the technical description and validation in its 510(k) summary. FDA's clearance is a separate regulatory decision and does not verify this patient's benefit. [1] [2]
