What happened

Jason Sheridan, a 31-year-old office worker from Liverpool living in Vauxhall, South London, noticed a new 1cm mole on his left arm in March 2018. He did not see a doctor. Twelve months later it turned pink and he checked it with SkinVision, an AI app that grades photographed moles low, medium or high risk. It returned a high-risk rating and urged him to see a doctor. His GP referred him to a dermatologist and two weeks later the mole was removed in a 40-minute operation. The biopsy showed stage 1A melanoma, a further biopsy found no spread, and he is cancer-free. The Daily Mail reported the case on 17 January 2020 without dates for the app check or surgery.

What it was built to do. SkinVision is built to grade a phone photograph of a mole low, medium or high risk. It tells users with a high-risk result to see a doctor, with dermatologist advice sent within 48 hours. This benefit is that intended use.

The system's part

The app's classifier graded a phone photograph of the mole high risk and told him to see a doctor at once. By his account that prompt, not the color change, is what sent him to his GP. He said he only goes to the doctor when absolutely desperate. The referral, excision, biopsy and staging were done by the GP, the dermatologist and pathology, none of whom the Daily Mail quotes. The article does not report the app's score, its version, or whether the 48-hour dermatologist advice it mentions was used.

Replication

No replication cited.

What produced it. A smartphone photograph of a changing mole submitted to SkinVision's risk classifier. A high-risk rating with an instruction to see a doctor, acted on by the user. A GP referral and an excision with pathology that confirms and stages the lesion.

What must hold again. The lesion is one the classifier can score from a phone photograph. The article reports no score or app version. The user follows the app's advice rather than treating the rating as noise. Clinical follow-up is available quickly enough for early-stage removal. Here the operation came two weeks after referral.
