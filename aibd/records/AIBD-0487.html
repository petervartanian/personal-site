CreditConnect-EV helps finance over 20,000 electric two-wheelers

AIZEN reports that CreditConnect-EV has helped partner lenders finance more than 20,000 electric two-wheelers in Indonesia since 2022.

  • 2 a person

    AIZEN reports that partner lenders financed more than 20,000 electric two-wheelers, which gave borrowers access to a vehicle and to credit. No source establishes higher income or lasting financial security for borrowers.

  • 2 an organization

    AIZEN reports that CreditConnect-EV lets partner lenders approve loans for electric two-wheelers at scale, which traditional scorecards made infeasible. No source reports the profitability of these loans or a fall in defaults.

  • Not rated society

    AIZEN intends to lower pollution from transport. No source measures the emissions that the financed vehicles avoided.

What happened

AIZEN reports that CreditConnect-EV has been live in Indonesia since 2022. On 25 November 2022 AIZEN Global announced an agreement with NOA and SWAP, two Indonesian firms that run shared electric bikes. The company said that CreditConnect was already in use in Indonesia and Vietnam. In November 2023 AIZEN announced a partnership with Grab to finance electric motorcycles. In an account current as of June 2025, AIZEN reports that CreditConnect-EV has financed more than 20,000 electric two-wheelers in Indonesia. No source measures the effect on the income of borrowers or on net emissions. On 24 July 2025 the Korea Economic Daily reported that JB Financial Group had signed an agreement for a 40 percent stake in the Indonesian unit of AIZEN. The newspaper described AIZEN as a provider of rental and credit services for Grab and Gojek. JB Financial Group announced that JB Woori Capital planned to fund the purchase of motorcycles by AIZEN and loans to drivers.

Details

AI system

Inputs and outputs

The financing models combine battery and vehicle telemetry with rider behavior and financial data to estimate credit risk and vehicle residual value.

Workflow

Conditions that could produce the benefit are unspecified.

Human oversight

AIZEN reports that CreditConnect-EV enables partner lenders to approve or price loans. No source describes how lenders review the scores or handle disputed decisions. [1] [2] [3] [4]

Benefits

Beneficiaries

The program’s direct recipients are borrowers obtaining financed electric two-wheelers in Indonesia; the reported output exceeds 20,000 financed vehicles.

Reach

AIZEN reports that partner lenders financed more than 20,000 electric two-wheelers in Indonesia, which counts vehicles. No source gives the number of borrowers or shows that any of them gained income. [1] [2] [3] [4]

Duration

AIZEN reports that CreditConnect-EV has run in Indonesia since 2022 and had financed more than 20,000 electric two-wheelers by June 2025. On 24 July 2025 JB Financial Group announced an agreement for a 40 percent stake in the Indonesian unit of AIZEN. The group also announced that JB Woori Capital planned to fund loans to drivers.
