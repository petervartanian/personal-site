WeChat Pay reminder stops a 190,000 yuan romance-scam transfer

WeChat says its anti-fraud reminder, with Tencent Cloud online service, led a Yunnan man to cancel a 190,000 yuan transfer to a “goddess” he had met online; reported by ChinaZ, 14 December 2021.

ⓘ

What happened

According to WeChat (ChinaZ, 14 December 2021; ZOL, 16 December 2021), WeChat Pay launched an “anti-fraud customer service reminder”. When a payment is flagged as a risky transaction, the screen shows an “answer the reminder” button and the system connects automatically. Tencent Cloud Zhifu then provides online customer service to help judge the risk. ChinaZ reports that a Mr. Zhang in Yunnan had met a woman on a video platform. Within a week she asked to borrow 190,000 yuan for medical treatment. As he initiated the transfer, a WeChat Pay voice reminder appeared. The customer service, judging from experience that this looked like a dating scam, explained the pattern and advised him to cancel. He avoided a loss of 190,000 yuan. The Monitor bundles this with Shanghai police figures for 2021 (115,000 scam calls blocked daily; 510 million yuan directly recovered).

Xinmin attributes those to police, the communications administration and carriers without naming AI.

Purpose. The reminder was built to warn WeChat Pay users during risky transactions and connect them to online customer service that helps judge the risk. Stopping a transfer to a suspected scammer was that purpose.

AI’s contribution

The automated part the sources describe is the flagging of a risky transaction. That triggers the on-screen reminder and connects the payer to Tencent Cloud Zhifu, a service the Monitor calls AI. ChinaZ says the WeChat Pay customer service judged “from experience” that this was a dating scam, which reads as a human agent. Neither report says how the transaction was flagged or whether any model was involved. The advice, not the flag alone, led Zhang to cancel.

Beneficiaries

  • a person

    A 190,000-yuan transfer to a suspected romance scammer was canceled on the reminder, a serious financial loss for one person averted on WeChat's account. The man is identified only by surname and province, no police or bank confirmation is reported, and the case date is not given.

Further evidence

Setting. A WeChat Pay transfer flagged as a risky transaction. This shows an “answer the reminder” button and connects the payer to Tencent Cloud Zhifu online customer service. A customer-service exchange in which the agent asks about the transaction context and recognizes a known scam pattern.

Conditions. The flag fires before the transfer completes. The payer answers the reminder and accepts the advice to cancel. The scam matches a pattern the service recognizes; here a week-old online acquaintance asking for medical money.
