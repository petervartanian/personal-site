ML poverty maps chose 1,163 poorest urban wards for Nigeria's COVID-19 cash register; 49,000 beneficiary households as of 15 Oct 2021

What happened

Lacking a social registry for urban areas, Nigeria's National Social Safety Nets Coordinating Office built the Rapid Response Register for a government-funded COVID-19 cash transfer. Ward-level poverty maps, produced by applying machine-learning algorithms to satellite imagery with household-survey labels (work by Berkeley and Columbia researchers with World Bank economists), selected 1,163 of the poorest urban wards, population over 20 million. Residents self-registered by USSD after SMS blasts (first wave June 2021), followed by in-person verification. Human Rights Watch records that the program began in January 2021 and the PNAS authors state disbursement began mid-January 2021 (day not given; first day of month used). World Bank monitoring reports 49,000 beneficiary households of the government's COVID-CT as of 15 October 2021 and over 1.75 million registrations by November 2021, against a government target of one million households.

What it was built to do. Geographically target a COVID-19 urban cash transfer to the poorest wards where no social registry existed.

The system's part

Decisive at the geographic stage: the ML poverty map alone chose which 1,163 wards were eligible. Individual inclusion depended on USSD self-registration and in-person verification, not AI. Payment and registration counts come from World Bank operational documents, not from the map developers; the PNAS paper and World Bank blog are co-authored by the developers.

Replication

What produced it. Deep-learning poverty map trained on geo-located household surveys. Government decision to adopt the ward ranking for eligibility. SMS blasts and USSD self-registration. In-person verification, de-duplication and payment onboarding.

What must hold again. Ground-truth survey data for training labels. Ward boundaries compatible with map resolution. Mobile-phone access in target wards. Government funding for transfers and identity verification capacity.
