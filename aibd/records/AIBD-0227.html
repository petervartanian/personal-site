ZeroEyes alert sends Hobbs police to clay shooters, one charged

charges were filed against one

On 30 Nov 2023 ZeroEyes alerted Hobbs police to a gun on a camera at Del Norte Pool's lot; officers detained people shooting clays near a street and charged one, per the police chief.

What happened

The Odessa American (reproduced by GovTech) reports, from a ZeroEyes news release, that at 6:35 p.m. MDT on 30 November 2023 ZeroEyes alerted the Hobbs Police Department and the Eagle I.C. dispatch center to a gun detected on a security camera in the parking lot of Del Norte Pool; the people involved were shooting sporting clays close to North Grimes Street, responding officers detained them and charges were filed against one. Hobbs Police Chief August Fons is quoted saying the detection and rapid human verification helped stop the activity before anyone was hurt. Lubbock Lights later reported ZeroEyes co-founder Sam Alaimo showing images from the incident and saying it resulted in an arrest with no one hurt, and listing the City of Hobbs and its school system among local clients. No court record was fetched.

What it was built to do. Detect visible firearms in existing security-camera feeds, verify with human analysts and alert police within seconds to reduce response times to potential shootings.

The system's part

The chain (AI detection, human verification, dispatch) is described only in ZeroEyes' release and the vendor's later interview; the chief's endorsement is quoted within that release. No police report, court filing or independent reporter observation was fetched, so the AI's necessity (whether anyone else reported the shooting) is undocumented.
