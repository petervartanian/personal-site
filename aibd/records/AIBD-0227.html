What happened

The Odessa American (reproduced by GovTech) reports, from a ZeroEyes news release, on an alert at 6:35 p.m. MDT on 30 November 2023. ZeroEyes alerted the Hobbs Police Department and the Eagle I.C. dispatch center. A gun had been detected on a security camera in Del Norte Pool's parking lot. Those involved were shooting sporting clays close to North Grimes Street. Responding officers detained them and charges were filed against one. Hobbs Police Chief August Fons is quoted as saying that detection and rapid human verification helped stop the activity before anyone was hurt. He said that in the past something like this would have been missed entirely. Lubbock Lights later reported ZeroEyes co-founder Sam Alaimo showing images from the incident. He said that it resulted in an arrest with no one hurt, and he listed the City of Hobbs and its school system among local clients. Lubbock Lights describes the images from the Del Norte Pool lot that the co-founder showed as a person pointing a gun in the general direction of a distant vehicle. The ZeroEyes release describes people shooting sporting clays near North Grimes Street. No court record is among the sources.

Details

AI system

Inputs and outputs

A security-camera image of a visible gun triggered an alert that people verified before notifying police and dispatch.

Autonomy

Detection initiated a review and alert chain; police decided how to respond and whether to detain or charge anyone.

Workflow

A gun visible to a municipal security camera connected to the detector. Human verification at the vendor's operations center. Dispatch integration with the local police department.

Human oversight

Human analysts verified the detection at the vendor's operations center before the alert reached the Hobbs Police Department and the dispatch center, and officers then responded and detained those involved.
