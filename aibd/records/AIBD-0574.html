What happened

On 19 November 2024 the Liverpool City Region Combined Authority announced that multilingual, interactive digital humans would assist visitors and host its AI Summit on 21 November 2024. The supplier, Sum Vivas, named the assistants Ava, Alison and James. The Local Government Association account of 25 March 2025 reports that over 300 people attended the summit on 21 November 2024. It reports that the firm that showed the digital humans was in talks with local authorities about digital tour guides. In September 2025 the Combined Authority and Sum Vivas trialed a multilingual assistant named Jimmy at the Labour Party conference. In October 2025 they placed Jimmy at the Mersey Ferries terminal at Pier Head until early 2026, with a planned move to a train station. On 16 October 2026 Sum Vivas stated that its assistant Alison served at the first summit in 2024 and that James served at the second in 2025. The company reports that Jimmy was to join the 2026 summit. The accounts provide no measurements of changes in access to event information, in language barriers or in the organizers' workload. The assistants' contribution to attendance and investment interest is unknown. No source reports how many people the assistants helped.
