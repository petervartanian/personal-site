Liverpool plans multilingual AI concierges for summit attendees

Liverpool's 2024 AI Summit planned interactive digital humans to assist attendees and host proceedings. Easier access to event information remains an intended benefit without a measured outcome.

ⓘ

What happened

The Liverpool City Region Combined Authority announced that multilingual, interactive digital humans would assist visitors and host its November 21, 2024 AI Summit. Supplier Sum Vivas named the assistants Ava, Alison, and James. The accounts provide no measurements of changes in access to event information, language barriers, or organizers' workload. The assistants' contribution to attendance and investment interest is unknown.

Purpose. Help summit attendees access information through multilingual AI interaction and support event hosting.

AI’s contribution

Not assessed. Multilingual, interactive AI digital humans were planned to assist visitors and host the summit. Their effect on visitor experience and event operations remains unevaluated.

Intended beneficiaries

  • unrated
    a person

    Attendees are intended to receive multilingual AI assistance. Improvements in access to event information and visitor experience are unmeasured.

  • unrated
    an organization

    Organizers are intended to receive support with visitor assistance and hosting. No operational saving or service improvement attributable to the digital humans is measured.

Further evidence

Setting. The planned service uses multilingual, interactive AI digital humans to assist attendees and host proceedings.

Conditions. Likely requirements include accurate event information, usable interaction, and suitable language support. These have not been validated as sufficient for a beneficial outcome.
