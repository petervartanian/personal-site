Conditions for replication

A disclosure must reach an agency able to investigate. Investigators must establish identity and assess the threat using supporting evidence. A general detection-and-review policy does not establish the details of a particular referral. Arrest and court restrictions do not prove that an attack would otherwise have occurred. Field

Public safety - stalking and threat investigation. Consumer chatbot trust-and-safety referral to law enforcement.

Purpose

OpenAI says that when it detects users planning to harm others, it routes the conversations to a small team trained on its usage policies. It says that if human reviewers find an imminent threat of serious physical harm to others, it may refer the case to law enforcement. This referral is that designed use. ChatGPT itself is a general-purpose assistant, which Zhou used to rehearse his plans.
