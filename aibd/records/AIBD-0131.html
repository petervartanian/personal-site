What happened

On 15 May 2025 software-engineering students from Karadeniz Technical University were presenting their Erken Uyarı Merkezi (Early Warning Center) to members of parliament. DHA's report describes it as an AI-based earthquake early-warning system. During the presentation, in the TBMM Public Relations building in Ankara, a quake centered in Kulu, Konya, was felt in the capital. DHA gave the magnitude as 5.2. USGS lists M5.0 at 15:46 local time (12:46:37 UTC), 14 km east-north-east of Kulu at 10 km depth. Student Birkan Yılmaz said notifications had reached their phones 30 seconds before the quake. Students, some MPs and building staff went outside. The read reports mention no damage or injuries.

What it was built to do. A student-built earthquake early-warning system meant to notify phones before shaking arrives. Warning the room before the Kulu quake was felt was that intended function, demonstrated live during the team's own presentation to MPs.

The system's part

The system is called yapay zeka tabanlı (AI-based) in DHA's report, on the students' description. None of the read reports explains how it detects quakes, what data it uses, or whether the alert time was logged. The 30-second lead is the statement of student Birkan Yılmaz, a member of the team that built it. DHA carried it and Haberler, Yeni Akit and Yeni Çağ reproduced it. No MP, AFAD or Kandilli comment on the alert was found. Leaving the building was a human response to the notification and to the shaking.

Replication

No replication cited.

What produced it. A quake large enough to be felt in Ankara from an epicentre in Kulu, Konya (DHA 5.2; USGS M5.0 at 10 km depth). This left a gap between detection and the arrival of shaking in the capital. The team's own phones carrying the app, with connectivity inside the TBMM building, during a live demonstration to MPs. An audience already primed by the presentation to treat the notification as a cue to leave.

What must hold again. The alert must arrive before the shaking. Here the lead time is the developer's statement, with no independent log or official timing. The detection source and false-alarm rate are undocumented in the read reports. Reliability beyond this one event is therefore unknown. Recipients must act on the notice. Here students, some MPs and staff went outside.
