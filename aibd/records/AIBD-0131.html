Student app alerts phones about 30 seconds before a quake reaches Ankara

Erken Uyarı Merkezi, an AI-based app built by Karadeniz Technical University students, alerted their phones about 30 seconds before a quake was felt at Turkey's parliament.

  • 1 a person

The system’s part
The system is called yapay zeka tabanlı (AI-based) in DHA's report, on the students' description. None of the read reports explains how it detects quakes, what data it uses, or whether the alert time was logged. The 30-second lead is the statement of student Birkan Yılmaz, a member of the team that built it. DHA carried it and Haberler, Yeni Akit and Yeni Çağ reproduced it. No MP, AFAD or Kandilli comment on the alert was found. Leaving the building was a human response to the notification and to the shaking.
Limits
The 30-second lead time is a developer's statement without an inspected alert log. The reports describe the same news-agency account and do not establish the algorithm, its error rate, or an injury that the alert prevented.
Costs and harms
The reports mention no damage, injury or other cost. The 30-second lead rests on the developer's statement and was not independently timed.
Later
Nothing later is known. No report after 15 May 2025 on the students' system was found in Turkish or English news, on KTÜ's site or in the literature. Birkan Yılmaz said that the team was planning meetings with ministers, and no outcome of those plans has been reported.

What happened

On 15 May 2025 software-engineering students from Karadeniz Technical University were presenting their Erken Uyarı Merkezi (Early Warning Center) to members of parliament. The report by the news agency DHA describes it as an AI-based earthquake early-warning system. The presentation took place in the Public Relations building of the Turkish parliament (TBMM) in Ankara. During it, a quake centered in Kulu, Konya, was felt in the capital. DHA gave the magnitude as 5.2. The U.S. Geological Survey lists M5.0 at 15:46 local time (12:46:37 UTC), 14 km east-north-east of Kulu at 10 km depth. Student Birkan Yılmaz said that notifications had reached their phones 30 seconds before the quake. Students, some members of parliament and building staff went outside. None of the cited reports mentions damage or injuries.

Details

AI system

Workflow

A quake large enough to be felt in Ankara from an epicentre in Kulu, Konya (DHA 5.2; USGS M5.0 at 10 km depth). This left a gap between detection and the arrival of shaking in the capital. The team's own phones carrying the app, with connectivity inside the TBMM building, during a live demonstration to MPs. An audience already primed by the presentation to treat the notification as a cue to leave.

Human oversight

The students told nearby legislators about the phone notification, and people chose to leave the building.

Benefits

Beneficiaries

Students, legislators, and staff near the demonstration were the immediate recipients of the warning.

Reach

No count is reported. The notice reached the team's phones, and students, some members of parliament and building staff went outside. [1] [2] [3] [4] [5]

Duration

The benefit was a single warning on 15 May 2025. No later report on the system was found, and the team's planned meetings with ministers have no reported outcome. [1] [2] [3] [4] [5]

Follow-up

Conditions for replication

The alert must arrive before the shaking. Here the lead time is the developer's statement, with no independent log or official timing. The detection source and false-alarm rate are undocumented in the read reports. Reliability beyond this one event is therefore unknown. Recipients must act on the notice. Here students, some MPs and staff went outside.
