CGU, with ALICE support, avoids R$8.33m in overpriced purchases

depth 2 for an organization, depth 1 for society

prevented at least R$8.33 million of overpriced purchases

With ALICE support, CGU cut R$2.38m from a 2021 Ipanema hospital glove tender and R$5.95m from an INCA tender; R$8.33m avoided

What happened

A CGU statement of 4 October 2023 describes five preventive audits of tenders by federal hospitals and institutes in Rio de Janeiro carried out with support from the automated tool ALICE, which the CGU says prevented at least R$8.33 million of overpriced purchases. At the Instituto Nacional de Câncer, a glove auction estimated at R$11 million was re-estimated at R$5.1 million after ALICE indicated prices above market, avoiding about R$5.95 million. At the Hospital Federal de Ipanema, Pregão 15/2021 for gloves (estimated R$34 million) had reused the price survey of Pregão 05/2021; on CGU's recommendation it was suspended and re-estimated, cutting R$2,381,663.00. The auction held on 21 July 2021 bought every item at or below the new estimates, totaling R$14,315,793.42.

What it was built to do. Preventive audit of federal procurement notices using automated risk alerts to stop overpriced purchases before award

The system's part

The CGU states the audits were done 'com apoio da ferramenta automatizada ALICE' and that at INCA 'com a ajuda do ALICE' the overpricing risk was identified; for the Ipanema tender the suspension followed a CGU recommendation after analysis of the price survey. ALICE's documented role is alerting and supporting the audit; auditors and managers decided. Developer and deployer are the same public body. The gov.br page was retrieved from the Internet Archive because the live page was restricted during Brazil's 2026 electoral period.

Replication

What produced it. ALICE alerts on price surveys inconsistent with prices practiced in comparable purchases in the same region. CGU preventive audits completed before the auction, with recommendations to re-estimate prices. Hospital managers suspending and re-running the price estimate.

What must hold again. Tender notices published on the federal purchasing portal where ALICE reads them daily. Time between publication and auction sufficient for audit and correction. Availability of market price references.
