CGU, with ALICE support, avoids R$8.33m in overpriced purchases

With its ALICE tool, the CGU cut R$2.38m from a 2021 Ipanema hospital glove tender and R$5.95m from a glove tender at Brazil's National Cancer Institute; R$8.33m was avoided

The system’s part
The CGU states the audits were done “com apoio da ferramenta automatizada ALICE”. At INCA, it says, the overpricing risk was identified “com a ajuda do ALICE”. For the Ipanema tender, suspension followed a CGU recommendation after analysis of the price survey. ALICE's documented role is alerting and supporting the audit; auditors and managers decided. Developer and deployer are the same public body. The CGU page is on record as an archived copy, because the live page was restricted during Brazil's 2026 electoral period.
Limits
Revised tender estimates, awarded prices, suspended purchases, and realized savings are different financial measures. The record does not isolate ALICE’s contribution from auditors’ decisions or show that all initially estimated expenditure would have occurred.

What happened

Pregão 15/2021 of the Hospital Federal de Ipanema, a glove tender estimated at R$34 million, reused the price survey of Pregão 05/2021. On the recommendation of the Controladoria-Geral da União (CGU), it was suspended and re-estimated, which cut R$2,381,663.00. The auction held on 21 July 2021 bought every item at or below the new estimates, for a total of R$14,315,793.42. In March 2023 Fernanda Odilla of the University of Bologna reported in an independent study that Alice has helped auditors stop irregular tenders across Brazil, with accuracy that fell from 88.27% in testing to 73.91% in later auditing. She did not examine these tenders. A CGU statement of 4 October 2023 describes five preventive audits of tenders by federal hospitals and institutes in Rio de Janeiro, among them the Ipanema tender. The automated tool ALICE supported them. The CGU says that this prevented at least R$8.33 million of overpriced purchases. At the Instituto Nacional de Câncer (INCA), a glove auction estimated at R$11 million was re-estimated at R$5.1 million after ALICE indicated prices above market. That avoided about R$5.95 million. The statement gives no date or tender number for the INCA auction.

Details

AI system

Inputs and outputs

ALICE flagged tender prices that appeared high compared with market references, providing leads for preventive audits of hospital purchases.

Workflow

ALICE alerts on price surveys inconsistent with prices practiced in comparable purchases in the same region. CGU preventive audits completed before the auction, with recommendations to re-estimate prices. Hospital managers suspending and re-running the price estimate.

Human oversight

CGU auditors assessed the price surveys and recommended changes. Hospital managers suspended or revised tenders before completing purchases.

Benefits

Duration

The audits acted before award, in the interval between publication and auction. By the end of 2024 the CGU reported that 126 bodies were registered to receive ALICE alerts and that concluded 2024 audits had saved R$257.25 million. [1] [2] [3] [4]
