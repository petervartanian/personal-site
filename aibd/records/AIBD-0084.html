DGFiP's Foncier innovant leads to 20,000+ pools taxed for 2022

DGFiP deep-learning pool detection: 20,000+ pools taxed for 2022 (~€10m for communes); 150,000 taxable pools found across metropolitan France excluding Paris and overseas departments

ⓘ

What happened

France's Direction générale des Finances publiques (DGFiP) runs “Foncier innovant”. A deep-learning algorithm finds building and pool outlines in public IGN aerial images. An automated check compares them to land-tax files. A tax agent verifies every anomaly before the owner is contacted. DGFiP's October 2021 note says Capgemini provided project-management and build assistance and Google the cloud infrastructure and services for developing the AI models. The 2022 experiment in nine departments led to more than 20,000 pools being taxed for 2022. That was close to €10 million of extra revenue for the communes concerned (DGFiP, 2 June 2023). The sources do not date the result separately from the announcement. The Sénat's April 2024 report puts the cost at €24 million.

After generalization in 2023, the first pass covered metropolitan France, excluding Paris and the overseas departments (DROM). It detected 150,000 untaxed taxable pools, which the Sénat report calls “potentiellement taxables”. Industrialization began in June 2024.

Purpose. Detect undeclared pools and buildings from aerial imagery to bring them into the local property-tax base

AI’s contribution

Not assessed. Official sources describe the chain (AI detection, automated file check, human verification, owner contact) and attribute the taxed pools to it. No evaluation isolates the algorithm's contribution from the human verification and declaration campaign. The Assemblée nationale written answer of 14 December 2021 confirms DGFiP's use of AI on IGN imagery and that contractors do not access tax data.

Further evidence

Setting. Deep-learning segmentation of IGN orthophotos producing pool and building outlines. Automated cross-check against cadastral and land-tax files. Systematic verification by a tax agent and a declaration campaign to owners.

Conditions. Regular high-resolution public aerial imagery (IGN triennial orthophotos). Reliable cadastral records to compare against. Legal basis to tax detected constructions and agent capacity for verification.
