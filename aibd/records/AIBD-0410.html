What happened

The International Telecommunication Union account names the Cooperative Open Intelligent Computing Industry Alliance, working with C2itech Limited of Hong Kong, as the submitter. The submission describes testing by biotechnology partners and hospital laboratories in Hong Kong and Shanghai. It reports latency below 80 milliseconds and user satisfaction above 4.7 out of 5 from that testing. It also claims that virtual drug screening becomes 50% faster. The submitters support the 50% figure with citations to earlier studies by other teams, and they give no comparison from the project itself. Pandemic response and personalized treatment are intended downstream benefits. The C2itech home page describes the company's organoid services and does not mention the AI platform.
