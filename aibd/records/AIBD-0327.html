IDF has not confirmed Iron Beam's first combat interception

one drone intercepted near Shlomi

Ynet and JPost (2 Mar 2026) call the video unconfirmed; Times of Israel (27 Mar, 30 Jun 2026) quotes officials that Iron Beam 'has not yet been declared operational'. No fetched source calls it AI.

What happened

The OECD AI Incidents Monitor record 2026-02-28-d62f says that Israel 'deployed its AI-powered Iron Beam laser defense system for the first time in combat to intercept Hezbollah missile barrages', and separately bundles a Hankyoreh item (dated 10 October 2023) on Hamas rockets penetrating Iron Dome. The Iron Beam claim rests on social-media footage from Monday 2 March 2026, not 28 February: Ynet (Yair Kraus) reports that the footage 'appeared to show the first operational use' of the system, delivered to the Israeli Air Force in late December 2025, as Hezbollah fired rockets and drones from Lebanon after the killing of Iran's Supreme Leader, with one drone intercepted near Shlomi; the Jerusalem Post (Seth Frantzman, 2 Mar 2026) says that the circulated video 'did not show lasers intercepting rockets'; TimesNow (2 Mar 2026) says that whether this was Iron Beam's first combat use 'awaits official confirmation'; Army Recognition (3 Mar 2026) says that officials 'have not formally confirmed the specific engagements' and that no authoritative combat log names the system; The Conversation (4 Mar 2026) calls the reports unconfirmed; Moneycontrol (3 Mar 2026) sources its 'first-ever use' to an 'Israel War Room' social-media video. The Times of Israel reports the Defense Ministry's handover of the first operational system on 28 December 2025 after tests against rockets, mortars and drones; Times of Israel/AFP (16 Mar 2026) that the IDF 'has so far refused to comment on its use during the ongoing war'; Times of Israel (Stav Levaton, 27 Mar 2026) that 'military officials said it has not yet been declared operational within the IDF'; and Times of Israel (Emanuel Fabian, 30 Jun 2026) that the one deployed system 'has seen limited use and has not yet been declared operational within the IDF', with a senior officer calling its coverage partial. The only confirmed laser combat use is aggregate and earlier: a lower-powered, shorter-range variant shot down some 35 drones from Lebanon in 2024 (Times of Israel/AFP), announced by the Ministry of Defense in May 2025 (JPost), with the MoD saying that no Iron Beam components were involved (Wikipedia). No fetched source describes Iron Beam as an AI system; the earlier verifier found the only AI mention to be Rafael's CEO framing machine learning as a future capability (Breaking Defense, 22 Oct 2025), which could not be re-fetched here. Metro, Economic Times, MoneyControl's second item, Hankyoreh and others among the Monitor's 20 listed sources are syndications or explainers.

What it was built to do. Iron Beam is a high-energy laser built to intercept rockets, mortars and drones at short range as a low-cost complement to Iron Dome, David's Sling and Arrow; interception of Hezbollah projectiles is its stated purpose.
