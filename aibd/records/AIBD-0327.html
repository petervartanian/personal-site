AI system

System

Iron Beam ('Or Eitan') 100 kW-class high-energy laser air-defense system by Rafael Advanced Defense Systems, with beam director and electro-optical targeting; the Monitor calls it 'AI-enabled', which no cited source supports

Application developer

Rafael Advanced Defense Systems with the Israel Ministry of Defense (Lockheed Martin collaboration for the US market, per Wikipedia)

Workflow

A ground-based high-energy laser that holds its beam on a short-range target within line of sight until it is destroyed. JPost describes the terrain, weather and dwell-time limits. Integration into the Israeli Air Force's layered air-defense array as a complement to Iron Dome.

Relation to AI

No cited source describes Iron Beam as an AI or machine-learning system. Those sources are Ynet, JPost, Army Recognition, TimesNow, The Conversation and Moneycontrol. They also include Times of Israel 28 December 2025, 16 Mar, 27 Mar and 30 June 2026, and Wikipedia. They describe a high-power laser with a beam director and electro-optical targeting. The Monitor's “AI-enabled” label is its own. Rafael's CEO described machine learning as a future capability (Breaking Defense, 22 October 2025). The IDF has not confirmed the engagement itself, so neither an AI role nor a contribution can be assessed.

Entry details

Field

Defense: air defense. Directed-energy interception of rockets, mortars and drones.

Purpose

Iron Beam is a high-energy laser built to intercept rockets, mortars and drones at short range. It is a low-cost complement to Iron Dome, David's Sling and Arrow. Interception of Hezbollah projectiles is its stated purpose.
