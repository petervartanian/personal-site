What happened

KYield announced HumCat on 18 July 2017. Its press release describes a distributed AI system that operates within organizational workflows. The release also describes a prevention program that could be combined with insurance or catastrophe bonds. In a blog post of 23 November 2017, the founder describes risk mitigation and performance guarantees as proposed commercial features. Neither the release nor the post names a customer or reports an observed catastrophe that was avoided. Neither source gives an evaluation design or a measured reduction in realized losses. The HumCat product page remained on KYield's site through at least August 2024 and still listed its benefits as expected. The page stated that independent actuaries would confirm and value prevented events. As of 29 October 2026 no published source documents a prevented catastrophe.
