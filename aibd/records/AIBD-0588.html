HumCat proposes AI support for catastrophe prevention

KYield proposes HumCat for organizational risk prevention, but the reviewed sources do not document a catastrophe that it prevented.

Benefits

Benefit

KYield announced HumCat on July 18, 2017. Its release describes a distributed AI system operating within organizational workflows and a prevention program that could be combined with insurance or catastrophe bonds. A later founder account describes risk mitigation and performance guarantees as proposed commercial features. Neither source supplies a named customer, observed avoided catastrophe, evaluation design, or quantified realized loss reduction. [1] [2]
