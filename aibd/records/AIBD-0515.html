University team in Tanzania proposes AI to realign home satellite dishes

Mbeya University of Science and Technology in Tanzania proposes training an AI model on dish misalignment to speed up realignment; no test of the model has been reported.

  • Not rated a person

    The proposed model is intended to speed up dish realignment and reduce household inconvenience. Benefits from an implemented model are unreported.

  • Not rated an organization

    Faster AI-assisted realignment is intended to reduce servicing effort. Savings from the proposed learned model are not established.

What happened

A team from Mbeya University of Science and Technology in Tanzania lists Rehema Benson Richard, Sospeter J. Salawa and Cuthbert John Karawa as its members. Its project account, current as of June 2025, proposes a satellite dish prototype for homes. The dish would use stored alignment angles for each service provider, motor controls and signal feedback. It would serve the providers Azam, DStv and Zuku. The team also proposes training an AI model on misalignment patterns to speed up realignment. The report states that labeled data from manual installations was used to calibrate alignment. It states that further AI training was planned after deployment. Operation with several providers and savings on hardware concern the wider design. Deployment of the model and its effect on reliability, adjustment time and maintenance effort remain unverified. No later report of a test or of a working AI model had appeared by October 2026.
