What happened

North East Derbyshire introduced Copilot alongside governance, data, and skills programs to improve decisions and service design. The council describes more evidence-seeking staff discussions and changes in decision practice. It does not isolate Copilot’s contribution from the wider program. Reductions in repetitive work and better resident outcomes remain expected.

What it was built to do. Reduce routine drafting, reporting, and information-handling work so that staff have more time for analysis and service improvement.
