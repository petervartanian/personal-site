Wexton addresses the House with an ElevenLabs clone of her voice

Rep. Jennifer Wexton, who lost her speech to PSP, addressed the House on 25 July 2024 with an ElevenLabs clone of her own voice.

ⓘ

What happened

On 25 July 2024, Representative Jennifer Wexton of Virginia gave a House floor speech for Disability Pride Month. She was diagnosed in 2023 with progressive supranuclear palsy. She used an augmentative and alternative communication device playing an AI-generated clone of her voice. The Associated Press reported that ElevenLabs built the voice from recordings of her congressional speeches and public appearances. The Congressional Record has her saying that PSP “has robbed me of my ability to use my full voice”. She said hearing the recreation was “the most beautiful thing I had ever heard, and I cried tears of joy”. The Office of the House Historian states she was the first Member to speak on the House floor using this type of assistive technology.

Purpose. ElevenLabs voice cloning is built to reproduce a person's voice from recordings. Restoring her own voice for speech was that purpose.

AI’s contribution

The cloned voice is the AI output. Wexton wrote the content and the AAC device played it. A generic synthetic voice would also have carried the words, so the AI-specific benefit is the restoration of her own voice.

Beneficiaries

  • a person

    A legislator who lost intelligible speech regained the ability to address the House in a voice recognizably her own. She told AP it was “more me than I ever could have hoped I could hear again”.

  • society

    First use of an AAC device on the House floor per the House Historian, a small visibility benefit for disability accommodation.
