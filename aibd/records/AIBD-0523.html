SK Telecom says CareVia cut recording work and self-harm in care

SK Telecom reports that its CareVia vision AI cut behavior-recording time from three hours to five minutes; its reference for 76.5% fewer self-harm incidents names no such study.

  • 1 an organization

    SK Telecom reports that behavior-recording work fell from about three hours to five minutes per session. In June 2026 it reported that recording time fell by up to 90%.

  • 2 a person

    SK Telecom reports 76.5% fewer self-harm incidents in a 2024 intervention study, and its supplied reference does not identify that study. In June 2026 it reported that challenging behaviors fell by 34.6% after tailored intervention. No independent source confirms either figure, and the separate effect of the AI is unknown. No source reports injury severity or how long the improvement lasted.

What happened

SK Telecom and project partners developed CareVia, a vision AI that detects and summarizes behavior to support care planning for people with developmental disabilities. On 7 March 2025, Bizhankook described the deployment at a center in Dobong and quoted outside voices, without outcome data. In its account for the International Telecommunication Union (ITU), with information current as of June 2025, SK Telecom reports that recording time fell from three hours to five minutes per session. It also reports 76.5% fewer self-harm incidents in a 2024 intervention study, which it describes as a clinical study with Severance Hospital. The ITU account cites a 2011 World Health Organization and World Bank report as the source of the 76.5% figure and lists no Severance Hospital publication. Its supplied reference therefore does not identify that study. The clinical result remains unverified, and the effect of the AI is not separated from the other elements of care. SK Telecom's later reports give different outcome figures. On 10 June 2026 it reported that recording time fell by up to 90% and that challenging behaviors fell by 34.6% after tailored intervention. Dealsite reported on 6 October 2026 that CareVia still ran at 12 sites, short of the company's target of 18 sites by the end of 2025. A company official linked the shortfall to budgets.

Details

AI system

Role

CareVia recognizes and records behavior to support professional care for people with developmental disabilities.

Inputs and outputs

CareVia combines video frames, body keypoints, and time and location information to recognize behavior, identify the person involved, and produce records for intervention planning.

Workflow

Conditions that could produce the benefit are unspecified.

Human oversight

Vision AI detects and summarizes behavior to support care planning. SK Telecom reports that behavior-intervention specialists analyze the recorded data and plan care and interventions for each person. [1] [2] [3] [4]

Benefits

Reach

No source counts the people whose behavior CareVia recorded. Dealsite reported that the system ran at 12 sites in 2026, short of the company's target of 18. [1] [2] [3] [4]

Duration

SK Telecom reported outcome figures again on 10 June 2026, and Dealsite reported on 6 October 2026 that CareVia still ran at 12 sites. No source follows the same care recipients over time. [1] [2] [3] [4]

Follow-up

Conditions for replication

CCTV/edge processing in care settings. Professional review and intervention planning.

Evidence

The outcome figures come from SK Telecom and from an official at a Seoul center that uses the system. The ITU account cites a 2011 World Health Organization and World Bank report for the 76.5% figure and lists no Severance Hospital publication. [1] [2] [3] [4]
