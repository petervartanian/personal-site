Kent reports fewer homeless households after targeted support

Kent reports under 1% homelessness among proactively supported households versus 40% in an unsupported comparator. The groups were not randomized.

ⓘ

What happened

Kent describes Maidstone’s use of an AI-enabled Xantura platform to flag households for proactive homelessness support and a wider county pilot. The council presenter reports that fewer than 1% of supported alerted households became homeless, compared with 40% of a group that lacked support because of limited capacity. The comparison was not randomized and does not separate AI’s effect from the support intervention. Outcome dates are unspecified.

Purpose. Identify households at risk of homelessness so that councils can offer proactive support.

AI’s contribution

Not assessed. The presenter describes an AI-enabled platform that combines council data and flags households for human support. The prevention comparison concerns the combined targeting-and-support program. Because support was capacity-constrained and not randomized, AI’s separate contribution to the difference is not established.

Beneficiaries

  • a person

    The council reports that proactive support prevented homelessness in a high-risk cohort. The groups were not randomized, and AI’s separate effect is unknown.

  • an organization

    The reported prevention program improves targeting and preventive results for councils. The source does not supply sample sizes or an independent evaluation.

Further evidence

Setting. Maidstone combined local data with data shared by Kent County Council, including social-care information. The comparison group did not receive proactive support because the housing team lacked capacity.

Conditions. The presenter states that protected characteristics are not included in the live prediction model. The source does not establish causal conditions for reproducing the prevention result.
