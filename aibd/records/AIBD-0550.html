Citable summary

Kent reports under 1% homelessness among proactively supported households versus 40% in an unsupported comparator. The groups were not randomized. The less-than-1% versus 40% comparison concerns households receiving proactive support versus a capacity-constrained unsupported group. Allocation was not described as randomized, and comparable baseline risk, sample sizes, and observation windows are unavailable in the reviewed transcript. It cannot isolate AI targeting from the support itself. The presenter describes an AI-enabled platform that combines council data and flags households for human support. The prevention comparison concerns the combined targeting-and-support program. Because support was capacity-constrained and not randomized, AI’s separate contribution to the difference is not established.
