GPT-5.5 Pro solves Erdős problem #38; solution Lean-formalized

GPT-5.5 Pro solved Erdős problem #38; human-checked 26 April 2026, Lean-formalized by Aristotle 1 May, recorded as solved on erdosproblems.com

ⓘ

What happened

Erdős problem #38 is an open question in additive number theory. It asks for a set B that is not an additive basis yet raises the Schnirelmann density of every set A. OpenAI's GPT-5.5 Pro solved it. On 25 April 2026 a user (gebyjaff) posted the model's solution on the erdosproblems.com forum, with a PDF cleanup by Liam Price. On 26 April forum commenter Nat Sothanaphan reported that a standard check found no issues in the write-up. On 1 May the Aristotle system autoformalized the argument in Lean, which Sothanaphan confirmed matches the paper. Site maintainer Thomas Bloom added an alternative sketch on 2 May 2026. The problem page now records the positive solution by GPT 5.5 Pro. Terence Tao's wiki lists it as an AI-standalone full solution.

Purpose. General-purpose reasoning model prompted to solve an open research problem

AI’s contribution

The model produced the proof after a human prompt. Humans cleaned up the write-up and checked it, and the maintainer accepted it. A separate AI system (Aristotle) produced the Lean formalization that a human confirmed matches the paper. Tao's wiki classifies the case as AI standalone with non-significant human involvement. The attribution of GPT-5.5 Pro to OpenAI rests on the chatgpt.com share link in gebyjaff's forum post. No cited source names OpenAI in prose. The cited sources do not name Aristotle's developer.
