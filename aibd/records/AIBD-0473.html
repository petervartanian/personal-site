Citable summary

AgileSoDA reports that partner deployments cut floor-planning time by 76%, from more than a week to roughly a day. The figures are first-party aggregate claims. The 76% reduction applies to floor planning in reported partner work, while the source also gives smaller overall-duration improvements. It cannot represent a 76% reduction in the complete chip-design process. Design-quality checks, sample sizes, and realized production effects remain unverified.
