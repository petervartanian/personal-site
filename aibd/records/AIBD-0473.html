AI shortens semiconductor floor planning

AgileSoDA reports that its reinforcement-learning physical-design system cut floor-planning time in partner deployments by 76%, from more than a week to roughly a day.

Reinforcement learning automates physical chip layout and floor planning. The reported time reductions are attributed to this design workflow, without an independent component comparison.
Limits
The 76% reduction applies to floor planning in reported partner work, while the source also gives smaller overall-duration improvements. It cannot represent a 76% reduction in the complete chip-design process. Design-quality checks, sample sizes, and realized production effects remain unverified.

What happened

AgileSoDA and ASICLAND announced their chip design partnership in January 2023. ASICLAND is a data and deployment partner. In its submission to the ITU AI for Good report of 2025, AgileSoDA reports that partner deployments cut floor-planning time by 76%, from more than a week to roughly a day. It also reports that overall duration fell by 20% for high-density designs. These aggregate first-party results are not allocated to individual designs. On 8 December 2025 the Korean newspaper Hankyung reported that a KOSDAQ-listed company had tested the placement tool in a TSMC process. Hankyung cited no named source and gave no outcome figures. It also reported that AgileSoDA was still developing ChipNSoDA.

Details

AI system

Role

The reinforcement-learning system assists physical chip design by generating or optimizing floor plans.

Workflow

The account does not separately establish the conditions responsible for the benefit.

Human oversight

The reported development process checks candidate designs with commercial electronic-design-automation tools and adjusts model parameters after verification.

Benefits

Duration

The source reports that the floor-planning step fell from more than a week to roughly a day, and it gives no period over which the saving held. Hankyung reported on 8 December 2025 that a listed company had tested the placement tool and that AgileSoDA was still developing ChipNSoDA, and it gave no figures on design time. [1]

Follow-up

Conditions for replication

Access to compatible physical-design data such as LEF/DEF files. Integration with the partner's design workflow.

Evidence

Original evidence

The floor-planning figures come from AgileSoDA's own submission to the ITU AI for Good report of 2025. Hankyung reported on 8 December 2025 that a KOSDAQ-listed company had tested the placement tool in a TSMC process, citing no named source, and it gave no figures on design time. [1]
