Apple Watch calls an ambulance for an unconscious motorcyclist

depth 3 for a person

Independently reported, 3 sources.

Apple Watch Fall Detection called an ambulance and alerted his girlfriend after a hit-and-run left a Singapore motorcyclist unconscious.

What happened

On the evening of 25 September 2021, Muhammad Fitri, 24, was riding home when a gray van struck his motorcycle. That was at the junction of Ang Mo Kio Avenue 6 and Ang Mo Kio Street 31, Singapore. The driver fled; Fitri lost consciousness on a near-empty road. His Apple Watch's Fall Detection registered the impact. Via the paired phone it automatically called an ambulance and texted and phoned his emergency contact, his girlfriend. The Singapore Civil Defence Force said it was alerted around 8:20pm. Fitri was regaining consciousness as paramedics arrived. He was conveyed, conscious, to Khoo Teck Puat Hospital with abrasions, a neck collar and a suspected bleed at the back of the head. Police confirmed a hit-and-run.

What it was built to do. Fall Detection is built to recognize a hard fall. If the wearer stays immobile for about a minute, it calls emergency services and messages emergency contacts with the location. That is what it did. The trigger was a motorcycle collision rather than a fall from standing. Apple's documentation notes only that high-impact activity may register as a fall.

The system's part

The watch's fall-detection classifier read the collision's impact as a hard fall. With the wearer unresponsive, it placed the automatic ambulance call through the paired phone and sent the location texts and calls to his girlfriend. SCDF paramedics treated and conveyed him. The counterfactual is unproven. A passer-by later picked up his phone and spoke to the girlfriend. The family at first assumed that person had called the ambulance, and Fitri was regaining consciousness as paramedics arrived.

Replication

No attempt to repeat it has been recorded.

What produced it. Fall Detection is enabled on a worn Apple Watch (Series 4 or later) with a connected paired iPhone or cellular service. A collision throws the wearer down with an impact the classifier reads as a hard fall. The wearer stays immobile for about a minute without dismissing the alert. The ambulance service and the emergency contact can act on the automatic call and the shared location.

What must hold again. The watch and phone keep working and connected after the impact (here the watch screen cracked but still functioned). Nobody else present is able to call for help, otherwise the watch's call adds little. An ambulance can reach the location within the window in which treatment matters.
