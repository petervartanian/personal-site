Students' AI flags sick geese, and survival on a Shantou farm rises 30%

An AI camera program built by Shenzhen University students with Tencent engineers alerts a Shantou goose cooperative to sick geese, and Xinhua reports that survival rose 30 percent.

What happened

In 2018 an avian influenza outbreak left five of thousands of geese alive in Houxi Village, Chenghai District, Shantou. In 2022 goose farmer Jin Shutao invited 16 students from the Shenzhen University-Tencent Cloud artificial intelligence Bachelor of Engineering program to his lion-head goose cooperative in the village. They worked in a 500 m² space holding over 4,000 geese. They used existing farm cameras and manually labeled 6,000 images of 300,000 geese. With Tencent engineers they improved recognition in crowded scenes and the tracking of how long each goose stays still. The resulting AI program identifies sick geese by measuring that stillness, supplemented by detecting feverish goslings. Xinhua reported on the program on 13 October 2023, and CGTN syndicated the report the next day. Xinhua reports that the program now gives real-time alerts for “listless geese” and “feverish geese” and displays trends in temperature, humidity and PM2.5. It states that this has helped the farm increase survival of lion-head geese by 30 percent. It quotes the student Wang Yifeng and Shen Linlin of Shenzhen University's visual research institute. The article recalls the 2018 outbreak. The 30% figure is undated, and the article gives no method behind it. The OECD AI Incidents Monitor lists the event. On 7 September 2024 Shenzhen University and Tencent Cloud authors, including Shen Linlin, published a paper in Scientific Data. It describes six cameras on a goose farm in Chenghai District and video recorded in July and November 2022. The paper reports no figure on goose survival.

Details

AI system

Inputs and outputs

Camera images were used to track how long geese remained still and to flag possible illness, alongside alerts for feverish goslings and environmental readings.

Workflow

Existing farm cameras and 6,000 manually labeled images of 300,000 geese. Stillness duration as the sickness proxy, with fever detection in goslings. Over 4,000 geese in a 500 m² space.

Human oversight

The program alerts breeders to listless and feverish geese in real time. The sources do not describe who checked the flagged birds or what care they received. [1] [2] [3] [4]

Benefits

Reach

One cooperative in Houxi Village benefited, by Xinhua's account. Xinhua reports that the students worked in a 500 m² space holding over 4,000 geese. It does not say how many geese the survival figure covers. [1] [2] [3] [4]

Duration

Xinhua gives no date or period for the 30 percent figure. The 2024 dataset paper reports no survival figure. No later report of the cooperative's results was found. [1] [2] [3] [4]

People and places

Dependencies

The deployment reused farm cameras and needed labeled local images plus engineering support to track individual geese in crowded scenes.

Follow-up

Conditions for replication

Camera coverage in dense flocks. Continued tuning of the algorithm to the specific farm (the students found no one-size-fits-all algorithm).
