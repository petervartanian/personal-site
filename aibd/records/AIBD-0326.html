X-Guard decoy may have diverted Pakistani missiles from IAF jets

Every account traces to ex-USAF pilot Ryan Bodenheimer via idrw.org (6 July 2025). No IAF, MoD, Dassault or Rafael confirmation; CDS Gen Anil Chauhan admitted on 31 May 2025 that India lost jets.

Workflow

A towed decoy on a fiber-optic cable emitting a jamming signal that mimics the aircraft's radar return and Doppler shift (as the outlets describe it). Radar-guided missiles (PL-15E, per the outlets) engaging the decoy rather than the aircraft.

Not assessed

Relation to AI

No source independent of the commentary chain describes X-Guard as an AI system. idrw writes “AI-enhanced jamming decoy” and “AI-enhanced algorithms dynamically adjust signals” without attribution. ET, India TV, LatestLY, Oneindia and TFIPOST repeat the label. The Week gives no source for “AI-enabled”. Rafael's own page was unreadable. Deployment itself is unconfirmed by the IAF, MoD, Dassault or Rafael. The no-loss outcome is contradicted by the CDS. Neither the system's AI character nor any contribution to the engagements can therefore be assessed.

Follow-up

Conditions for replication

Official confirmation that X-Guard was carried and deployed by the IAF in the 7–10 May 2025 engagements. Evidence that missiles struck decoys rather than aircraft. India TV attributes this to Jane's Defence Weekly, but no other outlet corroborates it. It is at odds with the CDS's admission of losses.

Replication attempts

Not assessed

Later findings

31 May 2025. India's chief of defence staff admitted on 31 May 2025 that an unspecified number of Indian jets were shot down in the May conflict, which contradicts the outlets' claim that the decoy kept missiles from hitting real aircraft.

Field

Defense: electronic warfare. Airborne towed radar decoys.

Purpose

A towed decoy is built to present a false target to enemy radars and radar-guided missiles. They then engage the decoy rather than the aircraft. The outlets describe a 30 kg body on a 100 m fiber-optic cable emitting a 500 W jamming signal.
