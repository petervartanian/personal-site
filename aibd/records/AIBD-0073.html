What happened

On 9 October 2021 the Beethoven Orchester Bonn under Dirk Kaftan gave the world premiere of a Tenth Symphony completed with artificial intelligence. It was at the Telekom Forum in Bonn. Deutsche Telekom funded the project and organized the premiere, for Beethoven's 250th anniversary. Beethoven left only sketches for the work. A team led by musicologist Matthias Röder, with AI researcher Ahmed Elgammal and Mark Gotham, trained a model on Beethoven's output. It generated sketch continuations. Composer Walter Werzowa and musicologists chose and arranged the generated themes. Euronews reported on 13 October 2021 that the premiere had taken place in Bonn on 9 October. The benefit is a performed and recorded cultural artifact; the sources do not claim the result is Beethoven's.

What it was built to do. Generate a completion of Beethoven's unfinished Tenth Symphony from his sketches in his style

The system's part

The model generated candidate continuations of Beethoven's sketches. Humans (Werzowa and the musicologists) selected, arranged and orchestrated them, so the AI contribution was generative input within a human-curated process. The vendor-side accounts (Elgammal in The Conversation; Telekom) and the orchestra's own program page agree on the premiere.

Replication

No replication cited.

What produced it. Beethoven's surviving sketches for the Tenth Symphony. A generative model trained on Beethoven's complete works. Human selection, arrangement and orchestration by Werzowa and the musicologists. A sponsor and a professional orchestra willing to premiere the result.

What must hold again. Enough sketch material and corpus to condition the model. Human curation of generated material. A performing institution programs the work.

Harm on record

AI Incident Database 526, Novel Deepfake Song Pulled from Music Streaming Services After Allegedly Violating Artist's Rights. Same task, AI generating new music in an existing artist's style: the “Heart On My Sleeve” track mimicking Drake and The Weeknd was pulled after Universal Music Group's takedown notices, harming the label and artists.
