Beethoven X AI completes the Tenth Symphony; premiered in Bonn

depth 1 for an organization, depth 1 for society

AI-completed Beethoven Tenth premiered 9 Oct 2021 by Beethoven Orchester Bonn under Dirk Kaftan at the Telekom Forum, Bonn

What happened

On 9 October 2021 the Beethoven Orchester Bonn under Dirk Kaftan gave the world premiere at the Telekom Forum in Bonn of a Tenth Symphony completed with artificial intelligence, in a project funded by Deutsche Telekom, which organized the premiere, for Beethoven's 250th anniversary. Beethoven left only sketches for the work. A team led by musicologist Matthias Röder with AI researcher Ahmed Elgammal and Mark Gotham trained a model on Beethoven's output to generate continuations of the sketches; composer Walter Werzowa and musicologists chose and arranged the generated themes. Euronews reported on 13 October 2021 that the premiere had taken place in Bonn on 9 October. The benefit is a performed and recorded cultural artifact; the sources do not claim the result is Beethoven's.

What it was built to do. Generate a completion of Beethoven's unfinished Tenth Symphony from his sketches in his style

The system's part

The model generated candidate continuations of Beethoven's sketches; humans (Werzowa and the musicologists) selected, arranged and orchestrated them, so the AI contribution was generative input within a human-curated process. The vendor-side accounts (Elgammal in The Conversation; Telekom) and the orchestra's own program page agree on the premiere.

Replication

No attempt to repeat it has been recorded.

What produced it

  • Beethoven's surviving sketches for the Tenth Symphony
  • A generative model trained on Beethoven's complete works
  • Human selection, arrangement and orchestration by Werzowa and the musicologists
  • A sponsor and a professional orchestra willing to premiere the result

What must hold again

  • Enough sketch material and corpus to condition the model
  • Human curation of generated material
  • A performing institution programs the work
