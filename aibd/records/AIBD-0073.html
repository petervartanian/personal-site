Bonn orchestra premieres Beethoven's Tenth, completed with AI help

The Beethoven X AI model generated continuations of Beethoven's sketches, which humans arranged into a Tenth Symphony that the Beethoven Orchester Bonn premiered on 9 October 2021.

  • 1 an organization

    The orchestra programmed a premiere that a sponsor funded and that was broadcast live, according to its concert page.

  • 1 society

    The project produced one new performance and recording of a hypothetical completion. Euronews states that humans chose and arranged the generated themes, and the BR-Klassik critic judged that the music sounds conventional.

The system’s part
The model generated candidate continuations of Beethoven's sketches. Humans (Werzowa and the musicologists) selected, arranged and orchestrated them, so the AI contribution was generative input within a human-curated process. The vendor-side accounts (Elgammal in The Conversation; Telekom) and the orchestra's own program page agree on the premiere.
Limits

What happened

Beethoven left only sketches for a Tenth Symphony. For his 250th anniversary, Deutsche Telekom funded a project to complete the work with artificial intelligence. A team led by musicologist Matthias Röder, with AI researcher Ahmed Elgammal and Mark Gotham, trained a model on Beethoven's output. The model generated continuations of the sketches. Composer Walter Werzowa and the musicologists chose and arranged the generated themes. On 9 October 2021 the Beethoven Orchester Bonn under Dirk Kaftan gave the world premiere of the completed symphony at the Telekom Forum in Bonn, in a concert that Deutsche Telekom organized. On 10 October 2021 BR-Klassik critic Henning Hübert reported that Werzowa had to sort through 200 to 300 unusable proposals for each new phrase. He judged that the result sounds conventional and rarely like Beethoven. He also reported that Kaftan told the premiere audience that the work is not Beethoven. Euronews reported on 13 October 2021 that the premiere had taken place in Bonn on 9 October. The benefit is a performed and recorded cultural work. No source claims that the result is Beethoven's own.

Details

AI system

Inputs and outputs

A model trained on Beethoven's works generated possible continuations of surviving sketches for the unfinished symphony.

Workflow

Beethoven's surviving sketches for the Tenth Symphony. A generative model trained on Beethoven's complete works. Human selection, arrangement and orchestration by Werzowa and the musicologists. A sponsor and a professional orchestra willing to premiere the result.

Human oversight

Composer Walter Werzowa and the musicologists chose and arranged the themes that the model generated, and the orchestra performed the result. The BR-Klassik critic reports that Werzowa had to sort through 200 to 300 unusable proposals for each new phrase. [1] [2] [3] [4] [5]
