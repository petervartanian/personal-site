About

I’m Peter H. Vartanian, and I want more people to benefit from AI that has already helped someone else.

Therefore, I have undertaken to build this database so that others can judge whether a similar use of AI could help them. I document AI’s benefits as carefully as others have documented its harms; in so doing, I (i) trace each claim to its source and (ii) grade it by who made it.

I admit a benefit only when I can identify the AI system that contributed to it (but benefits intended but not yet realized also qualify).

Because I record benefits alone, the database has no denominator: From it one can infer neither how often AI succeeds nor whether its goods outweigh its harms. The number of records measures the diligence of reporters, and my own, rather than the frequency of the “good.” A benefit absent from the database is, for all one knows, merely unrecorded.
