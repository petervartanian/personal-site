About

If AI has helped someone, I want others to have a chance to benefit, too.

The AI Benefits Database collects reported and intended benefits of AI, with the sources behind each case.

Why this database?

  • In 2024, AI-governance researchers Airlie Hilliard, Emre Kazim, and Stephan Ledain wrote in AI and Ethics that “there is a lack of an equivalent for positive outcomes” to the databases that document AI harms.
  • Following a joint workshop in 2024, Amazon Web Services and Minerva Technology Policy Advisors recommended establishing a centralized “AI Success Database” to “document and share positive case studies of AI adoption in the public sector.”
  • In 2026, Valerie Wirtschafter, a fellow at the Brookings Institution, called for “documenting and sharing AI success stories and lessons learned” in her assessment of federal AI adoption.
