Data

The dataset is one file, records.json, released under CC BY 4.0. Three flat tables are derived from it: records.csv, one row per record; ratings.csv, one row per stakeholder group rated; and echoes.csv, one row per attempt to make a benefit happen again. The schema describes every field. Each record is also available on its own page as JSON.

Counting

One system, one setting, one benefit is one record. A benefit reported in several sources is one record with several sources. A benefit that happened again elsewhere is an echo on the original record, not a new record.

Fields

id, version
A stable identifier and the version to cite.
title, summary, date_observed, account
What happened, in one line and in one paragraph, and when.
brief
Peter H. Vartanian's reading of the evidence and its gaps, where one has been written.
headline, metric
A short title that adds no fact, and the key quantified outcome copied verbatim from the summary or account (null when the record states no number).
category
The kind of event: benefit created, harm prevented, protective response (prevention unproven), or general benefit (no attributable event; kept off the scale).
unexpected, purpose
Whether the benefit was what the system was built to do, and what it was built to do.
system, model_version, developer, deployer, location, domain, segment
The system, its version where known, who made and ran it, and where it was used.
benefit_type, taxonomy_note
The benefit type from the taxonomy, with a note where the fit is loose.
ratings
For each stakeholder group that benefited, a depth from -1 to -5 or null for unrated, and the reason.
ai_role
What the system contributed, assessed separately, or marked not assessed.
evidence, confidence
The sources, each with kind, citation, link, date, a short quote, and who wrote it (the makers, the deployer, the beneficiary, or someone independent of them); confidence (high, medium, low) is computed from them as the method page describes.
replication
The conditions that produced the benefit, the conditions that must hold again, and the echoes (replications): each attempt to repeat it, with setting, model version, and outcome.
paired_incident
A comparable harm to the same stakeholder group in an incident database, with its severity where the database gives one and a one-sentence basis for the pairing.
registry_ids
Identifiers of the same event in other registries.
revisions
Every change, with its date, source, and editor.
