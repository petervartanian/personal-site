Reading the marks

Color and number show the depth of a benefit, from 1 to 5.

  1. A convenience, or time or cost saved.
  2. A measurable improvement in a result or capability.
  3. A lasting change, a capability restored, or a risk removed.
  4. A serious harm averted or a serious condition resolved in time.
  5. A life or livelihood preserved, or a change documented across a population.

A circle represents a person, a square an organization, and a triangle society. A solid fill means that a benefit was found or independently studied. A pale fill means independently reported. An outline means claimed by the parties.
