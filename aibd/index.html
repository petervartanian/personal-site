How to read the marks

The marks describe benefits that people have reported or hope to achieve.

Depth

The color and number show how much changes.

  1. 1

    A task becomes easier, or takes less time or money.

  2. 2

    Results or capabilities improve by a measurable amount.

  3. 3

    A change lasts, a lost ability returns, or a risk is removed.

  4. 4

    Serious harm is avoided, or a serious condition is resolved in time.

  5. 5

    A life or livelihood is preserved, or a change is documented across a population.

Gray means that the depth has not been established.

Who benefits

The shape shows who the benefit is for.

  • Circle: A person

  • Square: An organization

  • Triangle: Society

Evidence

The fill shows what evidence is available.

  • Solid means that a court or regulator has made a finding, or that an independent study or report supports the benefit.

  • Hatched means that an independent news report, preprint, or dataset supports the benefit.

  • Outline means that the cited sources do not meet either standard.
