Dataset editions

Each edition preserves the records and assessments as published. Each archived edition has its own DOI at Zenodo, and the DOI 10.5281/zenodo.23763115 resolves to the newest one. An edition marked as replaced was not archived.

An archived edition holds the data files as JSON and CSV with a manifest of their checksums, and every record page as published. Its identifier, such as 2026-10-30-946693e19720, is its date followed by the first 12 characters of the SHA-256 digest of the data files’ checksums, written as one line of JSON with sorted keys and a space after each comma and colon. The record pages are outside that digest.
