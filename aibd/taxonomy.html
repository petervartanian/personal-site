How a record enters

Benefit created or restored
The system produced an advantageous real-world change that was not principally the prevention of a specified imminent harm: an identifiable system, a real deployment, an identifiable beneficiary, an observed outcome.
Specific harm prevented
A dangerous pathway was operating, the system's intervention occurred, and the evidence supports that the intervention changed what happened.
Protective response, prevention unproven
The safeguard did something observable and appropriate, but no defensible counterfactual shows that harm would otherwise have occurred.
General benefit without an event
Launches, simulations, accuracy studies, aggregate claims, cohorts without an individually attributable changed outcome. Kept in the evidence base, off the depth scale.
