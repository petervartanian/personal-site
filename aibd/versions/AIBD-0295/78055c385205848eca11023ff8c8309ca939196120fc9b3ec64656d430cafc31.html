Dexcom G6's Urgent Low Soon alert may cut time below 54 mg/dL

Dexcom's analyses of 1,424 and 15,000 users link the G6's 20-minute Urgent Low Soon alert to less hypoglycemia; authors of an independent trial in 24 adults found a reduction only below 2.8 mmol/L.

Relation to AI

The alert is generated when the G6 algorithm forecasts glucose at or below 55 mg/dL within 20 minutes. Users still have to act on it. The principal outcome evidence is the maker's own real-world analyses (Puhr et al. 2019, 2020). They compare the same users before and after the G6 transition without a control arm. The independent Imperial College PACE randomized trial found no difference at 3.0 or 3.9 mmol/L after exercise and a difference only below 2.8 mmol/L. The Imperial switch study cannot separate the alert from the change of device. None of the sources describes the prediction method as machine learning.
