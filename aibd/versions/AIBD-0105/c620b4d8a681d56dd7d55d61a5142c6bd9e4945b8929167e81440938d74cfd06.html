Subscribing to GLAD alerts lowers deforestation risk in Africa by 18%

Four researchers, two of them from the operator and the producer of the alerts, found that subscriptions to GLAD Landsat alerts cut the probability of deforestation in Africa by 18%.

Missing evidence

The effect applies to areas in Africa with subscribers. The authors found no effect on other continents and no significant effect from the availability of alerts alone. The authors priced the modeled avoided emissions at the social cost of carbon and estimated their value at US$149 million to US$696 million. The Peruvian trial's estimate of a 52 percent cut is imprecise, with a confidence interval that includes zero. [1] [2] [3]
