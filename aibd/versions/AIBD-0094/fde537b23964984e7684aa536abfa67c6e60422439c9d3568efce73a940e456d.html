Nigeria uses machine-learning poverty maps to target COVID-19 cash aid

Nigeria used machine-learning poverty maps from satellite imagery to select 1,163 of its poorest urban wards for a COVID-19 cash transfer that counted 49,000 beneficiary households by 15 October 2021.

Reasons

People: Households in selected wards were beneficiaries of the government's COVID-19 cash transfer. The PNAS authors say that disbursement began in mid-January 2021. The World Bank's status report records 49,000 beneficiary households as of 15 October 2021 without saying how many had been paid. Organizations: The National Social Safety Nets Coordinating Office stood up an urban registry within months through self-registration by mobile phone in the wards selected with the poverty map. The World Bank's appraisal document of November 2021 reports over 1.75 million registrations. Society: The sources report that the 1,163 selected wards, with over 20 million residents, were made eligible, and the 61.7% cut in inclusion errors is a simulation estimate. No source reports a realized change across that population.
