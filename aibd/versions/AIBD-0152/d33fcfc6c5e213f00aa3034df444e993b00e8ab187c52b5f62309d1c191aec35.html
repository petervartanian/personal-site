Relation to AI

The system's contribution was the trace. Police searched it in the direction the family gave. It returned images of the man walking along Nguyễn Tuân street toward Thanh Xuân Park and across other streets. That gave the police a route and pictures for a public appeal. A passing resident found him in Dịch Vọng about six hours and 45 minutes after the report. No source says the finder had seen the appeal or that officers reached him. So the discovery itself is not attributable to the system. The vendor is not named and every account comes from the police.

Purpose

Hanoi's AI traffic-camera system has 1,837 cameras with AI-controlled signals and was due to enter official operation in December 2025, per VOV. The Traffic Police run it, in VOV's words, “to ensure security and order and support the people” as well as to manage traffic. Tracing a lost resident for a ward police search falls within that stated remit.
