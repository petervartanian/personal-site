A Navy Corsair sea drone picked up two Apache crew off Oman and carried them to a helicopter hoist; CENTCOM reported that both were rescued within about two hours. Hawkins identified an unmanned Corsair as the recovery vessel. DefenseScoop describes the platform’s autonomous capabilities, but its suggestion that passive sensing helped locate the crew is an inference. The official sources do not identify the navigation mode, human supervision, or sensor contribution during this rescue. A crewed helicopter completed the recovery.
