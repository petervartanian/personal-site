Amazon Mining Watch maps 496,000 hectares of mining on protected lands

Amazon Mining Watch uses an AI detector to map gold mining, and its report shows 496,000 hectares affected between 2018 and 2025 in protected areas and Indigenous territories of the Amazon.

Workflow

Relation to AI

The convolutional networks produce the maps and area statistics. People hand-label the training patches, and Mongabay reports that partners on the ground feed information back into the platform. LatAm Journalism Review reports that journalists and NGO observers supply the context needed to interpret the output. The makers report holdout specificity of 0.976 and sensitivity of 0.991.
