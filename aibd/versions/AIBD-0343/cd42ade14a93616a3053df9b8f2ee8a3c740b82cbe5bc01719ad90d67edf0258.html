Relation to AI

UNEP reports that MARS detected the plume from satellite data and alerted the operator, who repaired a failed valve; ground optical gas imaging confirmed that the leak had stopped. At program level, UNEP's July 2026 report, as relayed by ForkLog, states that its algorithms flag 80 to 85% of confirmed emissions before experts do and that experts verify every alert. No source isolates the AI contribution to this alert, and the repair was the operator's work.
