Relation to AI

Fajgenbaum said that he had considered three drugs used for multiple myeloma and that Every Cure’s AI algorithm also scored them highly for POEMS syndrome. In an earlier AAMC interview, he described reviewing the scores and recommending the drugs to Coates’s treating physician. Wayne Gao weighed the risks and administered the treatment. The AI therefore supported the physicians’ assessment of candidate drugs. The sources do not show that it independently devised the regimen or that the benefit would have been impossible without it. The Lancet Haematology commentary contains a brief vignette of a matching unnamed POEMS patient, explicitly labeled unpublished data. No detailed clinical case report, underlying medical records, or case-specific model output was located.
