Brief

AI analysis of 2,600 drone frames flagged a red helmet in the Perotti couloir at 3,150 m; Ivaldo's body was recovered by 31 July 2025. The retained reports support locating and recovering a body, not rescuing a living person. They disagree on the disappearance date and age. ADIAT's identity rests on TEXSAR's attribution, while the local rescue reports describe unnamed software; the AI's incremental advantage over human inspection remains testimonial.
